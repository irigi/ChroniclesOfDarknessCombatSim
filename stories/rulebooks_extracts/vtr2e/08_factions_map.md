weirdness around them (player's choice of expression); Ventrue care about Dynasty Membership; Mekhet often Cacophony Savvy.

### Lost / legendary clans (p. 28–30) — rumor only
- **Akhud**: cursed kings of Sodom/Gomorrah, diablerie-soaked; mission to destroy all vampires; blood-promise against betrayal. Possibly linked to VII rumors.
- **Julii**: builders of the Camarilla, wiped out completely. Cautionary.
- **Pijavica**: Slavic strain with larval life cycle (bloated corpse → crawling blood mass → human form in 40 nights); extinct by late 19th c.

## Regional clan: Jiang Shi ("Phantoms") [Ch6 Triangle, p. 254–255]
Origin unknown (draugr? Hollow? Strix-like). **Embrace** = replace a person's blood with the blood of someone
extraordinarily pure (tasting tells suitability; usually two murders); also taking the donor's heart → **start at BP 2**.
Bite gives numbing cold, not euphoria; low Vitae → claw fingers, green mold (touching it chills). **1 WP: return to
their "grave"** (death site); not sleeping there → **2 Vitae to wake**; move grave via heart-transplant ritual.
**Bane (Ephemeral Curse)**: can't cause Swooning or Scarred by feeding. **Favored: Intelligence or Dexterity.
Disciplines: Animalism, Obfuscate, Celerity.** Common in Raleigh (rival the Mekhet), rare elsewhere (E. Asia, Iceland).

### Tokyo zaibatsu (each needs Zaibatsu Status 1 for its advantage) [p. 266–269]
- **Hototogisu** (mortal conglomerate that knows of Kindred; Status works like Mystery Cult Initiation): 1 Occult
  (Vampires) Specialty · 2 it acts as Interdisciplinary Specialty for Kindred influence · 3 **Indomitable** · 4 **Iron Will**
  and immune to 3rd-stage bonds · 5 craft charms (1 h, 1 WP, Int + Occult; 1 night/success) making the holder
  indistinguishable from a vampire.
- **Maeda** ("Towers"/"Cockroaches"; enforcers): Mind of Infinite Immutability — **+Maeda Status to Resistance-Attribute
  rolls; resisted rolls vs them −Status**.
- **Takahashi Family** ("The Family"): free Retainer, Herd and Haven dots = Zaibatsu Status; 90-member pyramid.
- **Ume House** ("Plum House"; spiritual): **Kigan** blood sorcery = Crúac rules, incompatible with Crúac teachers.
- Also: a dozen Lancea (anti-zaibatsu), independents, Nosferatu tunnel-equality.
