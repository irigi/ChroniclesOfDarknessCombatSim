# VtR 2e — Lore: how Kindred and their world work

Mechanics are summarised here with pointers; full procedures in `05_systems.md`.

## A. The All Night Society (Ch2, pp. 52–75) — setting facts
- **Needs**: blood + shelter from sun. Failing to feed → eventually torpor [p. 55].
- **The Kiss** [p. 55]: fangs descend; bite + Vitae traces cause euphoria in the victim; licking the wound closes it.
  Victim's memory of the feeding is hazy. The Kiss weakens the victim's mind to the vampire's will (diluted Curse).
  Using the Kiss on another vampire is possible "if one pushes" but is a grave social insult [p. 72].
- **Sunlight** [p. 56]: dawn compels sleep; young vampires blister and burn slowly, older ones may catch fire.
- **Lost Visage** [p. 60]: vampires DO have reflections, but are never quite seen in mirrors/photos (blurred,
  averted, over-exposed) — the Beast "knows where to stand". Not a hard power, a defence mechanism.
- **Ghouls** [p. 58–59]: mortals fed Vitae; get some powers, few weaknesses; become addicted (need Vitae, not human
  blood). Vitae can be slipped in many ways (kiss, spit, food, chalice, wound-to-wound).
- **The Traditions** [p. 59–61]:
  1. **Masquerade** — do not reveal your nature to those not of the Blood. Belief doesn't matter; *knowledge* does.
     Local, not a global conspiracy.
  2. **Progeny** — sire another at the peril of both; the sire pays a toll to the Beast (Embrace is a breaking point
     → `05_systems.md`).
  3. **Amaranth** — do not devour Kindred heartsblood (diablerie); victim's personality haunts the diablerist.
  Debate whether they are mystically enforced or just common sense.
- **Elysium** [p. 64–65]: neutral ground, no physical violence; Disciplines/treachery not forbidden; feeding often banned.
- **Domain governments** [p. 65–66]: Princedom (prince, Primogen/elders' council, Harpies, Sheriff & Hounds), Coalition,
  Theocracy (Lancea or Crone), **Cloister** (city cut off; e.g. Malkavia quarantine "Bedlam City").
- **Cacophony** [p. 68–69]: Kindred underground culture/communication network (graffiti, zines, web); started by young
  Invictus for Masquerade messages; renaissance in 1960s. Merit: Cacophony Savvy.
- **Conclaves** [p. 69]: multi-city cliques, not governments.
- **Danse Macabre strata** [p. 69–70]: neonate (<50 yrs; "coyote stories"), ancilla (50–150 yrs; politics), elder
  (>150 yrs; ideas, alien), ancient (>1000 yrs).
- **Malkavia** [p. 70–71]: contagious madness in the Blood (vector unknown, may lie dormant generations); named for
  "patient zero" Malkav. Story hook.
- **Predatory aura** [p. 71–72] ("Predatory Note"): Beasts recognise each other on meeting → posture, fight, submit.
  Rules: `05_systems.md` §Predatory Aura. **Blood sympathy** [p. 72]: related Kindred feel each other; easier to affect
  with powers/sorcery. **Cannibalistic note**: old vampires eventually must feed on Kindred (BP feeding restrictions).
- **Taboos** [p. 72]: Kissing Kindred; **perversion** = reciprocal blood bond between vampires (frowned on).
- **Solace** [p. 73]: rumored silver-syrette drug that makes a vampire alive for one night. Rumor/hook.
- **Burlesque Grotesque** [p. 69]: travelling performance conclave of Nosferatu/Gangrel etc.

## C. The Kindred condition (Ch3 rules summary; procedures in `05_systems.md`)
- **Embrace** [p. 93]: drain, then feed Vitae; a deliberate choice that costs the sire 1 Humanity. Never accidental —
  except **posthumous Embraces** (anyone who ever drank Vitae, dead < a week) and **revenants** (sireless, BP 1 fixed,
  wake starved; can be uplifted). Longinus and Dracula were Embraced sirelessly (curse of God, per legend).
- **Vitae** [p. 98]: the "pigment" in the blood; spending Vitae doesn't make a vampire lose blood. Vampires don't bleed
  when cut (except with the blush of life — inert blood). Severed parts take their Vitae with them. Smells sweet, like
  dessert wine, syrupy and dark.
- **Blood Potency** [p. 89]: power of the Curse. Rises with **age (≈ +1 per 50 active years)**, XP, diablerie; falls in
  torpor (−1 / 25 years). High BP restricts diet: **BP 3+ animals no longer nourish; BP 6+ only Kindred/unnatural blood**
  → the "cannibalistic note": elders must eventually feed on Kindred; many sleep in torpor to thin their blood.
- **Damage** [p. 90]: corpse bodies shrug off mundane trauma (bashing); only fire, sun, and certain powers cause
  aggravated harm — unnatural-looking wounds (burning paper, bubbling, black veins). Last box lethal → torpor; last box
  agg → Final Death; body rots/dusts to its true age.
- **Daysleep** [p. 98]: soul drags them to sleep at dawn; hard to wake; every waking costs 1 Vitae. Body reverts to its
  Embrace state while asleep (hair/nails regrow; unhealed wounds heal using Vitae).
- **Torpor** [p. 105]: death-like sleep (skin tightens, joints stiffen, ashen dust); nonsensical dreams keep the mind
  active so elders wake adaptable (sometimes a new person or with distorted memories). Rumored bloodlines read torpor
  dreams (haruspices). A stake through the heart = torpor until removed.
- **Sunlight** = "Pain of Purity" (truth/visibility; Lancea: God's judgment) — worse for inhumane and potent Kindred.
  **Fire** = "Pain of Knowledge" (humanity's tool) [p. 101–102].
- **The Beast & frenzy** [p. 103]: rage, fear (Rötschreck-equivalent, triggered by fire/sun/Frightened), hunger.
  Frenzying Beast is cunning like a predator animal; can't be talked/Dominated out of its desire, only redirected.
- **Humanity** [p. 105–107]: 10 Ascetic … 7–8 Humane … 5–6 Balanced (most neonates) … 4 Weathered (most ancillae) …
  3 Callous (most elders) … 2 Monstrous … 1 Animalistic … 0 **draugr** (lost to the Beast; usually destroyed on sight).
  Low Humanity looks corpse-like (ashen pallor → red-tinged eyes, tight dry skin).
- **Banes** [p. 108]: spiritual scars from detachment — up to 3 (Bells, Invitation, Repulsion of garlic/silver…).
  Explains folklore weaknesses (many folklore weaknesses are actually Strix banes, p. 198).
- **Clan banes** kick in when Humanity first falls to 6 (Ventrue's Touchstone rule applies from creation).
- **Ghouls** [p. 100, 290–300]: mortals given Vitae + Willpower; unaging, heal with Vitae, small Disciplines; addicted
  and usually blood-bound; valued property ("attacking a ghoul = attacking the haven"). Carthian **Anti-Obstructionist
  Army** targets vampires who keep large ghoul stables.
- **Blood bond / Vinculum** [p. 99]: three drinks → infatuation; lasts a year since last drink; stage 3 blocks other
  bonds (some Kindred deliberately bond to an ally to be immune). Mutual = **perversion**.
- **Diablerie / Amaranth** [p. 101]: drink past Final Death and devour the soul; gains BP-XP and a Discipline dot;
  always a breaking point; victim's soul haunts (Tainted); automatically addictive. Auspex shows diablerie in the aura
  (Uncanny Perception question) — and a Strix in a host looks identical.
- **Lashing out** (predatory aura) — monstrous / seductive / competitive Beast; Kindred society's courtesy exists to keep
  auras in check [p. 91–93].

## D. History & cosmology in brief
- **Camarilla**: first covenant, Rome; ended with Rome (the Lancea "presided over its fall"); Invictus claims to be its
  continuation; Julii (lost clan) built it and were wiped out [p. 28–29, 37–42].
- **Lancea et Sanctum**: Longinus transfigured by Christ's blood; *Testament of Longinus*; a Sanctified bishop in
  Constantinople formulated the **Traditions** and enforced the Masquerade [p. 41–42].
- **Theban Sorcery**: St. Daniel led by angel "Amoniel" to a cavern under Thebes [p. 150].
- **Circle of the Crone**: <200 years old; pagan coteries of Scotland/Ireland resisting forced Sanctified conversion;
  Crúac synthesised via the Cacophony [p. 35–36].
- **Carthians**: 1779 pamphlet *Contre Les Vampires Patriarcals* by "E. B. Carth" (Eric Giraud, d. 1790s); Carthian Law
  since late 20th c. [p. 32].
- **Ordo Dracul**: Vlad Tepes cursed God in 1476 and rose sireless; Kogaions; "Rites of the Dragon"; Coils [p. 43–46].
- **Strix**: owl-shadow vampires older than humanity; claim to be the Beast/creators of Kindred; legends say the Julii
  bargained with them; 1738 Transylvanian war (Kindred won); parliaments precede disaster; VII murders follow sightings
  [p. 197–200].
- **Other WoD beings** acknowledged: werewolves (feral aura raises Kindred goosebumps), mages, fae, ghosts, spirits,
  the God-Machine (one VII rumor) [p. 46–48, 92].
