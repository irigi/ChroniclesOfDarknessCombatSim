# VtR 2e — Character creation, advancement, derived traits

## Steps [VtR pp. 79–82; quick ref p. 82]
1. **Concept** + **3 Aspirations** (1–2 Kindred-focused, ≥1 about the mortal world; at least one easy) [p. 79].
2. **Attributes** 5/4/3 across Mental/Physical/Social priorities; **each Attribute starts with 1 free dot** [p. 79–80].
3. **Skills** 11/7/4 (no free dots) [p. 80].
4. **3 Skill Specialties** [p. 80].
5. **Kindred template** [p. 80–81]:
   - **Clan** → 3 in-clan Disciplines + **one favored Attribute: +1 free dot** (can raise to 5, not beyond) [p. 80]:
     Daeva **Dex or Manip**; Gangrel Comp or Sta; Mekhet Int or Wits; Nosferatu Comp or Str; Ventrue Pres or Res.
   - **Covenant** (optional; unaligned allowed). Covenant benefits need **≥1 dot Covenant Status** [p. 80]:
     Carthian → Carthian Law Merits (p. 115); Crone → Crúac (p. 150); Invictus → Oaths (p. 116); Lancea → Theban
     Sorcery (p. 150); Ordo → Coils & Scales (p. 154).
   - **Mask** and **Dirge** (replace Virtue/Vice) — archetype list below [p. 81, 84–87].
   - **Touchstone**: name one, written next to **Humanity dot 6** [p. 81, 87]. More via Touchstone Merit.
   - **Blood Potency 1 free**; may buy +1 or +2 dots at **5 Merit dots each** (max 2 bought → BP 3 max) [p. 81, 89].
   - **Disciplines: 3 dots**, ≥2 in-clan; 3rd anywhere (justify out-of-clan). With starting Covenant Status in Crone /
     Lancea / Ordo, that dot may be Crúac / Theban / a Coil [p. 81].
6. **Merits: 10 dots** [p. 81]. Kindred may take CoD core / God-Machine Merits **except Supernatural Merits** and
   Vice-Ridden / Virtuous. Kindred Merits p. 109ff (`04_merits.md`).
7. **Advantages** [p. 81–82]:
   - **Willpower = Resolve + Composure.**
   - **Humanity = 7.**
   - **Size 5.** **Health = Size + Stamina + Resilience.**
   - **Speed = Size + Strength + Dexterity (+ Vigor)** (quick-ref p. 82 includes Vigor).
   - **Defense = min(Wits, Dexterity) + Athletics.**
   - **Initiative mod = Dexterity + Composure.**

Beats otherwise); choosing a dramatic failure on a failed roll; taking **lethal** damage in one of the rightmost Health
boxes (**vampires get no Beat from bashing**); end of session; **risking detachment** (p. 107).

### Teachers & Vitae for Disciplines [p. 83–84]
- In-clan Disciplines: no teacher. **Anyone** may self-teach **Animalism, Celerity, Obfuscate, Resilience, Vigor**.
- Out-of-clan **Auspex, Dominate, Majesty, Nightmare, Protean** need a teacher **and drinking the teacher's Vitae**
  (any vampire with a higher rating; normal Vitae risks — blood bond steps).
- Coils, Crúac, Theban need teachers but no Vitae. **Bloodline signature Disciplines can't be learned** by outsiders, even via diablerie.

### Blood Potency growth — AGE, not just XP [p. 84, 89]  ← common trap
- **Every ~50 years active (awake), BP rises by 1 automatically.** XP (5/dot) can raise it faster. Diablerie can too.
- **Every 25 years in torpor, BP drops by 1.** Elders often sleep to reduce BP and ease feeding.
- So an elder's BP is mostly **time-granted** — on a sheet, show BP from age separately from XP-bought BP.
  Example: Embraced 1789, ~150 years active (rest in torpor) → +3 BP from age (BP 4); if 75 of the years were torpor,
  net −3 → ST adjudicates [interp]. Starting-character XP packages (table above) don't automatically grant BP; ST may
  grant age-appropriate BP for free in addition to bonus XP **[interp, consistent with p. 84/89 RAW that BP rises by
  time]**.
- When BP drops below 6, Attributes/Skills above 5 are **not** lost [p. 89].

## Masks & Dirges — anchors [p. 84–87]
- Three anchors: **Mask, Dirge, Touchstone(s)**. Each anchor: minor act → **+1 WP**; harsh act → **all WP**. Full refresh
  **once per chapter** per character (not per anchor) [p. 84].
- Mask: overcome a small hurdle defending the Mask → +1 WP; atrocious/existentially risky act defending it → all WP.
- Dirge: withdraw from outside life to defend truer self → +1 WP; terrible damning act → all WP.
- Touchstone: defend attachment → +1 WP; defense causes serious harm → all WP [p. 88].
- Archetypes (single WP / all WP examples on pp. 85–87): Authoritarian, Child, Competitor, Conformist, Conspirator,
  **Courtesan** (put yourself out for another's pleasure / be the last one dancing when the party's on fire), Cult Leader,
  Deviant, Follower, Guru, Idealist, Jester, Junkie, Martyr, Masochist, Monster, Nomad, Nurturer, Perfectionist,
  Penitent, Questioner, Rebel, Scholar, Social Chameleon, Spy, Survivor, Visionary. **Authoritarian**: shut down an
  inferior trying to rise / commit a widespread hit against a rival's mortal connections.

## Touchstones [p. 87–88]
- Usually a living mortal (rarely place/thing). First at Humanity 6. **Attached** while the Humanity dot beside it is filled.
- **Detachment roll bonus: +2 with one attached Touchstone, +3 with several; −2 with none** [p. 88].
- Losing the **last** Touchstone (e.g. it dies, attached or not): either lose 1 Humanity now and find a replacement within
  a month (else **Languid** Condition), or take **Languid** immediately [p. 88].
- New Touchstones need the Touchstone Merit; replacing a lost one requires gaining a Humanity dot. Merit Touchstones obey
  Sanctity of Merits [p. 88].
- Examples: the Ex, Former Partner, Friend With Benefits, Gravesite, High School Sweetheart, House of Birth, Intrepid
  Detective, Murderer, One That Got Away, Orphan, Spouse, Terminal Parent, Therapist, Victim's Lover, Witness.
