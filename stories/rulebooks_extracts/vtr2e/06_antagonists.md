# VtR 2e — Antagonists: Strix, sample NPCs, mortals & ghouls

Generic mortal templates (beat cop etc.), ghosts/spirits, Horrors → `../cod_core/06_antagonists.md`.

## A. The Strix ("Owls", "Birds of Dis", "Nemeses") [Ch5, pp. 197–237]
### Nature [p. 197–200]
- Owl-shaped smoke/shadow, never human; feed on **breath/life** as Vitae; possess corpses and **Kindred** to feel flesh.
  Coldly evil, hate the living and despise Kindred Humanity as denial of kinship. Claim to be the Beast / to have taught
  the first Kindred. Rare; most are patient infiltrators, a minority are rampaging.
- **Tells**: eyes **glow yellow** when light catches them (any form); a "vampire" **unharmed in sunlight** is possessed
  (host uses Strix banes; fire still burns the body). **Auspex reads a Strix-in-host exactly like a diablerist.** An
  elder suddenly able to feed on animals/mortals is likely possessed (Strix have no feeding restrictions).
- **Killing a possessing Strix**: destroy the body while it is completely surrounded by one of its banes — in practice,
  **burn the body to ash with no part exposed**.
- Many mortal vampire legends (crosses, running water, silver, garlic, thresholds) actually describe **Strix banes**,
  spread through the Cacophony as warnings.
- **Parliaments** (gatherings) precede calamity. VII murders follow Strix sightings (p. 46–48).
- Not spirits/ghosts/demons: immune to powers affecting those; not in Twilight (unless Twilight Form). Powers affecting
  shadows can work. **Blood sorcery can summon Strix** as if they heard the Gathering Cry [p. 203].
- Deals with Strix aren't supernaturally binding; "grant me power" ≈ "please possess me" [p. 209].
### Traits [p. 200–204]
- **Shadow Potency (SP) 1–10** = Supernatural Tolerance (substitutes BP vs/with Disciplines, Clash of Wills). +1 per
  100 years; or via diablerie (in a host, or devouring another Strix). Doesn't sleep/torpor. May split off a child:
  roll SP, successes become child's SP (lost by parent); at SP 10 must split.
- **Attributes**: Power (Int, Pres, and Str when unhoused), Finesse (Wits, Manip, Dex unhoused), Resistance (Res, Comp,
  Sta unhoused). **In a host: host's Physical Attributes, own Power/Finesse/Resistance for Mental/Social.**
  All Strix: **Brawl 2, Athletics 2, Occult 2**; total Skill dots ≤ Attribute-dot maximum, none > 5; learn Skills by
  feeding/reading hosts.
- **Willpower** = Finesse + Resistance (max 10); regain 1 WP when it perceives sunset (not if underground all night);
  3 Vitae → 1 WP once per scene; heal Corpus with WP like Vitae. Single Vice. Never accepts surrender; no WP cost to
  attack. **No Humanity/Integrity — use SP** where needed. Aspirations exist for Social maneuvering.
- **Corpus** = Resistance + Size, no wound penalties, no natural healing; never unconscious; destroyed only when all
  boxes aggravated. **Immune to all physical & ephemeral attacks** except **Disciplines/Devotions dealing aggravated
  (which deal bashing to Strix)** and some banes. Lethal/agg in Shadow Form also drain 1 Vitae per box. In a host, the
  **host** takes the injury.
- **Derived**: Initiative = Finesse + Resistance; Defense = higher of Power/Finesse (Shadow Form) or min(Dex, Finesse) +
  Athletics (host); **Speed 12 flying**, pours through any visible gap; Size 2 (+1 per SP above 7); **+3 Stealth**;
  telepathic speech by eye contact (eyes flash yellow).
- **Vitae**: per SP table; may spend 1 Vitae for **+2 to any Attribute** for a turn; no Vitae to wake; can heal a host
  like a vampire; can't make ghouls unless in a vampire host. **Immune to blood addiction and Vinculum** always.
- **Feeding** (Shadow Form, or Breath Eater): perch within a foot of the mouth, grapple & "bite" with **Power + Brawl**,
  then **Breath Drinking move**: win grapple → steal 1 Vitae + 1 lethal (exceptional: up to SP Vitae/damage, or steal
  a Skill dot); loses Defense and can't use powers meanwhile; no euphoria; conscious victims gain **Scarred**; ghouls
  lose Vitae first; can't drink vampires/revenants this way (Vitae bound to blood); soulless victims give nothing.
  Strix-on-Strix: merge (grapple), both feed each turn, then diablerie (WP + Power + Resistance vs victim SP) → +1 SP.
- **Predatory aura**: Strix trigger and sense it (even in Shadow Form); Kindred can't tell a Strix from a vampire by aura
  (probably think "Obfuscate"). Strix lash out with **Power + SP**. **Never frenzy.** Strix resolve Wanton by forcing a
  breaking point on someone.

### Banes [p. 202–203]
- **Common**: **sunlight and fire don't burn but are impenetrable barriers** — the Strix is forced back by any means
  (through gaps, or into a host). If it can't escape: **1 lethal to Corpus per minute**. A host shields it; a host body
  destroyed while fully engulfed ejects the Strix into the flames (dies as a Kindred would).
- **Uncommon** (from SP 3): Abjuration (as spirit; ends Possess), Bells (SP dice bashing/min), Counting, Crossroads
  (pools capped by SP), Hated by Beasts (−SP Animal Ken/Animalism), Invitation (private dwelling uninvited = sunlight),
  Loner (flee other Strix within SP yd), **Material Bane** (garlic/salt/roses/silver: repelled like sunlight; as a weapon
  deals **lethal even in Shadow Form**; most common), Plague of Purity (Integrity 8+ touch: SP dice bashing), Ravening
  (can't stop feeding SP turns), **Running Water** (= sunlight; immersed: 1 lethal/turn), **Symbols** (faith symbols:
  pools capped by SP; contact 1 bashing/turn).
### Common powers [p. 203–204]
**Gathering Cry** (1 WP; screech heard by Strix, some animals, children, keen-eared Kindred within Resistance + SP
miles) · **Owl Eyes** (no darkness penalty; +3 sight/perception with any light) · **Taint of Life** (sense living beings
& Vitae within SP × 2 yd; +Finesse + SP to track anyone it has fed on) · **Doom Sense** (calamity within SP months;
always aware of luck/fate powers active nearby).
### Embodiments [p. 204–208]
- **Shadow Form** (SP 1): native state.
- **Possess Corpse** (SP 2): 1 WP, 1 turn, no roll; any corpse not yet bare skeleton. Host Physical Attributes; damage
  to body's Health (Sta + Size), **always bashing** unless a bane says otherwise; never unconscious until all agg. No
  memories/skills from a dead brain (Spirit's Touch excepted). Feeds by eating flesh (1 Vitae per 2 lethal inflicted) or
  drinking vampire blood (like a ghoul, no bond). Decay: −1 each Physical Attribute per week (Str 0: can't lift/damage;
  Dex 0: can't move); then Health −1/week; collapse ejects it. If destroyed by damage: final turn's damage < SP →
  ejected unharmed; else that damage also hits Corpus. Exit: 1 turn.
- **Possess Revenant** (SP 3) / **Possess Kindred** (SP 4; the Kindred **must be torpid**, not just asleep):
  **1 WP + Finesse + Power + SP vs Resolve + Composure + BP**, 1 turn, within a foot. Dramatic: Strix takes victim's BP
  in lethal. Exceptional: no penalties using host's Skills/Disciplines. Victim in torpor-dreams; **host's banes replaced
  by the Strix's** (no sun damage, staking only physical damage, no torpor at full lethal — only full agg stops it).
  Uses SP for Vitae max/per turn; inherits host's Vitae up to max. First night: read mind Finesse −4, host Physical
  Skills −3, Disciplines & Mental/Social Skills −4; penalties drop 1 per night. Can use fangs and the Kiss, feeds
  without BP restriction, never bonded/addicted. Can commit diablerie (host gets the gains and Humanity loss afterwards).
  Kindred hosts: no blood sorcery, Coils, Carthian Law, Oaths (Oath partner loses benefits). Embraces from a Strix-host
  usually make revenants. Exit: 1 turn; roll host BP − Strix Resistance → Strix leaves that many Vitae; host wakes as
  from torpor (−1 BP per 25 years possessed, min 1; pays 1 Vitae).
- **Possess Living** (SP 5): 1 WP, instant, **Power + Finesse + SP vs Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance**.
  Success deals successes in lethal to host and gives that much Vitae (exceptional: may choose no harm/no Vitae). Host
  dies → becomes Possess Corpse (decay per month). Can drain host 1 lethal per Vitae, farm it; heal as a ghoul.
  Other creatures: Sin-Eaters/Mummies/Prometheans need Possess Kindred; Werewolves/Mages/Changelings/Demons need Possess
  Living; no soul-powers.
- **Synthesis** (SP 6): while embodied, 1 WP, extended SP dice (max SP rolls, 1/night): corpse 5 / revenant 10 / Kindred
  15 / living 20 successes. Then rolls Power + Finesse, loses that many SP (0 = destroyed): corpse → Kindred BP 1 (−Humanity
  = SP lost); revenant → full Kindred; **Kindred gain BP = SP lost, lose same Humanity**; living take lethal = SP lost, if
  killed rise as Kindred BP 1. Clan from last vampire it drank from etc.
- **Materialize** (SP 7): 1 WP, scene: solid; **talons 3L, beak bite 2L**; takes bashing from non-banes to Corpus; heal
  with Vitae or WP.
- **Twilight Form** (SP 8): 1 WP; intangible in Twilight; bane damage −1 step; hunt ghosts/spirits (1 Vitae per 3
  Corpus eaten); leaving: Power + Resistance and −1 SP.
- **Shadow Jump** (SP 9): roll SP, exit any shadow within SP miles; 1 lethal per mile + 1 (in host −3, host takes it,
  lose equal Vitae).
- **Dark Places** (SP 10): gateway to the Strix realm in total darkness for a scene (1 WP to cross).
- Embracing a Strix-occupied body just gives the Strix a Kindred host [p. 201].

## B. Ghouls (Appendix One "The Living", pp. 288–300)
### Creating & traits [p. 297]
- Vampire feeds **1 Vitae and invests 1 WP**; the ghoul gets both (the Vitae and the WP point). (Also p. 101.)
- Build as a mortal (CoD core), then add: **regnant** (whose clan matters), **2 dots of Disciplines in-clan for the
  regnant** (physical recommended), **Blood Potency 0** (Vitae pool, can't rise). BP 0 row: Vitae max = **Stamina**,
  1 per turn (p. 90).
- **Effects**: no aging; diseases halt; **Physical Intensity** as Kindred; **heal 1 Vitae = 2 bashing or 1 lethal; 1 agg
  = 5 Vitae over two consecutive nights** (not limited by per-turn); **don't fall unconscious from full bashing**; may
  attempt **A Taste of Blood** (no Kindred Senses). Kiss/Assault: ghouls count as "mortal" (p. 94).
- **Disciplines**: learn like Kindred (no Coils/Scales); **can't add Discipline dots to contest/resist a vampire's
  Discipline, and Disciplines automatically fail vs a vampire they are bonded to (any stage)**; can't use powers that
  require spilling/feeding blood (may buy them as stepping stones).
- **Fix**: auto-spend **1 Vitae per month**; none → **Deprived**, ages 1 year/week toward true age (kept); end withdrawal
  by drinking Vitae = **BP of the last vampire dosed from** (excess over Stamina retched up); in withdrawal no Disciplines.
  (p. 101: a month without Vitae loses all gifts.)
- **Ghoul Retainer**: Retainer + regnant Discipline dots (1/2/3 at Retainer 1–2/3–4/5) [p. 100].
- **Ghouled animals**: 1 dot of a physical clan Discipline; spend Vitae for physical boosts; keep last Vitae for deadly wounds.
- **Ghoul families** [p. 296]: age slowly without Vitae, can have children, sense Kindred (like Unseen Sense); some keep
  Disciplines after Vitae runs out / inherit them.
- **Blood bond in play** [p. 298]: no loss of free will; agrees more, argues less; harming the regnant takes heroic effort.
- Society: ghouls count as slightly above mortals; attacking a ghoul often treated like attacking the owner's haven [p. 101].

## C. Mortal threats & story hooks (App. 1, p. 288–289)
Masquerade handling (reporter shadowing, someone who was told everything), blood dolls, dressage kine (groomed for
Embrace), family/friends, shadow-crossed lovers. Use `../cod_core/06_antagonists.md` for mortal stat blocks (police
officer, SWAT, etc.). **VtR core contains no police/hunter stat blocks.**
