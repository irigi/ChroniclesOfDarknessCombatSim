# VtR 2e — Merits [VtR pp. 109–125]

Rules: covenant-prereq Merits need **1 dot of that Covenant Status**; leaving the covenant doesn't remove them [p. 109].
Kindred may also take core/GMC Merits **except Supernatural Merits, Vice-Ridden, Virtuous** [p. 81].
**Sanctity of Merits** [p. 109]: lost Merit dots can be re-spent at chapter end; may cash in for XP with ST ok; not for
innate Merits (Ambidextrous, Eidetic Memory, Fighting Styles). Mortal/ghoul-only Merits → `06_antagonists.md` (App. 1).

### Kindred Status [p. 113]
Three types — **City, Clan, Covenant** — each works as the Status Merit (core) within its sphere. Status ≠ title
(a Prince may have City 3). Combined dots in one type ≤ 5; Status in a covenant you're not in can't equal your own
covenant's. Can't have Status in another clan.

## Kindred Fighting Merits [p. 117–118]
**Kindred Dueling** (Style 1–5; Composure 3, Weaponry 2; edged weapon; one maneuver at a time):
1 Hamstring — specified-limb hit denies Physical Intensity on that limb for a turn. 2 Taunt — remove up to Weaponry
dice; on hit victim resists frenzy at −(removed). 3 Carving — weapon damage is **lethal to Kindred**. 4 Pincushion —
leave blade in: wound can't be healed with Vitae; others remove with Str + Sta − damage (instant). 5 Spray — sacrifice
Defense; split damage between Health and Vitae drained.
**Riding the Wave** (Style 1–5; Composure 3, Resolve 3; only while riding the wave): 1 Ravage — fangs **1L**. 2 Primal
Strength — double frenzy Str bonus for lifting/jumping/breaking. 3 In the Zone — WP spent on riding rolls also +3 to
the turn's action. 4 Unyielding — regain WP spent to start the ride after a successful ride. 5 Animal Grace — WP to
attack/defend gives both: +2 Defense and +3 attack.
