# VtR 2e — Disciplines, Devotions, Blood Sorcery, Coils [VtR pp. 125–159]

## 0. General rules [p. 125–126]
- Pool = **Attribute + Skill + Discipline dots** (full rating for every power of that Discipline). Can't use a power
  above your rating. Failures still show a minor uncanny sign.
- In-clan list: Daeva Cel/Maj/Vig; Gangrel Ani/Pro/Res; Mekhet Aus/Cel/Obf; Nosferatu Nig/Obf/Vig; Ventrue Ani/Dom/Res.
  Learning another clan's unique Discipline needs drinking ≥1 Vitae of a user (see `02_character_creation.md`).
- **Contested vs supernatural**: Kindred add **BP** to resist (e.g. Dominate: Resolve + BP); mortals use the Attribute
  alone; other creatures use their equivalent (Primal Urge, Azoth, Gnosis…). Vampires add BP to their Resistance
  Attribute when **contesting** other creatures' powers. **BP never adds to a *resisted* roll's subtracted trait** —
  only to contested pools [p. 125].
- **Clash of Wills** [p. 125–126]: opposed powers (two Dominations, Auspex vs Obfuscate…) → each rolls **BP +
  Discipline dots** (Devotion: BP + highest prerequisite Discipline; sorcery: BP + Crúac/Theban); ties reroll. Physically
  present & aware users may spend WP (+3). Long-lasting effects add dice: night +1, week +2, month +3, year+ +4.
- Notation below: **Cost / Pool / Action / Duration**. "vs X" = contested (resistance reflexive unless noted);
  "− X" = resisted.

## Celerity (Daeva, Mekhet) [p. 131] — physical Discipline
- **Persistent** (free): **+Celerity to Defense** and to Dodge. **If Firearms denies her Defense, the shooter still
  suffers −Celerity.** Must be aware of the attack; useless if restrained/asleep.
- **Active** (reflexive, **1 Vitae per effect**, each effect max once per turn; multiple effects can be bought same turn):
  1. **Jump to the head of the Initiative** for this turn (several vampires → Clash of Wills).
  2. **Interrupt** another character's declared action (after declaration, before dice) with a brief instant action
     of her own (attack, move within Speed, Discipline, dodge). Still only **one instant action per turn total** — no
     double attacks. Interrupted character continues his declared action if still possible (or declares dramatic failure
     for a Beat). Can't interrupt reflexive or unseen actions. **Max interrupts per scene = Celerity dots.**
  3. **Speed × (Celerity + 1)** for the turn (blink-like movement; surprise/avoid detection).

## Resilience (Gangrel, Ventrue) [p. 141] — physical Discipline
- **Persistent**: **+Resilience to Stamina** (can exceed BP max) → Health rises (Health = Size + Sta + Res).
  **Each dot downgrades 1 aggravated → lethal** (fire, acquired banes) **but not sunlight**.
- **Active** (reflexive, 1 Vitae each, each once per turn): (a) **subtract Resilience + 1 from all non-bane damage** this
  turn (armor-like; wounds look real; heal superficial signs for 1 Vitae at next sleep; makes staking harder — [interp]
  reduces the stake's damage vs the 5-damage threshold); (b) **subtract Resilience from fire / acquired-bane damage**
  (not sunlight).

## Vigor (Daeva, Nosferatu) [p. 141–142] — physical Discipline
- **Persistent**: **+Vigor to Strength** (can exceed BP max) → affects Speed (+Vigor, p. 82), Brawl/Weaponry pools,
  grapple Strength. **Jump: Strength + Athletics, yards = successes × (Vigor + 1).**
- **Active** (reflexive, 1 Vitae each, once per turn each): (a) **+Vigor as weapon bonus** to all Athletics, Brawl and
  Weaponry attacks this turn (improvised weapons take 1 damage per Vigor dot above Durability); (b) lift & hurl objects
  of **Size ≤ Strength** as improvised weapons: bonus = lower of Size or Durability; **Size > 5 lethal to mortals; Size
  10+ lethal even to vampires**.

## Blood Sorcery [p. 150–154]
### Learning
- **Crúac**: Circle Status ≥1 to learn Discipline/rites (lose Status → can still raise dots, no new rites). **Learning a
  Crúac dot = breaking point at Humanity 4+**. **Humanity capped at 10 − Crúac dots.** Rites ≤ Crúac dots. Bad luck/
  Strix attention for lone ex-Acolytes. Some Strix know Crúac (use captive Kindred Vitae) and regard it as theirs.
- **Theban Sorcery**: Lancea Status ≥1; oaths of secrecy. Miracles ≤ Theban dots. **Humanity must be ≥ miracle's
  rating** to cast it.
- Each dot of the Ritual Discipline (incl. 1st) gives **one free rite/miracle**; extra ones **2 XP** each [p. 151].
### Casting procedure [p. 151–152]
1. **Sacrifice** first (lost even on failure):
   - Crúac: **1 Vitae per dot of the rite** — first spent as a normal Discipline spend; rest **spilled** (can come from
     others; mortal blood must first be drunk and turned into Vitae). Spilled blood is inert.
   - Theban: **1 Willpower + the listed sacrament** (crumbles to dust on success or failed attempt).
2. **Request** — extended action: **Crúac = Manipulation + Occult + Crúac; Theban = Intelligence + Academics + Theban**.
   Max rolls = unmodified pool. **30 min per roll; 15 min if Ritual dots > rite's rating.** One attempt; interruption =
   failure; **no Defense while casting**; no near-miss bonus. Extended-action Merits don't apply (except Library for
   Theban at ST option).
   - Dramatic: fail, sacrifice wasted, **Tempted** (Crúac) / **Humbled** (Theban), next ritual −2.
   - Failure: no successes; may abandon or continue with **Stumbled**.
   - Exceptional: choose one — reduce target by Ritual dots; time per roll → 15 min (5 if dots > rating); or apply
     **Ecstatic** (Crúac) / **Raptured** (Theban) on success. (Also may add Ritual dots to Potency.)
   - **Potency** = 1 + successes above target.
   - Modifiers: blood sympathy with target +1 to +3 (**Crúac doubles: +2 to +6**); undisturbed 0; rushed/distracted
     −1 to −3 cumulative; elaborate ritual 0 to +3; **Theban: prior meditation +successes (0–+3)**; **Crúac: extra Vitae
     sacrificed +1 each (0–+3)**.
3. **Defence**: target needn't be present. Contested (reflexive) or Resisted as listed. **Vampires always sense a ritual
   being cast on them** (even asleep/torpid) from sacrifice to resolution. Conscious victims may spend WP: +3 to
   contest or +2 to Resistance; frenzying victims get that for free. During casting the victim's sympathy to the
   ritualist rises 2 ranks (same clan) or becomes thrice removed (other clan).

## Coils of the Dragon (Ordo Dracul) [p. 154–159]
- Choose one **Mystery** on joining. **Own-Mystery Coil: 3 XP/dot**; at creation one starting Discipline dot may be a
  Mystery Coil dot. **Other Coils: 4 XP/dot, dots ≤ Ordo Dracul Status, not at creation.** Coils cost no Vitae.
- **Scales**: procedures tied to a prerequisite Coil; **1 XP if you have the prerequisite, else 2**. New Scale: commit
  Haven/Herd/Resources dots (+1 die each), extended Int + Medicine/Occult/Science, 1 roll/week, target = prereq Coil ×3
  (×5 without it); breaking point at Humanity (5 − prereq dots) win or lose.
### Mystery of the Ascendant (sun & fire)
1 **Surmounting the Daysleep** — with blush of life, no roll to stay awake by day, no Lethargic. 2 **The Warm Face** —
blush of life lasts 24 h. 3 **Conquer the Red Fear** — no frenzy risk from fire/sunlight. 4 **Peace with the Flame** —
with blush of life, fire deals lethal not agg; each Resilience dot further turns 1 fire lethal → bashing. 5 **Sun's
Forgotten Kiss** — when activating blush, each extra Vitae spent counts BP −1 (min 1) for sunlight interval.
Scales: Day-Wake Conditioning (reverse sleep cycle), Epidermal Shielding Bath (+1 effective Humanity vs sunlight per
Vitae for 24 h), Flesh Graft Treatment (heal agg with Vitae as lethal; lets mortals heal lethal with Vitae).
### Mystery of the Wyrm (the Beast)
1 **Stir the Beast** — 1 WP, reflexive: enter frenzy with a chosen goal (also to pre-empt involuntary frenzy); −2 to
leave early. 2 **Beast's Hunger** — in frenzy add Wyrm dots to BP for senses and predatory aura. 3 **Leash the Beast** —
riding the wave costs no WP, +Wyrm dots to riding rolls. 4 **Beast's Power** — forgo all resistance in frenzy (until
sated): **+BP to Defense, Health, Speed**. 5 **Eternal Frenzy** — no torpor from full lethal while frenzied (torpor when
it ends); may keep frenzy across scenes.
Scales: Augmented Vitae Draught, Kindred Sense Endowment, Surgical Heart Removal (can't be staked; can't gain Vitae by
feeding; regenerates from heart).
### Mystery of the Voivode (blood bond)
1 **Taste of Fealty** — her Vitae counts double for addiction; addicts can only resolve Deprived with Coil-holder
Vitae. 2 **Into the Fold** — sympathy with those she has bonded (stage 1 = 4× removed, 2 = 3×, 3 = 2×). 3 **Call to
Serve** — 3 Vitae → straight to stage 2, 5 Vitae → stage 3 in one sitting (no per-turn limit). 4 **Voivode Undisputed**
— relatives get no sympathy bonus vs her; adds sympathy bonus to resist bonds and predatory aura of those she bound.
5 **The Vast Dynasty** — Embrace no longer costs Humanity automatically: breaking point at Humanity 3, +1 die.
Scales: Blood Cleansing Ritual (roll Dragon's BP − bond-maker's BP per Vitae; success breaks a bond), Fealty's Reward
(year-long ghoul; draugr if it lapses), Mass Embrace (chance die per subject: success fine, failure frenzy, dramatic →
draugr; costs 1 Humanity), Sanguinary Invigoration (subject's blood counts BP −4 for feeding restriction for a week).
(Book lists only these three Mysteries in core [p. 155–159].)

## Bloodlines — rules [p. 98]
Join: sire's bloodline at BP 1; via a blood-tied **Avus** at BP 2; any clanmate Avus at BP 4 (Avus feeds ≥1 Vitae —
bond/addiction risk); **found** a bloodline at BP 6+. One bloodline, can't leave. Bloodlines have **4 in-clan
Disciplines** (may differ from parent) and may have one unique Discipline unlearnable by outsiders (even diablerie;
Oath of Action exception), plus an **extra bane** (e.g. agg from sun regardless of Humanity; feed only on Kindred).
No specific bloodlines are statted in the core book.
