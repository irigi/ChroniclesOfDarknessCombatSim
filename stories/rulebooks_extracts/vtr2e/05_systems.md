# VtR 2e — Systems (Kindred physiology, feeding, Beast, Humanity, combat)

Generic combat/social/Conditions rules live in `../cod_core/05_systems.md`; VtR reprints a version in Ch4 (see §11+).
Where VtR differs for vampires, **VtR wins**.

## 1. Damage, torpor, Final Death [VtR p. 90–91]
- **Kindred downgrade damage**: most mundane sources that deal **lethal to humans deal bashing to vampires**
  (incl. most non-supernatural weapons — knives, swords, **bullets**). Things that deal **aggravated to humans deal
  lethal** to vampires (chemicals, some supernatural punishments, master fighting-style maneuvers).
  **Aggravated** to Kindred only from: **fire, sunlight**, some Disciplines/Devotions/sorcery (e.g. Protean claws).
  [interp] Fangs are an explicit exception: **0L even vs Kindred** (p. 94). Stake/fire/sun keep their own rules.
- Vampire stays **conscious with all boxes bashing** (still wound penalties). Bashing overflow upgrades as normal
  (core) [interp: CoD core wraparound applies].
- **Last box lethal → torpor** (checked at end of the vampire's turn, after reflexive healing) [p. 91, 105].
- **Last box aggravated → Final Death**; body reverts to true age within a minute.
- Beats: lethal in rightmost boxes gives a Beat; bashing never does [p. 83].

## 2. Tricks of the Damned (all Kindred) [p. 90–92]
Vitae spends are **reflexive** but limited by **Vitae/turn (BP table)**.
- **Blush of life**: 1 Vitae, scene → warm, pulse, can eat/drink (vomit later), sex, passes medical inspection.
  Without it: food/drink cause violent bloody vomiting.
- **Kindred senses**: no darkness penalty except **−2 in full darkness** for sight rolls; see even tiny traces of blood;
  hear heartbeats at **3 yd × BP**; smell blood at **10 yd × BP** (×Auspex dots) without rolling; +BP to track a human
  whose blood she has tasted; **+BP to detect hidden people/details by blood traces**.
- **Physical intensity**: **1 Vitae → +2 dice** to rolls with Str, Dex **or** Sta (choose) for the turn; doesn't raise
  derived traits (Speed, Defense, Health) but **does** increase relevant resistances [p. 91].
- **A Taste of Blood**: Wits + Composure. Success: blood type, clan, drugs, age of blood, diseases, if tasted before,
  if human, steps of blood sympathy; non-human type if known. Exc.: ghoul & whose clan, diet, hormones, cause of death.
  Mods: Auspex active +2, blood-tied +2, hungry +2, hour-old −1, day-old −3, week+ −5. Dramatic: 2 truths + 1 lie.
- **Healing**: **1 Vitae = 2 bashing or 1 lethal**. **1 aggravated = 5 Vitae + a full day's sleep.** Severed limbs
  reattach/regrow if the whole injury is healed in a turn. Only agg leaves a (natural-looking) scar [p. 91].
- **The Cleansing**: in daysleep body reverts to Embrace state; unhealed Health damage is healed unconsciously during
  daysleep (may leave too little Vitae to wake). Spend 1 WP per wound to preserve it permanently; WP to keep
  tattoos/haircuts etc. [p. 91–92].
- **Predatory aura**: see §3.

## 3. Predatory Aura & Lashing Out [p. 91–93]
- Unmasked Kindred **automatically recognise** a vampire they can smell or touch (one-sided possible).
- **Lash out** = instant action; **costs 1 WP vs Kindred, free vs mortals**. Choose Beast aspect:
  | Aspect | Pool | Condition inflicted |
  |---|---|---|
  | Monstrous (destroyer) | **BP + Strength** | **Bestial** |
  | Seductive (tempter) | **BP + Presence** | **Wanton** |
  | Competitive (alpha) | **BP + Intelligence** | **Competitive** |
  Disciplines do **not** add (no Vigor, no Majesty) unless stated.
  Modifiers (both sides): on own territory **+Feeding Ground dots**; hungry +1; starving +2; victim already targeted by
  predatory aura this scene −1 cumulative.
- Target chooses:
  - **Fight**: contest with any aspect's Power Attribute + BP. Costs **1 WP if responder's BP < aggressor's**.
    Winner imposes **their** aspect's Condition on loser and gets **+2 dice to pursue that aspect's interests** for the
    scene. Tie: both impose Conditions, nobody gets +2.
    **Mortals** can fight (roll Power Attr + 0 BP [interp]) but can't impose a Condition; if they win they get +2 to
    attack/flee/neutralise.
  - **Flight**: free; withdraw (look foolish); victim gains aggressor's Condition; aggressor may pursue.
- Frenzying vampires may (not must) lash out.
- Werewolves etc. may have similar auras (ST) [p. 92].

## 4. Embrace, revenants [p. 93–94]
- Embrace = drain to death + feed Vitae; deliberate choice; **costs the sire 1 Humanity dot** (not a roll).
- **Posthumous Embrace**: anyone who ever fed from a vampire, dead < 1 week; donor rolls **BP**; success → rises in
  (7 − successes) nights. Humanity cost only if deliberate.
- **Revenants**: exsanguinating a contaminated mortal → roll **Humanity + 2**; failure → rises as revenant within a week.
  Dead ghoul → regnant rolls BP; success → revenant in (7 − successes) nights. Creating a revenant = breaking point
  (Humanity 2). Revenant: BP 1 fixed; Vitae max 5 + Stamina, loses all Vitae each dawn (wakes starved); no clan/bane;
  Disciplines only out-of-clan; no Devotions/sorcery/Coils; Humanity 7; can't Embrace, ghoul or bond; diablerie
  uplifts it into the victim's clan; +5 sympathy to sire. Uplift by a surrogate sire: feed Vitae + spend 1 Humanity.

## 5. Feeding [p. 94–97]
- **Violent bite (the Assault)**: fangs = **0L weapon, Brawl**, lethal **even vs Kindred**; must be a **Damage move in
  a grapple** (p. 177) ; also steals **1 Vitae** (+1 lethal to mortals). Mortal gains **Scarred**. Afterwards, while
  grappling, the **Feed move**: win the grapple roll → steal **1 Vitae per success, max = BP**; vs mortals 1 lethal
  per Vitae.
- **Subtle bite (the Kiss)**: mortal gains **Swooning** (its modifiers apply to the vampire's Discipline rolls vs
  them); 1 Vitae per turn; lick closes the wound.
- "Mortal" = humans, ghouls, warlocks, mostly-human things. Kindred/werewolves/fae aren't affected by Kiss/Assault
  unless the vampire knowingly spends **1 WP** (abhorrent taboo vs Kindred).
- **Human blood**: max Vitae = victim's **unmodified Health dots**; each Vitae = 1 lethal; taking > victim's Stamina →
  **Drained** Condition. BP 6+: humans no longer nourish.
- **Cold blood** (corpse / >few minutes out / bagged, ≤1 night old): **BP × 2 pints per Vitae**.
- **Kindred Vitae**: 1:1, no extra damage, but addictive + blood bond; stored Kindred Vitae counts as cold.
- **Animals**: Size 0 → none; Size 1 → 1 Vitae; Size 2–3 → 2; Size 4–5 → 3; +1 per Size above 5. Only BP 1–2.
- **Below BP restriction: 1 WP per Vitae** (p. 89).
- **Starting Vitae roll** [p. 95] ([interp] = Vitae on hand at scene start, after the waking cost): roll **1 die, read the face value** (10 = 10), **+ Feeding Grounds dots**, capped by
  BP max. Use at session start / scene with unknown Vitae / after nights in which the PC could feed.
- **Feeding scene with dice** [p. 96]: pool = Attribute + Skill describing the approach (seduce: Manip + Persuasion;
  ambush: Str + Streetwise). **Spend 1 Vitae to add a Discipline rating** — then any failure is a dramatic failure (no
  Beat). Instant, = 1 hour. Mods: Feeding Grounds +1–5; multiple vessels −2 each; extra hours +2 cumulative; ½ hour
  −3; dressed wrong −2; repeat prey +1; animals urban +1 / rural +3; the Rack +2; poaching −2; curfew −3; crime
  spree −3; very specific vessel −2 to −5. Exceptional: ST gives 3 traits of the vessel; later invoke them for a Beat.
  Dramatic-failure examples: Witness (Touchstone), Inquisitive, Hunter, Relatives, Rival's herd, Wrong Turn, Resistant,
  Affliction, Anemic, Nemesis (Strix-possessed).
- **Feeding scene without dice** [p. 97]: narrative trade; spotlight player may override by invoking Mask/Dirge (ST
  marks a tick) or by frenzying (no tick). At end: detachment roll as a breaking point at **Humanity (6 − ticks)**.

## 6. Daysleep & waking [p. 98]
- At sunrise: resist with **Stamina + Resolve**; success → stays awake with **Lethargic**; exceptional → no Condition.
- Sleeping vampires wake only for direct threats (fire, sun, collapse): roll **Humanity**; success → awake for
  **Humanity turns**, then sleeps again (no new roll). Accumulating **> 5 + BP successes** during waking turns [interp:
  on actions taken] → stays awake indefinitely.
- **Every waking costs 1 Vitae** (also at normal dusk). Sleeping with 0 Vitae → slip into torpor (p. 105).

## 7. Blood: sympathy, addiction, bonds, ghouls, diablerie [p. 98–101]
- **Blood sympathy** steps: once removed (sire/childe) +3, unlimited range; twice (siblings, grandsire/childer) +2,
  same continent; thrice (cousins, great-grand) +1, same city; four times (any clanmate) +0, within a mile.
  Auto-triggers when a relative enters torpor or Final Death. Initiate: 1 WP + **BP roll**, each success contacts one
  relative (once/twice removed). **Detect**: **Wits + BP + sympathy bonus**, instant, no dramatic failure; success
  vague state & direction; exceptional also distance + one-sentence message. 1 WP to force detection of a specific
  relative in range. **Disciplines vs a relative add the sympathy bonus; Crúac doubles it.**
- **Vitae addiction** [p. 99]: any drinker of Kindred Vitae rolls **Resolve + Composure −1 per Vitae drunk**; fail →
  Persistent **Addicted**. Afterwards, each opportunity: indulge, or succeed Res + Comp, else **Deprived**. Tempter may
  contest (Manip + Persuasion vs Res + Comp). **BP 6+ vampires are immune to addiction** (not to bonds).
- **Blood bond (Vinculum)** [p. 99–100]: each drink (≥1 Vitae) from the same vampire adds a stage (1st/2nd/3rd).
  Kindred may resist a drink: **1 WP + roll BP − Vitae ingested** (WP adds no dice); success → no stage (addiction
  still rolled); each later resistance vs same vampire −1 cumulative. Mortals can't resist.
  | Stage | Regnant's impression on thrall | Bonus to Social & Discipline rolls vs thrall | Harming regnant = breaking point at |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | +1 step | +1 | Humanity 5 / Integrity −2 |
  | 2 | +2 steps | +2 | Humanity 3 / Integrity −3 |
  | 3 | **perfect** | +3 | Humanity 1 / Integrity −5 |
  Stage 3: all regnant's lingering Discipline durations step up (scene→night, night→week, week→month, month→year);
  **shatters all other bonds and prevents new ones**. A bond lasts **one year from the last drink** (≈).
  **Perversion** = mutual full bonds between two vampires (immune to outside bonds; taboo).
- **Ghouls** [p. 100–101; full rules App. 1 p. 290]: creation = **1 Vitae + 1 WP** by the vampire; monthly Vitae
  thereafter (no WP). Without Vitae for a month → loses all gifts. Ghoul Retainer: Retainer Merit + regnant's
  Discipline dots (1 at Retainer 1–2, 2 at 3–4, 3 at 5).
- **Diablerie** [p. 101]: drain victim's Vitae, then each further "Vitae" = 1 lethal; when all Health is aggravated
  [sic], the soul struggles: **1 WP per roll, Strength + Resolve (+Vigor)**, accumulate successes = **victim's BP**;
  abandon/run out of WP → fails, can't retry. Gains: **Experiences = victim's BP**, usable only on BP; **one dot of the
  victim's highest Discipline the diablerist lacks** (else a Skill dot). **Always a breaking point** regardless of
  Humanity; **Tainted** Condition. **Diablerie addiction** automatic: resist later chances with **Res + Comp − victim's
  BP**; fail → Persistent Addicted.

## 8. Great banes [p. 101–103]
### Sunlight — damage by Humanity, frequency by BP (Resilience does **not** help)
| Humanity | Damage per interval |  | BP | Interval |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7–10 | 1 lethal |  | 0 | — (none) |
| 6 | 2 lethal |  | 1–2 | every 10 minutes |
| 5 | 3 lethal |  | 3 | every minute |
| 4 | 1 agg |  | 4–5 | every turn |
| 3 | 2 agg |  | 6–7 | 2× per turn |
| 2 | 3 agg |  | 8–9 | 3× per turn |
| 1 | 4 agg |  | 10 | 5× per turn |
| 0 | 5 agg |  | | |
Only complete coverage avoids it; eyes-only exposure = treat BP one step lower. Mekhet: Humanity −1 for this.
### Fire — automatic aggravated per turn of exposure (no attack roll)
Size: torch 1, bonfire 2, inferno 3; heat: candle +0, torch +1, Bunsen +2, chemical/molten metal +3.
**Each turn, convert agg → lethal up to Resilience dots.** Mortals take lethal.
### Stake through the heart [p. 105]
Wooden stake: attack at **−3 after Defense**; if it deals **≥5 damage** it pierces the heart → immediate torpor until
the stake is removed (then wakes immediately).

## 9. Clan banes [p. 103] — apply only once Humanity has dropped to **6** (persist even if regained)
- **Daeva (Wanton)**: 2nd+ drink from the same mortal → roll **Humanity**; fail → Persistent **Dependent** Condition
  toward that mortal (ends only with the mortal's death).
- **Gangrel (Feral)**: all frenzy-resistance pools **capped at Humanity** (not riding the wave).
- **Mekhet (Tenebrous)**: at Humanity 6 choose one bane not tied to a breaking point (counts against max 3);
  Humanity counts as **−1** for Humanity-based banes (sunlight, torpor).
- **Nosferatu (Lonely)**: vs humans, Humanity counts **−2** for Social penalties and **any Presence/Manipulation failure
  is a dramatic failure** (not vs Touchstones or Kindred).
- **Ventrue (Aloof)**: first Touchstone sits on Humanity **7** (not 6) → first Humanity loss detaches it; others on 6–2.
  (This one applies from creation.)

## 10. Frenzy [p. 103–105]
- **Resist: Resolve + Composure** (reflexive) with modifiers. WP **cannot** add +3. Instead **1 WP holds the Beast off
  for one turn** (visibly fighting); when you stop, roll Res + Comp **+1 per WP spent**.
  Results: dramatic failure → frenzy that can't end until a breaking point is reached; failure → frenzy;
  success → resist, gain **Tempted**; exceptional → resist + regain 1 WP + all WP spent fighting this scene.
  **Entering frenzy gives a Beat** (+1 more for choosing dramatic failure).
- Modifiers: dead friend −2, dead lover −4, important property destroyed −1, minor +1, expecting provocation +1,
  **hungry (≤4 Vitae) −2**, hurt friend −1, hurt lover −2, inside burning building −4, insulted by superior −1, by
  inferior −2, on Elysium +2, provocation was Touchstone +2, publicly ostracized −2, trivial open wound +1, massive
  open wound −1, small fire (torch) −2, **starving (≤2 Vitae) −4**, sunlight causing agg −3 / lethal −1, surprised −1,
  **wounded at all −1, wounded in last three boxes −3**.
- **In frenzy**: Beast picks a desire (escape, punish, feed); ends when satisfied, on torpor, or certain Devotions/Coils.
  **+BP to all Str, Dex, Sta rolls and resistances** (not Health); **ignore wound penalties**; may **grab and bite as one
  instant action**: Str + Brawl grapple successes deal **lethal** and take 1 Vitae immediately. Acting against the
  Beast costs **1 WP/turn**. Social/Discipline coercion against the desire fails, but can redirect to a similar target.
- **Touchstone talk-down**: extended Social roll, one roll/turn, needs **3 × BP** successes.
- **Frenzy chicken**: if another character enters frenzy first, those still resisting with WP only get **half BP
  (round up)** on Physical if they then succumb (unless they give in immediately or ride the wave).
- **Riding the wave** [p. 105]: 1 WP + Res + Comp each turn (WP adds no dice); each success turn holds off; any failure
  = dramatic failure; at **5 accumulated successes** you ride the wave: full frenzy benefits, **you choose desire and
  target** (may even be diablerie). Riding the wave = breaking point at Humanity 8.
- Bane-provoked frenzy: resistance dice capped at Humanity [p. 108].

## 11. Torpor [p. 105]
- Enter: last Health box lethal (end of turn after healing); daysleep with no Vitae to wake; or voluntarily.
- Day 1: subconsciously spends Vitae to heal; if still wounded in last 3 boxes after spending all → Humanity −1 for
  duration purposes.
- **Duration = base(Humanity) × BP** (±25%): Humanity 10 → 1 night; 8–9 → 2 nights [book prints "9-10" and "8-9"];
  6–7 → 1 week; 5 → 1 month; 4 → 1 year; 3 → 5 years; 2 → 10 years; 1 → 50 years; 0 → 100 years.
- **−1 BP per 25 years in torpor.** A vampire with **BP ≥ sleeper's BP + 2** can wake her early with 1 Vitae (bond/addiction risks).
- Falling into torpor = breaking point (Humanity 6); a year+ in torpor = Humanity 4.

## 12. Humanity [p. 105–108]
Replaces Integrity; Embraced mortals start at 7 regardless of Integrity.
- **Relate-to-humans modifiers** (book: apply to rolls "relating to humans" such as Empathy to read emotional cues; NOT
  Subterfuge to manipulate for food or Intimidation; DO apply with Touchstones; Discipline pools that use Empathy
  (Confidant, Face of the Beast) are RAW-ambiguous in 2e — [interp] **don't apply** to Discipline activation pools
  (they are the Beast manipulating, like the exempted predatory Subterfuge/Intimidation; reportedly 1e p. 185 exempted
  Disciplines using Empathy/Persuasion/Socialize — unverified, 1e not in `rulebooks/`); ST may apply −Humanity mod to
  Confidant if the scene is about genuine connection):
  9–10 Ascetic **+2**; 7–8 Humane 0; 5–6 Balanced **−1**; 4 Weathered **−2**; 3 Callous **−3**; 2 Monstrous **−5**;
  1 Animalistic **chance die**; 0 **draugr** (lost to the Beast, NPC).
- **Breaking points**: only events at or **below** current Humanity count. Dice to resist detachment by level:
  | Hum | Dice | Examples |
  |---|---|---|
  | 10 | 5 | 1 night without human contact; lying for the Masquerade; spending >1 Vitae in a night |
  | 9 | 5 | watching humans eat; superhuman physical feat; **feeding from the unwilling/unknowing**; urging behaviour with a Discipline; an hour in the sun |
  | 8 | 4 | creating a ghoul; rejected by a human; **riding the wave**; **depriving consent with a Discipline**; most of a day in sun |
  | 7 | 4 | 1 week without human contact; surviving something that would hospitalise a human; **injuring someone over blood** |
  | 6 | 3 | falling into torpor; feeding from a child; reading your obituary; car crash / immense trauma |
  | 5 | 3 | 2 weeks w/o contact; reaching BP 3; death of mortal family member; gaining covenant Status |
  | 4 | 2 | learning a dot of Crúac; **impassioned violence**; a year+ in torpor; surviving a century; accidental killing |
  | 3 | 2 | 1 month w/o contact; reaching BP 6; death of spouse/child; impassioned killing |
  | 2 | 1 | 1 year w/o contact; premeditated killing; seeing a culture that didn't exist in life; surviving 500 years; creating a revenant |
  | 1 | 0 (chance) | 1 decade w/o contact; heinous/spree/mass murder; killing your Touchstone |
  More personal → lower level; distant/unpreventable → one level higher. No breaking points in torpor.
- **Detachment roll**: dice above + Touchstone mods (**+2 one attached, +3 multiple, −2 none**), protecting your
  Masquerade −1, protecting your Requiem +1, **−1 per bane**. **No Willpower.** Always take a Beat.
  Dramatic failure: −1 Humanity + **Jaded**. Failure: −1 Humanity + Bestial/Competitive/Wanton. Success: no loss but
  Bestial/Competitive/Wanton. Exceptional: **Inspired**.
- **Banes** [p. 108]: when losing Humanity, may take a bane + a Beat → immune to Humanity loss from *that* breaking
  point forever; each bane −1 to future detachment; max 3. Bane-provoked frenzy capped at Humanity.
  Samples (most scale with **10 − Humanity**): Bells ((10−H) bashing dice/min + frenzy), Blood of the Unwilling (first
  10−H Vitae from unwilling give nothing), Crossroads (pools capped by H for scene), Face of Hunger (<5 Vitae: Social
  capped by H; hunger-frenzy capped by H), Grave Soil, Hated by Beasts (−(10−H) Animal Ken/Animalism), Holy Day,
  Invitation ((10−H) bashing, unhealable while inside), Open Wounds, Plague of Purity (touch by Integrity 8+ human →
  (10−H) bashing), Rat King/Queen (fail non-Intimidation Social), Repulsion (garlic/salt/silver… within 10−H feet costs
  WP; in wound (10−H) bashing dice), Symbols (pools capped by H vs brandished faith symbols), Webs (Durability 10−H).

## 13. Core rules as reprinted in VtR Ch4 [p. 163–193] (consistent with `../cod_core/05_systems.md`)
- **Dice**: 8–10 success, 10-again; pool < 1 → chance die (10 only; 1 = dramatic failure). Exceptional = 5+ successes
  (beneficial Condition, usually Inspired). Choose dramatic failure on a failed roll for a Beat. Modifiers usually
  ±3 (max ±5); equipment +1–+3 (+5 max total) [p. 169–170].
- **Unskilled**: −1 Physical/Social, −3 Mental [p. 164, 170].
- **Willpower**: 1 per action: **+3 dice, or +2 to a Resistance trait** (e.g. Defense +2 vs one attacker) [p. 170].
- **Actions**: instant, **reflexive (may be taken at any time, doesn't use your turn** — so reflexive Disciplines like
  Idol or Celerity's Vitae effects can be triggered outside your Initiative slot, p. 170), contested (count total successes for exceptional, initiator's action, resistance
  reflexive), extended (max rolls = Attr + Skill + Specialty; failure → take a Condition or lose progress; exceptional:
  −Skill dots from target / −¼ time / exceptional result), **resisted** (subtract Resolve/Stamina/Composure/Defense).
  Rule of thumb: resisted when number of successes matters; contested when only success matters.
  **Supernatural Tolerance for Kindred = Blood Potency** [p. 170–171].
- **Attribute tasks**: Memorizing Int + Comp; **Perception Wits + Comp (reflexive)**; resisting coercion Res + Sta;
  breaking barrier / lifting Str + Sta; balance Dex + Comp; staying awake Sta + Res; first impressions Pres + Comp;
  poker face Manip + Comp; meditation Res + Comp (extended) [p. 163–164].
- **Common actions** [p. 171–172]: Argument Int + Expression − Resolve; Carousing Pres + Socialize/Streetwise;
  **Fast-talk Manip + Subterfuge − Composure**; Interrogation Manip + Empathy/Intimidation − Resolve; **Intimidation
  Str or Manip + Intimidation − Composure**; Investigating a scene Int + Investigation; Jumping Str + Athletics (feet =
  successes); Repair Int + Crafts; Research Int + Academics/Occult; Shadowing Wits + Stealth/Drive vs Wits + Comp;
  **Sneaking Dex + Stealth vs Wits + Comp**.
- **Permutations**: 9-again, 8-again, rote (reroll failures once), extra successes, successive attempts (−1 cumulative
  under pressure), **teamwork** (helpers roll, each success +1 die to leader; helper dramatic failure → −4) [p. 172].
- **Mortal Virtue/Vice**: Vice +1 WP (1/scene), Virtue all WP (2/chapter) [p. 169].
- **Speed species factor**: human 5, wolf 8, horse 12, cheetah 15 [p. 169].

## 14. Social Maneuvering [p. 173–175]
Restatement: declare goal → victim's **Doors = lower of Resolve or Composure** (+2 if goal is a breaking point, +1 if it
blocks an Aspiration, +1 if against Virtue/**Mask**). **Impression** sets time per roll: **Perfect 1 turn / Excellent 1
hour / Good 1 day / Average 1 week / Hostile can't roll**. Each success opens **one** Door (exceptional: two); failure
−1 cumulative to further rolls (ST may lower impression, +Beat). Raise impression: setting, a roll (Wits + Socialize,
Manip + Persuasion), tempting Vice/**Dirge** offer accepted, or leverage/bribe (Merits). Knowing an Aspiration and
offering a clear path opens a Door (failing to help later closes two). Fails on dramatic failure, discovered
manipulation, or a week at Hostile.
- **Forcing Doors**: one roll at **−(closed Doors)**; success opens all; failure = never again. **Hard leverage**
  (threats, drugs, blackmail) is a breaking point for the persuader: if |breaking-point level − his Humanity| ≤ 2 remove
  1 Door, ≥ 3 remove 2 (before rolling).
- Resolution: ST character does it. Next attempt on same victim: −1 Door if success by opening, +2 if failed/hard
  leverage (min 1).
- Only PCs on NPCs (PC vs PC by roleplay). Blood bond raises regnant's impression (+1/+2/perfect) [p. 99–100].

## 15. Combat [p. 175–182] — vampire-relevant points
- **Intent** first ("I want…"). Killing when intent was non-lethal: −1 WP [p. 175].
- **Optional Beaten Down & Surrender**: > Stamina bashing or any lethal → Beaten Down: 1 WP per violent action (Defense/
  Dodge/flee free). Surrender: +1 WP, +Beat, out of fight; attacking a surrendered foe costs WP (+likely breaking
  point). Not applicable if the other side intends to kill; ignored by those with no compunction about killing.
- **Down & Dirty**: PC-only single contested roll (combat pool vs combat pool or escape Str/Dex + Athletics), no
  Defense; winner deals difference + weapon; exceptional +1 WP [p. 176].
- **Initiative** = 1d10 + Dex + Comp + weapon's Initiative penalty (two weapons: largest −1 more) [p. 176].
- **Surprise**: Wits + Comp vs attacker's Dex + Stealth; losers lose first turn and Defense that turn [p. 176].
- **Attack pools**: unarmed **Str + Brawl − Defense**; melee **Str + Weaponry − Defense**; ranged **Dex + Firearms**
  (no Defense); thrown Dex + Athletics − Defense. Damage = successes + weapon bonus [p. 176].
- **Defense**: vs aware unarmed/melee/thrown; −1 per extra application until your next turn; **WP +2 vs one attacker**;
  may choose not to apply. **No Defense vs firearms** (but **Celerity still imposes −Celerity on the shooter**, and
  Quicken Sight lets Defense apply) [p. 176, 131, 145].
- **Dodge**: forgo action, roll **Defense × 2** (−1 per extra opponent before doubling) as a pool; each success removes
  one attack success (before weapon bonus). Celerity adds to the Dodge [p. 176–177, 131].
- **Touching**: Dex + Brawl / Dex + Weaponry (Defense applies), no damage [p. 177].
- **Bite**: human −1B; **vampire fangs +0 lethal (even vs Kindred), only via grapple Damage move** [p. 177, 94].
- **Grapple (procedure)** [p. 177]:
  1. **Grab = Strength + Brawl − Defense** (ordinary attack roll; target doesn't roll). Success → both grappling;
     exceptional → also pick a move now.
  2. Each subsequent turn: **contested Strength + Brawl vs Strength + Brawl** on the **higher** Initiative of the two;
     no Defense. Winner picks **1 move (2 on exceptional)**: **Break Free** (reflexive, may then act), **Control
     Weapon**, **Damage** (bashing = successes, + weapon bonus if he controls a weapon; **vampire may bite → lethal +
     1 Vitae, then Feed move**), **Disarm** (needs Control Weapon), **Drop Prone**, **Hold** (neither applies Defense
     vs incoming attacks), **Restrain** (needs prior Hold; with tape/zip ties can leave the grapple), **Take Cover**
     (opponent as shield).
  3. **Handcuffs** = extra grapple option: **Str + Brawl − opponent's Strength** [p. 191].
  Vigor raises Strength in both rolls; Barbed Hands adds Protean; **frenzy adds BP** to Str rolls and lets her grab &
  bite as one instant action (grapple successes = lethal + 1 Vitae) [p. 104].
- **Autofire**: short burst (3) +1; medium (10, 1–3 targets) +2, −targets; long (20) +3, −targets [p. 177].
- **Range**: medium −1, long −2, beyond = chance die. Thrown range Str + Dex + Athletics − Size (×2, ×4; aerodynamic ×2) [p. 177].
- **Concealment**: barely −1, partially −2, substantially −3 (concealed shooter fires at one less). **Cover**: Durability
  ≥ weapon bonus blocks; else subtract Durability from damage (transparent: half) [p. 177–178].
- **Human shield**: Durability = victim's Sta + armor; victim takes all damage; breaking point (Kindred Humanity 2+) [p. 178].
- **Reloading**: instant; loose rounds = no Defense that turn [p. 178]. **Movement**: Speed + action, or 2× Speed [p. 178].
- **Prone**: ranged −2 vs you; standing melee +2 vs you; dropping/rising costs action [p. 178].
- **Specified targets** [p. 178]: arm −2 (dmg > Sta → Arm Wrack), leg −2 (Leg Wrack), head −3 (dmg ≥ Size → Stun),
  **heart −3 (≥5 damage pierces; stake → torpor)**, hand −4 (Arm Wrack), eye −5 (Blinded). Specified targets ignore
  armor elsewhere. Effects summarised: Arm Wrack (drop item, off-hand −2; both: chance die manual, −3 Physical);
  Blinded (−3 sight & halve Defense one eye; −5 & no Defense both); Leg Wrack (half Speed, −2 Defense & movement; both:
  prone, Speed 1); **Stun** (miss next action, Defense halved until you act).
- **Killing blow**: helpless target: damage = full pool + weapon, ignores armor; breaking point [p. 179].
- **Weapons vs Kindred**: every weapon is lethal to mortals; **Kindred take bashing from all mundane weapons incl.
  knives and guns** [p. 179, 181]. Exceptions: fire, sun, fangs, agg/lethal powers, Size 10+ thrown objects
  (Vigor), Kindred Dueling Carving, Protean talons, Feeding the Crone, etc.
- **Improvised weapons**: damage Durability − 1, Init penalty & Str req = Size, −1 to attack, weapon takes the damage
  it deals [p. 179].
- **Armor**: ballistic (vs firearms) converts lethal → bashing per point (irrelevant vs Kindred, who already take
  bashing); general subtracts damage, most severe first; ballistic first; lethal vs armor always ≥1 bashing.
  **Armor-piercing** subtracts from ballistic first then general (melee AP: general only) [p. 180–181].
- **Wound penalties**: third-to-last box −1, second-to-last −2, last −3 (incl. Initiative; not Stamina to stay
  conscious) [p. 182]. Frenzy ignores them.
- **Marking/upgrading**: bashing /, lethal X, agg *; full of bashing → further damage upgrades leftmost bashing to
  lethal; full of lethal → upgrades to agg [p. 181–182].
- **Mortals**: full bashing → Stamina roll each turn to stay conscious; full lethal → 1 upgrade to agg per minute
  without medical aid (dead when all agg). Mortal healing: bashing 15 min, lethal 2 days, agg 1 week per point [p. 182].
- **Vampires**: full bashing = conscious; full lethal = torpor; full agg = Final Death; heal only with Vitae [p. 182].
- **Objects**: Durability (wood/plastic/glass 1, stone/aluminium 2, steel/iron 3, +1 per reinforced layer; none vs agg),
  Size (pistol 1, crowbar 2, assault rifle 3, door 5, sports car 10, SUV 15), Structure = Dur + Size [p. 183].
- **Disease/drugs**: Sta + Res reflexive; **poison**: Sta + Res − Toxicity, each success −1 damage; overdose as
  poison Tox 3–7 [p. 183–184]. **Extreme environments** levels 1–5 [p. 184]. (Kindred: ST — many don't apply [interp].)

## 18. Optional rules / Storyteller twists (Ch7, pp. 271–287) — only if the table opts in
- **Mask/Dirge scenes** (lull fillers that refresh WP); **Mirrors** (NPCs with the same anchors).
- **The Whole of the Monster**: replace Attributes with Mask & Dirge traits (5 dots between them); vampiric actions use
  Dirge, hiding uses Mask; named-trait actions +2 instead of WP.
- **The Will to Achieve**: WP dots assigned to Aspirations; WP only spent pursuing them; each spend = Beat.
- **Blood Potency pacing** [p. 275]: confirms BP +1 per 50 active years or 5 XP; justify XP purchases by heavy Vitae
  use, frenzy, feeding on Kindred, feeding beyond capacity, many ghouls, Embracing. **Generational chronicles: ~15 XP
  per century, plus a free BP dot per 50 years active** [p. 276] ← useful benchmark for elders.
- **Blood Beats** (Beats only for BP): dramatic/exceptional Discipline rolls, Embracing, ghouling, revenants,
  exsanguinating, frenzy/riding the wave, last Vitae, drinking beyond capacity, joining a bloodline, gaining a Discipline
  dot, detachment rolls.
- **Above All, Feed**: Vitae-as-dice abstraction. **Vitae tokens** for feeding scenes.
- **Conditions-as-Health**: Sta + Size ≥3 → Incapacitated slot, ≥6 Maimed, +1 Bruised per 2 above; each damaging hit
  = one Condition (two if ≥5 damage; bashing on Kindred only one); agg (or lethal vs mortals) = Persistent. Bruised −1
  Init & instant actions (1 Vitae / ½ hr); Maimed −2 & half Speed (2 Vitae / 2 days); Incapacitated = torpor (2 Vitae /
  1 week); Dying (mortals) Stamina per minute.
- **Personification** (a mortal per Discipline); **Learning Through Suffering** (learn a Discipline only after being its
  victim; checks = level).
- **Beast Guides** (another player plays your Beast and holds your WP).
- Touchstone play: **Black Box** scenes, **Holding Hands** (players portray each other's Touchstones, Beat for doing so).
- Time: "Close?" scene ending, flashbacks/cutaways, visual clocks, **sand-timer turns** (overrun → step down success).
- **Climbing the Ladder** [p. 282–287]: 12 collaborative backstory steps (Confront Vulnerability, Believe Someone Has
  Answers, Meet Your Maker, Find Fear, Leave a Witness, Suffer Your Defects, Beg Someone to Fix You, Remember Your Victims,
  Embrace a Wrong, Find Like Minds, Seize the Night, Winding Up) — **each completed step = 1 bonus Experience** at
  creation; each creates an NPC (Abandoned, False Prophet, Maker, Nightmare, Breach, Victim, Opportunist, Body, Bête Noire,
  Dupe, Enabled…).
