# GtS 2e — Merits (pp. 85–90; App. 1 Iconography pp. 288–290; Supernatural Merits p. 224; krewe Merits p. 175)

**Tags** [p. 85]:
- **Krewe**: the krewe or an individual can buy it.
- **Krewe Only**: only the krewe can buy it.
- **Multiple**: buy several instances.
- **Style**: you get every ability at or below your rating.
- **Shared**: several characters pool their dots and all use the total. Normally paired with Krewe; prefer buying it as a krewe Merit. A contributor can withdraw their share at any time.

**Sanctity of Merits** [p. 85]: lost Merit dots are refunded as Experiences (1 per dot). You can't buy them straight back. **One dot can be rebought at the end of the session, then one more each following session.** Supernatural Merits vanish if the character gains a template, and can be reallocated.

The book reprints core Merits only selectively. Use `../cod_core/04_merits.md` for anything not listed here (Fighting Styles, Contacts details, etc.).
