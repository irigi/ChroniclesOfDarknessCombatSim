# GtS 2e — Factions map (Ch1 pp. 19–49; Ch5 enemy archetypes; Ch6 settings)

## Burdens: why the Sin-Eater couldn't rest [p. 19–34; mechanics p. 73]
| Burden | Core | Affinity Haunts | Suggested build |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Abiding** | died without a legacy; wants something that lasts beyond them | Caul, Memoria, **Tomb** | Social primary, Mental secondary; Socialize/Subterfuge/Persuasion plus a legacy Skill. Merits Library, Striking Looks, Encyclopedic Knowledge, Iron Stamina, Fame, Inspiring. **Architect** Merit bonus |
| **Bereaved** | lost someone (maybe in the same incident); searches the realms of the dead for them | Curse, **Oracle**, Shroud | Closed Book, Trained Observer. **Manic States** and **Sympathetic** bonuses |
| **Hungry** | left something behind they can't let go of (wealth, status, stolen things) | **Boneyard**, Caul, Marionette | Resources, Good Time Management. **Grave Goods** bonus |
| **Kindly** | did great wrong in life; returns to make amends and dismantle exploitative systems | **Dirge**, Marionette, Shroud | Patient, Reconciler, Sympathetic. **Reconciler** bonus |
| **Vengeful** | blames someone (rightly or wrongly) for their death | Curse, Memoria, **Rage** | Retribution, Fleet of Foot, Safe Place. **Retribution** gets 8-again |
Each Burden chapter section also gives stereotypes of the others and sample faiths.

## Krewe archetypes: Sin-Eater faiths [p. 35–49; starting Ceremonies p. 80]
| Archetype | Ethos | Attribute emphasis | Signature Merits | Starting Ceremonies (•/••/•••) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Furies** | balance the scales of justice; punish wrongs against the dead, sometimes police other Bound | **Finesse**, then Power | Contacts, Council of Faith, Anonymity | Ishtar's Perfume / Skeleton Key / Black Cat's Crossing |
| **Mourners** | remember the forgotten dead, archive their stories, bring wisdom up from below | **Resistance** | Safe Place, Resources, Supernatural Membership, **Library** | Diviner's Jawbone / Gifts of Persephone / Bloody Codex |
| **Necropolitans** | care for the dead, protect them from the Underworld, reunite them with loved ones (originated "krewe" via Le Krewe LaBas, New Orleans) | — (safety and support) | — | Lovers' Telephone / Crow Girl Kiss / Dumb Supper |
| **Pilgrims** | death is a journey; help ghosts release Anchors and move on | **Power, Resistance** (monastic models) | Safe Place, Resources, Council of Faith, Library | Dead Man's Camera / Gifts of Persephone / Bloody Codex |
| **Undertakers** | prepare the living for death so that fewer ghosts are trapped; change perceptions and metaphysics | **Finesse**, then Power (Resistance weak) | Supernatural Membership, Resources, Contacts | Death Watch / Crow Girl Kiss / Dumb Supper |
Sample krewes: Bizango, le Quatrième Etat, the Sodality of the Door (Furies); Convivial Society of St. Christopher Souterrain (Pilgrims); Romsa Ludographical Institute (Undertakers); the **Forty-Third Nome** (Pilgrim, Cairo; MCI sample p. 87); **Church of the Brighter Morning** (fiction); Death Valley Krewe (guards Dead Man's Hand); Fifth Street Titans (rival, fiction).

## Antagonist archetypes (Bound who aren't Sin-Eaters) [p. 229–231; 06 §E]
| Archetype | Ethos | Ceremonies |
|---|---|---|
| **Elysians** | immortality and power are their right; usually Tyranny | Ishtar's Perfume / Ghost Trap / Black Cat's Crossing |
| **Bonepickers** | death is a gold mine; deal with necromancers and ghost eaters | Dead Man's Camera / Ghost Trap / Bloody Codex |
| **Thanatologists** | the dead as research data; hoard files | Diviner's Jawbone / Skeleton Key / Bloody Codex |
| **Gatekeepers** | keep the worlds separate and the status quo; militant ones use Tyranny | Death Watch / Ghost Trap / Crow Girl Kiss |

## Other forces
- **Reapers** (masked ghosts serving the Chthonic Gods), **Kerberoi** (Dominion law-enforcers), **Chthonians** (Underworld natives), **Eaters of the Dead** (living ghost-eaters with Cuisines and Rapacity), **necromancers** (graverobbers, ritualists, cults like the Church of Death's Shards), **the Absent** (hustling ghosts): 06.
- **Underworld powers**: the Leviathan, the Admiral and the Freighter, the Hermit (Ocean of Fragments); merchant kings of the River Cities; Ferrymen; Irkalla Gate guardians: 01, 05 §10.
