# GtS 2e — Conditions & Tilts (App. 2 pp. 292–309; krewe Conditions in 05 §9)

Referenced but **not defined in GtS**: Guilty, Shaken, Broken, Madness, Swooning, Informed, Steadfast, Obsessed (≈ Obsession), Leveraged details, Beaten Down for ghosts. Use `../cod_core/07_conditions.md` for those.

## Tilts [p. 292–298] (core reprint; summary table p. 298)
**Personal Tilts**:
- **Arm Wrack**: one arm: drop held items, off-hand penalties. Both arms: chance die on manual dexterity, −3 other Physical. Caused by a targeted arm blow (−2) dealing more than Stamina, or any damage to the hand. Aggravated → permanent.
- **Beaten Down**: violent actions cost 1 WP. Ends by surrendering (+1 WP, Beat).
- **Blinded**: one eye −3 vision rolls and half Defense; both −5 and no Defense. Eye attack −5. Sand in the eyes: Dex + Athletics −3 vs Defense, blinded 1 turn.
- **Deafened**: one ear −3 hearing; both: chance die on hearing and −2 combat. Lasts (10 − Sta − Res) turns from noise.
- **Drugged**: −2 Speed, −3 combat rolls (incl. Defense, Perception), ignores wound penalties. Lasts (10 − Sta − Res) hours.
- **Immobilized**: no combat actions or Defense. Break free: Str + Athletics − Durability (bound −2, hogtied −4), 1 bashing per attempt.
- **Insane**: +1 combat, acts last, −3 Social, no WP in combat. End: Resolve + Composure vs (10 − WP), giving up Defense.
- **Insensate**: no actions, but can use Defense. Damage or scene end ends it; 1 WP acts for a turn.
- **Knocked Down**: lose the action if not yet taken; prone (Defense applies; −2 to attack from the ground). Avoid: Dex + Athletics − weapon. Caused by a melee weapon +2 or firearm +3, or a leg attack (−2, damage halved).
- **Leg Wrack**: one leg: half Speed, −2 Physical movement and Defense. Both: Knocked Down, Speed 1, movement at chance die.
- **Poisoned**: moderate 1 bashing per turn, grave 1 lethal per turn. Reflexive Sta + Res each turn (−3 if acting) negates it for that turn.
- **Sick**: −1, growing by 1 every 2 turns to −5. Grave also 1 bashing per turn in combat.
- **Stunned**: lose next action, half Defense. Caused by damage ≥ Size in one hit (head: Size −1). 1 WP ends it at −3 that turn.

**Environmental Tilts**:
- **Blizzard**: −1 sight per 10 yd (applies to ranged); −1 Physical per 4" of snow.
- **Earthquake**: −1 to −5 Dex pools and Defense; 1–3 lethal per turn (Sta + Athletics downgrades to bashing).
- **Extreme Cold / Extreme Heat**: no bashing healing; −1 per hour cumulative; at −5, 1 lethal per hour.
- **Flooded**: −2 Physical per foot; over the head: swim or hold breath.
- **Heavy Rain**: −3 sight and hearing.
- **Heavy Winds**: grade 1–5 = Physical penalty and bashing per turn (Dex + Athletics to avoid); −3 hearing.
- **Ice**: half Speed, −2 Physical and Defense (−4 at full Speed); dramatic failure → Knocked Down; Drive −5.

**Haunt Tilt**:
- **Boneyard** (Environmental):
  - The Sin-Eater has perfect awareness of the layout. **Nobody inside can hide** from her (supernatural concealment → Clash of Wills).
  - Reflexive cosmetic poltergeist effects (lights, doors, sounds). These can't attack, but environmental hazards can hurt.
  - **The area is Open for all ghosts.**
  - Ends if its creator leaves, falls unconscious or dies, or faces their geist's Ban or Bane.

## Ephemeral Influence & Manifestation Conditions [p. 303–306]
**Chain**: Anchor → (Resonance/Infrastructure) → **Open** → **Controlled**.
- Advancing a Condition **freezes** the duration of its prerequisites. Removing a prerequisite collapses everything built on it, and the most advanced remaining Condition resumes its timer.
- **Sin-Eaters' auras supply these** (05 §5), and **Plasm coating gives Open**.

| Condition | Effect / creation / ending |
|---|---|
| **Anchor** | Within the ghost's sphere. **Ghosts within (Rank × 3) yd of an Anchor suffer no Essence Bleed.** Created when a ghost forms, by summoning rituals (temporary), or by Create Influence. Ends when the subject is destroyed or the business resolved. Anchorless ghosts bleed to dormancy, then an Underworld Gate opens and takes them |
| **Bound Geist** | The geist's Attributes come from the host. **No Influences, Numina or Manifestations** (except via Unleashing). No Essence upkeep or bleed. Uses the host's WP and Plasm. Created by the Bargain Manifestation; ends only at the host's **final death** |
| **Claimed** | Permanent possession and merger. A living host is **Urged** during fusion, and **+1 ghost Attribute dot per day permanently** (Power → Str/Int/Pre, Finesse → Dex/Wits/Man, Resistance → Sta/Res/Com); the body mutates. Corpses start with Mental/Social 0. Objects add Resistance to Structure/Durability, Power to Acceleration, Finesse to Handling. The Claimed can use the ghost's Influence but not Numina or Manifestations; the ghost is safe from bleed. The hybrid has the ghost's Essence, Virtue, Vice, Ban, Bane. Permanent in living hosts unless the ghost detaches (Power + Finesse − Rank vs Res + Com). Non-living hosts lose 1 Physical per 3 days until they disintegrate. Removing it against its will (e.g. Bane contact) reverts to Open |
| **Controlled** | A subject that is Open to the ghost and has been successfully Possessed **(the higher of ghost or subject WP) separate times**. Interrupted possession resets progress. Prerequisite for Claim |
| **Engulfed** | Hibernating inside the captor and **can't regain Essence** (the captor gets it). The captor can **eat it without resistance**. Lasts **1 day**; the captor re-pays the cost to extend. When it ends, victims are expelled |
| **Fettered** | Bound to a person or object: **no Essence Bleed**, stays in Twilight **within 5 yd**, pays 1 less Essence for Influence on the Fetter, but **can't use Influence or Numina on other targets**. Requires Open. Ends with the prerequisites, the Fetter's destruction or death, or Unfetter |
| **Materialized** | Physical. No bleed. **Lasts 1 hour per success.** If cast from an Open object or person, it must appear within Rank yards. Ended early by Bane contact or losing a prerequisite |
| **Open** | Conditioned to accept the ghost: it can Fetter, Possess, Materialize or Manifest. Created by an extended fine-tuning of Anchor (scenes = higher of Rank or the living subject's Resolve), by Control Influence (instant, temporary), or by **Plasm coating** (until it decays) |
| **Possessed** | Temporary control **for a scene**. **Living hosts black out** (missing time). The ghost can't use Numina or Influence while possessing, but has no bleed. **1 Essence per turn heals 1 wound or Structure point.** Objects: operates them (Finesse for rolls). Corpses and articulated hosts use their own Physical and the ghost's Mental/Social; 1 Essence gives the ghost's Physical for a turn, at 1 lethal to the host. **Living hosts use their own Attributes**: reading the mind is Finesse −4, host Physical Skills −3, Social/Mental −4, each reduced by 1 per day Fettered to the host. **The Bane hurts the ghost through the host** |
| **Underworld Gate** | An open Gate. Ghosts on the living side **lose 1 Essence per scene** near it. Created by Avernian Gateway on Open, or a Key, or other opening methods (05 §10). **Fades at scene end** (Crash: story end) |
| **Urged** | A living Fetter. The ghost reads surface thoughts (Power + Finesse vs Resolve + ST) and **urges an action** (Power + Finesse vs Resolve + Composure + 1 die). The host gets a Beat for following it. Ends with the Fetter |

## Haunt Conditions [p. 306–309] (charges = Plasm spent, doubled at the ••• tier)
Almost all **resolve when the victim, or the Sin-Eater for self-Conditions, is confronted with the creating geist's Ban or Bane**. For Conditions inflicted on others, **the creating Sin-Eater also takes a Beat when it resolves**.
- **Actor** (Memoria ••••):
  - Your actions are dictated by the echo, and **the phantasms are solid and real to you**: you can be hurt by phantom bullets.
  - Break free with a reflexive **Resolve + Composure** when compelled to a breaking or crisis point, on taking lethal damage, or when reminded of your identity.
  - Resolves when the illusion completes, you break free, you fail a breaking point caused by your persona, or you meet the Ban or Bane.
- **Caul** (self):
  - Spend a charge: (a) **exceptional on 3** when the viscous body helps (grapple, squeeze, intimidate); (b) **ignore wound penalties for 1 turn**; (c) **substitute a geist Attribute** for one of yours in the same category for one action (Power ↔ Str/Int/Pre, Finesse ↔ Dex/Wits/Man, Resistance ↔ Sta/Res/Com).
  - Ends when the charges run out. Resolves on death or unconsciousness, the geist leaving, a geist crisis point, or Ban/Bane.
- **Curse**: each charge, reflexively and **one per turn**: **−2 on one roll**, or an option added by Enhancements (equipment haywire, no fire, 3 lethal, a Tilt, chance-die Social, block WP gain, WP to be noticed, block a Social Merit, see the dead). Ends when charges run out. Resolves when another Sin-Eater counters it, or the mark touches the Ban/Bane.
- **Dirge**:
  - Compelled to listen; you understand the urged emotion or action. **+2 on all rolls acting in concert with it; 1 WP per action against it.**
  - Ends when the singing stops. Resolves if you take damage or a breaking point, someone without Dirge spends an instant action to snap you out, or Ban/Bane.
- **Marionette**:
  - The Sin-Eater controls your actions. **Each time she makes you act, you lose your next turn.** Conscious targets contest each puppeted action with **Strength + Stamina vs Synergy + Marionette**.
  - Ends at scene end. Resolves on lethal damage or a breaking point from a command (objects ignore this), **breaking line of sight with the Sin-Eater**, or Ban/Bane.
- **Memoria** (self):
  - Per charge: **8-again on one roll** investigating or resolving the event, or leverage that **removes one Door** and improves impression.
  - Vision events can trigger crisis points and Remembrance scenes.
  - Ends when the charges run out. Resolves on Ban/Bane, or failing to intercede in a crisis caused by the memory.
- **Oracle** (self):
  - You're **insensate** apart from answering. Up to **Oracle-dots** named characters may ask (or anyone).
  - **1 charge per question**: the ST answers truthfully with suggestive imagery.
  - Base questions: what here is most touched by death? what most feels the Underworld's pull? who here will help me get what we desire? what here is most treasured by someone? what here has gone unseen?
  - Resolves if you suffer damage, or Ban/Bane.
- **Rage** (self):
  - **Unarmed attacks get a weapon rating = Plasm spent on Vent the Rage**, and **deal lethal to ghosts**. You may **use Rage dots instead of Brawl** (no untrained penalty).
  - Ends at scene end. Resolves on Ban/Bane.
- **Servant**:
  - Obeys any command from the controller but is otherwise free. Objects and corpses repeat the last command or stand inert.
  - Uses its own traits; objects use the Sin-Eater's Synergy + Marionette. **Corpses: their living Physical traits, −1 per month dead, Physical actions only.**
  - Ends at scene end (unless Servant •••• extends it to 24 h). Resolves like Marionette.
- **Shroud** (self):
  - **No need to eat, sleep or breathe; can't be blinded or deafened by mundane means.**
  - Per charge: **enter Twilight for Shroud-rating turns** (minutes with Haunting Presence), plus options added by Enhancements.
  - Ends when the charges run out. Resolves on Ban/Bane, or **drawing significant attention** (attacking, screaming, using another Haunt).
- **Tomb**:
  - A precise replica at any point in its history (blood and DNA included). **People and animals appear as fresh corpses** unless Empty Graves animates them. **Tangible to both the living and ghosts.**
  - Lasts **Tomb-dots days**; extend with Plasm = Size (Empty Graves removes the time limit).
  - Resolves when the object touches the geist's **Ban**.
