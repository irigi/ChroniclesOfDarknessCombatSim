# GtS 2e — Gaps, book inconsistencies, rulings

Labels: **RAW** = explicit text · **[interp]** = resolving ambiguous text · **[house]** = an added procedure or number the book doesn't give.

## A. Book inconsistencies (resolved)
1. **Geist Defense**: p. 76 and the Unleashed Condition (p. 303) both give the **lower of Power or Finesse**. The geist character sheet (p. 315) says "lower of Power or Resistance", which is a sheet error. The Sin-Eater quick reference (p. 77) also drops "+ Athletics" from Defense; use the full formula on p. 75.
2. **Return-from-death timing**: p. 98 says she "awakens at the next dawn or dusk", face under a Plasm caul. The **Dead Condition** (p. 299) says the geist is Unleashed for the rest of the scene and she returns when the Condition resolves at scene end. Neither passage overrides the other.
   **Ruling [interp]**:
   - (1) Death → **Dead** Condition, and the geist is **Unleashed for the rest of that scene**.
   - (2) At scene end the Condition resolves:
     - Health is all lethal.
     - **Physical Tilts are removed.**
     - **Current Plasm = 1.**
     - **Synergy −1.**
     - A Krewe Beat.
     - **Someone near her dies** in her place.
   - (3) She physically **awakens at the next dawn or dusk**, wherever her body lies.
   - **Her body need not be intact.** It is made whole **even if it was dismembered**, and parts removed elsewhere wither to grave dust (p. 98, RAW).
   - Simpler alternative **[house]**: she awakens at scene end and the dawn/dusk wait is dropped. Pick one per chronicle.
   - **The caul**: eating it "can help refill her drained reserves" (RAW), but **no amount is given**. **[house]**: after the reset to 1, eating the caul (an instant action) restores **+Synergy Plasm**, capped at max. For example, Synergy 3 returns with 1 and the caul brings her to 4.
3. **Ghost Virtue**: the general ghost rules (p. 189) give **Virtue +1 WP once per scene** and Vice all WP once per chapter. App. 1 (p. 290) says the Absent "may do the same" (refill all WP) with Virtue once per scene. **Ruling [interp]: +1 WP for all ghosts, Absent PCs included.** This is recorded as a resolved contradiction. It may have been a deliberate PC exception, so a table can choose the generous reading for the Absent.
4. **Remembrance Trait cap**: p. 91 and p. 100 say dots up to **effective Rank**; p. 267 says spend "up to (Synergy) Plasm". **Ruling**: 1 Plasm per dot, **max dots = effective Rank** (p. 91 names Remembrance Traits as bounded by effective Rank). Other rules (RAW):
   - The dots **may exceed trait maximums**.
   - Fixed-cost Merits are all-or-nothing: a 3-dot Merit costs 3 Plasm.
   - It lasts a scene.
   - The first use in a scene gives **Echoes**.
5. **Bound Geist Condition** (p. 304) says the geist's Attributes are "replaced with those derived from the host". p. 75 says its own Attributes are used **only when Unleashed**. These reconcile: on the leash it has no independent traits, and Unleashed it uses its own Power/Finesse/Resistance and the derived traits on p. 303.
6. **Synergy relationship bands** (table p. 91): **1 Coercive; 2–4 Positional; 5–7 Sympathetic; 8–10 Empathetic.**
7. **Abjuration** in GtS (p. 149, **RAW**):
   - Resolve + Composure vs Power + Resistance; instant and contested, but **requires 5 minutes**.
   - Success suppresses the entity's Manifestation Conditions within (WP) yards for **1 day**; exceptional, **1 week**.
   - Dramatic: no retry this story.
   - It differs from core; **using GtS's version in GtS games is a rules-priority convention**.
8. **Haunt pools**:
   - **Sin-Eater**: Synergy + Haunt (+ the Unlock Attribute when unlocked, which also applies to Enhancements that roll Synergy + Haunt).
   - **Unleashed geist**: **Power + Rank + Haunt**, and it **can't unlock Keys** (p. 76, p. 303).
   - **Supernatural Tolerance** [p. 101, RAW]: when a Haunt is **contested** by X + Synergy, other supernaturals use their equivalent (Blood Potency, Primal Urge, Wyrd, Gnosis; Rank for ghosts; Rapacity for eaters; 0 for mortals). This applies to contested resistance only, not to every formula containing Synergy.
9. **Burden-unique affinities**: the italics are lost in the .txt, but each of these Haunts appears in only one Burden's list: Tomb (Abiding), Oracle (Bereaved), Boneyard (Hungry), Dirge (Kindly), Rage (Vengeful). The reconstruction is reliable.
10. **Dread Geist** (•••, geist Rank 4): p. 76 frames it as a creation choice, and there's no later-purchase procedure. **Campaign restriction**: creation only unless the ST allows it. Later Rank comes from the non-XP sources (02 §C).
    **[interp]**: p. 76 says to give a Rank 3 Ban and Bane, but a Dread Geist is Rank 4, so give it **Rank 4 complexity Ban and Bane** (p. 190).
11. **Numina not defined in GtS** but used by its Reapers (core pp. 136–137):
    - **Aggressive Meme**: 7 Ess, vs Res + Com + ST, spreads by retelling.
    - **Dement**: 1 Ess, vs Int + ST → Insane for the scene.
    - **Essence Thief**: 1 Ess. It **lets the entity consume Essence from other types of ephemeral beings**, using that being's normal consumption rules. It doesn't add a separate drain roll.
    - See `../ctl2e/06_antagonists.md` §Numina and `../cod_core/06_antagonists.md`.
12. **Conditions referenced but not defined** in GtS: Guilty, Shaken, Broken, Madness, Informed, Steadfast (use core).
    - "**Swooning**" = core **Swooned**.
    - "**Obsessed**" = GtS's own **Obsession** (07; use that, don't import or invent "Obsessed").
    - Medium's "Resonance/Infrastructure" tiers come from core/WtF.
13. **"Council of Faith"** (archetype advice, pp. 37, 46) isn't a defined Merit. Treat it as descriptive and represent it with Allies, Contacts, Retainer or krewe resources.
14. **Area tables differ**:
    - **Raise the Boneyard** (1–3 Plasm, or up to 5 with No Escape): 1 = several rooms or a floor, 2 = ballroom or small house, 3 = large house or building.
    - **Dirge Communion** (1–5 Plasm): 1 = small room, 2 = large room, 3 = several rooms or a floor, 4 = ballroom or small house, 5 = large house or building.
    - Dénouement uses the Communion scale.
    - Communion turns the Dirge into an **area effect** contested once by the **highest Composure + ST** when individual rolls are impractical.

## B. RAW points worth stressing, and rulings
- **Starting Plasm is full** (RAW, p. 74): **10 / 11 / 12** at Synergy 1 / 2 / 3. **10 Merit dots**; 5 buy Synergy 2, all 10 buy Synergy 3; Tolerance for Biology free.
- **Merit dots spent on Ceremonies** at creation: 1 Merit dot = 1 Ceremony dot **[interp]**. After creation it's 2 XP/dot. Keep the currencies separate in ledgers.
- **Plasmic Healing** (RAW, p. 98):
  - 1 Plasm downgrades one box by one step, and the same box can be downgraded repeatedly.
  - **Downgrading damage *and* negating physical Tilts as they are inflicted ignore the per-turn cap**; doing either later is capped. For example, a Synergy 1 Sin-Eater hit for 4 lethal can pay 4 Plasm at once to make it bashing; next turn she could downgrade only 1.
  - Bane damage can't be downgraded.
  - Downgraded bashing still heals normally (15 min per point).
- **Unlocking a Key without a Haunt** (RAW, p. 121 sidebar): reflexive. **You gain the free Plasm and Doomed automatically**: no roll, so no exceptional success to avoid it, and **no WP buy-off** (that option belongs to unlocked Haunts).
- **Key free Plasm** (RAW): it can exceed max (the excess is lost at scene end; thematic +2 likewise) and is **exempt from the per-turn limit**. This exemption covers that free Plasm only; it doesn't raise the general per-turn cap. Several instances of the same Key stack free Plasm and Doomed only, **not the Unlock dice**.
- **Doomed by the same Key** (RAW): Doomed is tracked per **(Key, Source)**. You can't unlock with that **source** again while Doomed, but other instances (a second Memento with the Key) still work.
- **Ectophagia** (RAW, pp. 95, 99):
  - **Costs Synergy = the Rank consumed** (a Rank 2 ghost −2; a Rank 3 geist −3). This hits Plasm max, per-turn limit, aura, effective Rank and relationship.
  - The target must be **incapable of fighting back**: Immobilized, hibernating, Engulfed, unconscious-equivalent. A ghost that's grappled but still able to contest **doesn't qualify**.
  - **[interp]** Speed: 1 aggravated per Plasm consumed, **at most the per-turn Plasm limit each turn**, as an instant action.
  - Barghests and Castoffs are exempt only from the breaking or crisis point. **[interp]** Synergy loss per Rank still applies (usually 0–2).
- **Hard to Kill** (RAW): no unconsciousness or bleeding out, but wound penalties apply. Further damage upgrades boxes normally. **Death comes when the last box is aggravated**, or from the specific fatal exceptions. A big Plasm pool makes her very hard to kill, not unkillable.
- **Death exceptions (no return)**, all together:
  1. Killed by the **geist's Bane**.
  2. A death **resonating with her original death**.
  3. Dying at **Synergy 0**.
  4. **Old age**.
  5. The **geist is destroyed or consumed while she has the Dead Condition**.
- **Unleashed endings** (RAW, p. 303):
  - Scene end: it **ends unresolved**.
  - Geist Corpus filled with lethal or aggravated: it **resolves**, the Sin-Eater takes a Synergy Beat and **loses all remaining Plasm**. That alone is **not** her death; permanent death needs the destruction to happen **while she is Dead**.
  - While Unleashed the Sin-Eater has Weakened Bond (no Plasm spending). The geist uses the shared pool and can use **Down and Dirty combat** against lower-Rank ghosts and template-less humans.
- **Crisis points** (RAW, pp. 92–94):
  - The ST picks the response. At Sympathetic or Empathetic, a player who **forgoes intercession** may choose it.
  - A crisis Unleashing **doesn't count** against once per chapter. It lasts until the cause is gone or destroyed, or scene end, and ignores other triggers meanwhile.
  - **Universal triggers** (Bane damage, deliberately ignoring the Ban, a wound in the rightmost 3 boxes): **put them on every sheet**.
  - **[interp]** On a failed intercession, "the geist responds **or** Wavering" is **the player's choice**.
- **Touchstone Synergy boost** (RAW, p. 84): +2 (one Touchstone pursued) or +3 (several) effective Synergy.
  - It is excluded **only** from granting new Touchstones and from buying traits above normal max. **Everything else uses the effective value**: Haunt pools, **max Plasm and per-turn**, intercession, aura radius and Condition, contested resistance, **effective Rank (capped by geist Rank)** and therefore the Remembrance cap.
  - The higher max isn't auto-filled. **[interp]** When the boost ends, the max returns to normal and the excess Plasm is lost.
  - Example: Synergy 1 with one Touchstone → effective 3: max 12, 3 per turn, effective Rank 3 (Rank 3 geist).
- **Liminal Aura** (RAW): Condition by Synergy: 1 none, 2–5 Anchor, 6–8 Open, 9–10 Controlled. **Each 1 WP shifts it one step** (repeatable, for the scene). Any ghost in 10 × Synergy yards can use it as its own.
- **Fetter on a Sin-Eater**: allowed, a strong reading of RAW.
  - Possession Immunity (p. 97) blocks only Possess, Claim, body-swaps and mind replacement; control effects still work.
  - Fetter needs **Open** on the target (the aura can supply it at Synergy 6+, or via WP), costs 2 Ess, rolls **Power + Resistance vs Resolve + Composure + Synergy**, and gives **Urged**.
  - Consequence: powerful Sin-Eaters are easy for ghosts to approach, but never to possess.
- **Ghost damage** (RAW, pp. 195, 197, 308):
  | Attack on a ghost | Damage |
  |---|---|
  | Ordinary punch or gun (via Ghost Touch) | bashing |
  | **Rage** unarmed | lethal |
  | **Castoff weapons** | lethal |
  | **Bane as a weapon** | lethal (Twilight or Underworld), aggravated (Materialized) |
  | **Ectophagia** | aggravated |
  Overflow upgrades normally.
- **Dispersion ≠ destruction** (RAW, p. 195): with **≥1 Essence**, a full Corpus means it disperses and reforms in hibernation. With **0 Essence** it's destroyed: Rank 1–2 leave Plasm, Rank 3+ leave a **Deathmask**.
- **Geists are Broken** (RAW, p. 197): every Social roll by or at a geist, except to intimidate or frighten, is a **chance die**. You can't simply talk a geist round; that's what Remembrance and Synergy are for.
- **Reaper mask removal**: RAW requires the **Deathmask's Bane present**; the reaper itself dons or doffs it instantly. **[house]** procedure:
  1. Physical interaction must be possible.
  2. Bring the Bane in.
  3. Gain control: grapple (Restrain / Take Item-style move) or a contested disarm.
  4. On success the mask comes off and the Reaper's traits recalculate to its **Rank 2** unmasked state (it isn't destroyed).
- **Remaining dead** (RAW): requires Empathetic (Synergy 8+). Roll **(Synergy − 7)** dice: 1 / 2 / 3 at Synergy 8 / 9 / 10.
- **Geist Rank without XP** (RAW, pp. 77, 84, 267), max 5:
  - Burden Aspiration resolved: +1 Synergy **and** +1 Rank.
  - Each Remembrance scene: +1 Synergy.
  - Final Remembrance scene: +1 Rank.
  - A Touchstone resolved: +1 Rank.
  - Never charge XP for these. XP alone doesn't raise geist Rank.
- **Experienced starts** (no age table in GtS):
  1. Build a legal starting character with a Rank 3 geist.
  2. Spend the bonus XP at **normal costs (Synergy 5 XP/dot)**.
  3. Apply any **documented** Burden, Remembrance or Touchstone resolutions free.
  4. Recalculate effective Rank and derived traits.
  - No hard Synergy cap; any cap is a campaign benchmark.

## C. Common misreadings to avoid
- Sin-Eaters have **no Integrity and no breaking points**. Their analogue is **crisis points** (the geist's triggers). Mortals around them still get breaking points (seeing Vitiate, Obsess…).
- **The geist isn't a separate combatant** unless Unleashed (once per chapter, or by crisis). On the leash it's an immaterial image.
- **Haunts aren't Numina**: they cost **Plasm**, roll **Synergy + Haunt**, and create Conditions with **charges**.
- **Ghosts don't gain Essence from being remembered by a Sin-Eater** [p. 11, 189].
- **Abjuration doesn't destroy** a ghost; it suppresses its Manifestation Conditions. Destruction needs Corpus damage with no Essence, ectophagia, Ghost Binding, or a Rank-3+ Deathmask death.
- **The Bound in the Underworld are nourished by its food** and gain Plasm; living mortals aren't.
- **Haunt Conditions and the geist's Ban/Bane**: most end or resolve when the creating geist's Ban or Bane is confronted, but **check who must face it**. The **Boneyard** ends when its **creator** faces it; Curse, Marionette, Dirge and similar resolve when the **affected target** (or the curse mark) meets it; self-Conditions (Caul, Rage, Shroud, Oracle, Memoria) resolve when the Sin-Eater does. It isn't a universal off-switch.
- **Ectophagia isn't a refuelling trick**: it costs Synergy equal to the Rank eaten.

## D. Self-test findings (2026-10-13)
The test scene was a Vengeful Sin-Eater (Synergy 2 via Merits) plus a dead celebrant against the masked Mourning Groom hunting a Rank 1 ghost in a cemetery. It covered Aura Flare, Key unlocks with Doom, failed Haunts, a grapple and Engulf, a universal crisis trigger and intercession, Plasmic healing limits, the ghost Corpus-full case, opening the Gate, and a mortal witness's breaking point.
**No extract errors were found.** The subagent built the character and scene correctly: free traits, Synergy 2 via 5 Merit dots, affinity Haunts, Keys, Plasm caps, Doom on failure, and the fact that bashing can't be downgraded. Rulings for the RAW-silent points it raised:
1. **Ghost attack pools**: Power + Finesse (core). Ghost Touch is mutual. Patched in 06 §A.
2. **Engulf** needs a **successful grapple contest** ("winning a grapple"), not just the initial grab [p. 193].
3. **The captor's Corpus fills** (it dissolves to reform) → **Engulfed victims are expelled** at that spot and begin regaining Essence **[interp]**, because the prison is gone. Engulfed lasts at most 1 day unless re-paid.
4. **Anchorless ghosts (geists, Reapers) whose Corpus fills** reform in hibernation **on the shore of the nearest River in the Underworld** [p. 195 rule for the Underworld; interp for the living world]. They're swept through the nearest Gate, which doesn't stay open.
5. **A Gate opened by a Reaper's passage** stays open only for that passage (no lingering Underworld Gate) **[interp]**.
6. **A Different Key (suffer the Doom to open a Gate)** works **from either side** **[interp]**. p. 121 says anyone with the knowledge can use Keys to open Avernian Gates. It isn't usable if that Key source is already Doomed.
7. **Aura Flare**: it affects everyone in the area (no exclusions **[interp]**). A ghost's "Composure + Synergy" = **Resistance + Rank**. Willing targets may choose not to contest.
8. **Wound penalties apply to intercession** (they apply to all rolls except where stated).
9. **Plasm paid into a failed Haunt is lost**, and **a failed Haunt still risks Doom** (only an exceptional success or 1 WP avoids it).
10. **Chance Doom's "+3 bonus"**: the net bonus dice to the roll from any source (equipment, WP, teamwork), excluding Key Unlock dice **[interp]**.
11. **Eating Plasm beyond max**: only Key free Plasm and thematic +2 can exceed it. Everything else is capped.
12. **Dead celebrant**: Twilight Form + **1** Manifestation (krewe creation overrides "Rank Manifestations"). Starting Essence **half of max** (8 at Rank 2) **[interp]**.
13. **A Deathmask's Bane** (needed to rip it off) = the Reaper's **masked-form Bane**. For the Desperate Spouse: motor oil.
14. **Child ghost Size 3–4**; Corpus = Resistance + Size.
15. **Root/Bloom archetype triggers**: now listed in 05 §6.
