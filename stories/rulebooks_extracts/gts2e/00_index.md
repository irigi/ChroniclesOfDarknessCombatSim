GtS is built on the CoD core engine (`../cod_core/`). **GtS overrides core for Sin-Eaters and ghosts**:
- **Synergy** replaces Integrity.
- **Plasm** is the fuel.
- **Haunts** are the powers (**Synergy + Haunt**), unlocked with **Keys**.
- **Crisis points** replace breaking points.
- It has **its own ghost rules** (Ch4) and abjuration.
