# GtS 2e — Lore (Intro, Ch2 "The Road Back")

Mechanics live in 02–07; this file is the world model a Storyteller needs.

## Truths and lies (sidebar) [p. 11–12]
- **Sin-Eaters aren't possessed.** The Bargain is a symbiosis between a powerful ghost (the **geist**) and a recently dead person. The geist doesn't subsume or control the body.
- **They are metaphysically dead.** The Bargain restores biological life (breath, heartbeat, eating), but **magic that specifically affects the living doesn't work on them**, and **ghosts don't regain Essence when a Sin-Eater remembers them** [p. 11].
- **They aren't immortal.** The geist brings them back from fatal wounds, disease and deprivation (see 05 §Death & resurrection). **Old age** still kills them. So do attacks that **echo their original death** or that use their **geist's bane** [p. 11].
- They see and touch ghosts as if solid. Their **Liminal Aura** makes Manifesting easier for ghosts nearby [p. 11].
- Most organise **krewes**: mystery cults of Sin-Eaters, living mortals and ghosts. Many are religious, from built-from-scratch faiths to fringe sects [p. 11].
- "Sin-Eater" = a Bound who **actively protects the dead** from the Underworld, Reapers and their like. "**Bound**" = anyone who has made the Bargain. Bound who don't care are "Storyteller characters" [p. 11, 14].

## Ghosts and the dead [p. 55–57]
- Ghosts are tied to **Anchors** in **Twilight**. Leaving an Anchor bleeds Essence. Weak ghosts loop their death; lucid ones watch "through a foggy window" with gaps in memory [p. 55–56].
- Being **remembered or mourned** keeps a ghost strong. The dead **don't grow**: changing a ghost requires outside force [p. 57].
- **Plasm** is Essence "congealed" by a Manifestation: fingerprints, blood leaking from a cabinet, sludge. It is what Sin-Eaters harvest (05 §Plasm) [p. 56].
- When its Anchors are gone, a ghost is pulled or blown through an **Avernian Gate** into the Underworld. **Reapers** (masked ghost-hunters with hooks and chains, Chthonic-God worshippers) come up to drag ghosts down [p. 57].
- **Geists** are ghosts who **drank from a River** of the Underworld (or were otherwise grown by it). They lost most of their human identity and became archetypal/elemental, "something older, darker". They long for what they lost; this is their **Remembrance** [p. 14, 57, 58].
- Non-human ghosts exist: animals, objects, buildings [p. 57 sidebar].
- **Moving on** (passing beyond) is a one-way door. Resolving business doesn't guarantee it. Most ghosts need help from the sympathetic living [p. 57].

## The Bargain [p. 57–60]
- At death, a geist drawn by the dying person's **Burden** grabs their shade and offers the Bargain. Those who refuse aren't around to say what happens [p. 58–59].
- The Bargain needs **death resonance**: an intense death event (a pileup, a murder room, a plague, a war). Historically Sin-Eaters rose in waves after mass death and then faded. **Since WWII** the limit seems gone, so Sin-Eaters now arise steadily [p. 60–61 sidebar "Pebbles and Avalanches"].
- After the Bargain, the geist isn't always present. It watches, sometimes "pulls", aching to act through the Bound (Synergy and crisis points, 05) [p. 59].
- If the death was public, the Bound must handle coming back "from the dead" [p. 60].

## Three exits (end-games, details in 05 §End game) [p. 62]
- **Catharsis**: the Bound lays down the Burden and the geist recovers its identity. Both move on.
- **Catabasis**: a sacred journey into the Underworld to confront the Chthonic Gods and change the Underworld. Corpses of failed Catabases litter the Lower Mysteries.
- **Cabeiros**: the road of power. The Bound force-feeds the geist from the Rivers, "becomes one with the Underworld", and maybe a Chthonic God, or maybe it's a trap.

## Underworld geography (overview; travel rules in 05) [p. 62–67]
- **Low places / cenotes**: Twilight areas holding an **Avernian Gate**. **Every cemetery** has one; so do places stained by death [p. 14, 62].
- **Upper Reaches / the Depths**: they look like whatever lies underground nearby (catacombs, subway tunnels, caverns, submarine). Always cold and wet. Every tunnel slopes **down**. Detritus (ghosts of lost objects) drifts down and still holds Essence; ghosts eat it. Strictly, the Upper Reaches **aren't the Underworld proper**: that's the lands touched by the Rivers [p. 63].
- **Rivers of the Dead**: you must **sail** them to go anywhere real. Bridges only lead back into the Reaches or River Cities. Named Rivers [p. 65 sidebar]:
  - **Acheron** (woe): drinking helps recall painful memories.
  - **Anahita** (life): heals; the waters are limited and guarded by Reapers.
  - **Eresh-ki-gala** (dead seed): its seeds cure infertility.
  - **Id-Kura** (consumption): ghosts feel sympathy for the drinker, at the cost of unquenchable thirst.
  - **Kohan-Il** (pus): repels ghosts and Reapers, but leeches the drinker's health.
  - **Lethe** (memory): the drinker can answer any question but forgets a life detail per answer.
  - **Urdabrunnr** (fate): shows two futures, success and failure, for each action.
- **Boatmen/Ferrymen** pole the Rivers. Fee: two coins traditionally, or solidified Plasm, a pint of blood, secrets, or a valuable item. **The Bound can act as boatmen.** Krewemates must pay their Sin-Eater boatman in full, not with loans. A properly paid boat **keeps passengers safe from attack and River dangers**. Those who sail without paying come back missing an eye or a finger. Mechanics: 05 [p. 65].
- **River Cities**: shantytowns of ghosts living on dredged castoffs, run by gangs, merchant kings and cults. Reapers raid any that grow strong. Example: **Dead Man's Hand**, guarded by the Death Valley Krewe [p. 63–64].
- **Lower Mysteries / Dead (Deep) Dominions**: there are thousands, each behind a Sumerian-style gate with a **Guardian** ghost who recites the **Old Laws**. Inside, the **Underworld's Essence bleed is staunched**. Laws get stranger the deeper you go. Breaking one brings the **Kerberos** of that Dominion, who knows instantly and may send Reapers after those who flee [p. 65–66]. Named Dominions: An-Shot-Ka (the Triptych), Lowgate Prison (the Faceless Warden), Gehenna, the Forge of Orcus (souls forged into goods), the Vault (a library of lost knowledge) [p. 66 sidebar]. Full Dominions with Laws: 06 §Dominions.
- **Ocean of Fragments**: every River ends here. Its waters strip **identifiers** ("I am a mother"…) until only "I am" is left, then that too. It can strip unwanted identity ("I am a vampire"). Residents: the **Leviathan** (its Kerberos), the **Freighter** and its **Admiral** (collects identifiers; rumoured to hold the boatmen's), and the **Hermit** on the Black Beach [p. 66–67].
- **Chthonic Gods** are the deities of the Underworld, worshipped by Reapers. They may be literal or may simply *be* the Underworld [p. 64].

## Society [p. 60–62, Ch1]
- Krewes are personal religions built from the members' lives, geists' fever-dreams and Underworld lore. Modern krewes look like mahjong groups, DEA cells or jazz bands, and connect online [p. 61–62].
- Sin-Eaters find each other by **Twilight sigils** visible to the dead [p. 60 sidebar].
- The 5 **Burdens** and 5 krewe **Archetypes**: see 08.
