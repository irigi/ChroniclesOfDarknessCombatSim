# GtS 2e — Character creation, geist creation, advancement, krewe creation

## A. Sin-Eater creation (quick ref p. 77; detail pp. 71–75)
1. **Concept** + **3 Aspirations** (2 short-term, 1 long-term) [p. 72].
2. **Attributes**: 1 free dot in each, then **5/4/3** by category. Max 5 [p. 72].
3. **Skills**: **11/7/4**. Max 5. Untrained penalty as core (−3 Mental, −1 Physical/Social) [p. 72].
4. **3 Skill Specialties** [p. 73].
5. **Sin-Eater template** [p. 73–74]:
   - **Burden** → **3 Haunt affinities** + an extra **Burden Aspiration** (agreed with the ST; resolving it = 08/05 bonus below).
     | Burden | Affinity Haunts (*unique to Burden*) |
     |---|---|
     | Abiding (no legacy) | Caul, Memoria, *Tomb* |
     | Bereaved (lost a loved one) | Curse, Oracle, Shroud |
     | Hungry (can't let go of something) | Boneyard, Caul, Marionette |
     | Kindly (seeks to make amends) | *Dirge*, Marionette, Shroud |
     | Vengeful (wronged, seeks revenge) | Curse, Memoria, *Rage* |
     The italics are lost in the .txt. These unique ones are inferred, since each Haunt appears in only one Burden list: Tomb, Dirge, Rage, Oracle (Bereaved), Boneyard (Hungry). The ×2 Haunts are Caul, Memoria, Curse, Shroud and Marionette. **[interp]** Bereaved→Oracle and Hungry→Boneyard are therefore also unique.
   - **Root** (relationship to the dead) and **Bloom** (to the living). Choose one of each from the archetypes or make your own. They work like Virtue/Vice-style Willpower anchors (05 §Anchors) [p. 81].
   - **Touchstone**: 1, tied to the Burden [p. 83].
   - **Synergy 1** [p. 90].
   - **Haunts: 3 dots**, at least 2 of them in affinity Haunts [p. 74].
   - **Key: 1**, reflecting the circumstances of the character's death (03 §Keys). The geist's **innate Key** is a second Key (below).
   - **Plasm**: start with a **full pool**, max set by Synergy (05 §Synergy table) [p. 74].
   - The quick ref also lists **Remembrance Skills** at this step. These are the geist's Remembrance Trait, usable via Synergy (05 §Remembrance Traits) [p. 74, 77].
6. **Merits** [p. 74–75]:
   - **Tolerance for Biology** is **free** (04).
   - **10 Merit dots**, no category priority. They can also buy **Ceremonies** (04 Ceremonies; 1 dot of Merit = 1 dot of Ceremony **[interp]**; XP cost 2/dot).
   - **Synergy increase**: 5 Merit dots → Synergy 2; all 10 → Synergy 3. **Max starting Synergy 3.** Starting at Synergy 3 gives a **second Touchstone** tied to the geist's Remembrance.
7. **Advantages** [p. 75]:
   - Defense = lower of Dex or Wits, + Athletics.
   - Size 5. Health = Size + Stamina.
   - Willpower = Resolve + Composure.
   - Initiative = Dex + Composure. Speed = Str + Dex + 5.
   - **No Integrity**: Sin-Eaters use **Synergy** as their morality-like trait (05).

## B. Geist creation [p. 75–76]
1. **Concept**: archetypal and nameless (known by titles), tied to a cause or concept of death, always recognisably human. Example: the Snow Queen (death by exposure).
   **Remembrance**: one sensory image clue to who it was; the ST builds the rest (07 §Remembrance).
2. **Remembrance Trait**: **one Skill, or one Merit of ≤3 dots**. Example: Snow Queen → Intimidation. Use: 05 §Remembrance Traits.
3. **Attributes**: Power/Finesse/Resistance, 1 free each **+12 dots**, **max 9** at creation. These are used **only while Unleashed** [p. 75]. Example: Power 7, Finesse 3, Resistance 5.
4. **Virtue and Vice** (ghost-style, p. 145).
5. **Crisis point trigger**: choose one (05 §Crisis points). Example: Betrayal.
6. **Ephemeral traits** [p. 76]: **Rank 3** (higher with the Dread Geist Merit). **Ban and Bane** appropriate to Rank 3 (Rank 4 complexity if Dread Geist **[interp]**). **Innate Key**: the Sin-Eater can unlock Haunts with it; **while Unleashed the geist treats it as an Influence at dots = Rank**.
   "Missing" traits [p. 76 sidebar]:
   - **Willpower is shared** with the Sin-Eater.
   - **Essence**: the **shared Plasm pool**. The geist spends Plasm whenever a rule says Essence.
   - **Numina**: none. An **Unleashed** geist can use the same Haunts as its Sin-Eater **but can't unlock them with Keys**.
   - **Influence**: only the innate Key, only while Unleashed.
   - **Manifestation**: only via Unleashing. Otherwise it **projects an image** of itself in Twilight.
7. **Advantages** (used only when Unleashed) [p. 76]: Defense = lower of Power or Finesse. Size 5. Initiative = Finesse + Resistance. Speed = Power + Finesse + 5. **Corpus** = Resistance + Size (standard ephemeral rule, p. 189) **[interp: not restated]**.

## C. Advancement [p. 76–77]
- **Beats** come from Aspirations, end of chapter, resolving Conditions, dramatic failures (and chance-die dramatic failures), and other ST awards. **One Beat per category per scene.**
- **Common pool**: all PCs' Beats go in one shared pool. When the pool holds **(5 × number of players)** Beats of a type, **each player gets 1 Experience** of that type. Types: normal, **Synergy**, **Krewe**. Types don't combine. You can split a cost between types within their restrictions [p. 76–77].
  - Krewe Experiences → only krewe traits. Synergy Experiences → only Synergy.
- **XP costs** [p. 77]:
  | Trait | Cost/dot |
  |---|---|
  | Attribute | 4 |
  | Skill | 2 |
  | Specialty | 1 |
  | Merit | 1 |
  | Haunt (affinity) | 3 |
  | Haunt (non-affinity) | 4 |
  | Ceremony | 2 |
  | Synergy | 5 |
  The costs are flat per dot, as in other 2e games (no "new rating ×").
- **Non-XP advancement** [p. 77]:
  - **Resolving the Burden Aspiration**: +1 Synergy **and** geist Rank +1 (max 5).
  - **Resolving a Remembrance scene**: +1 Synergy per scene resolved. Resolving the **whole** Remembrance: geist Rank +1 (max 5).
  - **Resolving a Touchstone's core issue**: geist +1 Rank [p. 84].
- **Experienced starts**: there's no age table. Spend bonus XP at normal costs (**Synergy 5 XP/dot**) and apply documented Burden, Remembrance or Touchstone resolutions free (see 99 §B).

## D. Krewe creation [p. 77–81]
- Cast: the PC Sin-Eaters (or a ghost PC, App. 1), **dead celebrants** and **living celebrants**.
- **Dead celebrant** [p. 78–79]:
  - **Rank 2 ghost**, **9 dots** in Power/Finesse/Resistance. **[interp]** No base dot is mentioned; treat the 9 as a total, each Attribute ≥1.
  - **2 Aspirations**, Virtue and Vice.
  - **2 Anchors**: one is always its remains; the other is a person, place or thing (mobile is useful).
  - **1 Manifestation** (Materialize is rare at Rank 1–2) plus Twilight Form free.
  - **2 dots of Influence** over its Anchors. **3 Numina**. Standard derived traits.
- **Living celebrant** [p. 79]: name, concept, an Aspiration, and **3 actions** (general tasks, not Skills) rated **5, 4 and 3 dice**. **Any other solo action: 2 dice.** No supernatural Merits unless the krewe has Exceptional Membership.
- **Krewe sheet** [p. 79–80]:
  1. **Archetype** (08).
  2. **3 Doctrines**: active statements of faith.
  3. **Virtue** (highest ideal) and **Creed** (easy devotion, like a Vice).
  4. **Attributes** Power / Finesse / Resistance: 1 free each **+6 dots**. Resistance is also a membership yardstick: **~10 × Resistance members**, not counting main characters.
  5. **Merits**: **7 dots** with the Krewe or Krewe Only tag, plus a free **Safe Place •** (the centre of worship). Also design the krewe's **Mystery Cult Initiation** Merit (most celebrants have dot 1; advanced ones via Supernatural Membership).
  6. **Ceremonies**: a **1-, a 2- and a 3-dot Ceremony set by archetype** (08) **plus Bestow Regalia**. **Any celebrant with sufficient understanding** of the mysteries can use krewe Ceremonies.
  7. **Advantages**: **Esotery 1**; **Congregation = 5 + Resistance** (works like Health).
- **Krewe XP costs** [p. 80]: Attribute 6/dot, Merit 1, Ceremony 2, **Esotery 5 (Krewe Beats only)**.
- Run a no-dice **krewe prelude** (organisation, great works) [p. 80–81].
