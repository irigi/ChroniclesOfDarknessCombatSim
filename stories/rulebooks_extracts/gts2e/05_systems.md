## §1 Synergy [p. 90–91]
The bond with the geist, 1–10. It **replaces Integrity** (Sin-Eaters have **crisis points**, not breaking points; mortals keep breaking points) [p. 72 sidebar].

| Synergy | Max Plasm / per turn | Trait max | Touchstones | Liminal Aura Condition | Relationship |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10/1 | 5 | 1 | none | Coercive |
| 2 | 11/2 | 5 | 1 | Anchor | Positional |
| 3 | 12/3 | 5 | 2 | Anchor | Positional |
| 4 | 13/4 | 5 | 2 | Anchor | Positional |
| 5 | 15/5 | 5 | 2 | Anchor | Sympathetic |
| 6 | 20/6 | 6 | 3 | Open | Sympathetic |
| 7 | 30/7 | 7 | 3 | Open | Sympathetic |
| 8 | 40/8 | 8 | 3 | Open | Empathetic |
| 9 | 50/9 | 9 | 3 | Controlled | Empathetic |
| 10 | 100/10 | 10 | 3 | Controlled | Empathetic |
[table p. 91]

- **Trait max**: at Synergy 6+, Attributes and Skills can rise to Synergy.
- **Every Haunt pool includes Synergy** (Synergy + Haunt).
- **Effective Rank** = **lower of Synergy or geist Rank**. It applies **only against ghosts, geists and death-aligned spirits** (e.g. for Rank-based modifiers, and **an entity can't use powers on a higher-Rank target without Clash/Willpower per core rules [interp]**). It also **caps** Remembrance Trait dots and some aura effects [p. 91].
- **Relationship** tells you how much you sense of the geist. You always know where it is. Positional: its general emotional state. Sympathetic: specific emotions. Empathetic: the basic reasons [p. 91].
- **Touchstone boost**: while actively pursuing one Touchstone, **effective Synergy +2**; pursuing several, **+3**. RAW: it **doesn't unlock new Touchstones or let you buy traits above normal max, but "in all other ways functions normally"**. So it raises Haunt pools, **max Plasm and per-turn**, intercession, aura radius and Condition, and effective Rank (up to geist Rank). The higher max isn't auto-filled; **[interp]** the excess is lost when the boost ends [p. 84].

## §2 Crisis points [p. 91–95]
A crisis point is when the geist wants something and the Bound won't relent. The geist lashes out.

**Procedure**:
1. An **unlocked trigger** occurs.
2. The ST picks a **response** without telling the player. **Exception**: at a Sympathetic or Empathetic relationship, if the player **forgoes intercession**, they **choose** the response.
3. The player may **intercede**: a reflexive roll of the **relationship pool**.

| Relationship | Intercession pool |
|---|---|
| Coercive | chance die |
| Positional | Synergy |
| Sympathetic | (lower of Presence or Manipulation) + Synergy |
| Empathetic | (higher of Presence or Manipulation) + Synergy |

**Intercession results**:
- **Dramatic failure**: the geist responds **unless the Sin-Eater sacrifices 1 Synergy**, and her next crisis point is resolved as though the relationship were one step lower.
- **Failure**: the geist responds, **or** the Sin-Eater takes the **Wavering** Condition (her choice **[interp]**).
- **Success**: the geist doesn't respond, and the Sin-Eater gains **Bonding**.
- **Exceptional success**: as success, plus 2 dice to her next Synergy roll.

**If it responds**:
- The geist is **Unleashed** (§5). This **doesn't count** against the once-per-chapter Unleash limit.
- The **player still controls the geist** but must act according to the response.
- It stays Unleashed until the trigger is destroyed or gone, or the scene ends. While Unleashed for a crisis it **ignores other triggers**.

**Responses**:
- **Defend**: human shield, and proactively attacks threats to the Sin-Eater.
- **Escape**: gets them both away, attacking anyone in the way. If she won't come, it carries her.
- **Lash Out**: destroys the trigger, attacking anyone who keeps opposing it on the way.
- **Obsess**: fixates on the trigger and attacks only distractions. **Humans studied this way suffer a breaking point.**

**Triggers** [p. 92–93]:
- Each geist has a **set of three** triggers (Hazy → Vague → Specific). **Only one is unlocked at a time.** The **Hazy** trigger is unlocked at start; the **first Remembrance scene** unlocks Vague; the **final Remembrance scene** unlocks Specific.
- **Universal triggers** (all geists, always):
  - taking damage from the geist's **bane**
  - deliberately ignoring its **ban**
  - suffering a wound in one of the **rightmost 3 Health boxes**
- Geists aren't omniscient. A trigger needs perception, but a strong (even wrong) suspicion can set it off [sidebar p. 92].

| Set | Hazy | Vague | Specific |
|---|---|---|---|
| Betrayal | significant loss caused by a friend or ally | a friend or ally fulfils one of your Aspirations in a way that robs you of it | a friend or ally undermines your Burden |
| Helplessness | harm you can't mitigate or control | **dramatically fail a contested action** | watch a loved one die despite your efforts |
| Jealousy | neglect the Remembrance for the living | intercede in a crisis specifically to protect a loved one | actively pursue severing the Bargain |
| Malice | victim of a deliberate attempt to discredit, harm or disempower you | lose Social Merits to deliberate blocking | an Aspiration deliberately thwarted |
| Misfortune | freak circumstances (near-miss car, pickpocket) | material harm from bad luck (big losses, a car-crash injury) | material harm from bad luck in the same form as the geist's death |

## §3 Losing Synergy [p. 95]
| Event | Loss |
|---|---|
| **Return from the dead** | 1 |
| Forcing the geist to **drink from an Underworld River** | 3 |
| **Ectophagia** | 1 per Rank consumed |
| **Creating a Memento** | 1 per Memento |

- **Synergy 0** → **Flatlining** Condition. The three options:
  1. **Die**: at Synergy 0 the geist won't bring her back (no Dead Condition; she may return as a ghost, App. 1).
  2. **Complete a Remembrance scene** → Synergy 1, Flatlining removed.
  3. **Tyranny**: ritually **destroy the geist's (Remembrance) Touchstone**, forcing it to obey. Synergy is replaced by **Tyranny •** (06) and she becomes an **ST character**. This requires having reached Synergy 3 at some point.
- Gaining Synergy: XP (5/dot), Synergy Experiences, Burden Aspiration, Remembrance scenes (02 §C).

## §4 Plasm [p. 95–96]
Plasm is physical: milky, viscous, sometimes jelly or sludge. Anyone can touch it. Only the Bound can use it.

**Gaining** (it must be **physically consumed**: eaten, drunk, smoked, injected…):
- **Consuming is an instant action.** You can consume per turn as much as you can spend per turn. A source that **fully refills** the pool is consumed in one turn.
- **Thematic resonance**: consuming it in a way that echoes your death gives **+2 Plasm**. This can exceed the max; the excess vanishes at scene end. Extruded Plasm never counts.

**Sources**:
- **Key unlock**: Plasm = your rating in that Key's **Unlock Attribute** (03 §Keys).
- **Materialized ghost**: leaves Plasm = its Corpus.
- **Other Manifestations** in the living world: a trail of Plasm = their Essence cost.
- **A ghost moving on** (resolving Anchors): concentrated Plasm that **fully restores** a pool.
- **Underworld feasts**: the same amount as the Essence a ghost would gain (p. 203).
- **Krewe rites** (p. 177).
- **Ectophagia** (§5).
- **Cenote** Merit (Plasm = dots per chapter).
- **Grave Goods** (eat an item: Availability).
- Your **caul after resurrection** (§5; RAW gives no amount. **[house]** +Synergy Plasm after the Dead reset to 1, capped at max; see 99 A2).

**Spending** (always **reflexive**; capped per turn by Synergy):
- If an effect costs more than the per-turn cap, spend over consecutive turns. The effect happens on the turn the cost is met.
- Uses: Haunts; Remembrance Traits; Plasmic Healing.
- **Extrusion**: create Plasm from your pool for others to eat.
- **Open**: spend **(target's Size)** Plasm to coat a target, which gains the **Open** Condition until chapter end or until washed off. If the whole cost fits in one turn's spend you may coat an **unwilling** target with a **touch attack**; otherwise the target must be willing, inanimate or helpless.
- **Feeding a ghost**: **3 Plasm → 1 Essence**.

## §5 Sin-Eater abilities [p. 96–100]
### Physical Medium
- **Ghost Touch**: sees, hears, talks to and **touches** ghosts and other ephemeral beings **with death-related Influences or Numina**, as easily as the living. This extends to **anything she wears or uses, including ranged weapons**. She can punch, grapple or kiss them.
- It does **not** let her see non-death Twilight entities (spirits, angels).
- **Free Tolerance for Biology.**
- **Effective Rank** = lower of Synergy or geist Rank.
- **Second Sight**: automatically perceives ghosts possessing humans, and recognises other Bound by their geists. A concealing supernatural effect → **Clash of Wills** [p. 97].
### Possession Immunity [p. 97]
- **Any** attempt to possess a Sin-Eater **automatically fails**: Possess and Claim Manifestations, body-swaps, mind replacement.
- It does **not** cover mind or body control by other means (Dominate, Marionette…).
### Plasmic Healing [p. 97–98]
- **1 Plasm downgrades one Health box** (agg → lethal, or lethal → bashing). The same box can be downgraded repeatedly.
- **Remove a Tilt caused by physical damage**: **2** Plasm (bashing), **4** (lethal), **8** (aggravated).
- Reflexive. **Downgrading damage or negating a Tilt as it is inflicted ignores the per-turn Plasm cap**; done later, the cap applies.
- **Not usable on damage from the geist's Bane.**
- **Hard to Kill**: the Bound **never fall unconscious from damage and never bleed out** (wound penalties still apply).
- It can regrow or replace lost parts with Plasm (narrative) [p. 98].
- Plasm doesn't heal bashing; it only downgrades. Normal healing times apply afterwards **[interp]**.
### Death and return [p. 98–99]
- A Sin-Eater who dies gains the **Dead** Condition (07) and **wakes at the next dawn or dusk**, whole (even if dismembered), face covered by a **Plasm caul** she can eat. Removed parts crumble to grave dust.
- **A life for a life**: someone **near her dies** in her place. She experiences the victim's last minute.
- She **loses 1 Synergy** (§3).
- **No return** (no Dead Condition) if:
  - she is at **Synergy 0**
  - the death **resonates with her original death** (agree what counts at creation: a knife through the heart → a similar stabbing or the same knife; a snake species → any similar venom)
  - she is killed by the **geist's Bane**
- **Old age** also kills [p. 11]. So does the **geist being destroyed or consumed while she is Dead** [p. 299].
- **Timing**: the Dead Condition resolves at **scene end** (Health all lethal, Tilts gone, Plasm 1, −1 Synergy). She physically awakens at the next dawn or dusk **[interp]**; see 99 A2.
- **Remaining dead by choice**: only with an **Empathetic** relationship. Roll **(Synergy − 7)** dice (i.e. a chance die at Synergy 8 **[interp: 0 → chance die]**); success means the geist lets her stay dead [sidebar p. 99].
### Liminal Aura [p. 99]
- **Recognition**: other Bound, ghosts and death-aligned ephemera recognise the Bound on sight within the aura.
- **Area**: **10 × Synergy yards**.
- **Persistent Manifestation Condition** per the Synergy table (none / Anchor / Open / Controlled). It applies to the area and to every creature or object in it. **Any ghost can use it as if it were tagged to him.**
- **Shifting it**: 1 WP raises or lowers the Condition one step (none → Anchor → Open → Controlled). Repeatable. The change ends at scene end.
- **Aura Flare**:
  - Once per scene, 1 WP, instant and contested, area 10 × Synergy yards.
  - **Presence + Synergy vs. highest (Composure + Synergy)** among targets, or vs. another being's aura pool.
  - Success: living targets gain **Spooked**, dead targets gain **Inspired**; against another aura, yours supersedes it.
  - Exceptional: targets' **impression improves one level** for Social Maneuvering.
  - Failure: no effect.
  - Dramatic: targets get **+2 on their next action against her**, or impression worsens one step.
### Ectophagia [p. 99–100]
- Literally eating a ghost's Plasmic viscera.
- The **target must be helpless** (incapable of fighting back).
- **1 aggravated Corpus damage per Plasm consumed** (subject to the per-turn limit **[interp]**).
- A ghost killed this way **doesn't reform**.
- **Geists**: only **Unbound** geists, or those whose Sin-Eater is **Dead**. Eating another Bound's geist means killing that Sin-Eater first, and **she stays dead**. Fully consuming a geist **refills your Plasm** and **teaches you its innate Key**.
- Costs **1 Synergy per Rank consumed** (§3).
### Remembrance Traits [p. 100]
- **Spend Plasm** to gain dots in the geist's Remembrance Skill or Merit, **up to effective Rank**. **1 Plasm per dot [interp: cost per dot not stated; "spend Plasm to gain dots" read as 1:1]**. These dots **can exceed trait maximums**.
- Fixed-cost Merits are **all or nothing**.
- Lasts **one scene**. First use in a scene → **Echoes** Condition.
### Unleashing the geist [p. 100–101]
- **Once per chapter**, **instant action**: the geist gains **Unleashed**, the Sin-Eater gains **Weakened Bond** (07).
- Crisis-point Unleashing doesn't count against the limit.
- To Unleash more often the Bound "needs to hurt", a reference to Weakened Bond and Unleashed Condition terms; see 07.
- **On the Leash** [p. 100 sidebar]: a non-Unleashed geist lives where her soul was. It shares her senses and has no body control. It projects an **image in Twilight** that ghosts and other Bound can see but that is **immaterial even to Twilight beings**. Sin-Eater and geist can touch each other but **can't damage each other**.

## §6 Anchors: Root, Bloom, Touchstones [p. 81–84]
**Root** (relationship to death and the dead):
- Once per scene, regain **1 WP** when the Root leads you to put the dead or your own death first.
- Once per story, regain **all WP** when the Root leads you to risk your life or living-world status for the dead, or to protect the dead from the living at great cost.

**Bloom** (relationship to the living):
- Once per scene, **1 WP** when it leads you to ignore the dead or your death to tend to the living.
- Once per story, **all WP** for risking your life or status among the dead for the living, or protecting the living from the dead at great cost.

**Archetypes**: sample acts for 1 WP / all WP [p. 82–83].
- **Advocate**: win a significant concession for the ignored / create a lasting benefit for a community.
- **Antihero**: an unconventional, ignoble or blunt solution / take major risks to be known as the right kind of trouble.
- **Casual**: show someone their duty or scheme isn't worth it / defuse an impending climax to everyone's satisfaction.
- **Cowl**: remind someone in power of their mortality / publicly crack a powerful figure's composure.
- **Enabler**: convince someone to give in to their dark side / lead someone to a major breaking point and through it.
- **Gardener**: set up a win-win by pitting one problem against another / set a major player on a path you chose.
- **Pollyanna**: gamble on trusting someone's motives / put everything on the line for what you want over what you fear.
- **Saved**: apply old-life knowledge to the new life / pay it forward, risking your gains.
- **Servant**: obey a command or request despite personal cost / put someone else's catharsis above your own safety.
- **Surrogate**: devote yourself to someone's need / help someone resolve a breaking-point Condition.

**Touchstones** [p. 83–84]:
- They unlock at **Synergy 1** (**Burden** Touchstone), **3** (**Remembrance** Touchstone, the key to the geist's humanity) and **6** (**Cause** Touchstone, your fight against the Underworld).
- Falling below a level **suspends** that Touchstone until Synergy rises again.
- Defending your attachment against a significant obstacle → **+1 WP**; at serious risk or major cost, **all WP**.
- Actively pursuing one → **effective Synergy +2**; several → **+3** (§1).
- If pursuing one hinders another → **Indebted** Condition.
- **Resolving a Touchstone's core issue → geist +1 Rank.**

Examples: 9 to 5, Albatross, First Friend, House Keys, Inherited Anchor, Murder Weapon, New Neighbor, Our Song, Replacement Love, Roadside Memorial.

## §7 Supernatural conflict & Clash of Wills [p. 101]
- Haunts contested by Synergy use the **closest equivalent** trait against other supernaturals (Primal Urge, Azoth…). This applies only to contested rolls, not when Synergy is subtracted.
- **Sin-Eaters add Synergy to every contested pool against a supernatural power that uses a power trait** (their Supernatural Tolerance).
- **Clash of Wills**: each side rolls **Haunt + Synergy** or the **Ceremony pool** (others use their own book's pool). Most successes wins. WP can only be spent if both are physically present and aware of the clash.
- **Duration modifiers** for long-lasting effects: **+1** night, **+2** week, **+3** month, **+4** year.

## §8 Ch4 "Old Laws": deltas from the core rules [pp. 139–173]
Ch4 reprints the core engine: Attributes, Skills, Specialties, Willpower, actions, rolling, Social Maneuvering, combat, injury, sources of harm, equipment. Use `../cod_core/05_systems.md` for the full rules. Geist-specific points:
- **Willpower Anchors**: for Sin-Eaters, Root/Bloom/Touchstones replace Virtue/Vice (mortals and geists keep Virtue/Vice) [p. 145]. Max WP 10.
- **Exceptional success** default: take a beneficial Condition, usually **Inspired** [p. 152]. **Voluntary dramatic failure**: take a Beat [p. 152].
- **Abjuration** (Ch4 version) [p. 149]: instant and contested, **Resolve + Composure vs the entity's Power + Resistance**, **requires 5 minutes**.
  - Success: **every Manifestation Condition the entity tagged within (your Willpower) yards is suppressed for 1 day**. Exceptional: 1 week.
  - Dramatic: you can't try to abjure that entity again this story.
  - (The core abjuration ends with the entity driven off and has a different pool; **Geist's version wins for GtS games**.)
- **Warding and Binding** [p. 151]: instant and contested, **Resolve + Composure vs Power + Resistance**, **requires 15 minutes**, and needs the entity's **Bane**. You mark a boundary.
  - Success: crossing it counts as violating the entity's **Ban** for **(successes) days** or until the boundary is broken. Exceptional: (successes) **weeks**.
  - Dramatic: no further tries against that entity this story.
- **Down and Dirty combat** [p. 156]: combat pool vs the opponent's combat pool or escape pool (Str or Dex + Athletics). Success: damage = difference + weapon, and intent achieved. Exceptional: +1 WP. Failure: the opponent's difference + weapon is dealt to you if they rolled combat, or they escape. Dramatic: the opposite of your intent. If your intent was non-lethal and someone dies anyway, lose 1 WP.
- **Beaten Down & Surrender** (optional) [p. 156]: more than Stamina in bashing, or any lethal → **Beaten Down**. The victims of a violent intent don't get it. Killers like Reapers never get it. **The Bound don't get Beaten Down** unless something would stop their resurrection (Synergy 0, a resonant-death attack, bane).
- **Grapple, Dodge, bite** as core: grab = unarmed attack → grapple; Strength + Brawl contests; everyone acts on the highest Initiative. "Humans (and most Sin-Eaters)" must grapple before biting [p. 158].
- **Injury**: core damage, healing (bashing 15 min, lethal 2 days, aggravated 1 week) and **bleeding out**. **Sin-Eaters don't fall unconscious or bleed out** (§5 Hard to Kill) [p. 162].
- **Sources of harm**: disease, poison (Toxicity, Stamina + Resolve − Toxicity), drugs, electricity, extreme environments (levels 1–4 as a penalty), falling (1 bashing per 3 yd under 30 yd; 30+ yd = 10 lethal), fire [p. 163–164].
- **Equipment** [p. 165–174]: core list plus **Talcum Powder** and **Luminol** as ghost-detection aids.

## §9 Krewes [pp. 174–184]
**Traits**:
- **Attributes**: Power (direct action), Finesse (soft leverage), Resistance (loyalty; resists infiltration). Rated 1–5, higher with Esotery. **Krewe pool = Attribute + Esotery** [p. 174–175].
- **Merits**: using a krewe Merit **isn't a krewe action** [p. 175].
- **Doctrines** (3) [p. 175]:
  - **End of chapter: 1 Krewe Beat per Doctrine actively lived or advanced** (the group decides).
  - **Rewriting** a Doctrine costs 1 Esotery (or happens after a Schism).
  - A krewe action that violates a Doctrine, or a **leader** (a PC, or an STC with MCI •••+) openly violating one → **Shaken Faith**.
  - A leader who violates one secretly gains **Obsession** (keeping the secret). When it resolves, the krewe gains Shaken Faith.
- **Virtue / Creed** [p. 175]:
  - Fulfilling the **Creed** (a group action in accord): every participating celebrant **+1 WP**, once per scene.
  - Fulfilling the **Virtue** (meaningful action despite risk): **all WP**, once per chapter.
  - **Ghost celebrants may take Essence instead.**
- **Congregation** = 5 + Resistance. It's the krewe's Health [p. 176].
- **Esotery** (1–10; above 5 is rare) [p. 176–177]:
  | Esotery | Krewe actions per story | Task limit | Attribute max | Regalia |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 1 | 3 | 5 | 0 |
  | 2 | 1 | 3 | 5 | 1 |
  | 3 | 2 | 5 | 7 | 1 |
  | 4 | 2 | 5 | 7 | 2 |
  | 5 | 3 | 7 | 9 | 2 |
  | 6 | 3 | 7 | 9 | 2 |
  | 7 | 4 | 9 | 12 | 3 |
  | 8 | 4 | 9 | 12 | 3 |
  | 9 | 5 | 9 | 15 | 3 |
  | 10 | 5 | 10 | 15 | 4 |
  - Starting a project beyond the action limit: **1 lethal Congregation damage per Effort roll**.
  - Esotery rises only with **Krewe XP** (5/dot) and some Condition resolutions.
  - **No member's MCI can exceed Esotery.**
- **Krewe Ceremonies** [p. 177]: the archetype's 1/2/3-dot set **+ Bestow Regalia**. Buy more with Krewe XP. **Any member with MCI ≥ the Ceremony's dots** can perform it.
- **Regalia** [p. 177–178]:
  - Start from the **Regalia** Condition (07) and add one **effect**. Each effect once per krewe; one wearer per Regalia at a time. Granted via **Bestow Regalia**.
  - **Glory**: **8-again** on one mythic Skill. Resolve: **½ Esotery (round down) automatic successes** on one roll with it.
  - **Eminence**: a Sin-Eater, ghost or geist wearer gets **+1 Rank** (the living count as Rank 1). Resolve: every lower-Ranked entity that can see you gains **Beaten Down**.
  - **Power**: use a krewe-mythic Key **as an innate Key** for a scene. Resolve: unlock with it **without Doomed**.
  - Sample: Orpheus the Lover: +1 impression with Sin-Eaters and 8-again on Expression.

**Krewe action** (a long-term project; an opposed roll or a Merit activation is not one) [p. 178–181]:
1. **Desired outcome.**
2. **Complexity 1–10**:
   - 1: find a famous ghost's Anchor; sway a councillor.
   - 3: an unremarkable ghost's Anchor; induct a politician; a district.
   - 5: an unrecorded or purged ghost's Anchor; regional branches.
   - 7: a geist's former Anchor; national stardom.
   - 10: a Kerberos's former Anchor; infiltrate a national agency; unify a region's Bound.
3. **Tasks** = Complexity, and must be ≤ the **Task limit**; otherwise split into several krewe actions.
4. **Structure** each Task: simple or contested; time (≥1 hour); order. **Pool = krewe Attribute + Esotery**, or a PC's or major STC's own pool if they do it. Avoid nested extended actions.
5. **Generate Effort** (one roll per Task):
   - Modifiers: no skill set −1 to −3; with a krewe Leader +2; **violating Doctrine −5**; watched by authorities (living or dead) −2; planning +1; time pressure −2; special equipment −1 to −3; potential breaking point −1 to −4.
   - Success: 1 Effort. **Exceptional: 2 Effort** plus a Condition (Connected, Leveraged…).
   - Failure: no Effort and **one Complication now**; dependent Tasks take −2.
   - Dramatic: also an ST-chosen negative Condition; if the Task was a prerequisite, the **Task limit drops by 1**.
   - **Effort** lasts until story end and is exclusive to that action. **Spend Effort 1:1 for temporary dots in a relevant Social Merit** (Retainer, Staff) for a chapter.
6. **Repeat.**
7. **Resolve**: krewe actions **always succeed** unless abandoned. **Effort ≥ Complexity: no complications.** Each point short adds 1 complication.

**Complications**:
- **Casualties**
- **Shaken Faith**
- **2 lethal Congregation damage**
- a new enemy
- **Indebted**
- attention from higher up
- a **schism**
- a deeper problem revealed
- side effects (Leveraged, Notoriety)

You can "zoom in" and play a Task as a scene [sidebar p. 180].

**Krewe vs individual** [sidebar p. 181]: a Sin-Eater backed by her krewe in a fight → Down and Dirty with a **Teamwork bonus = krewe Power**. If the foe can fight a group, use krewe vs krewe on a scale of seconds or minutes.

**Krewe vs krewe** [p. 181–182]:
- Destroying a krewe is a krewe action with no set scale. Each Effort = **1 lethal to the enemy Congregation**. Effort ≥ the enemy's Congregation → it gains **Coup d'Etat**.
- Tasks are **contested**; the winner takes the Effort.
- A lower-Esotery krewe may start a second krewe action to keep opposing. If it doesn't, the bigger krewe's remaining Tasks are unopposed at **−½ the smaller krewe's Power**.

**Congregation damage, healing and schism** [p. 182]:
- Bashing = slipping faith, lethal = unease with leadership, aggravated = apostasy.
- **Heals**: bashing 1 per 2 days, lethal 1 per week, aggravated 1 per month.
- **Track full of lethal → Schism.** Choose one, each of which clears all Congregation damage:
  - **Purge**: −1 Power permanently, plus **Notoriety**.
  - **Resolve**: −1 Finesse, plus **Leveraged**.
  - **Splinter**: −1 Resistance, plus **Diminished**.
- If you don't choose: no krewe actions, 1 aggravated damage, then 1 aggravated per day. **Full of aggravated: the krewe disbands.** Members keep Merits (Sanctity) but lose Esotery and Regalia.

**Krewe Conditions** [p. 182–184]:
- **Casualties**:
  - Effect: −2 Power; name an injured STC.
  - Resolve: declare that STC dead (all players consent), or take no krewe actions for a whole chapter.
- **Diminished**:
  - Effect: a leader spends 1 WP at the start of each krewe action.
  - Resolve: fulfil the Virtue, complete a krewe action with no complications, or gain Esotery.
- **Shaken Faith** (Persistent):
  - Effect: fulfilling the Virtue restores only 1 WP, Plasm or Essence. Each chapter the ST rolls **(10 − Resistance)** dice and keeps successes. At **10** accumulated it becomes **Coup d'Etat or Heresy**. Gaining Shaken Faith again converts it at once.
  - Sources: a leader's violation exposed; Congregation full of bashing.
  - Resolve: **gain 1 Esotery**, or it worsens.
- **Coup d'Etat** (Persistent):
  - Effect: **all krewe actions are reduced to a chance die**; once per chapter the ST adds a coup complication.
  - Sources: Congregation full of lethal; Shaken Faith worsening; outside subversion.
  - Resolve: disband, purge the disloyal, or a bigger threat unifies the krewe.
  - Beat: abandoned by disloyal members.
- **Heresy** (Persistent):
  - Effect: a temporary heretical Doctrine; the ST tallies Beats gained from it. **All Tasks become contested vs (Heresy Beats)**; the Heresy steals Effort. If it out-Efforts the krewe, it **replaces a Doctrine**.
  - Sources: Congregation full of aggravated; Shaken Faith worsening.
  - Resolve: schism, destroy the heretics, or adopt the Doctrine.
  - Beat: fulfilling the heretical Doctrine.
- The krewe system can be ported to other CoD organisations (rename Esotery to "Standing") [sidebar p. 184].

## §10 The Underworld [pp. 199–211] (lore and geography: 01)
**No Twilight in the Underworld.** Everything there is ephemeral, so the living, ghosts and the Bound all see and touch each other [p. 187]. Arriving Bound **manifest the wounds of their death** and stand out to the dead because of their aura [p. 204].

**Avernian Gates** [p. 199–200]:
- One stands at **every place marked by death** (graveyards, morgues, crossroads, battlefields, murder sites).
- Normally closed. **A ghost with no Anchors always opens one** (to pull it through). **They always open for Reapers** and their Deathmasks.
- Each Gate has a **key** (an object, tears, a verse…). Without the key you must pick the lock. **A Gate's key only works from the living side.**
- **Opening one gives the Underworld Gate Condition** (07).
- **Ways to open a Gate**:
  - **Bloody-Handed Payment**: contested extended **Wits + Subterfuge vs 6 dice**, needing **10 more successes than the Gate**, 1-hour interval, **one sacrifice per roll** (destroy the body or it becomes an Anchor). Expensive sacrifice (Resources •••+) +2. Exceptional: 30-minute interval. Dramatic: noticed by an unsympathetic party.
  - **Picking the Lock**: instant but takes **30 minutes**. **Attribute + Occult** (the Attribute depends on the mourning method; any meaningful mourning ritual works). **Mourning an actual death +3.** Exceptional: the ritual becomes a new key for that Gate. Dramatic: no lock-picking on that Gate for the story. **Phantom Limb Ghost Leg** turns this into an instant Str + Sta + Merit kick.
  - **Pay the Toll**: **ghosts only, once ever**: the two leaden coins open any Gate for that ghost.
  - **Crash the Gates**: a pound or two of semtex destroys one, leaving it open (Underworld Gate) until story end.
- **Crossing back** (from below):
  - **A Different Key**: anyone able to unlock a Key (Bound, geists) can **suffer that Key's Doom** to open a Gate, with no other benefit.
  - **Mirror ritual**: as Picking the Lock, but **celebrating life** rather than mourning.
  - Reapers can always open them.
- **Guardian Geist** [p. 200]: the **first person interred** at a site that births a Gate rises as a **Rank 3** ghost bound to it. Many Gates have lost theirs.
- **Shroud ••••• (Descent)** crosses with no Gate (03).

**Navigation ("Dead Roads")** [p. 201–202]:
- A journey is split into **legs**: landmark to landmark, or a transition between layers. Navigate **each leg** reflexively; an interruption of a scene or more forces a new roll. Identifying the legs to a destination is a **Research** action.
- Common results: **Failure** = off-trail and a hazard or threat (you can backtrack). **Dramatic** = **Lost** (or, for Society, an immediate hazard).
| Method | Pool | Modifiers | Exceptional |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Architecture** | Int + Academics | Upper Reaches +1, River Cities +0, Lower Mysteries −5 | **Informed** about the area |
| **Society** (move among and talk to the dead) | varies (Empathy, Persuasion, Intimidation, Subterfuge) | large population +2, sparse −2, deserted −5 | **Connected** to the destination's culture |
| **Instinct** | varies (Physical + Athletics/Survival) | first time −5, days −3, months −1, years +0, decades +1, centuries +2 | **Steadfast** |
| **Key** (Bound only; **innate or ectophagia Keys only**, not Mementos) | **Unlock Attribute + Occult** | **requires being Doomed by that Key**. The subject is a ghost whose death resonates with the Key; you needn't know where it is. Identical death +3 | **resolves the Doomed** Condition on arrival |
- Mystery Cult "Forty-Third Nome" •••••: navigation takes half as long [p. 87].

**Survival** [p. 202–204]:
- **Underworld Survival**: Wits + Survival, a few hours. Success: **1 Essence** of edible detritus. Exceptional: **5**. Dramatic: something dangerous.
- Ghosts in the Underworld pay **1 Essence per 24 h** to stay active. **No Essence → Trapped in the Walls**: they lose **1 Integrity per 24 h**; 3 Essence restores 1 Integrity; at **0 the ghost is gone forever**.
- **Following the Old Laws in a Dominion for a day: +1 Essence** (no Essence bleed there).
- **The living can't be nourished** by Underworld food. They starve without noticing, which feels like light-headedness. **The Bound *are* nourished** (they count as dead) and **gain Plasm** from it (05 §4) [sidebar p. 203].
- **Dark Markets / merchant kings**: River Cities are free of the Old Laws; almost anything can be bought there at exorbitant prices [p. 203–204].
- How River Cities react to a visiting Bound: mobbed by the desperate, sequestered by the ruling gang, or indifferent (when a Bound already rules there) [p. 204].

**The Rivers** [p. 204–206]:
- **A ghost fully submerged takes 1 lethal per turn.**
- **Drinking** (a ghost): **1 aggravated per turn for (10 − Integrity) turns, then +1 Rank**. This is the only normal way for ghosts to gain Rank. It's a ghost breaking point. **Forcing your geist to drink costs 3 Synergy** (§3).
- **Ferrymen**: payment makes the trip **safe**. They always know the way. **Putting to shore ends the journey**; hiring again means paying again.
- **A Bound becomes a Ferryman** by owning a river-worthy vessel and **swearing an oath** to convey passengers safely for a fair price → **Ferry Bound** Condition. Any navigation method works on the Rivers. Bound Ferrymen sense a Dominion's coming collapse.
- **Staying Silent** (unprotected boats): Composure + Stealth for the voyage. Hushed conversation −1, normal −2, shouting −3. Failure: a **Chthonian** notices you and you're in imminent danger. Dramatic: its attack is the first sign. Exceptional: ask the ST one true question about the River.

**Dominions** [p. 206–207]:
- **24 h in a Dominion: +1 Essence** for ghosts.
- **Breaking an Old Law → Defiant** Condition. The **Kerberos senses it** and punishes. For ghosts it's a breaking point.
- A Kerberos never breaks its own Laws. A paradox trap is theoretically possible, with "Bad Things" as the result.
- **Irkalla's Gates**: often a Dominion's entrance. A **guardian (Rank 3–5)** reforms at its gate when destroyed. **Toll**: a part of you (an arm, your voice, a garment that rots if replaced), often a **Persistent Condition, Tilt or Essence**. You can't normally pay twice. **Geists, and their Bound companions, pass free**; so do Reapers (who smuggle passengers in their bellies).

**The Undiscovered Country** [p. 208–210]:
- **Ghost Tracking**:
  - Contested extended, **5 successes**, 1-day interval. **Wits + Investigation or Empathy vs (10 − ghost's Integrity)**.
  - Modifiers: no tie −3, an object owned −1, an important object +1, **its Anchor +3**; −1 per Rank above 2. Dead <1 week +2, weeks 0, months −2, years −4, 10+ years −5. In the Depths or River Cities +2, near a River −2, in a Dominion −3, near the Sea of Shards −5. **Oracle Haunt +2**; knows the subject +1.
  - Success: found. Exceptional: Inspired. Failure: **Obsessed**. Dramatic: a roadblock.
- **Uncanny Tableaux / Break the Cycle**:
  - Mythic scenes on the riverbanks. Breaking one needs a symbol of the cycle broken (a Memento from a ghost who moved on, the keepsake of an averted death) for **each key role**. The bearers insert themselves into the roles and go off-script.
  - **Synergy + Attribute (ST picks)**, basic instant. +2 if your death resonates with the role.
  - **Every participant must succeed at least once.** Then the tableau moves on and the krewe takes a **Krewe Beat**.
  - Exceptional: +3 to the next attempt. Failure: compelled to act the role (damage, crisis points…). Dramatic: **Insensate**.
- **Ylem**: palm-sized, indestructible vortices of Essence and memory, the remains of failed krewes. A trance gives an ST-crafted Condition; resolving it gives a **Krewe Beat** and dissolves the ylem into Essence. They can seed Regalia. Adopting one may count as redefining or breaking Doctrine.
- **Suborning a Dominion (As Below, So Above)**:
  - Requires a Doctrine similar to one of its Old Laws (or change a Doctrine to match). Takes a **full story**.
  - At climaxes: **Taking Control**, contested instant: **Esotery + krewe Attribute vs Kerberos's Resistance + Rank**. Success replaces that Old Law with a **New Law** from a Doctrine (the first New Law is always the matching one). Exceptional: all members **Connected** to the Dominion. Dramatic: the Kerberos treats it as a violation.
  - When every Law is replaced, **the Kerberos dies**. The krewe's Sin-Eaters are bound to uphold the New Laws; violating Doctrine then has sympathetic effects across the Dominion, or on all bound Sin-Eaters.

**Ocean of Fragments** [p. 211]:
- **Each turn immersed: lose 1 trait dot** (Merits first, including supernatural traits counted as Merits; then Skills, then Willpower, then Attributes). PCs get the Experiences refunded. **Every trait lost = the character ceases to exist.**
- **Navigating the Ocean**: Resolve + Occult per voyage. Never sailed it −5, sailed before −3, visited a bordering Dominion −1. Exceptional: disturbingly fast. Failure: Lost. Dramatic: the **Leviathan or the Admiral**.

## §11 Remembrance [p. 266–267]
- **Creating it** (ST):
  1. Talk to the player: the core theme (tragedy, hubris, greed); what recalls the geist's best self and its greatest failing; how it relates to the Sin-Eater (maternal, protective, abusive).
  2. Write a **one-sentence sketch** of who it was and why it drank from the Rivers.
  3. Starting from the creation image, devise **3 more scenes** (discover → understand → resolve), each with flexible links to the next.
- A **scene is resolved** when the character has enough to start tracking the next one; the final scene when the story reaches a satisfying end. It needn't be what the geist wants. Use Investigation, Social Maneuvering with ghosts as eyewitnesses, and similar tools.
- Pacing: about **one Remembrance scene per character per chapter**.
- **Rewards** [p. 267]:
  - **+1 Synergy per Remembrance scene resolved.**
  - On resolving the **2nd and 3rd** scenes: choose an **additional Remembrance Trait**. You can spend **up to (Synergy) Plasm 1:1** for temporary dots in any Remembrance Trait.
  - **Final scene: geist +1 Rank** (max 5).
  - **First** scene resolved unlocks the **Vague** crisis trigger; the **final** scene unlocks **Specific** (§2).
  - A Remembrance scene also lifts **Synergy 0** to 1 (§3).

## §12 End game [p. 267–270]
- **Catharsis**:
  - Resolve **both** your **Burden Touchstone and the geist's Remembrance**. Sin-Eater and geist merge and move on, **bypassing the Underworld**. Some refuse the call and stay as bodhisattva-like guides.
  - Recommended: retire the character to an STC.
  - If one stays in play: **Synergy 10, no more crisis points, no Doomed from Keys; if she dies she moves on** (no resurrection).
- **Catabasis** (krewe only):
  - Ritual and mythic challenges to remake the Underworld, often alongside Catharsis.
  - Esotery milestones: **Esotery 3** "What do you stand for?" (an identity or Doctrine dilemma); **Esotery 5** "What will you sacrifice?"; **Esotery 7** "Who will you refuse?" (no-win); **Esotery 10** "Against impossible odds": descend and defeat an **embodiment of the Underworld** built from the krewe's archetype and Doctrines (the closest anyone gets to the Chthonic Gods).
  - Success: a **new Underworld** in the krewe's image. Failure: perhaps the Deep Dominions and their Kerberoi are failed Catabases.
- **Cabeiros**:
  - It starts with a Remembrance scene gone wrong. Requires having progressed through the **first 3 Remembrance scenes** and knowing the geist's **Touchstone**. In practice the Bound usually first converts to **Tyranny**.
  - **The Fatted Calf**: force the geist to drink from Rivers until **Rank 5**. **Each forced drink**: **chance die** (with Synergy) or **Str/Int/Pre + Tyranny** (with Tyranny), **vs the geist's Power + Rank**. About an hour each; **every drink from a different River**.
  - **The Final Act**: once the final Remembrance scene is known, ritually **devour your own geist**. You become one with the Underworld, a new **Chthonic God** (it leaves play).
