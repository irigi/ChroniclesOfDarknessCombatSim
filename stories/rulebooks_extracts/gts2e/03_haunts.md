# GtS 2e — Haunts, Keys, Ceremonies, Mementos (Ch3 pp. 100–135)

## §0 Haunt rules [p. 100–101]
- **Rated 1–5.** **Pool = Synergy + Haunt** (+ the **Unlock Attribute** of a Key if unlocked, §2). There's no resistance subtraction unless a contest is stated; contests use Synergy or a supernatural equivalent.
- **Cost**: the • power ("base effect") costs **1–X Plasm**. The Plasm spent usually sets the **charges** of the Haunt's **Condition** (07 §Haunt Conditions), or area, or Size. You **can't add Plasm to the base effect** of an already-active Haunt.
- **Enhancements** (••–•••••) cost extra Plasm. They can usually be added to an **active** Haunt **reflexively**. You **don't need to buy lower Enhancements** (pick and choose).
- **Per-turn limit**: if the cost exceeds Synergy's per-turn cap, spend over consecutive turns, and **roll only after all Plasm is paid** [p. 101].
- **Duration**: the Haunt and all its Enhancements end **when its Condition ends** [p. 101].
- **Exceptional success (all Haunts)**: when the Haunt ends **without resolving**, regain **half** the Plasm spent on the base power.
- **The "Free" ••• tier**: at ••• most Haunts gain a free upgrade, typically **the Condition gets 2 charges per Plasm** instead of 1, or a bigger area.
- **Haunt Plasm dissipates** after use; others can't eat it [p. 101].
- **Learning**: the first 3 dots come with the Bargain. **New Haunts** (a first dot in a Haunt you don't know) must be **found in the Underworld** (a scroll in a Dominion, a key from a River…). It's easier for affinity Haunts. Once a Haunt is known, higher dots grow by XP **[interp: ST may require a story hook only for the first dot]** [p. 101].
- **Supernatural conflict and Clash of Wills**: 05 §7.

## §1 The ten Haunts
Format: **Power (dots)**: cost · action · pool vs resistance · effect. Enhancement costs are per application. "×" = repeatable.

### The Boneyard: haunt a place [p. 101–103]
- **Raise the Boneyard (•)**:
  - Cost 1–3 Plasm; area 1 = several rooms or a floor, 2 = a ballroom or small house, 3 = a large house or building.
  - Instant to infuse; Synergy + Boneyard.
  - Success: the area gains the **Boneyard Environmental Tilt**. You know its layout and who is inside (living or dead, and where, but not who).
  - While it lasts you can roll **Synergy + Boneyard vs Composure + Synergy** to impose **Guilty, Shaken or Spooked** on someone inside.
  - Seizing another Sin-Eater's Boneyard: **Synergy + Boneyard vs Synergy + Boneyard**.
  - Dramatic: **Lost** Condition for the chapter when navigating anywhere except back there.
- **Eyes in the Paintings (••)**: 1 Plasm × — move your perception anywhere in the Boneyard (all senses). Returning it is free.
- **No Escape (•••)**:
  - Free: spend **up to 5** Plasm on Raise. Area 1 = small warehouse or parking lot, 2 = large warehouse or supermarket, 3 = small factory or mall, 4 = large factory or **city block**, 5 = **campus, small town or neighbourhood**.
  - 1–5 Plasm: leaving suffers **−Plasm spent**, and every attempt to leave **takes an action**.
- **Earthquake Weather (••••)**: 3 Plasm — create an Environmental Tilt (**Blizzard, Extreme Heat, Extreme Cold, Heavy Winds, Heavy Rain, Ice, Earthquake**), severity = Boneyard dots. You can end it reflexively. ×
- **The New Law (•••••)**:
  - 2 Plasm × (**only at creation**): declare a **rule**. Violators gain **Defiant** for the Boneyard's duration. **Rolls to break the law suffer −½ Synergy.** Entrants don't automatically know the rules.
  - 2 Plasm: rote quality on rolls to punish lawbreakers, and you **count as a Bane** to ghosts who violate the rule.

### The Caul: merge with the geist, transform the body [p. 103–106]
- **Extrude the Caul (•)**: 1–5 Plasm; instant; Synergy + Caul. Success: the **Caul Condition**, charges = Plasm. Dramatic: you're **trapped in Twilight** for the scene.
- **Cold Flesh (••)**: 3 Plasm — **general Armor = Caul dots, ballistic = ½ Caul (round up)**. Flavour: cold, bleeds Plasm, shrugs off fire and gas; details in the Caul Condition (07).
- **Vitiate (•••)**: inhuman shapes. **Seeing it is usually a breaking point for the living.**
  - Free: **2 charges per Plasm**.
  - 1–4 Plasm: **+Size = Plasm**, and +1 Strength per 2 Size.
  - 1 Plasm: spider-form, **double ground Speed**, climbs walls and ceilings at normal Speed.
  - 1 Plasm ×: the **next unarmed attack does lethal**.
  - 2 Plasm: **wings**, fly at Speed.
  - 4 Plasm: become a swarm or ooze that seeps through any crack that lets light in (1 turn per point of Size).
- **Disarticulation (••••)**: 1–5 Plasm ×, up to **Synergy** homunculi. Each homunculus has **Health = Plasm**, a **pool of 2 × Plasm** for Physical actions and a **chance die** for Social/Mental. It follows simple commands. Commanding any number of them is one instant action.
- **The Hungry Dead (•••••)**:
  - Cost: **victim's Size** in Plasm (free if the victim was eaten via ectophagia). **Swallow whole** a recently dead being or a recently incapacitated ghost of **Size ≤ 7**.
  - Until the Caul ends or another Caul Enhancement is used: **1 charge**: mimic its appearance for 5 minutes (extendable); a ghost appears as it did in life. **1 charge**: use its Attributes and Skills for one action; for a ghost, **Attribute + Rank**.
  - After that, the ability to mimic that victim is lost forever.

### The Curse: hex with a Plasm mark [p. 106–107]
- **Lay the Curse (•)**:
  - Requires **touch**. 1–4 Plasm; instant and contested; **Synergy + Curse vs Resolve + Synergy**.
  - Success: the **Curse Condition**, charges = Plasm. Bad luck; options in 07.
  - Dramatic: the victim regains 1 WP and **you** suffer the Curse until scene end.
- **Gremlin (••)**: 2 Plasm ×, adds an option to the Curse Condition:
  - (a) A piece of equipment goes haywire: its **bonus becomes a penalty** for the scene when the victim uses it. A second charge makes it **deal damage = its bonus** on next use.
  - (b) The victim **can't start fires** this scene, and fires they lit go out.
- **Malady (•••)**: Free: 2 charges per Plasm. 2 Plasm ×, add an option:
  - (a) **3 lethal damage, then the Condition resolves.**
  - (b) Inflict **Arm Wrack, Blinded, Deafened, Insane, Knocked Down, Leg Wrack, Poisoned or Sick** until scene end.
  - All Keys except Chance shape the misfortune; the Bastard's (Chance) Key makes it more random.
- **Exhaustion (••••)**: 2 Plasm ×, add an option:
  - (a) Turn the victim's **next Social roll into a chance die**.
  - (b) Block WP gain from one event.
- **Forgotten (•••••)**: 3 Plasm ×, add an option, each lasting the rest of the scene:
  - (a) The victim must **spend 1 WP** for anyone but you to register their presence.
  - (b) **Block one Social Merit.**
  - (c) The victim **sees and touches ghosts as if living** (and can't tell them apart).
- **Removing a Curse** [sidebar p. 106]: anyone with the Curse finds the mark and wipes it in a few minutes: **Synergy + Curse vs the curser's Synergy + Curse**. Success resolves it; exceptional also gives a Beat. Dismissing your own Curse is reflexive.

### The Dirge: a song of the dead [p. 107–109]
- **Sing the Dirge (•)**:
  - Requires a wordless song at conversational volume or louder, and choosing a simple emotion or action to urge ("calm", "stop fighting", "come to me").
  - 1–5 Plasm; instant and contested; subjects: **Plasm spent** targets who can hear; **Synergy + Dirge vs Composure + Synergy**.
  - Success: the **Dirge** Condition.
  - Dramatic: **−3** to Dirge that target for the story.
- **Paean (••)**:
  - 1–5 Plasm: give **Essence = Plasm** split among ghosts with the Dirge Condition (better than the 3:1 feeding rate).
  - 2 Plasm ×: a living subject with Dirge also gains **Inspired**.
- **Communion (•••)**:
  - Free: affects **everyone who can hear** in an area by Plasm: 1 small room, 2 large room, 3 several rooms or a floor, 4 a ballroom or small house, 5 a large house or building. If rolling for each target is impractical, contest against the **highest Composure + Synergy**.
  - 2 Plasm: **Perfect impression**, and you may roll **Synergy + Dirge to open Doors** against anyone with the Dirge Condition.
- **Exaltation (••••)**:
  - 2 Plasm ×: **remove (without resolving)** one of Beaten Down, Broken, Deprived, Guilty, Madness, Obsession, Shaken, Spooked. **Persistent Conditions are suppressed** until Dirge ends.
  - 3 Plasm ×: **give** one of Beaten Down, Connected, Deprived, Guilty, Inspired, Obsession, Shaken, Spooked, Swooning.
- **Visitation (•••••)** ("Voice of Orpheus"): spend Plasm = the **Rank** of a ghost with Dirge (1–5) ×; it gains a **Manifestation Condition of your choice** (Anchor, Open, Controlled, Materialized…) and so can manifest fully, be tied to objects, or possess.

### The Marionette: Plasm puppet strings [p. 109–110]
- **String the Marionette (•)**:
  - 1–3 Plasm; Synergy + Marionette. Instant against unattended objects or animals; **contested by Stamina + Synergy** against conscious humans (and other animals).
  - Success: a target of **Size ≤ Plasm spent** gains the **Marionette** Condition.
  - Dramatic: Marionette **automatically fails** on that target for the story.
- **Puppetmaster rules** [sidebar p. 109]:
  - Controlling a puppet for a turn is an **instant action**. Roll **Synergy + Marionette** for any action it takes.
  - A puppet does what it normally could (a car drives, a bird flies, a phone dials and reads messages).
  - Puppets can be moved telekinetically at **Speed = Synergy × Marionette**.
  - Heavy or sharp objects can be **thrown** as attacks (or puppets thrown into danger): improvised weapon damage, thrown range, with **Synergy + Marionette replacing Str + Athletics − Size** [p. 110].
- **Swarm (••)**: 2 Plasm: String affects targets **= Marionette dots**. Commanding several puppets is one instant action if they all do the same thing. **Attacking with multiple puppets halves the target's Defense.**
- **Phantom Strength (•••)**:
  - Free: Size limit becomes **Marionette + 2 × Plasm** (people, cars).
  - 2 Plasm: the **next resistance roll** against your control is at **−3** (once per action).
- **Servant (••••)**:
  - 2 Plasm ×: swap one target's Marionette for the **Servant** Condition (it obeys simple commands without your focus; also works on **recently dead bodies**, which still decay).
  - 4 Plasm: all Servant Conditions end without resolving after **24 hours** (i.e. they last 24 h) ×.
- **Traitor Flesh (•••••)**: full piloting of a person, who stays aware.
  - 2 Plasm ×: the next time a sapient puppet **takes lethal damage or suffers a breaking point**, the Condition **doesn't resolve**.
  - 3 Plasm ×: the next time a puppet resists you, it takes **2 lethal** (once per attempt).

### The Memoria: relive the past [p. 111–113]
- **Recall the Memoria (•)**:
  - Requires being at the site of a **traumatic death**, or near a ghost's **Anchor** related to the memory, and knowing **≥3 details** (name, date, killer, weapon…). It only works on **real** memories of a ghost's death or a significant event leading to it. "Channel surfing" fails.
  - 1–5 Plasm; instant; Synergy + Memoria.
  - Success: the **Memoria** Condition for the scene, charges = Plasm.
  - Dramatic: trapped in the vision, unable to interact with reality.
- **Dénouement (••)**: 1–5 Plasm (area as Communion: small room → large building): make the vision **visible to all present** as intangible illusion. Only you gain Memoria. Others may reflexively roll **Wits + Composure** to realise it's intangible before hurting themselves.
- **Memory in a Bottle (•••)**:
  - Free: 2 charges per Plasm.
  - 3 Plasm: resolve Memoria and bottle it. Anyone who **consumes** it gains Memoria with the same charges. **No Plasm** is gained from drinking it. While it's bottled, Recall on that memory fails. If the container is destroyed or emptied, the memory is lost forever.
- **Mystery Play (••••)**: 1–5 Plasm: draw **1 target per Plasm** into the scene as **Actors** (Actor Condition). Unwilling targets contest with **Resolve + Synergy**. You can't create more Actors than people present in the original. Ghosts may reclaim their original role.
- **Break the Cycle (•••••)**: 2 Plasm adds to all Actors:
  - **Go off-script** with a reflexive **Resolve + Synergy** roll, changing the outcome.
  - When Actor resolves, the target **also resolves a breaking-point or trauma Condition** tied to the memory.
  - A ghost resolving Actor may **resolve a relevant Anchor**.
  - A **Rank 1 ghost rises to Rank 2**.

### The Oracle: die briefly and send your ghost [p. 113–116]
- **Consult the Oracle (•)**:
  - The Sin-Eater briefly **dies** (ritual or will).
  - 1–4 Plasm; instant; **Oracle + Synergy**.
  - Success: the **Oracle** Condition, charges = Plasm. You ask questions and get cryptic sensory answers. The body answers bystanders' questions too.
  - Dramatic: your spirit gets lost and you gain the **Dead** Condition (a full death, so Synergy loss **[interp]**).
- **Wandering Shade (••)**: answers from across the **region**. The shade can't cross into the Underworld unless you're already past a Gate.
  - 1 Plasm: add questions: biggest threat to me and mine? who most needs our aid? who is guilty of crimes against the dead? what has been forgotten here?
- **Spirit Reading (•••)**:
  - Free: 2 charges per Plasm.
  - 2 Plasm: add questions: the **Ban** of [a ghost I know]? its **Bane**? what **ties** it to the world (its Anchors)?
- **Descent (••••)**: the shade descends into the Underworld unharmed.
  - 3 Plasm ×: answer **one question about the Underworld** (does an Irkalla Gate bar this Avernian Gate? the laws of this Dominion? does this Ferryman trade fairly?).
- **Nekyia (•••••)**: 4 Plasm: answer **one question about future events**.

### The Rage: violence [p. 116–117]
- **Vent the Rage (•)**:
  - 1–4 Plasm; instant; Synergy + Rage.
  - Success: the **Rage** Condition. Unarmed attacks become Plasm weapons; the weapon modifier and charges are in 07 Rage.
  - Dramatic: **1 lethal + 1 per Enhancement applied**, which **can't be downgraded** with Plasm.
- **Black-Iron Blade (••)**: 2 Plasm ×: your next damaging hit also inflicts **Arm Wrack, Blinded, Deafened, Knocked Down, Leg Wrack or Poisoned**.
- **Maelstrom (•••)**:
  - Free: **unarmed ranged attacks out to 30 yards**.
  - 2 Plasm ×: the next unarmed ranged attack is a **medium-burst autofire** attack (p. 159: **+2 dice, up to 3 targets** **[interp: core autofire rules]**).
- **Shatter (••••)**:
  - 3 Plasm ×: the **next unarmed attack deals aggravated**.
  - 2 Plasm ×: the next character who suffers a **breaking point** from Rage damage gains **Fugue**.
- **Breaking the World (•••••)**: 4 Plasm: the scene gains **Blizzard, Earthquake, Flooded, Heavy Rain, Heavy Winds or Ice** (even indoors). Severity = the Rage's weapon modifier. **You're immune.**

### The Shroud: become ghostly [p. 117–119]
- **Don the Shroud (•)**: 1–4 Plasm; instant; Synergy + Shroud. Success: the **Shroud** Condition, charges = Plasm (base: ghostly look and partial intangibility; see 07). Dramatic: the Plasm seals your face; you **suffocate** until you spend an instant action clearing it.
- **Vision of Mist (••)**:
  - 1 Plasm: no body heat; you don't trigger motion detectors, lasers or similar sensors.
  - 2 Plasm: **fly** at half Speed.
- **Haunting Presence (•••)**:
  - Free: **1 charge = Shroud-dots minutes in Twilight**.
  - 3 Plasm ×: gain one Manifestation or Numen usable in Twilight: **Discorporate** (instead of hibernation you reform 24 h later at **the last place you died**, with 0 Plasm), **Fetter, Image, Possess, Sign, Hallucination**. Roll **Synergy + Shroud** and **spend Plasm instead of Essence**.
  - **Anchors Aweigh** [sidebar p. 119]: Sin-Eaters have no Anchors or Influence, so the needed Manifestation Condition comes from their **Liminal Aura** (+WP to raise it), and anything **coated in Plasm is Open** (05 §4).
- **Harrow (••••)**: 2 Plasm adds a Shroud option: **bring a person you're holding into Twilight**. If they resist, **grapple** them first.
- **Descent (•••••)**: 3 Plasm adds an option:
  - Spend a charge to **cross into or out of the Upper Reaches** with **no Avernian Gate**.
  - Emerge anywhere with a strong emotional tie: a place lived in >1 year, or a major life event such as your death. With no destination you land somewhere random in the Upper Reaches, or near where you last entered when returning.
  - **+1 charge**: bring someone you're holding **into** (not out of) the Underworld; grapple if they resist.

### The Tomb: restore what death took [p. 119–121]
- **Open the Tomb (•)**:
  - Requires a piece of a **destroyed object** or a **dead** person or animal. Original Size up to **Tomb + Plasm**.
  - 1–4 Plasm; instant; Synergy + Tomb.
  - Success: the **Tomb** Condition: a Plasmic restoration or replica, identical down to blood stains and DNA. **Temporary**; see 07.
  - Dramatic: **Addled**.
- **Headstone (••)**: 2 Plasm (at activation): work from a **representation** (photo, recording) or a closely linked object (wedding ring, house keys) of a **specific** thing or person. A representation is recreated as depicted.
- **Empty Graves (•••)**:
  - Free: max Size becomes **(Tomb + Plasm) × 2**, and the **Tomb Condition no longer expires with time**.
  - Free: replicas of living beings follow simple instructions, with original **Physical** Attributes and Skills; **Social/Mental = chance die**.
  - 3 Plasm: the replica has **Open**, and the original's ghost **can Possess it** even without the Possess Manifestation.
- **Stygian Treasures (••••)**: 2 Plasm × (at activation): the item lets its user **see / hear / speak to the dead**, or **communicate between the living world and the Underworld**.
- **Terra Cotta Soldiers (•••••)**: 1–5 Plasm (at activation): use Open the Tomb on a **wholly symbolic** representation (joss money, ushabti) to create **equipment** (cost = Availability) or a **Merit** (cost = Merit dots: Retainer, Library…).

## §2 Keys [p. 121–124]
**Sources**:
- **Innate**: 1 from the Sin-Eater's own death, plus the **geist's innate Key** (shared via the Bargain).
- **Mementos**: holding or wearing one gives its Key.
- **Ectophagia** of a whole geist: its Key becomes a new innate Key.
- You can hold **multiple instances** of the same Key, which lets you use it more often.
- Anyone with the knowledge can also use Keys to **open Avernian Gates** (05 Underworld).

**Unlocking**:
- **Reflexive**, done as part of a Haunt's **• power**.
- **+Unlock Attribute** dice to that power, **and to any Enhancement that calls for a Synergy + Haunt roll**.
- **Free Plasm = your Unlock Attribute rating**. It can exceed max (the excess is lost at scene end) and **ignores the per-turn limit**.
- **Resonance**: if the use matches the Key's Resonance, the Haunt is **exceptional on 3 successes**.
- **Doom**: unless the Haunt rolls an **exceptional success** or you **pay 1 WP**, you gain **Doomed (Key, source)**.
- **One Key per Haunt.** Several instances of the *same* Key can be applied, but **only the free Plasm and the Doomed Conditions stack** (dice don't).
- **Unlocking without a Haunt** [sidebar]: reflexive; gives the free Plasm and the Doomed Condition **automatically** (no roll, so no exceptional; and no 1-WP buy-off). Used e.g. to fund damage downgrades.

**Doomed Condition** [p. 121–122]:
- Note it as Doomed (Key, Source). **Multiple Dooms can stack**, even from the same Key.
- **You can't unlock with the same Key from the same source** (the same Memento or the same instance of an innate Key) while Doomed by it.
- It ends **without resolving at story end**.
- **Resolve** it via the Key's Doom, **or** by helping a ghost whose death fits the Key resolve an Anchor (+Beat).

| Key (aliases) | Unlock Attr | Resonance | Doom (resolve by…) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beasts** (Primeval; Tooth & Claw) | **Wits** | a place where humanity isn't dominant (abandoned rat-infested buildings, wilderness, parks after dark); **any Haunt on an animal target** | automatically **fail an action targeting an animal**, or one an animal could plausibly hinder |
| **Blood** (Stigmatic; Veils & Shades) | **Presence** | a violent situation that **wasn't premeditated/intended** | the next attempt to **avoid violence** (de-escalate, flee) is an **automatic dramatic failure** (Beat for the dramatic failure + resolution) |
| **Chance** (Bastard's; Jinx & Hex) | **Dexterity** | risking something important (a treasured item, a friend, your life) on one action; **or** the target is a machine with ≥3 moving parts able to deal lethal damage (gun, press, wood chipper) | your next roll with a **+3 or better bonus** becomes a **chance die**; success on it counts as exceptional |
| **Cold Wind** (Breathless; Gale & Garrote) | **Resolve** | inside **Blizzard / Extreme Cold / Heavy Winds**; or noise too loud for spoken conversation | gain the **Extreme Cold** Tilt for **(10 − Synergy) hours**, or until you actively reveal a damaging personal secret; it resolves when the Tilt ends |
| **Deep Waters** (Tear-Stained; Wave & Whirlpool) | **Manipulation** | breathing impaired (CO₂ garage, feedlot, underwater); or the target is ≥half submerged | the next time you'd **fully refill WP**, gain only **1** |

| **Pyre Flame** (Burning; Ash & Brand; Lover's) | **Intelligence** | an area or target on fire, or in **Extreme Heat** | the **Extreme Heat** Tilt for **(10 − Synergy) h**, or until you deliberately destroy a valued possession |
| **Stillness** (Silent; Shroud & Shadow) | **Composure** | the target is **unaware of you, helpless, or alone with you** in the scene | the next time you speak **a single word**, the Doom resolves **and the Haunt's Condition ends**. Unlocked without a Haunt: **Mute** until chapter end |

Which Key fits a death is a table decision; one death can fit several [sidebar p. 121–122].

## §3 Ceremonies [p. 124–131]
**Anyone can learn them.** The Pass On and the core Warding/Exorcism actions are effectively Ceremonies that became common knowledge. XP 2/dot (Merit dots at creation). Krewes know their krewe Ceremonies via MCI (04).

**Performing one** [p. 124–125]:
- Assemble symbolic elements (**Actor, Target, Stage, Effect, Ritual**; samples in sidebars).
- **Action: instant, but it takes 15 minutes per dot.** Teamwork allowed.
- Subjects in **another world** (Underworld vs living) are only reachable if stated.
- Pool as listed. Success: the effect. **Exceptional: a Krewe Beat.** Failure: nothing. **Dramatic: Deprived** Condition (resolve by visiting the Underworld or performing a more powerful Ceremony).
- **Modifiers**: rare, expensive or dangerous element **+2**; takes an hour/dot **+1**, a day/dot **+2**, a week/dot **+3**.
- They don't draw on the geist ("they're just part of how the world works") [p. 124].

## §4 Mementos [p. 131–135]
- Inanimate objects **touched by death**: a murder weapon, an Anchor, an object brought back from the Underworld, a geist's remains, a Ghost-Bound Anchor. Bought with the **Memento ••• Merit** or found in play.
- **Liminal**: solid to both material and ephemeral things.
- **Key**: whoever holds, wears or appropriately uses it can **unlock with its Key**. Collecting several Mementos with one Key lets you unlock **several Haunts simultaneously** with that Key.
- **Effect**: one or more minor, strange powers (not numeric bonuses).
- **Identification**: Sin-Eaters and ghosts **identify a Memento and its Key on sight**.
- **Toughness**: never damaged by accidents or environment. **Durability ≥5 and twice normal Structure.** Apparel Mementos count as **armor** (usually a Kevlar vest, or riot gear for full suits); suitable shapes act as **shields**.
- **Feeding one to your geist**: instant action, **destroys it, fully refills Plasm**.
- **Memento Collector** Condition: owning and openly displaying **5+ Mementos with the same Key**, **9 with different Keys**, or **one famous/greater Memento** [p. 133].
- **Creating a Memento ("Vanitas")** [p. 133–134]:
  - Build it from scratch (restoring from the frame is OK; tuning a bought car is not).
  - **≥1 week**, ST sets the time. Optional equipment-building rules (core p. 100).
  - **Costs 1 Synergy.** It carries **one of your innate Keys** plus an effect agreed with the ST.
- **Deathmask** [sidebar p. 131]: the uneaten remains of a destroyed geist. For a Sin-Eater it's a normal Memento. **A ghost who puts it on becomes a Reaper.** It never counts toward Memento Collector, and the Bound find them disgusting.
- **Greater Mementos** (rumoured; huge powers) are worth krewe wars.
- **Examples**: Bone Music Vinyl (Disease), Cold Harbor Diary (Stillness: whoever dies holding it can't be identified), Digger's Mojo Bag (Grave Dirt: points to the nearest open/unmarked grave), Drowned Phone (Deep Waters: calls from anywhere while submerged, even the Underworld), Everest Oxygen (Cold Wind: suffocation and addictive visions), Offal Rosary (Beasts: summon or repel vermin), **Mourning Dove** (Blood Deathmask: the wearer sees Virtue and Vice; worn by a ghost it becomes the **Glass Martyr**, a Rank 4 Reaper: Power +8, Finesse +8, Resistance +4, max 12; Essence 25; Influence Pain ••; Descend, Discorporate, Engulf, Possess; Blast, Emotional Aura; Ban: can't contravene its Virtue; Bane: a libellus), Tricked-Out Hearse (Chance: at full speed drives through any reachable Avernian Gate, no roll), Witch's Finger (Pyre Flame: ignites combustibles after a turn) [p. 134–135].
