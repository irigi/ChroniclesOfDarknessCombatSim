# GtS 2e — Ghosts & antagonists

§A **Ghost rules** (Ch4 pp. 185–198; GtS overrides core for ghosts) · §B Reapers · §C Eaters of the Dead · §D Necromancers · §E Enemy Bound (Tyranny) · §F Kerberoi · §G Dominions (Ch6) · §H the Absent (App. 1) — B–H appended below.

## §A Ghosts [p. 185–198]
**Nature** [p. 185–187]:
- A ghost is an **afterimage of ephemera**, a copy of the dead person, **not a soul** (souls vanish shortly after death).
- Objects, buildings and pets leave ghosts too, as long as someone misses them.
- Ghosts in the living world are in **Twilight**: invisible and intangible to the material world, and vice versa. Material light is dim to them, sound muffled.
- **Ghosts and spirits are "out of phase"**: they can't sense or affect each other except through rare powers [sidebar p. 187].
- If its body is destroyed, a ghost **reforms at its nearest Anchor** while it has Essence.
- **Manifestations work only on Anchors** without preparation; most need prepared Conditions.

**Rank** [p. 187–188]:
| Rank | Name | Trait max | Attribute dots | Max Essence | Numina |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Spectre / Phantasm (non-sapient: objects, animals, phenomena) | 3 | 3–4 | 5 | 1 |
| 1 | Lare (sapient but **looping**) | 5 | 5–8 | 10 | 1–3 |
| 2 | Lemure (fully aware) | 7 | 9–14 | 15 | 3–5 |
| 3 | Lesser Mane (= **geist**) | 9 | 15–25 | 20 | 5–7 |
| 4 | Greater Mane | 12 | 26–35 | 25 | 7–9 |
| 5 | Lesser Kerberos | 15 | 36–45 | 50 | 9–11 |
| 6+ | Chthonians, Kerberoi: plot devices, no stats | — | — | — | — |

- **Rank** gives **−1 per Rank to attempts to forcibly bind** the ghost and **acts as Supernatural Tolerance**.
- **Starting Rank**: died at **Integrity ≤6 → Rank 1**; **7–9 → Rank 2**. Rank 3–5 at creation only via convergence or ritual.
- **Rank rises only by drinking from a River** (or a specific mechanic). It isn't normally lost.
- **Dominance**: a ghost's natural attacks count as the **Bane** of any ghost **2+ Ranks lower**.

**Essence** [p. 188]:
- Max by Rank. **No per-turn spending limit.**
- **Living world**:
  - **1 Essence per day to stay active**; otherwise **hibernation**.
  - **Essence Bleed**: **outside the Anchor Condition, lose 1 Essence per hour**. At 0 → **1 lethal and hibernation**.
  - **Essence Sense**: Anchors and Avernian Gates within **1 mile** (Seek extends this).
- **Underworld**: 1 Essence per day or **Trapped in the Walls**. Obeying a Dominion's Old Laws for a day gives +1.
- **Gains**:
  - +1 per day spent within the **Anchor Condition**.
  - **+1 when remembered *as the living person* by a living person.** **Sin-Eaters don't count** (they're metaphysically dead) [sidebar p. 189].
  - Sin-Eater feeding: 3 Plasm → 1 Essence (05 §4); Dirge Paean.
  - Krewe Creed or Virtue (Essence instead of WP).
- **Spends**:
  - **Attribute boost**: X Essence (max Rank + 2) for +X total to Attributes, instant action, **[interp: for a scene]**.
  - Manifestations, Influence, Numina.
- **Ghost ectophagia**: a bite on another ghost steals Essence up to the damage dealt.

**Attributes, Skills, Merits** [p. 188–189]:
- **Power** (substitutes for Str/Int/Pre), **Finesse** (Dex/Wits/Manip), **Resistance** (Sta/Res/Com). Each ≥1. Dots and caps by Rank.
- They keep Skills important to their identity. **ST shortcut: use Rank as the Skill dots for familiar things.**
- They keep Merits that still apply (Giant stays Size 6; Allies and Contacts usually don't carry over).

**Advantages** [p. 189–190]:
- **Corpus** = Resistance + Size (replaces Health).
- **Willpower** = Resistance + Finesse (max 10).
- **Initiative** = Finesse + Resistance.
- **Defense** = lower of Power or Finesse, **applied even against firearms**.
- **Speed** = Power + Finesse + 5. **Size** as in life.
- **Integrity**: fixed at death; changes don't alter Rank.
- **Ghost breaking points**, the only ones they have:
  - losing an Anchor without resolving it
  - first realising they're dead
  - drinking from a River
  - breaking an Old Law
  - committing ectophagia
- **Virtue**: +1 WP once per scene. **Vice**: all WP once per chapter (**reversed from humans**).

**Anchors** [p. 189–190]:
- People, places or things of great significance. Usually ≥1 (body or grave), rarely more than 3. **Ghosts and ghost objects can't be Anchors.**
- Every Anchor carries the **Anchor Condition** keyed to the ghost.
- A destroyed or dead Anchor loses the Condition.
- **Losing all Anchors** → the ghost is immediately and irresistibly blown through the nearest Avernian Gate.
- **Resolving an Anchor** (willing to let it go) removes its Condition; the ghost always knows.
- **Resolving all Anchors**: no more Essence Bleed and no dragging below. The ghost can be helped onward with **Pass On**.

**Bans** (Rank 1+; **can't be resisted**) [p. 190]:
- Rank 1: mild (a nun must use an offered rosary).
- Rank 2–3: moderate (must dematerialise on hearing a cat).
- Rank 4–5: complex and catastrophic (banished if a rhyme is sung).

**Banes** [p. 190]:
- By Rank: Rank 0 ubiquitous (sunlight), Rank 1 common (salt, fire), Rank 2–3 hard but natural (holy water, silver), Rank 4–5 very specific.
- **Aversion**: to voluntarily touch its Bane, a ghost spends **1 WP** and rolls **Resolve + Composure − Rank**.
- The Bane is **solid** to the ghost even in Twilight.
- **Contact** in Twilight or the Underworld: 1 lethal per turn. **Materialized**: 1 **aggravated** per turn, plus a reflexive **Rank** roll each turn or Materialized ends. **Fettered**: 1 lethal per turn, and it must use Unfetter to escape.
- **Bane used as a weapon**: aggravated against Materialized ghosts, lethal against Twilight or Underworld ones.

**Influence** [p. 190–191]:
- Dots = Rank (max Rank). Usually includes Influence (Anchors). Multiple specific spheres.
- When an Anchor is lost or resolved, its dots can be reassigned. Rank 2+ ghosts can trade Numina for Influence 1:1.
- **Use**: spend Essence and roll **Power + Finesse** (vs the higher of **Resolve + Synergy or Composure + Synergy** for sapient targets; that is, **Supernatural Tolerance**, written as Synergy).
- **Effect level + duration level must be lower than the Influence dots** ("must exceed the total").
- Effects:
  - **• Strengthen** (1 Ess): add **Rank to a loved one's Defense**, make an emotion strong enough to create a Condition, add **Rank to an Anchor's Health or Structure**. **Can shift Anchor → Open** for the duration.
  - **•• Manipulate** (2): minor changes.
  - **••• Control** (3): dictate. **Shifts Open → Controlled.**
  - **•••• Create** (4): new instance, including a **temporary Anchor Condition**.
  - **••••• Mass Create** (5): Rank instances, **or one permanent** (including a permanent Anchor; no permanent mind alteration).
- Durations: **0** 1 min/success; **•** 10 min/success; **••** 1 h/success (+1 Essence); **•••** 1 day/success (+2); **••••** permanent (+2).

**Manifestation** [p. 191–192]:
- Start with **Twilight Form + Rank** Manifestation Effects. Rank 2+ can trade Numina for Manifestations 1:1.
- **Prerequisite Conditions** must be keyed to the ghost or universal (a Sin-Eater's aura and Plasm-coated objects count).
- **Living world only** unless stated.
- **Roll Power + Resistance** and pay Essence.
- **When an effect ends it leaves Plasm = its Essence cost** (until chapter end).
| Effect | Requires | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Twilight Form | — | 0 | In Twilight in the living world. Leaves Size Plasm in the Underworld when it ends |
| Avernian Gateway | Open | 3 | Touch a Gate: open it, giving the **Underworld Gate** Condition. Works in the Underworld at Rank 3+ |
| **Bargain** | Rank 3+ | 10 | Touching a **dying** mortal, offer the Bargain. If accepted: a Bound, and the ghost gains **Bound Geist**. Works in the Underworld |
| Claim | Controlled + Fetter + Possess | 5 | Permanent control → **Claimed**. Living targets contest with **Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance**. Works in the Underworld. **Sin-Eaters are immune** (Possession Immunity) |
| Discorporate | — | 0 | Voluntarily discorporate as if Corpus were filled with lethal |
| Fetter | Open | 2 | Gain **Fettered** to an Anchor. Living targets contest (Res + Com + ST) and become **Urged** on success |
| Image | Anchor | 1 | Visible to the material world for a scene |

**Numina** (activation **Power + Finesse** unless stated; "ST" = Supernatural Tolerance, i.e. Synergy for the Bound) [p. 192–195]:
- **Anchor Jump** (3): teleport to another of its Anchors. Contested by Str + Athletics only if grappled.
- **Awe** (3): everyone looking contests with **Presence + Composure + ST**. Those with fewer successes can't move or speak for 1 turn (3 turns on an exceptional).
- **Blast**: range **10 yd per Power**, no range penalties, **+0L**. Every 2 Essence adds +1L, up to +Rank.
- **Descend** (Rank 3, **3+ ghosts** with the Numen, teamwork): tear an **Open** location into the Underworld, **10 Essence per city block**.
- **Drain**: vs **Stamina + Resolve + ST**. Whoever has more successes takes that much **Willpower** from the other.
- **Emotional Aura** (1): a scene, or until another Numen. Anyone within 5 yd rolls **Resolve + Composure + ST**; if the ghost wins, **−2 to all actions**; if they win, they're immune.
- **Empower Ghost** (Rank 5, 3): a Rank ≤3 ghost gets +1 Rank for a scene.
- **Engulf** (Rank 3+, 3): after **winning a grapple** against a ghost, or filling its last Corpus box with lethal → **Engulfed** (dormant inside it). Max prisoners = Rank.
- **Firestarter** (1): one small fire per success within Power yards.
- **Hallucination** (1): vs **Wits + Composure + ST**; each net success alters one sense.
- **Host Jump** (3): move Possessed or Claimed between touching hosts (prerequisites needed; Claim restarts).
- **Implant Mission** (2): a vision plus **Obsessed** with the mission.
- **Innocuous** (free, no roll): −2 to notice it.
- **Left-Handed Spanner** (1): a device with ≥3 moving parts malfunctions for successes turns. In combat it requires grappling the object.
- **Moliate** (1; 2 to affect other willing or restrained ghosts): reshape itself.
- **Omen Trance** (1 for self, 3 for another): once per 24 h, an extended action of ≥1 scene; a vision of a danger within a week.
- **Pathfinder** (1): fastest route. A Safe Place is contested by the owners' lowest Resolve + ST. Not in the Underworld.
- **Proxy** (3) / **Puppeteer** (3): lend its power to another ghost, or force another ghost to use one of its own. It must be able to Bane the target (2 Ranks higher; 1 for a Kerberos). Unwilling targets contest with Rank + Resistance.
- **Rapture** (2): **Insensate**. If the victim fails **Resolve + ST**, a binding Condition for Power days.
- **Regenerate** (1 per turn, no roll): heal one point (bashing first, then lethal).
- **Reap Memory** (4): touch the head, name the memory, vs **Resolve + Composure + ST**. Success: the ghost takes the **Memory** (App. 1), the target forgets it and gains **Spooked** (exceptional: no Condition, or Addled).
- **Seek**: Finesse roll; range 2 miles per Rank (1 Essence ×10). Direction and distance to the nearest suitable **Anchor, Infrastructure or Resonant** Condition.
- **Sign** (1): one message in any medium.
- **Speed** (2 doubles / 4 triples Speed for the scene).
- **Stalwart** (no cost): Defense = Resistance.
- **Telekinesis** (1): successes act as Strength for lifting and throwing; no fine control.
- **Kerberos-only**: Dominion Sense, Enforcement (§F). **Reapers**: The Moth, The Scribe (§B).

**Ghost combat pools** (not restated in GtS; core rule): attack and grapple = **Power + Finesse** (or the shortcut **Attribute + Rank**). Defense as above. **[interp]** Ghost Touch is mutual: a Twilight ghost can strike, grab or bite a Sin-Eater bodily, just as she can it. Ordinary mortals can only be touched via Manifestations.
**Unlisted costs**: **Blast** has no base cost (only +2 Essence per +1L). Where a Numen gives no cost, assume 0 **[interp]**.

**Damage** [p. 195]:
- Corpus works like Health. **Ghosts take bashing from all attacks** unless the attack uses their **Bane** or a power says otherwise. **A Sin-Eater's ordinary punch or gun does bashing to a ghost.** The Rage, Caul lethal claws, castoff weapons and Bane weapons do more.
- Aggravated damage also **drains Essence equal to the damage**. **No unconsciousness.**
- **Reforming**: a ghost with ≥1 Essence whose last Corpus box fills with lethal or aggravated **dissolves into Size Plasm** and reforms in **hibernation** at its nearest Anchor (in the Underworld: on the shore of the nearest River). It wakes when it regains **Essence = Corpus**, fully healed. A hibernating ghost is insensate.
- **Destruction**: a Rank 1–2 ghost **with no Essence** whose last box fills → destroyed (leaves Size Plasm). **Rank 3+ leaves a Deathmask** Memento instead.
- **Ectophagia** by a Sin-Eater (a helpless ghost, 1 agg per Plasm) **destroys it permanently** (05 §5).

**Ghost types** [p. 196–198]:
- **Doppelgängers**: ghosts of people who survived (trauma, near death, lost limbs). Phantom limbs are Rank 0. Doppelgängers of the same person are each other's **Bane**. One that consumes another gains +1 Rank.
- **Barghests** (animal ghosts):
  - Rank 2 if the death cost a human Integrity, 1 if it caused a breaking point without loss, otherwise 0.
  - Species Speed factor; **Defense = higher of Finesse or Resistance**; natural weapons deal **lethal to ghosts**; count as animals for powers.
  - **Ectophagia on them is no breaking point or crisis point.**
- **Castoffs** (object ghosts):
  - Rank by the same rule. They keep object traits (Durability, equipment bonus, vehicle traits). **Castoff weapons do lethal to ghosts.**
  - Mindless: they act only via Influence, Numina or Manifestation in prescribed triggers. **Users can operate their powers** with their own Attributes, paying their own or the castoff's Essence.
  - Ectophagia on them is free of breaking points.
- **Geists** (and Reapers) [p. 197]:
  - **No Anchors**, no Essence Bleed, never dragged under.
  - **Influence replaced by the innate Key at dots = Rank.**
  - **Iconic**: even those who knew them need a reflexive **Resolve + Composure** to recognise them.
  - **Broken**: **any Social roll by or against them, except to intimidate or frighten, is a chance die.**
  - Every geist has a **Remembrance**.
- **Kerberoi** [p. 197–198]:
  - **Influence (Dominion) •••••**; Dominion Sense and Enforcement; **+Rank Essence per scene** in their Dominion.
  - **Universal Bane**: they're the Bane of **all** lower-Rank ghosts.
  - They bleed Essence away from their Dominion. **Their Ban is their Dominion's Old Laws.**
  - **Violation Sense**: they know when a ghost breaks a Law and track the offender until it leaves the Underworld.
- **Chthonians** (Underworld natives) [p. 198]:
  - No Virtue, Vice or Aspiration; 1 WP per 5 Essence gained.
  - Influence and Numina but **no Manifestations**. **Rank Essence per day** in the Underworld (or per hour in a River or the Ocean). **Immune to Rivers and Ocean.**
  - They're the Bane of ghosts 2 Ranks lower, but not the reverse.
  - Ghosts that eat them take 1 lethal per Essence and gain nothing.
  - They leave a **Size Plasm trail** each scene.
  - **In the living world** they're Materialized but take **1 aggravated per turn outside an Underworld Gate area**.
  - They auto-open any living-world Gate they touch (none from below).
  - **Ectophagia by touch attack** against ghosts.

**Ghost advancement** [p. 198]:

## §B Reapers [p. 215–221]
- **Any ghost becomes a Reaper by donning a Deathmask** (the remains of a destroyed geist; 03 §4). They serve the **Chthonic Gods**: they believe the dead belong below and present themselves as teachers and pillars of riverside towns.
- Reapers feel **an innate drive to reap**; shirking it makes their skin crack.
- **Method**: they hunt alone. **Engulf** the ghost, **destroy its Anchors** (or leave them as bait), then vomit the haul up in the Underworld. For hard cases they **Descend** the area (whole houses or blocks sink; the living caught there die and the area sickens). **Posses** form for big jobs: one Reaper per Anchor, Descending simultaneously.
- **Mechanics** [p. 217]:
  - **Without the mask: a Rank 2 ghost** with no special powers. Indistinguishable from other ghosts.
  - **Mask on**: **Rank 3, 4 or 5** (Attributes, traits and max Essence rise accordingly). Gains the Aspiration "drag ghosts back to the Underworld". Gains **Engulf and Descend** plus Rank-appropriate Numina, Influence and Manifestations. New Ban and Bane at Rank 4–5. **No Essence Bleed.**
  - **Donning or removing the mask is instant.** **It can be forcibly removed only in the presence of the Deathmask's Bane.**
  - Gates always open for them. **Irkalla's Gates let them pass free.**
- **Stat blocks** ("unmasked / masked"):

## §C Eaters of the Dead (ghost eaters) [p. 221–225]
- **Living** humans who use a personal **Cuisine** (an occult or pseudo-scientific technique taught mentor to protégé) to commit **ectophagia** and render ghosts into Plasm. Most Cuisines need ghosts **trapped** in objects or food first. They're heavy on **Occult** (exorcism, abjuration and binding while feeding), plus Investigation and Stealth.
- **All Cuisines halt and reverse ageing.**
- **Rapacity** (new Advantage, 1–5; **each dot acts as Supernatural Tolerance**):
  | Rapacity | Plasm stolen (lifetime) | Max Plasm / per turn | Dread Powers |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 10 | 10/1 | 4 |
  | 2 | 70 | 12/2 | 8 |
  | 3 | 400 | 14/3 | 12 |
  | 4 | 1,000 | 17/4 | 16 |
  | 5 | 10,000+ | 20/5 | 20 |
  - **Immortality upkeep: Rapacity Plasm per week.** Missing it means rapid ageing to their true age. If they survive, **Rapacity × 2** Plasm reverses it.
- **Famous ghosts**: **+1 Essence per week per dot of Fame**, losing 1 Fame per decade dead. An eater who consumes one gains that Essence share in Plasm weekly [sidebar p. 223].
- **Dread Powers** [p. 223–224]:
  - **Anchor Eater**: identifies an eaten ghost's Anchors on sight and eats them for 1 Plasm per Size.
  - **Death Inurement** (Rapacity 3): eat a ghost that died unnaturally to become **inured to that cause of death** (guns, drowning…). **Rapacity Plasm per week**; one at a time.
  - **Abmortality** (Death Inurement + Rapacity 5): gains a Cuisine-related **Bane**. **Killed by anything else, they return a later scene, whole.**
  - **Numen**: masters one ghost Numen, paying Essence costs in Plasm, with a fixed Attribute + Ability pool.
  - **Regeneration •–•••**: reflexive once per turn, 1 Plasm per dot (within the per-turn limit). Each Plasm heals **1 lethal or 2 bashing**.
  - **Know Vice**: 1 Plasm reveals a target's Vice or equivalent.
  - **Learn Memories**: takes an eaten ghost's **Memories** (one set at a time) to supplement a Skill roll, then they fade. Risk of Memory Bleed. Ectophagia yields 2 less Plasm while using it.
  - **Supernatural Merit**: each Merit dot counts as a Dread Power. WP costs are paid in Plasm.
  - **The Dead Identity** (needs Learn Memories): with a Memory set, adopt the deceased's mannerisms and identity. Ectophagia yields 3 less Plasm.
- **Sample: Mr. Wong, Corpse-Untie-Immortal** (an 1,800-year-old Taoist *shijie xian* in Manhattan's Chinatown):
  - Int 5, Wits 3, Res 5; Str 2, Dex 2, Sta 5; Pre 2, Man 4, Com 5.
  - Academics 5, Investigation 5, Medicine 5, Occult 5, Politics 3, Science 1, Athletics 2, Larceny 2, Stealth 3, Weaponry 4, Empathy 4, Expression 1, Persuasion 1, Socialize 1.
  - Merits: Allies 2, Fleet of Foot 2, Hoarder 5, Influence 3, Languages, Sympathetic.
  - Health 7. **Rapacity 5**, Plasm 20/5.
  - Dread Powers: Abmortality, Death Inurement, The Dead Identity, Anchor Eater, Learn Memories, plus Numina.
  - **Bane**: eating the ghost of a paternal grandfather with no living descendants. He must eat a paternal grandfather every 37 years. Obsession (Immortality). Not a fighter: he flees.
- Eaters are mortal and can't face a krewe in open combat; they bolt [sidebar p. 225].

## §D Necromancers [p. 225–229]
- Living users of **Ceremonies, Mementos** and stranger magic who exploit the dead. **Killing them rarely helps**: they come back as powerful ghosts, Reapers or Bound.
- Other supernaturals (mages, elder vampires, changelings) who affect ghosts get lumped in as "necromancers" [sidebar p. 226].
- **Mortal Obsession**: a failed breaking point caused by a supernatural artifact (a Memento) can give **Obsession** instead of the usual result; a dramatic failure gives a persistent version [sidebar p. 226].
- **Graverobbers**: Memento collectors. **Dr. Jordan Thames** (pharma CEO):
  - Int 3, Wits 2, Res 3; Str 2, Dex 2, Sta 2; Pre 2, Man 3, Com 3.
  - Academics 2, Computer 1, Investigation 3 (Finding Mementos), Medicine 1, Politics 2, Drive 1, Firearms 1, Larceny 2, Empathy 1, Intimidation 3, Persuasion 2, Socialize 1, Subterfuge 2.
  - Merits: Library (Occult) 1, Resources 4, Staff 3, Untouchable.
  - WP 6, Health 7, Speed 9, Init 5, Def 2, Integrity 6. Revolver 3 dice, 1 damage.
  - About 6 Mementos; the **Ornate Reliquary** (Disease) halts disease and decay nearby.
- **Ritualists**: anyone can learn Ceremonies. **Erica Greenwood** (an 18-year-old goth):
  - Int 2, Wits 3, Res 3; Str 2, Dex 3, Sta 2; Pre 1, Man 3, Com 2.
  - Academics 1, Computer 2, Crafts 1, Investigation 1, Occult 1, Athletics 2, Brawl 2, Larceny 2, Stealth 2, Animal Ken 1, Empathy 1, Expression 1, Persuasion 2, Socialize 1, Streetwise 2, Subterfuge 3.
  - Merits: Eye for the Strange, Fleet of Foot 2, Resources 1, Safe Place 2, Ceremony.
  - WP 5, Health 7, Speed 12, Init 5, Def 5, Integrity 7. Fists 4 dice.
  - **Ceremony: Shattered Bone Binding (•••)**: Manipulation + Subterfuge vs **Resistance + Rank**. Draws the nearest ghost within 30 yd into a circle of agony: **all its actions are reduced to a chance die** (no Corpus damage), and its pain spills out as cold and breaking glass. Lasts until the bowl's water is gone.
- **Necromantic cults**: Mystery Cult Initiation organisations. **Church of Death's Shards**: • Occult (Ghosts) Specialty; •• Contacts or Resources •; ••• **recognise Mementos on sight**; •••• a personal Memento; ••••• a Ghost Trap variant that lets **physical weapons harm trapped ghosts**.

## §E Enemy Bound [p. 229–231]
- **Tyranny** [sidebar p. 230]: Bound who ritually destroy their geist's Remembrance (Touchstone) and break its will. **Tyranny works mechanically like Synergy**, except that **crisis-point intercession is always Tyranny + Intimidation**. Tyrants are ST characters (05 §3).
- **Antagonist archetypes** (sample Ceremonies):
  - **Elysians**: immortality is their right; usually Tyranny. Ishtar's Perfume •, Ghost Trap ••, Black Cat's Crossing •••.
  - **Bonepickers**: exploit the dead for profit. Dead Man's Camera •, Ghost Trap ••, Bloody Codex •••.
  - **Thanatologists**: the dead as data. Diviner's Jawbone •, Skeleton Key ••, Bloody Codex •••.

## §F Kerberoi [p. 232–233] (traits in §A)
- Bound to their Dominion. Their power scales with the number and complexity of its Laws. They ignore everything that isn't a violation. **Unreasoning and unbargainable.**
- **They punish rather than kill**: an itch-like compulsory **task or limitation** (the Lawbreaker Condition). They pursue escapees beyond their borders while their strength lasts (and send agents topside), but **can't leave the Underworld**.
- **Dominion Sense**: perfect knowledge of its Laws and its Dominion. It knows **instantly which Law was broken, by whom, and where**. Once the offender has left it knows distance and direction. Masking yourself → **Clash of Wills**.
- **Enforcement**: 1 Essence, **Power + Finesse vs Resolve + Synergy** (Supernatural Tolerance) → **Lawbreaker** Condition.
- Samples: **the Moth** (Cavern of Flame; 12-legged and double-winged; playful; punishments feed its Dominion, e.g. light every candle for a month) and **the Scribe** (the Vault; a pen-legged spider of eyes; punishment is adding to its collection).

## §H The Absent: ghost PCs (App. 1 pp. 280–291)
The Absent follow the §A ghost rules unless overridden. They're ghosts who "hustle" to survive, trading Memories and favours.

**Creation** [p. 290–291]:
- 3 Aspirations (2 short, 1 long). Virtue and Vice (Vice: all WP once per chapter; Virtue: all WP once per scene as printed, which **contradicts** the p. 189 rule of Virtue +1 WP; see 99).
- **Attributes**: **9 dots**, each Attribute **starts at 0**, max 7.
- **Skills**: none except **12 Memory Skill dots**. **No Specialties.**
- **Merits**: **10 dots** (no biological or supernatural Merits).
- **Rank 2. Integrity 7.**
- **Willpower** = Finesse + Resistance (max 10). Corpus = Resistance + Size. Defense = lower of Power or Finesse. Speed = Power + Finesse + 5.
- **Ban and Bane** of a Rank 2 ghost (personal and tricky: a live cat's meow; water from the lake they drowned in).
- **Influence 2 dots** (≥1 tied to an Anchor). **Anchors = Resistance** (usually including the remains; take a portable one).
- **3 Numina** and **Twilight Form + 2 Manifestations**. Numina, Manifestations and Influence dots can be swapped 1:1 at creation. Materialize is rare at Rank 1–2 but allowed for PCs.
- **4 Memories** (★ marks the ghost's own). Each has **2 Skills**.
- A **nickname** (real names can trigger breaking points).

**Advancement**: as ghost advancement (§A). XP-bought Skills are the ghost's own, not Memory Skills. Reaching Rank 3 by drinking from a River is a chronicle-ending arc [sidebar p. 290].

**Memories** [p. 285–288]:
- A short phrase for a significant moment (yours or someone else's). While held, it's **experienced as your own**.
- Potent sources: breaking points, fulfilled or failed Aspirations, resolving or gaining a Condition, dramatic Virtue or Vice.
- **2 associated Skills** each; some carry **Persistent Conditions**.
- **12 Memory Skill dots**, redistributed between chapters or when Memories change. **Max 3 Memory dots per Skill**, and never above the Rank trait max.
- **Buffers**: **sacrifice a Memory instead of** paying the daily Essence or suffering Essence Bleed, **or instead of a breaking point**. A sacrificed Memory is gone forever.
- **Breaking anyway**: a ghost's breaking-point roll is **Resistance + Rank**, modified by Integrity as for the living.
- **Acquiring Memories**:
  - **Trading**: costs 1 WP to give (several at once). Both parties must consent; they **can't be taken by force** between ghosts. Skills and Conditions transfer with the Memory.
  - **Scavenging**: from places that store Memories (an old oak, theme parks, museums); slow.
  - **Harvesting**: from the living with the **Reap Memory** Numen.
  - **Making**: requires a living person **aware** of the ghost and direct interaction. Each later Memory with the same person must escalate.
- **Memory Bleed**: holding more **borrowed** Memories than **Integrity** (own Memories don't count) → **Memory Bleed** Condition.

**Merits (ghost only)** [p. 288–290]:
- **Brain Eater •**: handle corpses as if in Twilight; eat organs for Memories (usually of the death), 1 per organ, **organs = the corpse's former Stamina**.
- **Dead Meat •**: you still inhabit your body.
  - **Health instead of Corpus.** Heal with Essence plus meat: ~10 lb of raw non-human or 1 lb of human meat per lethal or aggravated level.
  - Social interaction with the living costs 1 WP (people ignore you unless you're aggressive).
  - Each week without 10 lb of meat (or 1 lb human): −1 Finesse or Resistance. **At 0 → a frenzy** to eat an adult's weight.
  - **No Manifestations.** The body is an Anchor.
- **Deep Memory •**: max Memories = **Integrity + Resistance**.
- **Wake •–•••••**: mourners visit. **Once per chapter, gain Essence = dots.**
- **Iconography Merits** (max one per ghost; they show in Manifestations):
  - **Ajna •**: +3 perception, double sensory-overload penalties.
  - **Banda •**: +3 to command attention and get away with it, −2 Stealth.
  - **Crowned •**: anyone, living or dead, facing a breaking or crisis point near it gets +1, and the ghost loses 1 Essence.
  - **Immaculate Heart •**: −2 to hide feelings, +3 to inspire trust.
  - **Pierced •**: permanent −1 wound penalty, but ignores the first damage each scene.
  - **Shackled •**: once per chapter +2 Essence for a day near an Anchor; Speed halved.
  - **Veined •**: max Essence as one Rank higher; the first damage each scene is +1.
  - **Waters •**: 1 Essence gives a body of water (bathtub up to 6×6×6 m) the **Anchor** Condition for the scene; within such water it can't be forced out.
- Sample Absent concepts (with suggested traits): Family Man, the Vindictive, the Erased, the Banshee, the Grinder, Your Dead Boyfriend, the Priest, the Professor, the Open Wound, Zombie Baby, the Never Born.
