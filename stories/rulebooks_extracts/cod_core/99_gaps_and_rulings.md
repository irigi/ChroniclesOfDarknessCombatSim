# CoD core — gaps, contradictions, interpretations

## Book contradictions (pick the marked reading)
1. **Range penalties**: text p. 90 and weapon chart p. 268 say medium −1 / long −2; combat summary chart p. 91
   says −2 / −4. **Use −1/−2** (two sources agree).
2. **Autofire multiple targets**: text p. 90 "subtract the total number of targets"; chart p. 91 "−1 per roll for
   each target after the first". **Use text (−N)** [interp]; either is defensible — state which you use.
3. **Ephemeral Defense**: p. 127 "lower of Power or Finesse"; p. 129 "lower of Finesse or Resistance";
   Stalwart Numen p. 138 implies normal = lower of Power/Finesse. **Use lower(Power, Finesse)**; Rank 1 uses higher.
4. **Meditation pool**: p. 30 lists Resolve + Composure (extended); system p. 75 says **Composure + Wits**, 4
   successes, 30-min interval. Use p. 75.
5. **Integrity modifier table** p. 74 prints "3–2 −1" and "1–2 −2": read Integrity 2–3 = −1, Integrity 1 = −2.
6. **Horror Speed**: text p. 143 = Str + Dex + species factor; quick-ref box says "+ Size". Use species factor.
7. **Open Condition shortcut**: Influence table says *Strengthen* can shift Anchor/Resonant/Infrastructure to
   Open; Open's own entry says a *Control* Influence creates Open instantly. Either works; cost differs (1 vs 3 Ess).
8. **Deafened/Drugged duration** "10 − the victim's Stamina + Resolve": read as 10 − (Stamina + Resolve).

## Not in the core book (look elsewhere / house-rule)
- **No 5th-dot cap at creation** stated in core (later CoD books: max one 5 in Attributes/Skills). Flag if applied.
- **Destiny Merit** referenced (pp. 133, 137) but not defined — from *The God-Machine Chronicle* (2013).
- **Willpower dots can't be bought**; core has no "raise Willpower" cost.
- **Tie in contested rolls** not addressed in core [interp: no change/status quo, or re-roll for pure contests].
- No rules for **suffocation/drowning damage** beyond Hardy and "hold breath Stamina + Composure" (Flooded Tilt).
  [interp: use VtR/other splat or rule 1 bashing/turn after Stamina×? — flag as house rule].
- No **hunter/vampire/werewolf** stats — use the splat books. Supernatural Tolerance for mortals = none.
- Grapple **overpowering** vocabulary in Merits (Police Tactics, Double Jointed, Joint Lock) refers to the
  grapple "moves" (Hold, Restrain, Damage…) won by the contested Str + Brawl roll — there is no separate
  "overpower" roll in core 2e.

## Common misreadings to avoid
- **Defense does not apply to firearms** (except: Firefight •• during Covering Fire; guns larger than Size 1
  used in close combat → target's Defense + (gun Size + 1) applies). Ephemeral beings DO apply Defense vs guns.
- **Willpower: max 1 point per action**; +3 dice OR +2 to a resistance trait/+2 Defense vs one attacker.
- **Dodge** doubles Defense and is *rolled*; plain Defense is *subtracted*. Multiple attackers reduce Defense by 1
  per attacker **before** doubling.
- **Grapple** is two-stage: grab (Str + Brawl − Defense), then each turn a **contested Str + Brawl** — winner
  picks a move (2 on exceptional). Restrain requires a prior Hold. Damage move = bashing equal to successes.
- **Killing blow** requires a defenseless target and is automatic damage (= pool + weapon), plus breaking point.
- **Armor**: lethal attack vs armor always leaves ≥1 bashing; ballistic converts, general subtracts.
- **Wound penalties** are on the 3 rightmost boxes (−1/−2/−3), not "per wound".
- Extended action max rolls = Attribute + Skill (+Specialty), not unlimited.
- **Beaten Down** is an *optional* rule; surrender gives +1 WP and a Beat.
- XP costs are **flat** (Attribute 4, Skill 2, Merit 1/dot, Specialty 1, Integrity 2).
- Virtue regain = **all** WP (max twice/session, needs risk); Vice = 1 WP (no risk needed).
- Manifested ephemeral beings take **bashing from lethal weapons** unless bane.
