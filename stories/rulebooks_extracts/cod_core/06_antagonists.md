## B. Ephemeral beings (ghosts, spirits, angels) [pp. 122–140]
### Nature
- Made of **ephemera**; exist in **Twilight** (invisible, intangible; only same-type ephemera can touch them;
  walk at walking pace, through walls, no gravity; ghost-structures of destroyed things are solid to ghosts)
  [p. 124–125].
- **Ghosts**: echoes of the dead, held by **Anchors**; feed on Essence from memory; ghosts losing all Anchors
  fall to the **Underworld** (Great Below). Rank 1 (non-sapient recordings) or 2 (retain memories) when
  formed; can't raise Rank outside Underworld; summoned ones any Rank [p. 122–126].
- **Spirits**: reflections of everything (not humans) in the **Shadow**, behind the **Gauntlet**; grow by
  eating Essence and other spirits; those in our world are refugees needing **Resonant** sources [p. 123].
- **Angels**: tools of the **God-Machine**, need **Infrastructure**; single-minded missions [p. 124].
- Manifestation needs prepared conditions; possession victims are first **Urged**, then **Possessed**, then
  **Claimed** (mutated) [p. 122].

### Traits [pp. 125–128]
| Rank | Trait max | Attribute dots | Max Essence | Numina |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 | 5–8 | 10 | 1–3 |
| 2 | 7 | 9–14 | 15 | 3–5 |
| 3 | 9 | 15–25 | 20 | 5–7 |
| 4 | 12 | 26–35 | 25 | 7–9 |
| 5 | 15 | 36–45 | 50 | 9–11 |
Rank 6–10 = plot devices only. **Rank = Supernatural Tolerance**; each Rank = −1 to forcibly bind it.
Entities sense each other's Rank; hide it with contested Finesse.
- Attributes: **Power** (Str/Int/Pre), **Finesse** (Dex/Wits/Man), **Resistance** (Sta/Res/Com). Ghosts use
  their living average per category. No Skills; no unskilled penalty for actions fitting their nature:
  **Attribute + Rank**; perception/surprise: Attribute + Attribute.
- **Corpus** = Resistance + Size (acts as Health, **no wound penalties**).
- **Willpower** = Resistance + Finesse (max 10). Regain 1/day + type-specific.
- **Initiative** = Finesse + Resistance. **Speed** = Power + Finesse + species factor (objects 0; none given for ghosts —
  Lucas Kight's 15 = 7+3+**5**, so use +5 for human ghosts [interp]).
- **Defense** = lower of Power or Finesse [p. 127] (combat text p. 129 says "lower of Finesse or
  Resistance" — book contradiction; [interp] use lower of Power/Finesse, Stalwart Numen → Resistance);
  **Rank 1 uses the higher**. **Defense applies vs all attacks incl. firearms.**
- Size: ghosts usually 5; spirits often Size = Rank; angels human-size or up to 10+.
- Languages: Rank 1 ghosts mute; Rank 2+ ghosts their living languages; spirits speak First Tongue (Sumerian-
  like) + learn local; angels understood by all.
- Anchors: ghosts keep Virtue/Vice **reversed** — Vice regains ALL WP (once per chapter); Virtue up to 1 WP/scene.
  Ghost Fate "Forgotten" (optional rule). Ghosts have fixed Integrity (no breaking points). Spirits: no
  Integrity/Virtue/Vice; regain 1 WP per 3 Essence gorged/stolen. Angels: Virtue/Vice as operating rules
  (Silent, Obedient, Punctual…), no Integrity, no Fate.

### Essence [pp. 126–127]
- Spend **1/day** to stay active (0 → hibernation; spirits pushed to Shadow, anchorless ghosts to Underworld,
  angels stay dormant). Destroyed if loses all Corpus **and** Essence.
- **Outside a suitable Condition: bleed 1 Essence/hour**; running out by bleed = 1 lethal + hibernation.
- Boost a trait 1 Essence per dot for a scene; max +(Rank + 2) per trait; one trait per turn, takes a turn.
- Sense Essence sources within 1 mile (Seek extends).
- Regain: 1/day near its Condition; steal from same type (Power + Finesse vs Power + Resistance, take
  successes); ghosts +1 whenever someone remembers them; spirits once/day **gorge** Power + Finesse (−Gauntlet
  if in Shadow); angels from sacrifices (Resources value of item or human's Integrity).
- **Gauntlet strength** (penalty to cross, Reach, feed across): dense urban −3 · suburbs/towns −2 · villages −1 ·
  wilderness 0 · **Locus +2** (no Reaching needed; matching spirits heal 2×) [p. 126].

### Bans & banes [pp. 128–129]
- **Ban**: compulsion. Rank 1 mild/distracting; 2–3 curtailing; 4–5 complex, catastrophic.
- **Bane**: substance it can't abide. Rank 1 common (ghosts & salt); 2–3 hard but natural (holy water, pine
  stake); 4–5 highly specific.
  - Voluntary contact: 1 WP + Power + Resistance − Rank.
  - Banes are solid to entities even in Twilight (not across Gauntlet).
  - Touching bane: **Materialized: 1 aggravated/turn** and its Condition ends unless it succeeds a **Rank-dice
    roll** each turn; **Twilight: 1 lethal/turn**.
  - Fetter touched by bane: 1 lethal/turn to entity until it Unfetters.
  - Bane as weapon: **aggravated vs Manifested, lethal vs Twilight**.
  - Entities **2+ Ranks above** a same-type foe count as its bane with natural attacks.
- **Honorary Rank** (sidebar): e.g., Sin-Eaters vs ghosts, werewolves via Renown — counts only *against*
  the entity.

### Combat [p. 129]
- Attack **Power + Finesse** (weapon: + weapon damage). Damage **bashing** unless nature implies lethal.
- Twilight entities only fight same type (unless bane used).
- **Physical attacks on a Manifested entity: lethal → bashing** unless bane (no organs).
- Lose 1 Essence per aggravated wound. Heal like material beings (natural rates) [interp; Regenerate faster].
- **0 Corpus from lethal/agg → discorporates** (bursts); if any Essence left, reforms hibernating at a
  Conditioned place, wakes after regaining Essence = Corpus dots (1/day). Truly destroyed only if Essence 0 too.

### Influence [p. 130]
- Influence dots = Rank (max Rank per Influence; can split; trade 1 Numen per extra dot). Use: effect level
  + duration level ≤ Influence rating; pay Essence; roll **Power + Finesse**; vs a sentient mind contested by
  **higher of Resolve/Composure + Supernatural Tolerance**.
- Effects: 1 **Strengthen** (+Rank Health/Structure/Defense to a loved one; can make Anchor/Resonant/
  Infrastructure → Open for duration; 1 Ess) · 2 **Manipulate** (minor change; 2 Ess) · 3 **Control** (dramatic
  change; Open → Controlled for duration; 3 Ess) · 4 **Create** (new instance; temporary Anchor/Infrastructure/
  Resonant; 4 Ess) · 5 **Mass Create** (Rank instances, or one permanent incl. base Condition; never permanent
  mind alteration; 5 Ess).
- Durations: 0 = 1 min/success · 1 = 10 min/success · 2 = 1 hr/success (+1 Ess) · 3 = 1 day/success (+2) ·
  4 = permanent (+2).

### Manifestation [p. 131]
Starts with **Twilight Form + a number of Effects equal to Rank** (Rank 2 = Twilight Form + 2 others); trade
Numina 1:1 for more [p. 131]. Numina count comes from the Rank table (Rank 2: 3–5) [p. 125]; **book samples
deviate** (Lucas Kight R2: 1 Numen, 2 Effects; angels vary) — ST discretion. All need a **Power + Finesse** roll;
"living subjects contest" = a **contested roll** (Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance), not a subtraction.
No exceptional-success effects are defined for Manifestation rolls [interp: e.g. longer duration].

### Influence/Manifestation Conditions [pp. 132–136]
Fettered/Possessed block Numina & Influence only — Manifestation Effects (e.g. Image) stay usable [interp].
A suppressed Anchor = "outside a suitable Condition" → Essence bleed 1/hour [interp].
Ladder: **Anchor / Resonant / Infrastructure** (natural) → **Open** → **Controlled**; Manifestations:
Fettered, Possessed, Materialized, Claimed; also Reaching, Shadow Gate, Underworld Gate, Urged. Removing a
prerequisite removes everything stacked on it; durations of prerequisites freeze while advanced.
- **Anchor** (ghost): no bleed within Rank×3 yards. Ends: destroy subject / resolve unfinished business;
  abjuration suppresses. Created at ghost's birth, by summoning, or Create Influence.
- **Resonant** (spirit): anything matching its Influence; ends when phenomenon ends.
- **Infrastructure** (angel): never natural; built by extended actions; removal takes stories. Destiny Merit
  holders always have it.
- **Open**: prerequisite + extended action with subject in concert for scenes = Rank or living subject's
  Resolve (higher); or Control... (book: a **Control**-level? text says "Control Influence" creates Open
  instantly temporarily; table says **Strengthen** shifts to Open) [interp: either]. Allows Fetter/Manifest.
  Exorcism reverts it.
- **Controlled**: after successfully Possessing the subject on separate occasions = higher WP of entity/subject
  (progress lost if a possession is ended early). Allows Claim. Exorcism/bane → back to Open.
- **Fettered**: entity safe from bleed, in Twilight within 5 yards of Fetter; Influences on Fetter cost 1 less;
  can't use Influence/Numina on others. Ends with prerequisites, destruction/death, Unfetter, exorcism.
- **Urged** (living Fetter): entity reads surface thoughts (Power + Finesse vs Resolve + ST); urges an action
  (Power + Finesse vs Resolve + Composure **+1 die**); obeying gives host a Beat.
- **Possessed**: living host in coma, remembers missing time; entity can't use Numina/Influence; safe from
  bleed; 1 Essence/turn heals 1 bashing/lethal to host. Objects: can work switches etc. (Finesse). Corpses/
  robots: own Physical, entity's Mental/Social; 1 Ess → entity's Physical for a turn (1L to host). **Living
  hosts: own Attributes; read mind Finesse −4; host's Physical Skills −3, Social/Mental Skills −4; penalties −1
  per day Fettered to host.** Entity superimposed in Twilight — bane on host hurts it. **Lasts one scene.** Penalties *shrink* by 1/day Fettered (min 0). [interp] They hit Skill pools only, not
  Defense/Initiative; the entity spends its own WP while driving the body.
- **Materialized**: physical; no bleed; within Rank yards of Open object/person; **1 hr per success**.
- **Claimed**: permanent fusion; living host Urged during merge; +1 entity Attribute dot/day added to host
  (Power→Str/Int/Pre etc.); body mutates; Claimed gets entity's Essence, Virtue, Vice, Ban, Bane, Influences
  (not Numina). Detach: entity Power + Finesse − Rank vs host Resolve + Composure. Objects/corpses lose 1 Physical
  dot per 3 days until they disintegrate. Exorcism/bane → Open.
- **Reaching**: conduit through Gauntlet for a scene; Wits + Composure to sense; mortal abjuration at −3 closes.
- **Shadow Gate / Underworld Gate**: scene-long portals; Underworld Gate gives ghosts +1 Essence/scene. Every
  Avernian gate has a **key** (Int + Occult −3 to −5 to research).

### Numina [pp. 136–138] (Power + Finesse unless noted; "ST" = Supernatural Tolerance)
- **Aggressive Meme** (7 Ess): planted idea spreads to anyone told; vs Res + Com + ST.
- **Awe** (3): everyone looking contests Presence + Composure + ST; fewer successes → can't move/speak 1 turn (exc. 3 turns).
- **Blast**: range 10 yd/Power dot, no range penalties; **lethal weapon**; +1 damage per 2 Essence, max +Rank.
- **Dement** (1): vs Int + ST → **Insane** Tilt for scene.
- **Drain**: vs Stamina + Resolve + ST; winner gains WP or Essence = successes, loser loses same.
- **Emotional Aura** (1): scene; anyone entering 5 yd rolls Res + Com + ST vs activation; fail → −2 all actions.
- **Essence Thief** (1): can steal Essence from other ephemeral types.
- **Fate Sense** (1): vs Resolve + ST; learns Fate/Destiny.
- **Firestarter** (1): one small fire per success within Power yards.
- **Hallucination** (1): vs Wits + Composure + ST; each success **over the contesting roll** (net) alters one sense.
- **Host Jump** (3): move Possessed/Claimed to touched host meeting prerequisites.
- **Implant Mission** (2): vision + **Obsession** to carry out a task.
- **Innocuous** (free, no roll): Perception to notice it −2.
- **Left-Handed Spanner** (1): device with ≥3 moving parts malfunctions for successes turns (combat: must grapple).
- **Mortal Mask** (1): Materialized looks human for successes hours; Wits + Composure − Finesse to notice.
- **Omen Trance** (1, or 3 for others): extended ≥1 scene, vision of an event in the next week.
- **Pathfinder** (1): fastest route; vs Safe Place owners' lowest Resolve + ST.
- **Rapture** (2): **Insensate** Tilt; victim fails Resolve + ST → temporary derangement (Power days).
- **Regenerate** (1/level, no roll): heal 1 bashing then lethal per turn.
- **Resurrection** (10; Rank 4+ angels/healing spirits): raise the dead, −1/day dead; not natural deaths or undead.
- **Seek**: Finesse roll: direction/distance to nearest suitable Condition, 2 mi/Rank (×10 for 1 Ess).
- **Sign** (1): one message in any medium.
- **Speed** (2/4): ×2 / ×3 Speed for scene.
- **Stalwart**: Defense = Resistance.
- **Telekinesis** (1): successes = Strength for lift/throw.
(Reaching-compatible Numina are marked with an icon lost in the .txt.)

### Mortal interaction [pp. 138–140]
- **Unseen Sense** senses Twilight entities.
- **Research ban/bane**: extended Int + Occult; target **Rank 1: 5 · 2: 7 · 3: 10 · 4: 14 · 5: 20**; ghosts
  −2, angels +4; reveals ban or bane (exceptional: both).
- **Esoteric Armory (•–•••••)** Merit (sidebar p. 139): after researching a bane, if dots ≥ entity Rank you have it.
- **Contact**: slow trial-and-error unless entity has Image/Materialize/Sign; Medium Merit helps.
- **Summoning/Exorcism**: series of research actions + actions that create (or destroy) the Influence/
  Manifestation Conditions; summoning from other realms needs a Locus (spirits) or Avernian gate (ghosts).
- **Abjuration** [p. 140]: meditative; **Resolve + Composure vs entity's Power + Resistance**; +2 if method
  matches Virtue, −2 if Vice; Integrity 10 +3 / 9 +2 / 8 +1 / 6–7 0 / 5 −1 / 4 −2 / 3 −3 / 2 −4 / 1 −5 ("−1 per dot below 6").
  Action type/retries unstated [interp: instant; successive attempts −1 cumulative; entity may spend WP to resist;
  range = abjurist's Willpower **dots** in yards]. Success: all Conditions
  tagged by the entity within **abjurist's Willpower in yards suppressed for 1 day**; exceptional: abjurist
  also counts as a bane to it until Conditions return.
- **Warding/Binding** [p. 140]: mark boundary with the entity's **bane** (no bane = auto-fail); **Presence +
  Occult + Integrity modifier (as abjuration) − Rank** ± area (≤6 ft area +1, room/vehicle 0, two-story house
  −1, −1 per extra house-equivalent; skyscrapers etc. impossible). Success: crossing the boundary is treated as
  against its ban, for **successes in days**, or until the marking is disturbed (entity can take bane damage to
  break it).

## C. Horrors (corporeal monsters) [pp. 140–159]
### Full Horror creation [pp. 141–143]
1 Concept + Aspirations (intelligent: 3; animalistic: 1; feeding/compulsion should be one) · 2 Anchors
(Virtue/Vice, can be alien: "Murderous" Virtue) · 3 **Potency** (1–10) · 4 Attributes (lump sum) · 5 Dread
Powers · 6 Skills (lump; 3–5 Specialties optional) · 7 Merits · 8 Advantages.

| Potency | Trait max | Attribute dots | Skill dots | Dread Powers | Merit dots |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 | 15–18 | 10 | 3 | 3 |
| 2 | 6 | 19–22 | 15 | 3 | 5 |
| 3 | 7 | 23–26 | 20 | 3 | 7 |
| 4 | 8 | 27–30 | 25 | 4 | 9 |
| 5 | 9 | 31–34 | 30 | 4 | 11 |
| 6 | 10 | 35–38 | 35 | 4 | 13 |
| 7 | 10 | 39–42 | 40 | 5 | 15 |
| 8 | 10 | 43–46 | 45 | 6 | 17 |
| 9 | 10 | 47–50 | 50 | 7 | 19 |
| 10 | 10 | 51+ | 55 | 8 | 21 |
Multi-dot Dread Powers count each dot as one power.

Potency effects: **Willpower = Resolve + Composure + Potency**; may spend **Potency WP per turn** (but max 1 WP
on any single effect); **+Potency to contested rolls to resist supernatural powers** (Supernatural Tolerance).
Advantages: Health = Size + Stamina; Initiative = Dex + Composure; Defense = lower(Wits, Dex) + Athletics;
Speed = Str + Dex + species factor [p. 143] (quick-ref box says "+ Size" — [interp] species factor; human ≈ 8+);
Integrity only if partly human; optional Ban/Bane using Potency as Rank.
- **Horrors & Beats** (sidebar p. 141): ST characters gain **1 temporary WP (lost at end of scene)** whenever
  they'd earn a Beat (Aspiration, Condition resolved, dramatic failure…); feeding-Aspiration WP lasts until spent.
- Balance check: if Horror reduces PCs' best pools to chance die or rolls >2× their best pool, give PCs another
  way out or tone it down.

### Dread Powers [pp. 144–148] (ST picks a fitting pool; restrictions per concept)
- **Beastmaster**: 1 WP → swarm of vermin/birds/fish obeys; or one larger animal.
- **Chameleon Horror**: −3 to perceive it (−6 if still).
- **Discorporate**: on lethal damage/escape, dissolves to swarm; reforms later if one escapes.
- **Eye Spy**: remote viewing via a medium; 1 WP/hour.
- **Fire Elemental**: within 1 yd takes **3 lethal/turn**; immune to fire/heat.
- **Gremlin**: devices fail nearby; 1 WP disable or seize a device (bonus → penalty; harmful device attacks
  someone using its equipment bonus as lethal damage).
- **Home Ground**: there: **+3 dice physical pools & Influence, −3 damage taken, +3 to resist supernatural**.
- **Hunter's Senses**: vs chosen prey **+4 Perception, 9-again**.
- **Hypnotic Gaze**: 1 WP, Presence + Persuasion vs Composure → **perfect impression** for Social maneuvering this scene.
- **Immortal**: killed by anything but its Bane → returns in a later scene; even Bane only defeats it for now.
- **Influence (•–•••••)**: as ephemeral Influence, pays WP not Essence; no roll unless opposed.
- **Jump Scare**: 1 WP → resolve another's **Shaken** (choose which action auto-fails; victim still gets Beat),
  or turn that auto-failure into a dramatic failure (victim gets 2 Beats).
- **Know Soul**: 1 WP → learns Virtue, Vice, Aspirations, Integrity; +1 WP each → most recent failed breaking
  point, then older; using it vs victim: exceptional on 3 successes.
- **Madness and Terror**: 1 WP + pool vs Composure → Guilty/Shaken/Spooked; 3 WP → Broken/Fugue/Madness.
- **Maze**: 3 WP + touch building, 1 hour: others need Wits + Composure − its Resolve to progress/leave.
- **Miracle**: when asked by a living human: remove/apply Condition or Tilt; grant a Merit/Skill/Attribute at 5;
  cause a death; fulfil an Aspiration. Price: one of the wisher's Aspirations destroyed forever.
- **Mist Form**: 1 WP, scene: gaseous, fly at Speed, through cracks.
- **Natural Weapons (•–•••)**: weapon modifier **and armor piercing = dots**; can bite without grappling.
- **Numen**: any ephemeral Numen paid with WP; pool = fitting Attribute + Skill.
- **Prodigious Leap**: 1 WP: ~4 stories up or across 6-lane highway.
- **Reality Stutter**: reflexive 1 WP when moving: teleport within Speed meters in sight; +2 Defense that turn.
- **Regenerate (•–•••••)**: reflexive once/turn spend up to dots WP: each heals 1 lethal or 2 bashing.
- **Skin-Taker**: kill victim, 2 WP → permanently take their face/skin.
- **Snare**: 1 WP: 10-sq-yd area; grapple anyone in it with own pool **+3**.
- **Soul Thief**: meet its condition, 3 WP + pool vs Resolve → **Soulless** Condition.
- **Surprise Entrance**: 1 WP appear in scene; witnesses Resolve + Composure or **Shaken**; if barred, takes a
  turn to break in; else reflexive and acts once before anyone reacts.
- **Toxic (• / ••)**: inflicts moderate / grave **Poisoned** or **Sick** Tilt (automatic on damage, or 1 WP +
  pool vs Stamina).
- **Unbreakable**: attacks without exceptional success deal only **1 bashing**; exceptional normal; weakness bypasses.
- **Wall Climb**: walls/ceilings at full Speed.
