# CoD core — Merits (all, pp. 43–65)

Format: **Name (dots)** — Prereq — Effect `[p.]`. "Style" = buy maneuvers in order.
Supernatural Merits: mortals only; vanish if character becomes supernatural [p. 56].

## Mental [pp. 44–46]
- **Area of Expertise (•)** — Resolve 2 + a Specialty — chosen Specialty gives +2 instead of +1. [44]
- **Common Sense (•••)** — once/chapter, instant, Wits+Composure: ST answers one of: worst choice? what do I
  stand to lose? safest choice? am I chasing a worthless lead? Exc. success: +1 question. [44]
- **Danger Sense (••)** — +2 to reflexive Wits+Composure to detect an ambush. [44]
- **Direction Sense (•)** — always knows direction; no navigation penalties. [44]
- **Eidetic Memory (••)** — no rolls to recall experiences; +2 to Int+Composure (or Skill) recall of minute facts. [44]
- **Encyclopedic Knowledge (••)** — choose Skill; Int+Wits any time to get a relevant fact (even 0 dots). [44]
- **Eye for the Strange (••)** — Resolve 2, Occult 1 — Int+Composure on evidence: ST says if supernatural cause
  + one confirming detail; exc.: folklore hint at creature type (if mundane, +2 ongoing to investigate). [44]
- **Fast Reflexes (•–•••)** — Wits 3 or Dex 3 — +1 Initiative/dot. [44]
- **Good Time Management (•)** — Academics 2 or Science 2 — halve time between extended rolls. [44]
- **Holistic Awareness (•)** — stabilize/treat without medical gear unless patient has lethal/agg wound
  penalties; Wits+Survival to gather herbs. [44–45]
- **Indomitable (••)** — Resolve 3 — vs supernatural mental/emotional influence: **+2 dice to contest, or −2
  dice to the monster's pool if resisted**. Mundane persuasion unaffected. [45]
- **Interdisciplinary Specialty (•)** — Skill 3 with Specialty — apply that Specialty's +1 to any Skill with ≥1 dot when justifiable. [45]
- **Investigative Aide (•)** — chosen Skill 3+ — Uncovering Clues with that Skill: exceptional success on 3;
  Clues start with +1 element. Multi-buy. [45]
- **Investigative Prodigy (•–•••••)** — Wits 3, Investigation 3 — uncover Clues = lower of (successes, dots)
  in one instant action; only first gets extra elements. [45]
- **Language (•)** — one language fluently (speak/read/write). [45]
- **Library (•–•••)** — choose Mental Skill; +dots on extended rolls with it. Shareable. [46]
- **Meditative Mind (•, ••, ••••)** — 1: no environmental/wound penalties to meditate; 2: after meditating,
  +3 to Resolve+Composure rolls that day; 4: need only 1 success (not 4) to meditate. [46]
- **Multilingual (•)** — two languages conversationally; Int+Academics to read for context. [46]
- **Patient (•)** — +2 extra rolls on extended actions beyond the Attribute+Skill limit. [46]
- **Professional Training (•–•••••)** — Profession with 2 Asset Skills: 1 Networking (**2 free dots of Contacts** in the field);
  2 Continuing Education (**9-again on Asset Skills**); 3 Breadth (3rd Asset Skill + 2 Specialties in Assets);
  4 On the Job Training (+1 Asset Skill dot; Beat whenever you buy an Asset Skill dot); 5 The Routine (spend
  1 WP: rote action on an Asset Skill roll). [46–47]
  Professions/Assets: Academic Acad/Sci · Artist Crafts/Expr · Athlete Ath/Med · **Cop Streetwise/Firearms** ·
  Criminal Larc/SW · Detective Emp/Inv · Doctor Emp/Med · Engineer Crafts/Sci · Hacker Comp/Sci · Hit Man FA/Stealth ·
  Journalist Expr/Inv · Laborer Ath/Crafts · Occultist Inv/Occ · Politician Pol/Subt · Professional Acad/Pers ·
  Religious Leader Acad/Occ · Scientist Inv/Sci · Socialite Pol/Soc · Stuntman Ath/Drive · Survivalist AK/Surv ·
  Soldier FA/Surv · Technician Crafts/Inv · Thug Brawl/Intim · Vagrant SW/Surv. No EMT listed — create one (e.g. Medicine/Drive). [47]
- **Tolerance for Biology (•)** — Resolve 3 — no Composure/Stamina/Resolve rolls to withstand gore/biological strangeness. [46]
- **Trained Observer (• or •••)** — Wits 3 or Composure 3 — Perception rolls 9-again (•) / 8-again (•••). [46]
- **Vice-Ridden (••)** — two Vices, still 1 WP/scene. **Virtuous (••)** — two Virtues. [46]

## Physical [pp. 47–49]
- **Ambidextrous (•••)** — no −2 off-hand penalty. Creation only. [47]
- **Automotive Genius (•)** — Crafts 3, Drive 1, Science 1 — vehicle mod limit = Crafts×3 (not ×2) +1 per relevant Crafts Specialty. [47]
- **Crack Driver (•• / •••)** — Drive 3 — if only driving, +Composure to Drive rolls; attempts to disable vehicle −Composure; ••• one reflexive Drive action/turn. [47]
- **Demolisher (•–•••)** — Str 3 or Int 3 — ignore 1 Durability/dot when damaging objects. [47]
- **Double Jointed (••)** — Dex 3 — auto-escape mundane bonds; when grappled and not acting aggressively, subtract Dex from rolls to overpower her. [47]
- **Fleet of Foot (•–•••)** — Athletics 2 — +1 Speed/dot; pursuers −1/dot on foot-chase rolls. [47]
- **Giant (•••)** — Size 6, +1 Health. Creation only. [47]
- **Greyhound (•)** — Athletics 3, Wits 3, Stamina 3 — in chases, exceptional success on 3. [48]
- **Hardy (•–•••)** — Stamina 3 — +dots to resist disease, poison, deprivation, unconsciousness, suffocation. [47]
- **Iron Stamina (•–•••)** — Stamina 3 or Resolve 3 — each dot cancels 1 point of negative modifier from fatigue
  or **wound penalties** (never yields a bonus). [48]
- **Parkour (•–•••••, Style)** — Dex 3, Athletics 2 [48–49]:
  1 Flow: foot chase — subtract Parkour from successes needed; ignore env. penalties to Athletics up to rating.
  2 Cat Leap: falling Dex+Athletics gets 1 auto success; add Parkour to damage removable (not terminal velocity).
  3 Wall Run: climb 10 ft + 5 ft/Athletics dot as instant, no roll.
  4 Expert Traceur: 1 WP → one run/jump/climb roll is rote; no Defense that turn.
  5 Freeflow: after meditation, Athletics actions reflexively 1/turn; WP on foot-chase Athletics = +3 successes (not dice).
- **Quick Draw (•)** — Wits 3 + Specialty in weapon/style — draw/holster reflexively whenever Defense applies. [49]
- **Relentless (•)** — Athletics 2, Stamina 3 — chase opponents need +2 successes to catch/elude you. [49]
- **Seizing the Edge (••)** — Wits 3, Composure 3 — always has the Edge on turn 1 of a chase; opponent must
  succeed Wits+Composure (as vs ambush) or you ignore Speed/Initiative in first-turn success calc. [49]
- **Sleight of Hand (••)** — Larceny 3 — one Larceny instant action reflexively per turn; unnoticed unless someone specifically watches. [49]
- **Small-Framed (••)** — Size 4 (−1 Health); +2 to hide/go unnoticed etc. Creation only. [49]
- **Stunt Driver (•–••••, Style)** — Dex 3, Drive 3, Wits 3 — 1 Defensive Driving (−Drive dots to hit her moving
  vehicle); 2 Speed Demon (+10 Speed/success accelerating, not +5); 3 Drift (no maneuver roll to turn at speed);
  4 Clipping (ramming: ignore damage to own vehicle = Wits, before Durability). [49]

## Social [pp. 49–56]
- **Allies (•–•••••)** — per group. Favours rated 1–5 by ST; total ≤ Allies rating per chapter free. Beyond:
  Manip+Persuasion+Allies − favour rating; success = done; **either way lose 1 Allies dot** (returns end of
  chapter); dramatic: retribution; exceptional: keep the dot. Can **block** another's Allies/Contacts/Mentor/
  Retainer/Status (rating = blocked Merit's dots; roll only if it exceeds your Allies) for the chapter. [49–50]
- **Alternate Identity (•/••/•••)** — 1 superficial alias, 2 paperwork (beats PIs), 3 passes thorough
  inspection. +1 Subterfuge to defend it (•/••), +2 (•••). Multi-buy. [50]
- **Anonymity (•–•••••)** — no Fame — paper-trail searches −1/dot. [50]
- **Barfly (••)** — Socialize 2 — belongs in any bar; rolls to identify him as outsider −Socialize. [50]
- **Closed Book (•–•••••)** — Manip 3, Resolve 3 — **+dots Doors** vs Social Maneuvering; +dots to contested rolls
  to uncover her feelings/motives; ST may apply vs some supernatural truth effects. [50]
- **Contacts (•–•••••)** — one sphere per dot; info only. Manip + Social Skill, ±3 by relevance/danger/
  relationship. Can dig up Social Merits and Conditions (e.g., Embarrassing Secret). [50]
- **Fame (•–•••)** — 1 local/subculture, 2 regional, 3 worldwide; +1 die/dot on Social rolls with impressed
  people; drawback +1/dot to find/identify him. No Anonymity. [50]
- **Fast-Talking (•–•••••, Style)** — Manip 3, Subterfuge 2 [50–51]: 1 Always Be Closing (mark resisting/
  contesting your Social action: −1 to their Resolve or Composure); 2 Jargon (apply one relevant Specialty to
  any Social roll); 3 Devil's Advocacy (reroll one failed Subterfuge roll/scene); 4 Salting (opening a Door via
  conversation, 1 WP → open another Door); 5 The Nigerian Scam (if target regains WP from Vice in your presence,
  immediately roll Manip+Subterfuge to open a Door regardless of interval/impression).
- **Fixer (••)** — Contacts 2, Wits 3 — hiring a service: Availability −1. [51]
- **Hobbyist Clique (••)** — clique, all members have it + Skill 2 — 9-again on that Skill with group support;
  +2 dice on extended actions with it. Monthly meetings. [51]
- **Inspiring (•••)** — Presence 3 — Presence+Expression (−1 small group, −2 small crowd, −3 large crowd);
  listeners gain **Inspired** Condition. Not on self. [51]
- **Iron Will (••)** — Resolve 4 — when spending WP to contest/resist in a Social interaction, add **Resolve**
  instead of the usual +3/+2; if contested, 8-again. [51]
- **Mentor (•–•••••)** — 3 Skills (one may be Resources); once/session a favour in those; Mentor counts as
  successes = dots if a roll is needed; wants something in return. [51]
- **Mystery Cult Initiation (•–•••••)** — standing 1 initiate … 5 high priest. Benefits per level (guideline):
  1 Specialty or 1-dot Merit; 2 one-dot Merit; 3 Skill dot or 2-dot Merit; 4 three-dot Merit; 5 three-dot Merit
  or major advantage. Cult needs Purpose, Relic, Doctrine. Samples p. 52: Chosen of Mammon (5: +3 Resources;
  4: Thief of Fate); Sisters of the Machine Gun / Brothers of the Bomb (3: destroy holy artifact to let one weapon
  hurt spirits & ghosts; 4: 3 dots Retainers). [51–53]
- **Pusher (•)** — Persuasion 2 — when mark accepts soft leverage, impression improves as if Vice satisfied too. [53]
- **Resources (•–•••••)** — 1 spending money, 2 middle class, 3 upper middle, 4 wealthy, 5 rich.
  Once/chapter acquire Availability ≤ Resources freely; Avail = Resources+1 → −1 effective Resources for a month;
  Avail ≤ Resources−2 unlimited. [53]
- **Retainer (•–•••••)** — 1 barely useful … 3 professional … 5 best in class. Rolls in field: **dots × 2**;
  else dots. Multi-buy. [53–54]
- **Safe Place (•–•••••)** — Initiative bonus = dots (for investors, while there); breaching −dots; optional traps
  doing lethal ≤ rating (needs Crafts 1; avoid Dex+Larceny −dots). Shareable/poolable. [54]
- **Small Unit Tactics (••)** — Presence 3 — once/scene, 1 WP + instant action for a pre-planned coordinated
  action: up to Presence characters get the +3 WP bonus. [54]
- **Spin Doctor (•)** — Manip 3, Subterfuge 2 — Tainted Clues give −1 each (max −2 total) instead of ignoring successes. [54]
- **Staff (•–•••••)** — per dot one assistant type + Skill; they auto-get 1 success on mundane actions. [54]
- **Status (•–•••••)** — per group: add Status to Social rolls with those under its sway; group resources;
  once/chapter block a lower-rated Mentor/Resources/Retainer/Contacts/Allies if sensible; needs upkeep. [54]
  Examples: Police 1 informant, 2 beat cop, 3 detective, 4 sergeant, 5 chief. [55]
- **Striking Looks (• / ••)** — +1/+2 on Social rolls influenced by looks; same bonus to others noticing/remembering him. [54–55]
- **Sympathetic (••)** — at start of Social maneuvering, accept a Condition (Leveraged, Swooned…) to remove 2 of subject's Doors. [55]
- **Table Turner (•)** — Composure 3, Manip 3, Wits 3 — when someone starts Social Maneuvering on you, 1 WP to preempt with your own Social action. [55]
- **Takes One to Know One (•)** — investigating a crime matching your Vice: +2 and 9-again (normally −2); counts as fulfilling Vice. [55]
- **Taste (•)** — Crafts 2 + Crafts/Expression Specialty — Wits+Skill appraising art: per success ask a
  question (hidden meaning? creator's feeling? weakest point? which witness most moved? how best appreciated?)
  or +1 Social with interested groups for scene. [55–56]
- **True Friend (•••)** — won't betray; ST can't kill without permission; influencing them against you −5;
  once/story regain 1 WP via meaningful interaction. [56]
- **Untouchable (•)** — Manip 3, Subterfuge 2 — investigations of his crimes get Incomplete Clue tag unless exceptional. [56]

## Supernatural (mortals only) [pp. 56–60]
- **Aura Reading (•••)** — 1 WP, **Wits+Empathy − subject's Composure**, staring moment. Per success ask:
  most prominent emotion? telling truth? attention focus? going to attack? emotion most hidden? supernatural/not
  human? — or +1 Social vs them for scene. Drawback: once/chapter ST rolls Wits+Occult − Composure for a
  supernatural creature to sense she knows. [56]
- **Automatic Writing (••)** — meditate ≥1 min, 1 WP, Wits+Composure: 1 clue/statement per success. Fail or
  no personal item of a local spirit → haunted a week (failures become dramatic). [56–57]
- **Biokinesis (•–•••••)** — 1 WP + 1 min: move 1 Physical Attribute dot/Merit dot (max 5) for 1 hour; halve all healing times. [57]
- **Clairvoyance (•••)** — 1 WP + successful meditation + Wits+Occult; mods: object of place +1, never been −3,
  scrying for person −3, non-specific −4, spent much time +2, touching connected person +1. Needs her focus tool. [57]
- **Cursed (••)** — knows prophesied death; +2 Resolve+Composure vs fear/self-doubt; +1 Beat whenever taking
  lethal damage in last 3 Health boxes. [57]
- **Laying on Hands (•••)** — 1 WP, touch, Presence+Empathy: per success heal 2 bashing or 1 lethal; 3 successes
  = 1 agg. Healer takes 1 damage of same type per 2 healed (round up). Disease: extended, 1–10 successes. [57]
- **Medium (•••)** — Empathy 2 — hears the dead always. Ritual/meditation + Wits+Occult: raise a location's
  ghost-Condition one step (none → Anchor/Resonant/Infrastructure → Open → Controlled; see 06) until WP spent.
  Drawback: once/session disturbing message → Resolve+Composure or Shaken/Spooked. [57–58]
- **Mind of a Madman (••)** — Empathy 3 — sink into culprit's mind: 8-again on rolls to investigate/pursue/stop
  them; nightly dreams; a day without pursuit → breaking point as if committed the crime; −1 to own
  degeneration rolls until culprit caught. [57]
- **Numbing Touch (•–•••••)** — 1 WP + touch, Int+Empathy+dots vs Stamina + Supernatural Tolerance: victim
  ignores wound penalties but −dots to manual dexterity/sensory pools, fading 1/turn. Or 1 WP "psychic
  weapon" (1-ft range); on hit +1 WP more: victim loses WP = dots (excess → lethal). Usable with unarmed attack. [58]
- **Omen Sensitivity (•••)** — once/session Wits+Occult: 1 yes/no question per success, answered truly; gains Obsession or Spooked each time. [58]
- **Psychokinesis (••• or •••••)** — one force (fire, cold, electricity…); ••• needs force present, •••••
  creates it. 1 WP, **Resolve+Occult (− victim's Resolve if harming)**; each success: ±1 Size, move
  (Willpower dots ×2 yards), shape (Int+Crafts if detailed), attack (••• = 1L weapon, ••••• = 2L), creative use,
  manifest Size 1 patch (••••• only). Running through the effect: 1L (•••) / 2L (•••••). Drawback: at 0 WP
  wild manifestation unless Resolve+Composure (−2 if force nearby). [58]
- **Psychometry (•••)** — 1 WP, Wits+Occult: per success one yes/no or: strongest emotion? who remembers it
  most? am I missing something? where was object? what breaking point caused it? Mods: read before −2, >1 day
  −1, >1 wk −2, >1 mo −3, >1 yr −5, violent crime +2, vague tie −2, related spirits +3. [58–59]
- **Telekinesis (•–•••••)** — 1 WP activates for scene; dots = mental Strength for lifting. Attack: 1 WP each,
  **Telekinesis + Occult − target's Stamina**, bashing. Grapple using dots as Strength; overpowering costs more WP. [59]
- **Telepathy (••• or •••••)** — conversational range; 1 WP, Wits+Empathy (− Resolve if unwilling): foremost
  thought + one question per extra success (wants? fears? hiding? wants me to do? knows about X? turns on?
  shameful?). ••••• send one phrase per success. [59–60]
- **Thief of Fate (•••)** — touch steals luck unless 1 WP: subject's failures that day are dramatic; she gets
  **+4 dice** (not +3) when spending WP that day. [60]
- **Unseen Sense (••)** — one creature type: senses proximity; once/chapter accept Spooked to pinpoint (fails vs cloaking powers). [60]

## Fighting [pp. 60–65]
Defense can't be sacrificed more than once per turn [p. 60].
- **Armed Defense (•–•••••, Style)** — Dex 3, Weaponry 2, Defensive Combat (Weaponry): 1 Cover the Angles
  (Dodging: multi-attacker Defense penalty reduced by 1 — full Defense vs first two); 2 Weak Spot (if your
  Defense reduces armed attacker's pool to 0 → disarmed; if Dodging, when Dodge roll reduces his successes to
  0); 3 Aggressive Defense (1 WP, declare at turn start; when Dodging, each success over an attacker's = 1 lethal
  to him; not with Weak Spot/Press); 4 Iron Guard (each turn trade weapon bonus for Defense 1:1; when Dodging
  add full weapon bonus after doubling); 5 Press the Advantage (1 WP, Dodging and roll reduces attacker to 0 →
  unarmed attack at −2 vs him, 1/turn). [60–61]
- **Cheap Shot (••)** — Street Fighting 3, Subterfuge 2 — reflexive Dex+Subterfuge vs Wits+Composure: win → opponent loses Defense next turn; cumulative −2 per reuse/scene. [61]
- **Choke Hold (••)** — Brawl 2 — grapple move Choke (after a Hold): successes > 2×victim's Stamina →
  unconscious (6 − Stamina) minutes; successes accumulate over turns. [61]
- **Close Quarters Combat (•–•••••, Style)** — Wits 3, Athletics 2, Brawl 3: 1 Firing Lines (vs ranged attack,
  give up action to reach cover within 2×Speed instead of dropping prone); 2 Hard Surfaces (grapple Damage move
  deals **lethal** then ends grapple); 3 Armored Coffin (grappling: add opponent's general armor to your pool;
  your Damage move ignores his armor; not with Hard Surfaces); 4 Prep Work (surprise attack Dex+Stealth roll is
  rote; Brawl/Weaponry ambush only); 5 Turnabout (Disarm results step up: fail → he drops it; success → you
  take it; exceptional → you take it + 2B to him). [61–62]
- **Defensive Combat (•)** — Brawl 1 or Weaponry 1 (choose) — use Brawl or Weaponry instead of Athletics for
  Defense (Weaponry only with weapon in hand). Can buy both. [61]
- **Fighting Finesse (••)** — Dex 3 + Specialty in Weaponry/Brawl — use Dex instead of Str for attacks with that Specialty. Multi-buy. [61]
- **Firefight (•–•••, Style)** — Composure 3, Dex 3, Athletics 2, Firearms 2: 1 Shoot First (gun drawn: +Firearms
  to Initiative; with Quick Draw applies to first turn); 2 Suppressive Fire (when using Covering Fire p. 90
  opponents can't benefit from aiming at her; she applies Defense vs Firearms plus cover; may do it with
  semi-auto); 3 Secondary Target (hit collateral objects: bashing, ignore cover penalties, no weapon damage bonus). [61–62]
- **Grappling (•–•••, Style)** — Stamina 3, Str 2, Athletics 2, Brawl 2: 1 Sprawl (opponent can't use Drop Prone
  / Take Cover in grapple); 2 Takedown (on grapple roll may make opponent prone instead of grappling, and/or
  deal bashing = successes); 3 Joint Lock (move: next turn opponent takes bashing = successes; lead-in to
  Restrain; your successful overpower moves add 1L). [62]
- **Heavy Weapons (•–•••••, Style)** — Stamina 3, Str 3, Athletics 2, Weaponry 2; two-handed weapons: 1 Sure
  Strike (reflexively −3 dice after all penalties → +1 weapon damage); 2 Threat Range (if not moving/Dodging,
  anyone entering reach takes 1L and −weapon rating to Defense 1 turn); 3 Bring the Pain (sacrifice Defense;
  damage dealt = penalty on victim's next turn actions); 4 Warding Stance (1 WP reflexive: weapon damage rating
  as armor for turn, not vs firearms); 5 Rending (1 WP before attack: hit adds 1 aggravated). [62–63]
- **Improvised Weaponry (•–•••, Style)** — Wits 3, Weaponry 1: 1 Always Armed (reflexive Wits+Weaponry at turn
  start: weapon +0, Init −1, Size 1, Dur 2, Structure 4; exc.: +1 dmg & Size, Init −2; no −1 improvised
  penalty); 2 In Harm's Way (weapon's Structure as general armor vs one Brawl/Weaponry attack; damage goes to
  weapon bypassing Durability; only when applying Defense); 3 Breaking Point (all-out attack: spend 2 Structure
  per +1 weapon modifier; 0 Structure = destroyed). [62–63]
- **Iron Skin (•• or ••••)** — Martial Arts 2 or Street Fighting 2, Stamina 3 — armor vs bashing 1 (••) / 2 (••••);
  1 WP when hit to downgrade 1 (••) / 2 (••••) lethal → bashing. [63]
- **Light Weapons (•–•••••, Style)** — Wits 3 or Fighting Finesse, Dex 3, Athletics 2, Weaponry 2; one-handed
  weapons damage ≤2: 1 Rapidity (weapon becomes 0 damage this turn, +Weaponry to Initiative); 2 Thrust (trade
  Defense 1:1 for attack dice; not if Defense already used; not with all-out attack); 3 Feint (attack does no
  damage; next attack ignores Defense = successes+weapon? book example: 2-dmg weapon, 3 successes → next attack
  ignores 5 Defense and +3 damage) **[interp: ignore Defense = successes + weapon rating; +successes damage]**;
  4 Flurry (Defense available, not Dodging: anyone entering proximity takes 1L per turn); 5 Vital Shot (sacrifice
  Defense: hit adds 1 aggravated). [63]
- **Marksmanship (•–••••, Style)** — Composure 3, Resolve 3, Firearms 2; no Defense on turns used; must aim ≥1 turn:
  1 Through the Crosshairs (aim bonus cap = Composure+Firearms, not 3); 2 Precision Shot (reduce weapon damage
  1:1 to cancel specified-target penalty); 3 A Shot Rings Out (fire into crowd at a target without penalty; never
  hits unintended target); 4 Ghost (Firearms as penalty to spot her position / investigate it). [63]
- **Martial Arts (•–•••••, Style)** — Resolve 3, Dex 3, Athletics 2, Brawl 2; unarmed or Brawl weapons: 1 Focused
  Attack (specified-target penalties −1; ignore 1 armor); 2 Defensive Strike (+1 or +2 Defense, −1 attack die per
  point; on a turn you attack, not with Dodge); 3 Whirlwind Strike (Defense available, not Dodging: anyone
  entering reach takes 1B/turn; 1 WP → 2B until next turn); 4 The Hand as Weapon (unarmed strikes **lethal**);
  5 Touch of Death (unarmed counts as **2-damage weapon**). [63–64]
- **Police Tactics (•–•••, Style)** — Brawl 2, Weaponry 1: 1 Compliance Hold (+2 to overpower to disarm or
  immobilize); 2 Weapon Retention (disarm/turn-weapon attempts must exceed your Weaponry in successes);
  3 Speed Cuff (cuff an immobilized opponent reflexively). [64]
- **Shiv (• or ••)** — Street Fighting 2, Weaponry 1 — concealed weapon (−Weaponry to detect): • 0-damage,
  •• 1-damage weapon; usable with Brawl. [64]
- **Street Fighting (•–•••••, Style)** — Stamina 3, Composure 3, Brawl 2, Streetwise 2; unarmed/Brawl weapons:
  1 Duck and Weave (reflexive −1 to all actions this turn: Defense uses higher of Wits/Dex; not if already
  rolled unpenalized); 2 Knocking the Wind Out (successful unarmed hit: −1 to his next roll); 3 Kick 'Em While
  They're Down (successes > target's Stamina → may apply Knocked Down; 2B reflexively when prone foe in reach
  tries to rise); 4 One-Two Punch (1 WP on hit: +2 bashing); 5 Last-Ditch Effort (already wound-penalised and
  about to be hit/overpowered: 1 WP + sacrifice Defense → attack first, even if acted; not if WP already spent this turn). [65]
- **Unarmed Defense (•–•••••, Style)** — Dex 3, Brawl 2, Defensive Combat (Brawl): 1 Like a Book (vs unarmed,
  not Dodging: +½ Brawl (round down) Defense); 2 Studied Style (one opponent's attacks don't reduce your Defense;
  if Defense drops his pool to 0 he loses 10-again vs you); 3 Redirect (Dodging: when roll zeroes an attack,
  attacker re-rolls it vs another attacker of your choice; 1/turn, not back at same attacker); 4 Joint Strike
  (1 WP: roll Str+Brawl instead of Defense; extra successes over attacker = bashing + Arm/Leg Wrack); 5 Like the
  Breeze (declare at turn start; Dodging and zeroing an attack → Knocked Down). [65]
