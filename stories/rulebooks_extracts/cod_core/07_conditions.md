# CoD core — Tilts & Conditions (Appendix Two pp. 280–287, Appendix Three pp. 288–291; rules p. 75–76, 93)

Rules recap: **Tilts** = combat-only, no Beat on ending; **Conditions** = narrative, **Beat on resolution**
(not on timing out); Persistent: Beat once per chapter when it impacts life. ST characters: +1 WP (scene)
instead of Beats. Ephemeral Influence/Manifestation Conditions (Anchor, Open, Possessed…) → 06_antagonists §B.
Equipment Conditions Fragile/Volatile → 05 §14.

## Personal Tilts [pp. 280–286]
- **Arm Wrack**: one arm → drop held item, can't attack with it, off-hand penalties (−2) on manual rolls unless
  Ambidextrous; both arms → chance die on manual-dexterity rolls, −3 other Physical. Cause: arm hit (−2) for
  damage > Stamina; hand hit any damage; powers. Ends when that damage heals (mark X under leftmost box);
  aggravated → permanent.
- **Beaten Down**: must spend 1 WP per violent action (can still run, Dodge, Defense). Cause: bashing > Stamina
  or any lethal (optional rule p. 87). Ends: surrender → +1 WP + Beat, out of fight.
- **Blinded**: one eye −3 vision rolls (incl. attacks), **half Defense**; both −5 and **no Defense**. Cause: eye
  hit (−5; exceptional to blind both), or dirt/sand/brow-cut: **Dex + Athletics − 3 − Defense** → blinded next
  turn. Ends when damage heals; agg → permanent. Out of combat → Blind Condition.
- **Deafened**: one ear −3 hearing Perception; both: chance die hearing Perception and **−2 all combat rolls**.
  Cause: loud noise within 10 ft, or ear attack (−4). Noise deafness lasts (10 − Stamina − Resolve) turns
  [interp: book says "10 − the victim's Stamina + Resolve", read as 10 − (Sta + Res)].
- **Drugged**: −2 Speed (and static Defense), **−3 all combat rolls incl. Defense & Perception**, **ignore wound
  penalties**. Administer: Dex + Weaponry −1 (improvised); specific body part = specified target. Lasts
  (10 − (Stamina + Resolve)) hours; medical help halves.
- **Immobilized**: no combat actions, no movement, **no Defense**; held down: 1 WP for a headbutt. Cause:
  Restrain move, bindings. Ends: break grapple (only Break Free allowed on a win) or Str + Athletics − item
  Durability (arms+legs bound −2, hogtied −4); each roll 1 bashing.
- **Insane**: +1 combat rolls, acts after everyone, −3 Social, **can't spend WP**. Cause: Resolve + Composure to
  resist anxiety (with suitable Condition), unavoidable for truly horrific sights, powers (Dement). Ends: end of
  scene; or instant Resolve + Composure vs (10 − her Willpower) pool, no Defense that turn.
- **Insensate**: no actions (can apply Defense; can move per summary); any damage ends it; 1 WP to act normally
  one turn; ends at end of scene; drug-induced → becomes Drugged. Administer drug: Dex + Weaponry −1.
- **Knocked Down**: lose action if not yet taken; prone (ranged −2 vs, melee +2 vs); attack from ground −2;
  Defense applies. Cause: melee weapon +2 or more, or firearm +3 or more (declare; **halve total damage, round
  down**) or leg attack (−2) with melee/unarmed. Avoid: if not yet acted, Dex + Athletics − weapon modifier
  instead of action. Ends: stand up (an action).
- **Leg Wrack**: one leg: half Speed, −2 Physical movement rolls & Defense; both: Knocked Down, can't rise,
  Speed 1 (only action), movement rolls → chance die. Cause: leg hit (−2) damage > Stamina; ends as Arm Wrack.
- **Poisoned**: moderate 1 bashing/turn; grave 1 lethal/turn. Injection: Dex + Weaponry −1. Each turn
  reflexive **Stamina + Resolve** (−3 if taking a non-reflexive action): success negates that turn's damage.
  After combat, normal poison/Toxicity rules.
- **Sick**: moderate: −1 to all actions, +1 more every two turns (max −5 at turn 9); grave: same + 1 bashing
  per turn of combat. Penalties fade 1/turn of rest.
- **Stunned**: **lose next action; half Defense until next acts**. Cause: head hit with damage ≥ Size (head
  attacks count Size as 1 lower; "stun" weapons double modifier for this check). End early: reflexive 1 WP, then
  −3 that turn.

## Environmental Tilts [pp. 281–284]
- **Blizzard**: −1 vision within arm's length, −1 more per 10 yd (also ranged attacks); −1 Physical per 4" snow.
  Gear +1–+3. Often with Extreme Cold / Heavy Winds / Ice.
- **Earthquake**: −1 to −5 Dex pools & Defense; 1–3 lethal/turn, reflexive Stamina + Athletics downgrades to
  bashing (exc.: none). Rarely > 1 min.
- **Extreme Cold** (< 0 °C) / **Extreme Heat** (> 40 °C): bashing doesn't heal (fast healers halve rate); −1 to
  all rolls per hour, at −5 → 1 lethal/hour. End: warmth/shade; hypothermia needs medical care.
- **Flooded**: −2 Physical per foot; over head → swim Dex + Athletics or hold breath Stamina + Composure.
- **Heavy Rain**: −3 sight & hearing Perception; > 1 hour → Flooded.
- **Heavy Winds** (grade 1–5: 40 mph … 150+ mph): −3 hearing; −grade to Physical (incl. Drive); grade bashing per
  turn, reflexive Dex + Athletics avoids.
- **Ice**: half Speed, −2 Physical & Defense (−4 at full Speed); dramatic failure → Knocked Down; Drive −5,
  half acceleration. Dex + Crafts can create slick surfaces.
- **Zombies!** (p. 150): see 06 Horde.

## Conditions [pp. 288–291] (Example Skills that can grant on exceptional success in brackets)
- **Amnesia (Persistent)**: missing period of memory. Resolve: something problematic from it arises.
- **Blind (P optional)**: sight rolls chance die; substitute sense at −3; combat → Blinded Tilt. Resolve:
  limitation heightens immediate danger.
- **Bonded** [Animal Ken]: +2 to influence the animal once/scene; animal adds your Animal Ken to resist
  fear/coercion and to one roll. Resolve: animal dies.
- **Broken (Persistent)**: −2 Social & Resolve rolls, −5 Intimidation. Beat: back down / fail due to it. Shed:
  regain or lose an Integrity dot, or exceptional breaking point.
- **Connected** [Politics, Socialize]: +2 rolls relating to a group, or shed for one-time +3. Resolve: shed /
  bridge burned.
- **Crippled (Persistent)**: Speed 1; manual wheelchair Speed = Str (hands busy), electric 3. Beat: danger or
  severe inhibition. Temporary version resolves on healing.
- **Deprived** (addiction; P optional): −1 die to Stamina, Resolve, Composure pools (not derived traits).
  Resolve: indulge, therapy.
- **Embarrassing Secret**: if it gets out → Notoriety. Resolve: secret gets out.
- **Fugue (Persistent)**: in similar circumstances Resolve + Composure or ST controls you for the scene (you flee
  the conflict). Beat: suffer it. Shed as Broken.
- **Guilty**: −2 to Resolve/Composure rolls defending against Subterfuge, Empathy, Intimidation. Resolve: make
  restitution / confess. (Common after successful breaking point.)
- **Informed** [Academics, Investigation, Occult, Science]: shed on a relevant (non-combat) roll: failure → 1
  success; success → exceptional. Resolve: use it.
- **Inspired** [Crafts, Expression]: on a related action, resolve: **+1 WP** and that roll is exceptional on 3
  successes. (Default Condition for exceptional successes.)
- **Leveraged (Character)** [Empathy, Persuasion, Subterfuge]: shed by doing what that character asks without
  resisting; or turn the tables.
- **Lost**: no headway until Wits + Streetwise (city) / Wits + Survival (wild) succeeds. Resolve: navigate or abandon goal.
- **Madness (Persistent)**: ST has (10 − Integrity) dice, once per chapter applies as penalty to a Mental or
  Social roll. Beat: fail a roll due to it. Shed as Broken.
- **Mute (Persistent)**: can't speak. Beat: limitation heightens danger. Temporary resolves on regaining voice.
- **Notoriety** [Subterfuge, Socialize]: −2 Social with those who know; **+1 Door** in Social Maneuvering.
  Resolve: debunked/cleared.
- **Obsession**: 10-again while pursuing it; **no 10-again** otherwise. Resolve: fulfil it, therapy.
- **Shaken** [Brawl, Firearms, Intimidation, Weaponry]: when fear could hinder an action, **opt to fail before
  rolling** and shed it (Beat). From breaking points. (Jump Scare Dread Power interacts.)
- **Soulless (Persistent)**: no abjuration/warding/binding; −2 to resist possession; **no WP from rest or
  surrender; Virtue/Vice reversed** (Virtue 1 WP/scene, Vice full WP once/chapter but is a breaking point at −5
  unless Integrity 1). Beat: lose Integrity through indulging Vice. Resolve: regain soul.
- **Spooked**: fascinated by the supernatural; shed when fear/fascination makes you hinder the group (go off
  alone, run instead of holding ground).
- **Steadfast**: after a failed roll, resolve → treat as 1 success (chance die: roll one regular die instead).
- **Swooned (Character)** [Persuasion, Subterfuge]: −2 to rolls that would harm them; they get +2 Social vs you;
  their Social Maneuvering impression +1 step. Resolve: endanger yourself for them, or opt to fail resisting them.
- Also defined elsewhere: **Fragile/Volatile** (equipment, p. 102), Ephemeral ladder (p. 132–136).

## Breaking-point Condition menu (p. 74)
Success/failure: Guilty, Shaken, or Spooked. Dramatic failure: Broken, Fugue, or Madness (+ Beat).
