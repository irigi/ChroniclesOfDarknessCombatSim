- **Weapons always deal lethal.** Str below req: −1 attack. Two-handed used one-handed: Str req +1.
- **Concealed** (shields): not attacking with it → add Size to Defense and Size as concealment vs ranged.
- **Stun**: halve victim's Size when aiming at the head to stun (→ Stunned Tilt needs damage ≥ Size).
- Size: 1 hide in hand, 2 in coat, 3+ can't hide.
- Unarmed: fists 0B (bashing). Human bite −1 (grapple only). Improvised: Durability − 1, −1 to hit (05 §11).
