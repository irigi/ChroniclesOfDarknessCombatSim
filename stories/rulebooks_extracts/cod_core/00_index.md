# Chronicles of Darkness core rulebook (2015) — extract index

Source: `../../../rulebooks/Chronicles of Darkness.txt` (16,521 lines). Book originally titled *The World of
Darkness Second Edition*. This is the **base engine** for every CoD 2e splat (VtR 2e, WtF 2e, CtL 2e, GtS 2e, MtA 2e);
splat books override it for their own creatures.
