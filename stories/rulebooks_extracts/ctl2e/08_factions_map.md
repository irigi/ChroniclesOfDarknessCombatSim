# CtL 2e — Seemings, Kiths, Courts (map + exact mechanics)

Source Ch1 "The Face in the Mirror" pp. 21–59 (L2186–5443). Seeming & kith are chosen at creation (see 02).
Every seeming: **Blessing** (+1 dot to an Attribute of a category at creation, free, + a Glamour-gated power),
**Curse** (an extra breaking point, Clarity-damage pool = **½ Wyrd rounded up**), **Regalia** (favored Arcadian
Contract category — see 03). Kith: **Kith Blessing** (a Skill use where **3 successes = exceptional success**) + a
named power. Core book kiths are 13; more are in supplements (Ch7 has kith-creation guidelines, p. 305–306).

## C. Courts [pp. 33–50]
Seasonal courts are the common North-American/European model, not universal (also Dawn/Dusk, Roses, Land/Sea;
Ch6 has Hong Kong Dragon Court, Iceland Seasonal Loeg, Massachusetts Tide Courts, New Orleans Traders' Courts).
Each court has a **Bargain** with its season that hides members from the Gentry; the **ruling court rotates by season**
(failing to hand off power draws the Fae). Joining = a pledge; membership ties you to the season's **Court Contracts**
(Mantle is the Merit; Court Goodwill for other courts — see 04). Glamour harvested by court emotion is easier
(see 05 §Glamour).
| Court (titles) | Emotion | Ethos / Bargain gist | Mantle sign |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Spring** (Antler Crown, Emerald Court, Court of Desire) | **Desire** | live joyfully so the Gentry can't understand; "Verdant Rule": *Your Desires Are Your Own* | warmth, scent of flowers, moving air; flowers in footprints at peak |
| **Summer** (Iron Spear, Crimson Court, Court of Wrath) | **Wrath** | militant defense; channel aggression; be too strong to take | heat, iron smell, heat-shimmer |
| **Autumn** (Leaden Mirror, Ashen Court, Court of Fear) | **Fear** | use the Fae's own weapons (sorcery, tokens); first choice of enchanted spoils | cool breeze, rattling branches, dead leaves, witch-fire glyphs |
| **Winter** (Silent Arrow, Onyx Court, Court of Sorrow) | **Sorrow** | stillness, secrecy, elude notice | subtle: starkness, stillness, a few snowflakes; full flurry = danger |
| Courtless | — | no court Contracts/Mantle | — |

## D. Other groups (map only)
- **Motley**: small changeling group, usually bound by a motley pledge (Merits in 04: Motley/Style merits). [p. 80]
- **Freehold**: local society; newcomers get ~1 month of hospitality, then may swear the **freehold pledge**
  (protect members, never aid the Fae); breaking it invites the Wyrd's sanction and exile. [p. 79]
- **Loyalists** (Bridge-Burners, Privateers, True Loyalists) — see 06. **Fae-touched** — App 1, see 02 §F.

## F. Making new kiths, courts, Contracts (Ch7) [pp. 304–309]
- **Kith**: purpose + primary category; **Skill blessing** (3 successes = exceptional in a narrow circumstance) +
  **trickery** power (usually 1 G + Attribute + Skill; vs others resisted, or contested with Wyrd vs Tolerance in pools);
  availability (prolific / handful / unique); origin (Keeper's pet / changeling's will / Arcadia's gift).
- **Court**: theme (themed set, opposites, single court), coping emotion (also its Bedlam emotion), Bargain with a patron,
  power-transfer method, Mantle (•–•• bonus dice; •••–•••• rerolls, WP regain, temporary Merits; ••••• combat rerolls,
  auto-success or unique powers; plus a Glamour trigger), Crown blessing (temporary, scales with Mantle).
- **Contracts**: Common = manipulate/enhance; Royal = create/reforge/control. Pool = Attribute + Skill + **Wyrd**
  (Arcadian) or **Mantle** (Court); resisted (− resistance Attribute) if successes matter, contested (Resistance Attr +
  Tolerance) if not; no roll if only self/objects. Every Contract has a thematic Loophole. **Goblin Contracts**: single
  narrow effect, cost 1–2 G, rarely WP, easy Loopholes, rarely rolls; creditors tracked by the ST.
