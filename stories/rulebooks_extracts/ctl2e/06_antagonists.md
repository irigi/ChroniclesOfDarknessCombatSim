# CtL 2e — Antagonists: fetches, the Loyal, Hedge ghosts, hobgoblins, Huntsmen, True Fae

Source Ch5 "Shared Nightmares" pp. 233–273 (L20072–23843). Mortals/hunters → `../cod_core/06_antagonists.md`.
Cold iron is a bane for **all fae** (changelings, Hedge ghosts, hobgoblins, True Fae) **except Huntsmen**.

## A. Fetches [pp. 233–239]
Made by the Keeper from a piece of the victim's **shadow** + detritus (clothes, leaves, animals, fae-stuff); given the
victim's memories at abduction; lives her life, usually unaware until she returns. Five questions at creation: where
abducted; who suspects; what is it made of; how is it flawed; did the changeling see it.
**Traits** (only if it matters mechanically): built like a changeling (Ch3 dot totals). Innate Attributes/Merits may
mirror the changeling; learned Skills/Merits reflect its own life (may develop defensively once she returns); ST may add
XP for its years.
- **No Contracts, seeming or kith; no Mantle** (Court Goodwill possible). Has **Needle & Thread** (Threads may be
  odd: Obedience, Surveillance). **Wyrd = its changeling's Wyrd** (rises with hers, free). **Integrity, not Clarity**.
- Can't seal, swear oaths or bargains; nearly immune to being sealed. Clash of Wills: **Resolve + Wyrd**.
- **Echoes**: automatic **Attuned to the Wyrd** + **one more Echo per Wyrd dot** (another each time her Wyrd rises):
  - *Attuned to the Wyrd* (auto): sees miens; senses any changeling within **50 ft** (knows if it's *its* changeling);
    **changelings can't surprise it**.
  - *Normalcy* (W1, always on, can switch off reflexively): undetectable by fae magic — reads as human; must be off to
    use other Echoes (except Attuned).
  - *Enter the Hedge* (W1): as a changeling.
  - *Heart of Wax* (W1): **ignores wound penalties**; 1 G reflexive: shed a physical-impairment Tilt for the scene.
  - *Summon Shard* (W1): touch glass + 1 G reflexive: blade **1L** (2L from a mirror), lasts the scene.
  - *Mimic Contract* (W2): use any Contract its changeling has (must have met her face to face); cost 1 G or the
    Contract's cost, whichever greater; no Loopholes.
  - *Shadow Boxing* (W2): 1 G reflexive: rest of scene **its changeling gets no Defense against it** (armor still works).
  - *Shadow Step* (W3): 1 G: step into a shadow, out of another comparable one within **100 yd** (instant; 3 G =
    reflexive).
  - *Death of Glamour* (W4): **10 G** + Resolve + Wyrd (instant; spend over turns): **no Contracts within 50 ft**, every
    Glamour-holder (itself too) loses 1 G/turn; lasts 1 turn per success.
  - *Call the Huntsmen* (W5): instant, spend all its Glamour → beacon summoning nearby Huntsmen (or Gentry, ST).
- Killing your own fetch = 4-die breaking point; someone else's = 3-die; meeting it first time = 1-die. Resolutions:
  kill, forgive, or **reclaim** (merge — ST-defined reward, e.g. +max Clarity). Parallel Lives Merit links them.

## B. The Loyal [pp. 239–245] (map; courts call all courtless "loyalists")
- **Bridge-Burners**: hate the Fae; destroy wonder, art, dreams, fetches to starve Arcadia of interest; skilled
  oneiromancers. Usually exile. Sample: **Carey Ives** (Darkling Leechfinger, courtless; Int 3 Wit 4 Res 4 / Str 3
  Dex 3 Sta 3 / Pre 1 Man 2 Com 2; Weaponry 3, Stealth 3, Intimidation 4, Brawl 2…; Wyrd 3, Glamour 12/3, WP 6,
  Clarity 3/6, Init 5, Def 5, Speed 11, Health 8; frailty: minor taboo — must engage in formal court ceremony; Regalia
  Mirror, Sword; Contracts Dreamsteps, Glimpse of a Distant Mirror, Know the Competition, Oathbreaker's Punishment, Red
  Revenge, Touch of Wrath; Merits Anonymity 2, Defensive Dreamscaping, Dreamweaver, Fleet of Foot 3, Glamour Fasting).
- **Privateers**: sell changelings (and others) to the Gentry/Goblin Markets for Glamour, tokens, protection
  ("contracts of marque"); punished by cold-iron execution. Often ally with hunter cells.
- **True Loyalists**: serve the Others (regret, dependency on Arcadian substances, coercion by Huntsmen).

## C. Hedge ghosts [pp. 245–251]
Dead who died in the Hedge, emotions made manifest, loose Icons, or soul-scraps sewn together. Built like core
ephemeral entities (core p. 122) but with **Wyrd instead of Rank**:
| Wyrd | Trait limit | Attribute dots | Max Glamour | Numina | Frailties |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 5 | 5–8 | 10 | 1–3 | 1 minor |
| 3–4 | 7 | 9–14 | 15 | 3–5 | 2 minor |
| 5–6 | 9 | 15–25 | 20 | 5–7 | 1 major, 2 minor |
| 7–8 | 12 | 26–35 | 25 | 7–9 | 1 major, 3 minor |
| 9–10 | 15 | 36–45 | 50 | 9–11 | 2 major, 3 minor |
Wyrd = its Supernatural Tolerance; **−1 to bind it per 2 Wyrd**.
- **Attributes**: Power (Str/Int/Pre), Finesse (Dex/Wits/Man), Resistance (Sta/Res/Com); no Skills, no unskilled
  penalty; rolls **Attribute + Rank** [sic — read as Wyrd, [interp]] or Attribute + Attribute. Spend Glamour to boost an
  Attribute 1:1 for the scene, max +½ Wyrd ↑, one per turn.
- **Corpus = Resistance + Size** (no wound penalties). **Willpower = Finesse + Resistance (max 10)**, +1/day (or a
  Hedge-cycle event; Hedge-born: +1 per 3 Glamour stolen). **Initiative = Finesse + Resistance; Defense = lower of
  Power/Finesse, applies vs firearms too; Speed = Power + Finesse**; Size 5 (ex-human) or ½ Wyrd ↑ (Hedge-born).
  No Clarity/Integrity. Anchors: a **Thread** (affirming it refreshes WP; a PC who makes it affirm → Beat once/scene);
  ex-living keep their Vice or Needle (fulfil → all WP once/chapter). No Virtue.
- **Injuries**: **lethal attacks deal only bashing** unless a bane; each aggravated wound costs 1 Glamour. Destroyed by
  lethal/agg with Glamour left → reforms after regaining Glamour = Corpus. Heal like changelings; ST WP: 1 WP = 2 bashing
  or 1 lethal (instant); 5 WP + a day = 1 agg. **Cold iron: 1 agg per turn of contact; cold-iron weapons deal agg.**
  Frailties tied to their origin (may discorporate/trap rather than damage).
- **Glamour**: harvest from ambient emotions (not reap), only from mortals or other Hedge ghosts; a changeling may give
  Glamour by touch; from dreams: 1 WP, **Finesse + Wyrd − Resolve**, enter via Gate of Horn (breach Bastion) or Gate of
  Ivory by touch → on waking gains Glamour = successes (×2 exc); DF: trapped till natural waking, takes 1 lethal + victim's
  Tolerance. **At 0 Glamour: 1 lethal per hour.**
- **Influence** dots = ½ Wyrd ↑ (also max per Influence; can split; trade 1 Numen per extra dot). Use: effect level +
  duration level ≤ rating; pay Glamour; roll **Power + Finesse**; minds resist with higher of Resolve/Composure +
  Tolerance. Levels: • Strengthen 1 G, •• Manipulate 2 G, ••• Control 3 G, •••• Create 4 G, ••••• Mass Create 5 G (½ Wyrd
  instances, or one permanent; no permanent mind changes). Duration: 0 = 1 min/success; • 10 min/success; •• 1 hr/success
  +1 G; ••• 1 day/success +2 G; •••• permanent +2 G.
- **Manifestation**: in the Hedge they're physical and visible free (need Dematerialize to hide). **The Hedge has no
  Twilight.** In the mortal world or Arcadia they act at **half effective Wyrd** (traits, Numina, Influences reduced); in
  the mortal world they're auto-dematerialized and need **3 G to materialize for a scene** (only where an Influence
  sphere is present); depleted Glamour there = 1 lethal/turn (bashing in Arcadia). Other ephemeral entities entering the
  Hedge automatically materialize.
- **Common powers** (all): *Path Through the Thorns* (1 WP: pass the Thorns unscathed, know direction); *Reach* (1 G at a
  Hedge gate: use Influences and [R] Numina on targets within Wyrd yd beyond it for the scene; Wits + Composure senses
  it); *Sense Glamour* (within Wyrd × 5 yd); *To Market, To Market* (2 G: nearest active Goblin Market).
- **Numina** (Power + Finesse, instant, unless noted; more in core p. 136): Aggressive Meme (7 G; vs Resolve + Composure
  + Tolerance; spreads by retelling); **Blast** (range 10 yd × Power, no range penalty, lethal; +1 weapon rating per 2 G,
  max ½ Wyrd); **Clarity Drain** (2 G; Clarity attack with dice = ½ target's Wyrd ↑ instead of the roll); Dematerialize
  (3 G: invisible, incorporeal, can't touch anything incl. incorporeals); Dement (1 G; vs Int + Tolerance → Insane Tilt,
  scene); **Drain** (vs Sta + Res + Tolerance; winner takes Glamour or WP = successes from the loser); Emotional Aura (1 G,
  scene; anyone within 5 yd rolls Res + Com + Tolerance vs the activation successes: lose → −2 all actions); Entrap (2 G;
  vs Dex + Tolerance; one target within Finesse yd **Immobilized** with −4, Durability ½ Wyrd ↑ +1 per 2 more G); Failed
  Token (3 G + 1 WP vs Tolerance: target's tokens dead for the scene); Hallucination (1 G; vs Wits + Com + Tolerance; one
  sense per net success); Implant Mission (2 G: mortal gets Obsessed with a task); **Keeper's Calling** (2 G; vs Composure
  + Tolerance: she believes her Keeper is right there, scene); Rapture (2 G: Insensate; failed Res + Tolerance → binding
  mental Condition); Sign (1 G); Speed (2 G ×2, 4 G ×3 Speed, scene); Stalwart (Defense = Resistance); Stolen Masks (1 G;
  vs Presence + Tolerance: become its exact replica until next sunrise/sunset).
- Examples (no stats): the Lingering Dead, Hollow-haunts, the Phantom Hitchhiker of the Trods, the Ragdoll Ghost.

## D. Hobgoblins [pp. 252–262]

e.g. Murderous Virtue), ≥1 Aspiration, frailties incl. **cold iron** (handling painful, cold-iron weapons deal agg).

- Attributes/Skills distributed freely (no 5/4/3). **Health = Size + Stamina; Init = Dex + Com; Defense = min(Wits,
  Dex) + Athletics; WP = Res + Com.** Clash of Wills: **best Finesse Attribute + Wyrd**. Each dot of a multi-dot Dread
  Power counts as one power. **Dread Powers cost Glamour, not WP.** Contracts: **only Common Arcadian and Goblin**.
- **Can't harvest or reap Glamour** from humans: goblin fruit, deals (Wyrd delivers agreed Glamour), Bottle Glamour.
- In the Hedge: −1 success needed to win navigation. In the mortal world: **Deprived**; Wyrd 6+ must carry a piece of
  the Hedge and vanish back at the next sunrise/sunset absent other magic.
- **Hobgoblin deals** (one-off services or few-use items): **+1 Goblin Debt each**. Mortals can't shed Debt normally:
  at 10 they become hobgoblins (**Hedge Denizen**); paying down requires something dear (firstborn, a memory, a voice
  for a year and a day) or an impossible task.

  Bottle Glamour (store own Glamour: spend amount + 1; or 1 G + touch: steal Glamour = Wyrd into a vessel); Chameleon

  Miracle (on request from a living human only: remove/apply any Condition/Tilt, grant a trait at 5, kill a mortal, fulfil

## E. Huntsmen (the Wild Hunt) [pp. 262–266]
Original beings of Arcadia's wild fringes whose hearts the Gentry tore out and replaced with a **Fae Title's desire**
for a specific escaped changeling. Herald (hawk, hound, etc.) announces the hunt; they study prey for weeks. **Killing
one only delays**: while its **heart** sits hidden in some **mortal's Bastion**, it reforms in the Hedge within a month;
destroying the heart sends the Title back to the Keeper to make a new one. Changelings in iron or a Huntsman's snare
can't portal out. In the Hedge, closer to their hearts, they're more swayable: a changeling may find the Bastion with
the heart and swear an oath heart-to-heart (Huntsman regains own desires for a scene); returning the heart expunges the
Title (oath remains).
**Bargains vs Huntsmen** (sidebar): Spring — while Spring rules, it may do violence only to the changeling it hunts (not bystanders/other Lost); Summer — can't circumvent, so it traps;
Autumn — the herald's warning usually satisfies it; Winter — uses nonlethal methods.
**Build as hobgoblins**, except:
- Concept = carried **Fae Title + panoply** (2 signature items, one a weapon, **+1 per 3 Wyrd**) + one core Aspiration
  (only in the Hedge) + **two Title Aspirations** (one = the Fae's craving). Virtue = Keeper's expression, Vice = own
  suppressed desire (swap if heart restored).
- **No Contracts, no goblin deals**; may swear oaths if a changeling/Gentry initiates. Free Dread Powers: **Among the
  Sheep, Command the Herald, Heart of Iron, Hunter's Panoply**; plus core Dread Powers and those below.
- **No frailties**; stay in the mortal world indefinitely. **Reap** Glamour (can't harvest); goblin fruit. While bearing
  a Title: subtle oneiromancy shifts only; Hedgespin like changelings. Clash of Wills: **Resolve + Wyrd**. **+½ the
  Fae master's Wyrd ↑** to resist being forced/convinced against the Fae's wishes. **+changeling's Wyrd dice to track
  her when her Mask is down — always in the Hedge/Arcadia** (and auto-success within range when she drops it, 05 §6).
  Hedge navigation: −1 success needed (−2 in Thorns).
- **Huntsman Dread Powers** (only Among the Sheep, Command the Herald, Heart of Iron, Hunter's Panoply are free; the
  rest count against the hobgoblin-table Dread Power allowance; panoply extra items = Wyrd ÷ 3 rounded down [interp]): *Among the Sheep* (2 G: any humanoid form, not a specific person, Size 4–6; Title's tell
  shows subtly; panoply never changes); *Apex Predator* (1 G: swarm of small predators/birds or one larger animal obeys);
  *Command the Herald* (1 G/hour: remote-view & speak through a touched small creature; utterly loyal); *Heart of
  Iron* (no fear of cold iron; uses it in tools); *Hungry Heart* (1 WP on a panoply-weapon hit vs changeling/True Fae:
  steal Glamour = successes); *Hunter's Panoply* (**8-again** on actions with its tools; 1 G recall a lost tool (instant);
  missing tool → no 10-again; +1 WP on exceptional success with signature panoply); *Hunter's Senses* (**+4 and 9-again
  to perception vs the Lost; 8-again in Hedge/Arcadia**; if the changeling has a **bargain** with a human it must win a Clash
  of Wills to use this); *Inescapable Snare* (1 G: area ≤10 sq yd, grapple with own traits **+3 (+5 with a panoply
  tool)**; **counts as iron** — blocks portaling and fae magic); [interp] the cast is a grab per victim in the area (Dex + Athletics or
  Str + Brawl, + bonus, − Defense); the bonus applies to every later grapple contest with the snare; victims escape by
  winning a contest and choosing Break Free (or cutting it: Str + Athletics vs Durability 3); "blocks fae magic" = on the
  ensnared; *Kindred Spirits* (1 G: changeling's Needle, Thread,
  Aspirations, current & max Clarity; more G: latest Clarity damage circumstances & Condition, closest Touchstone's identity,
  older ones; using it against her → exceptional on 3); *Surprise Entrance* (1 G: appears in the scene; witnesses
  reflexive Resolve + Composure or **Shaken**; if barred/trapped it takes a full turn to break in, else it's there
  reflexively with one action before anyone reacts; Hollow owner may Clash of Wills); *Watchful Gaze* (1 G + eye contact
  (or via herald), Presence + Resolve vs Composure → **Paranoid**).

## F. True Fae (Gentry, the Others) [pp. 266–273]
A True Fae = a **Name** wrapped in **Titles** (roles/faces, 0–5) and pacts; only a Title's manifestation (a person, a
castle, a flock) ever appears. Killing a manifestation doesn't end it; only breaking the pacts that made a Title unravels
it (or another Fae devours it). Each Title has a **tell** (its theme always shows). Keepers may forget captives for years.
**Traits per Title** (build like a changeling):
- 3 Aspirations (one = **craving**, permanent; Keepers: one to (re)capture a changeling); fulfilling gives a scene WP.
- **Wyrd = 5 + number of Titles the Fae has (max 10)**. Full Glamour each scene in Arcadia; elsewhere recovers like
  changelings; **Glamour-addicted outside Arcadia/Hedge**: must gain ≥ Wyrd Glamour per day or **Deprived**; at 0 loses
  WP then Health 1/day. Frailties + iron bane.
- **Attributes: 5 × Wyrd dots; Skills: 5 × Wyrd dots**; no Specialties. Merits = 2 × Wyrd dots (Social Merits specify
  Arcadia/Hedge vs human world). Advantages as changelings, **no Clarity**. No kith/court/anchors; one seeming's
  blessing and trappings.
- **Contracts: every Contract in (Wyrd − 4) Regalia** (one matches its seeming).
- **In Arcadia and the Hedge**: may perform any oneiromantic/Hedgespinning act fitting its legend, treating others as
  important eidolons; **automatically succeeds unless the target spends 1 WP to get a chance to resist**. In the real
  world: Regalia + any form, no reality-shaping. Mask hides it imperfectly (tell shows).
- **Names**: a Fae Contract used on someone while using her **true name** → **Persistent Obsession** with that Fae. A
  changeling speaking a Fae's **true Name** while acting against any of its Titles → **any successful Contract targeting
  it is an exceptional success**. A heard substitute (sound, image) works if used with honest intent.
- **Pledges**: can seal anyone's statement (even fae) but must swear on a possession (captive, servant, token…) —
  forfeits it to the subject if she follows through. Personal/hostile **oaths sworn on itself**: if it breaks one, no
  Oathbreaker Condition but **permanently loses one Regalia** and is **vulnerable to lethal for that scene**; losing the
  last Regalia lets the other party kill the Title, extract three tasks/wishes, or **bind it into the Regalia's physical
  key as a token**. **Bargains** sworn on its true Name: failing = permanent destruction; the other party swears on
  something crucial (own name/life, a loved one, a memory, home), forfeit if she fails. Changelings breaking Fae oaths:
  Oathbreaker + disproportionate restitution. Can't be forced into pledges; one in mortal danger may offer one.
- **Vulnerability**: takes **bashing only from banes (incl. iron)**; **lethal only from banes, or from an attacker
  speaking its true Name, or in the scene after it breaks an oath**; **only cold-iron weapons deal aggravated**.
  Each Regalia it masters needs a **physical representation** (a Sword might be a hawk or a cliff); destroying it or
  tricking the Title into breaking the binding pact (learned from goblins, observation, clever pledges) strips power
  (e.g. Storm King of the Bloody Throne loses rule when someone pulls its Sword from the stone).
- Sample Gentry (no stats): **Grandmother, Grandmother** (forest cottage; takes troubled children; family-role seemings),
  **The Man with the Ergot Smile** (exiled; walks dreams to make 100 madmen dream as one), **The Three Androgynes**
  (UFO "grays"; starship laboratory), **The Year of Plague** (a recurring year-long epidemic; plague-doctor livery).
