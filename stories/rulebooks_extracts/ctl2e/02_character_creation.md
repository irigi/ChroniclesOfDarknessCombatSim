# CtL 2e — Character creation, free traits, XP, anchors

Source: Ch3 pp. 89–100 (L7352–8348). Seeming/kith/court details → `08_factions_map.md`.

## 1. Steps [pp. 89–93]
1. **Concept + 3 Aspirations** (≥1 changeling-life, ≥1 mortal-world; mix short/long term).
2. **Attributes 5/4/3** (after the free 1st dot in each). Max 5 at creation.
3. **Skills 11/7/4**. Max 5.
4. **3 Skill Specialties**.
5. **Lost template**:
   - **Seeming** (fixed for life) → **+1 dot free to one of its 3 favored Attributes** (Beast/Elemental: Resistance;
     Darkling/Wizened: Finesse; Fairest/Ogre: Power) — can't exceed 5. Gives **1st favored Regalia**.
   - **Kith** — optional ("if any"), any seeming can take any kith.
   - **Mien** description.
   - **Court** — optional; a courtier gets **Mantle • free** (Merit, p. 117). Court can change in play.
   - **Needle** (≈Virtue-replacement identity) and **Thread** (≈core drive). Archetypes §4.
   - **Touchstone**: write it beside Clarity box number **Composure + 1** (counting from left).
   - **Second favored Regalia**: player chooses any (Crown/Jewels/Mirror/Shield/Steed/Sword).
   - **Contracts**: **4 Contracts** chosen from *Common* Arcadian Regalia, *Common* Court, or Goblin Contracts — **≥2 of
     the 4 in favored Regalia**; Court Contracts need the requirements (p. 150: Mantle/Goodwill dots). **Plus 2 Royal
     Contracts** from court or favored Regalia. (Total 6 starting Contracts.) **A Royal *Court* Contract still needs
     Mantle 3 (or Goodwill 5)** [p. 150] — a starting courtier has only Mantle •, so either buy Mantle up to ••• with Merit
     dots or take both Royals from favored Regalia. Court Contracts have **no seeming benefits**.
   - **Wyrd 1 free**; +1 Wyrd for 5 Merit dots, +2 for 10 (max Wyrd 3 at creation).
   - **Frailties**: by Wyrd (05 §1): none at Wyrd 1; **1 minor at Wyrd 2–3** — so buying Wyrd with Merit dots adds a frailty.
     Buying Wyrd 3 is also a 3-die breaking point (reaching Wyrd 3).
6. **Merits: 10 dots** (minus dots spent on Wyrd). Any core-book Merit **except Supernatural Merits, Vice-Ridden,
   Virtuous**; plus CtL Merits (04).
7. **Advantages**: Size 5; Health = Size + Stamina; Speed = 5 + Str + Dex; **Willpower = Resolve + Composure**;
   **Max Clarity = Wits + Composure**; Initiative = Dex + Composure; Defense = min(Wits, Dex) + Athletics.
   Glamour: the book's text gives no figure. The official 2e character sheet reportedly prints "Starting Wyrd = 1 • Starting
   Glamour = 10" (external report, not in the .txt; unverified). Default: **a new character starts at full Glamour**; a scene
   starting mid-story uses recent history, or half max if unknown (99 §B).

## 3. Experienced Lost (bonus Experiences at start) [p. 93]
Established in the Freehold 5 · Rising Star 10 · Squire 15 · Knight 25 · Court Queen 35 · Faerie Legend 50 ·
Timeless Fae 100. (No age-based automatic Wyrd: Wyrd is bought, 5 XP/dot; reaching Wyrd 3/6/10 are breaking points.)

## 4. Advancement [p. 94]
**Beats** (5 Beats = 1 Experience): fulfil an Aspiration (replace at session end); resolve a Condition (once/scene);
Condition-specific Beats; turn a failure into dramatic failure; surrender in a fight; take damage in a rightmost Health
box; end of session; **any time you take Clarity damage**; **involuntarily unleash Bedlam** (once/scene, p. 110).
ST characters gain a scene-limited Willpower point instead of Beats. Group-Beat pooling optional.

| Experience costs [p. 94] | XP |
|---|---|
| Attribute | 4/dot |
| Skill | 2/dot |
| Specialty | 1 |
| Merit | 1/dot |
| Common Regalia/Court Contract | 3 (favored Regalia: **2**) |
| Royal Regalia/Court Contract | 4 (favored Regalia: **3**) |
| Goblin Contract | 2 (+ Goblin Debt, see 03) |

| Wyrd | 5/dot |

Learning: favored-Regalia and Court Contracts need **no teacher**; all others need a teacher (someone or something).
Raising Wyrd in play is also narrative (remembered dreams justify purchases) [p. 100].

## 5. Anchors: Needle, Thread, Touchstones [pp. 95–99]
Each anchor has a **minor condition (regain 1 WP)** and a **harsh condition (regain all WP)**; full refresh from an
anchor **once per chapter**. Not supernatural traits.
- **Needle** — how she acts. 1 WP: overcome a small hurdle to affirm it; all WP: existentially risky/terrible act
  affirming it. Archetypes (1 WP / all WP): Bon Vivant (leave a personal problem unfixed to enjoy the moment / abandon
  an important relationship for pleasure); Chess Master (manipulate someone / harm others while manipulating);
  Commander (convince someone to drop their goals for yours / work against people you care about for the many);
  Composer (eschew standards of beauty / embrace something vile for its beauty); Counselor (put your goals on hold
  to help / take responsibility for another's dangerous mistake); Daredevil (risk endangering others / endanger a
  loved one); Dynamo (solve before all info / jump to conclusions into danger); Protector (ignore own needs to
  protect / miss last chance at an important goal to protect); Provider (give up something you care about on request /
  risk self or loved one to provide); Scholar (handle the unknown / travel Hedge or unearthly realm to categorize);
  Storyteller (story that makes someone look bad / compromising story about someone important who suffers);
  Teacher (advice someone uses to succeed / risk yourself to teach); Traditionalist (refuse new ideas / refuse help in
  danger); Visionary (refuse same method twice / endanger self with new method).
- **Thread** — what holds her together. 1 WP: withdraw, put herself at a disadvantage, or lash out in pursuit of it;
  all WP: terrible acts or **risking Clarity damage** for it. Archetypes: Acceptance, Anger, Family, Friendship,
  Hate, Honor, Joy, Love, Memory, Revenge (each with 1/all WP examples, p. 97–98).
- **Touchstones** [p. 98]: person/place/(ideal), usually mortal. First at Clarity box **Composure + 1**; extra ones
  (Touchstone Merit, p. 120) go to the right of the rightmost existing one; max = boxes right of the first.
  Raising Composure pushes them right. **Attached** = its box not filled with severe damage.
  - Clarity **damage rolls**: **−1 die with one attached, −2 with multiple attached, +2 dice with none attached.**
  - Defending an attached Touchstone: +1 WP; if it causes serious harm, all WP.
  - Healing severe damage in a box re-attaches its Touchstone.
  - Losing a Touchstone (death etc.) = breaking point (2 dice); losing the **last** → **Delusional** immediately.
    Replacement requires resolving Delusional + forging a new meaningful bond. Merit Touchstones obey Sanctity of Merits.
  - Meaningful scene with a Touchstone heals Clarity (see 05 §3).
