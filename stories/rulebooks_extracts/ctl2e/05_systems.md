# CtL 2e — Systems

Ch3 "Wonders and Terrors" pp. 100–110 (L8348–9470); Ch4 core-rule deltas, Hedge, goblin fruit, pledges,
oneiromancy, tokens pp. 163–232 (appended below). Core combat/social engine → `../cod_core/05_systems.md`.

## 1. Wyrd [pp. 100–102]
Wyrd = changeling **Supernatural Potency and Tolerance**. If a CtL rule calls for Wyrd from a creature lacking it, use
its Tolerance (resisting) or Potency (forcing); a changeling uses Wyrd wherever another rule asks for Potency/Tolerance.
- Each Wyrd dot = **+1 die to any fae creature's rolls to track/find her (max +5)**.
- Adds **Wyrd dice to rolls to remember/interpret her own dreams** while awake (not oneiromancy successes).
  Losing last Touchstone: treat as +1 Wyrd for this.
- Lifespan +~20 years per Wyrd dot; beyond a human lifetime mien shows through the Mask more.
- Reaching **Wyrd 3** = 3-die breaking point, **Wyrd 6** = 4-die, **Wyrd 10** = 5-die (see §3 list).

disease outside action scenes (Sick Tilt).

## 2. Frailties & cold iron [pp. 101–102]
Two types: **taboo** (compulsion/restriction) and **bane** (harmful thing). Banes **ignore all armor and protective
magic**; bane damage **can't heal while in the bane's presence**. Acting **against the source** of a frailty: **1 WP per
action (adds no dice)** and **−3 (minor) / −5 (major)** to related rolls.
- **Minor**: rare circumstances. Taboo: 1 WP resists it for the scene. Bane: **1 lethal/turn** of exposure; bane
  weapon = lethal that can't heal until away from the bane.
- **Major**: daily, common. Taboo: 1 WP per turn, or sacrifice 1 WP dot for the scene. Bane: **1 aggravated/turn**;
  bane weapons do aggravated. Death by bane → body unravels into thorns/leaves/wings.
- **Iron** (all fae): fae-magic armor/Defense bonuses (Contracts, tokens) **don't apply vs iron weapons**; **no portaling
  out of iron bindings/enclosures**; no fae magic can create/manipulate/destroy iron (Elementals can't have iron).
  "Iron" = what an average person calls iron (gate yes, steel sword no).
- **Cold iron** = mostly pure, hand-forged, no magic, not cast/machine-made: **touching it inflicts aggravated damage**
  on changelings & Gentry (a bane). [interp: rate 1 agg per turn of contact / weapon damage becomes aggravated — RAW
  just says "aggravated damage"; see 99.]

## 3. Glamour [pp. 102–103]
Spend Glamour to: activate a Contract without a Loophole; oneiromancy/Hedgespinning shifts; create/open a Hedge
gateway; strengthen or scour the Mask; enact pledges; kith/seeming blessings (some); see another changeling's Mask;
escape confinement; incite Bedlam. Max per turn and pool by Wyrd (§1).
- **Harvest**: only from non-fae beings with emotions (humans, ghosts, vampires…; **not** changelings, hobgoblins,
  True Fae, **animals**). Must be close enough to feel the emotion firsthand. **Roll a relevant Attribute + Skill;
  each success = 1 Glamour**. Target loses **1 Willpower**; each target harvested max **once per scene**.
  **Court's emotion → +1 Glamour on a success.** Also from dreams (oneiromancy p. 219), goblin fruit, Hedge pools/beasts.
  Modifiers: repetitive emotion −3; rushed −2; repressed −2; momentary −1; new emotion +2; strong long-held +2; much time
  with the subject +2; powerful deep-seated +3.
- **Reaping**: no roll, must **touch**; **breaking point** (3 dice); victim loses **1 WP per Wyrd dot** and gains
  **Ravaged**; changeling **refills Glamour to max**. Hunters/Huntsmen can detect cause with Wits + Occult.
- **Deprivation**: at **0 Glamour** (or **< Wyrd at Wyrd 6+**) she starves (no nourishment from food) and has
  **Deprived**. At Wyrd 6+, harvesting/reaping every day for a week → **Glamour Addicted**.

## 4. Clarity [pp. 103–107]
Track of **Wits + Composure** boxes (can't buy directly; rises with Wits/Composure and recovered **Icons**; no upper
limit). Damage types: **mild** (mark "/", in leftmost empty box) and **severe** ("X", in leftmost empty *or* leftmost
mild box). In dreams Clarity contributes to the dream form's Health (p. 216).

**Clarity attack procedure (breaking point)**: roll the breaking point's dice (base severity ± modifiers).
- Success → roll **Wyrd as a dice pool**; successes = **mild** damage (extra attack successes don't add). Damage in
  any of the **3 rightmost boxes → a Clarity Condition**.
- Exceptional success (5+) → the Wyrd-roll damage is **severe**; in 3 rightmost boxes → **Persistent** Clarity Condition.
- Failure → no damage. Dramatic failure → no damage, **+1 Willpower**.
- Damage roll (Wyrd dice) modified by Touchstones (−1 one attached / −2 several / **+2 none**), some Conditions, powers.
- **Taking Clarity damage = a Beat.**
Other effects (dream-form damage, some Contracts) attack Clarity without being breaking points.
Seeming Curse breaking points use pool **½ Wyrd (round up)**.

Attack modifiers (stack): strongly tied to durance or surrender to a True Fae +3; forced to act against her will +3;
strong emotional investment +2; affects close friend/family +2; coerced +1; some investment +1; not personally present
−1; aligns with Needle or Thread −1; **spent Willpower on the action −1**; no real investment −1; accidental −2; only a
complete stranger −2; rejection of or triumph over a True Fae −3.

**Sample breaking points (base dice)** [p. 105–106]:
- **1**: told experiences are unreal by a convincing stranger; changing someone via Dream Infiltrator; spending all
  Glamour in one day; one day without human/changeling contact; breaking a mundane promise; meeting your fetch first time.
- **2**: told unreal by an authority figure; Dream Intruder change; mortal shuns/disparages you; **losing a Touchstone**;
  eating only goblin fruit for a day; **a week without contact**; someone breaks a mundane promise to you; discovering a
  minor lie to you; gaining a non-Clarity Condition that confuses/jars your senses (Lost, Spooked)*; someone else
  tampering with your dreams*; actively searching out durance memories / taking psychotropic drugs (2 or 4)*.
- **3**: told unreal by someone you trust; victim of a **non-fae supernatural power** that confuses senses/reenacts what
  your Keeper did; torturing*; **using Glamour to force someone to change behavior***; brainwashing via repeated dream
  manipulation*; two weeks without contact; killing someone else's fetch; **reaching Wyrd 3**; someone breaks a formal
  oath/pledge to you.
- **4**: presented with "evidence" experiences are unreal; accidentally killing a human; **breaking formal oaths/
  pledges**; Dream Assailant change; discovering an important lie; death of a family member; killing another
  changeling; killing your own fetch; a month without contact; kidnapping/keeping someone captive*; reenacting/reliving a
  durance memory*; **reaching Wyrd 6**.
- **5**: "deprogramming"/extended torment to convince you it's unreal; premeditated killing of a human; a year+ without
  contact; spending time in Arcadia; prolonged/intimate contact with a True Fae; killing your Touchstone; **reaping
  Glamour**; **reaching Wyrd 10**.
- \* The sidebar's columns are interleaved in the .txt (L9017–9112). Reconstruction rule: consecutive bullets belong to one
  column, while blank-separated single bullets alternate between two columns, anchored by escalation (contact: day 1 /
  week 2 / two weeks 3 / month 4 / year 5; Wyrd 3/6/10 → 3/4/5). Starred items are the least certain. The block "torturing /
  Glamour-forcing / brainwashing" sits under the Three Dice header, but an external review read Glamour-forcing as **5** —
  if a scene hinges on it, the ST picks 3 or 5 and says so.
- **Powers as breaking points**: Bedlam, Spring Contracts, oneiromancy that **force a desire on someone** in a way they
  wouldn't agree to = breaking point, severity by ST.

**Perception**: undamaged Clarity → **+2 dice to perception rolls**. **−1 per 2 points below Clarity 5**: −1 at current
Clarity 3–4, −2 at 1–2 (applies even if max < 5; e.g. max 4 undamaged = +2−1 = +1). Clarity ≤3: dramatic failure on
perception → hallucination. ["Current Clarity" = max − damaged boxes, [interp].]

**Full track**: more **mild** after full → **Comatose** (no more Clarity damage until 1 healed). **Severe** after full →
Comatose becomes **Persistent**, risk of death, resolvable only with friends' help.

**Healing**: each scene of meaningful interaction with a Touchstone (attached or not) heals **1 severe or all mild**
(doesn't resolve Conditions). No Touchstones at all → must gain one first. Resolving a Clarity Condition heals 1;
resolving a Persistent Clarity Condition heals 2. (Icons also restore — see Hedge §.)
Clarity Conditions: Broken (P), Comatose, Confused, Delusional (P), Dissociation, Distracted, Fugue (P), Numb (P),
Shaken, Sleepwalking (P), Spooked, Withdrawn — ST picks one germane to the cause.
"Meaningful contact" = personal face-to-face interaction (not paying a cashier; flirting with the barista counts).

## 5. Kenning [p. 106]
Usable while Clarity damage is in **fewer than half** her boxes: spend **1 WP**; ST secretly rolls **current Clarity**.
Success: each success reveals presence of one supernatural creature/item/active effect (not its kind; no Clash of
Wills vs active concealment — needs Contracts). If nothing present, WP refunded. Exceptional: general nature (vampire
using a Discipline; spell vs Contract) or that something is concealed nearby. Dramatic failure: disastrous misread.

## 6. Mask & mien [pp. 107–108]
Mask hides fae beings and **fae objects** (not mundane gear) from mortal senses and technology, even after death
(mundane corpse). Fae beings and supernatural creatures with mystical senses see the mien. Blessing *effects* still work
(explained away). Mien grows more pronounced with Wyrd; Mantle flavors it.
- **See a changeling's Mask** instead of mien: 1 Glamour, a few seconds.
- **Strengthen Mask**: 1 Glamour, **reflexive**: rest of scene, Mask hides her from fae/supernatural eyes; sensory magic
  → Clash of Wills. Her shadow still shows the true form to fae.
- **Scour (drop) the Mask**: 1 Glamour, **instant**; whole scene all observers see her mien; reinstated at scene end or
  with another Glamour (instant). While down: **every successful rolled Contract counts as an exceptional success**;
  where successes matter use the highest of rolled successes, Wyrd, or Mantle; un-rolled Contracts let her **remove one
  Door** vs one witness for her next Social maneuvering roll (no stacking). Mortals who see → **Spooked**.
  Cost: **opens every Hedge gateway within 10 yd/m × Wyrd**, leaves a trail; **Gentry and Huntsmen auto-succeed on
  tracking her within that range** while down. (Dull Beacon Merit reduces effective Wyrd for this.)

## 7. Portaling [p. 108–109]
- **Escape confinement**: 1 Glamour, **reflexive** → automatically free of grapple, manacles, cell, etc. Supernatural
  bonds → **Clash of Wills** vs creator; may retry freely (exception to successive-attempt rule). **Not vs iron.**
  Doesn't remove mundane consequences (police record etc.).
- **Open a door to the Hedge**: a closeable portal she fits through (door, window, manhole); 1 Glamour + a spoken
  request ("open sesame" to a sonnet) → opens at once to a place in the Hedge she doesn't choose (each door always
  leads to the same Hedge spot). Reverse from the Hedge works the same way.

## 8. Bedlam [pp. 109–110]
**Incite Bedlam**: Cost **1 Glamour + 1 WP**; **Manipulation + Wyrd vs Composure + Wyrd** (Tolerance), each target
contests separately, resistance reflexive. Affects **everyone within sensory range who can perceive her** (cameras
convey it), friend and foe, can't exclude anyone.
- Success: targets who fail gain one chosen Condition fitting the emotion (Seasonal: **Wanton** desire, **Competitive**
  wrath, **Frightened** fear, **Lethargic** sorrow). Exceptional: pick a second Condition. Failure: nothing.
  Dramatic failure: crowd feels antipathy toward her.
- Changeling mods: own court's emotion **+3**; allied court (Spring/Summer, Autumn/Winter) +1; **+1 per extra Glamour (max
  +5)**; minor-opposition court (Summer/Autumn, Spring/Winter) −1; major opposition (Summer/Winter, Autumn/Spring) −3.
  Target mods: feeling strongly opposed emotion +2; calm +1; already feeling similar −1.
- **Involuntary Bedlam**: once/scene player may trigger it for a **Beat**, no cost, ST picks emotion.
- Forcing desire on someone = breaking point (§4).

## 9. Core-engine deltas in CtL Ch4 [pp. 169–197]
Ch4 reprints the core engine (Attributes/Skills, actions, Conditions/Tilts, Down & Dirty, combat, weapons/armor,
injury, Social maneuvering, investigation, chases, Build Equipment) — use `../cod_core/05_systems.md`. CtL-specific:
- **9-again on a roll that already has 9-again → 8-again; 8-again twice → may become rote** (ST) [p. 176].

- Mortals have Virtue/Vice; **changelings don't** (Needle/Thread instead).

## 10. The Hedge [pp. 198–207]
**Entering**: changelings open any portal with 1 Glamour (05 §7). The gate stays open **Wyrd turns**, then is a
**dormant Hedgeway until the season changes** (other Lost then open it **without Glamour**); dormant gates are Masked,
found by kenning. **Humans** can't open gates by touch; they (a) **follow** through an open gate, (b) use a gate's
**Key** (belief-born legend: Resolve + Composure; or object/song/circumstance found by research), or (c) are lured when
indulging their **Vice near a Hedgeway**: reflexive **Wits + Composure** to resist (may waive). Success: resists.
Exc: glimpse of the doom avoided. Failure: gate opens with a Vice-temptation; entering grants an extra WP (beyond
1/scene) and a Beat; gate slams shut behind. DF: pulled in immediately. Mods: +1/previous visit (max +3); WP ≥8 +1;
WP ≤3 −1; Occult 2+ or supernatural Merit −1; Virtue supports entering −1; Aspiration supports −2; lost
friends/family mysteriously −2. Beings without Vices can be called by other weaknesses (ST).
- **No Mask in the Hedge** for fae beings/objects. **Clarity attacks −1 die in the Hedge** unless the Hedge itself caused it.
- Mundane objects work but whimsically (flashlight → lantern…).

**Navigation = a chase** (core chase rules, p. 195) whenever characters have a goal; the **Hedge is always an
opponent** (lures them into the Thorns / toward Arcadia). New goal → new chase.
- Hedge: **Speed 10** (15 in Thorns; on a trod 10 − trod rating). Initiative mod = **10 − highest current Clarity**
  among present characters (min 0; 10 if no changelings). **Base pool 5** for all rolls (incl. the Edge), modified:
  on a trod −rating; per character with a positive mental/emotional Condition −1; each new turn +1 (cumulative);
  per character with a negative mental/emotional Condition +1 (both cancel per character); any character's current
  Clarity below half its max **+1** (once, not per character; the .txt row reads "current Clarity +1"); pressing time limit +2; Hedge has the Edge +2; any changeling
  incited Bedlam this scene +2 (exceptional +3); in the Thorns +3.
- Characters' navigation pool: RAW silent — [interp] **Wits + Survival** (Occult/Streetwise if fitting; teamwork allowed).
  Pursuers (Huntsmen, hobgoblins) run their own chase against the party under core chase rules, with Hedge modifiers to
  their own target. Hedge's own target: [interp] 8. The ST's 8-die Hedgespinning roll: [interp] once per chase turn.
- Characters need **8 successes** (not 5), modified: −1 per Wyrd dot (applies to any character incl. Huntsmen/hobgoblins); −1 per Goblin Debt accepted for a hobgoblin's
  help (max −3); Huntsman −1 (−2 in Thorns); current Clarity 3/2/1 → +1/+2/+3; on a trod +1 per milestone desired; time
  limit +1/+2/+3 by urgency (plus normal chase-table modifiers).
- Success = goal reached. Failure without opposition = the Hedge puts them where it wants; **anyone who doesn't
  succeed gains Lost**. Failure vs opposition = confrontation.
- **Trods**: rating 1 (<1 mile) · 2 (1–10 mi) · 3 (10–100) · 4 (100–1,000) · 5 (anywhere in the world); ±1–2 for
  upkeep, −3 abandoned; pass through **milestones = rating**. On a trod, **no chase needed** to reach milestones/goal
  unless opposed or time-limited.
- **The Thorns**: most dangerous parts; negative mental/emotional Conditions manifest as scenery and usually cause
  breaking points; dramatic failures there → Lost, Madness, Insensate, Insane, a door to Arcadia, etc.
- **Icons**: soul-pieces torn off in the Thorns during the escape; every changeling has ≥1 out there. **Recovering an
  Icon: refills Willpower and +1 maximum Clarity permanently** (+ memories). A fae holding someone's Icon can spend 1 G
  to view one of her memories; winning a Clash of Wills vs her (even absent) → **Informed** about her. Returning an
  Icon earns Merits (Allies, Mantle, Goodwill).

**Hedgespinning** [pp. 204–207]: any fae can do subtle shifts; only changelings, Gentry and Huntsmen do paradigm
shifts.
- The **Hedge itself**: whenever a character navigates/investigates/deals with denizens/interacts, ST rolls **8 dice**
  (no 10-again on a trod, **9-again** elsewhere, **8-again in the Thorns**); successes beyond 1 buy shifts reacting to the
  characters. A Hedge-made paradigm shift gives a Beat to each adversely affected player.
- **Subtle shift**: declare before a mundane action; penalty **−3 on a trod, −2 in Thorns, −1 elsewhere**; successes in
  excess of what the action needs → pay **1 Glamour** to spend them on shifts (each rolled success used once — e.g.
  attack successes split between damage and shaping; weapon bonus successes can't be used). Extended actions: apply
  per roll, no accumulation. **DF on a designated action incites Bedlam in everyone present.** Hedge Brawler, Dream-
  style Merits interact.
  Costs: 1+ equipment bonus = successes (max +5) / armor = successes 1 turn (max 5) / weapons of total Availability =
  successes / ±Durability for 1 turn (max 5); 1: cosmetic change; −1 Speed or Initiative to an opponent for the scene
  (cumulative); direction to a *type* of place; minor twist of luck/complication. 2: personal Tilt; hazard. 3: direction
  to a specific unconcealed place; minor location change; new terrain feature. 4: temporary Condition (fades on leaving
  the Hedge) from a present feature; turn a failure into a Beat-less dramatic failure; auto-Edge next turn; change one
  physical property (Size ±1, brick→gingerbread).
- **Paradigm shift**: on an **exceptional success** on a designated action, pay **2 Glamour** to spend **all** successes
  (forfeit the exceptional-success Condition). Costs: 2+ bank (spent−1, max 5) for a later shift this scene; 3 transform
  an object/feature; major twist; 5 create a goblin fruit / Environmental Tilt / force a Clash of Wills on others'
  Hedgespinning; 6 direction to a concealed/protected place (Clash if magic protects it); 7 Persistent Condition (fades
  on leaving) / completely change scenery; 8 ignore one attack or a Tilt for one turn / one impossible environmental
  action; 9 temporary Hollow enhancement or Fae Mount ability (buyable with XP) / shift area radius Wyrd × 10 yd one step
  trod↔Hedge↔Thorns / transfer an emotional Condition to another. Thread-aligned shift: +1 success to next
  Hedgespinning this scene (once/scene). **End of a scene with ≥1 paradigm shift: gain an emotional Condition** matching
  the change (may prompt a breaking point, player's call); draws local attention. Contracts/tokens that already do the
  change need no successes. Kenning + Empathy dice reads paradigm shifts from the last week.
- Places: Deep Caves (p. 257), Dreaming Roads, Gloomwood (Goblin Market each solstice/equinox; la Duquesa de las
  Arañas), the Sleeping Badger inn.

## 11. Goblin fruit & oddments [pp. 207–209]
Most fruit: **+1 Glamour** when eaten. Carry limit outside the Hedge by Wyrd: 1 → 3; 2–3 → 7; 4–6 → 13; 7–8 → 29;
9 → 101; 10 → unlimited (excess rots oldest first). Eating only goblin fruit for a day = 2-die breaking point.
- **Amaranthine**: heals 1 aggravated. **Faerie Peach**: Gentry-like majesty; Thorns count as Hedge and Hedge as
  trod; all fae nearby sense her; lasts until she leaves the Hedge or a day and a night. **Liar's Apple**: can't tell the
  truth until the sunrise after next (immune to interrogation). **Odinroot**: +1 WP as well, but 1-die Clarity attack.
  **Ogre Peppers**: until next midnight, pursuing a believed goal, **auto-succeed resisting fear**, −2 on self-control
  rolls. **Vines of Bacchus**: taste foul to anyone wishing harm on those around them (loyalist test); smoked → prophetic
  haze.
- Oddments: **Cogleaf** (universal small hidden replacement part; repaired device ignored by beings without
  Supernatural Tolerance); **Jennystones** (stench: −1 to all non-reflexive actions nearby; swallow + Stamina roll →
  1 Glamour); **Lancebeet** (thrown, Dmg 2, Init −2, Str 2, Size 2, **+3 dice** to the attack, withers after hitting).

## 12. Pledges [pp. 209–215]
Three kinds: **sealing** (bind someone to a statement of intent), **oath** (between fae: changelings, Huntsmen, True
Fae), **bargain** (changeling ↔ mortal/non-fae). No magic can force anyone into an oath (coercion/blackmail can).
**Sealing** [pp. 210–212]:
- Must witness the statement of intent live (speech or watching it be written; not recordings/feeds). It needn't be
  sincere or achievable. **Spend 1 Glamour.** A subject with Glamour may **undo it with 1 Glamour** (both know).
  Changelings/Wyrd-creatures are thus mostly immune; letting your words be sealed = **1 Beat (max 1/chapter)**.
- Subject who fulfils it without wriggling: **+1 Beat**. Sealer may release it any time.
- **Broken** when the subject tries and fails or becomes unable (refusing forever ≠ broken while still possible;
  contradictory sealed statements = catch-22, ST may escalate to oath-level). Penalty chosen at sealing or breaking:
  −1 WP; 1 bashing; −1 all rolls for a scene; −2 one Skill for a scene; −3 one roll; minor supernatural effect for a
  scene. **Strengthened** (1 G + 1 WP): no WP regain for a day; 1 lethal; 3 bashing; can't spend WP for a scene; −2 all
  rolls for a scene; −3 one Skill for a scene; −5 one roll; or a **pre-rolled Contract** triggered on breaking.
- vs **Huntsman**: also reflexive **Presence + Wyrd vs Resolve + Wyrd**; success = strengthened-level penalties but she
  gains **Paranoid** (exc: no Paranoid); DF: marked, Paranoid. vs **True Fae**: auto-noticed and refuted free.
- Seal threat can count as leverage in Social maneuvering. Abuse (Beat farming) draws Huntsmen/harsher penalties.

**Oaths** [pp. 212–214]: all participants speak or write it, **each spends 1 Glamour**, plus a physical gesture
(blood, kiss, handclasp). Open or closed. Permanent — "breaking" changes the relationship, never erases it.
**Every oath violated → Persistent Oathbreaker Condition**; breaking formal oaths = 4-die breaking point; unspecified
consequences let the Wyrd punish freely. Categories:
- **Societal** (court, freehold, motley): court → **Mantle • free** + court's restrictions; freehold → **+1 to Hedge
  navigation** where the freehold holds it; motley → pick one: (a) once/chapter a Contract roll as **teamwork** (all
  know it); (b) meditate a turn and **pool & evenly redistribute Glamour** (remainder lost); (c) once/chapter, when
  representing the motley, use the **highest Social Skill** in the group and any member's Merit or kith blessing
  (consequences fall on all). Breaking: lose all benefits + e.g. −3 Contract rolls in a forsworn leader's lands, Sick
  Tilt during the betrayed court's season, Notoriety.
- **Personal** (two changelings: lovers, friends, protectors): Beat when suffering a setback upholding it (1/chapter);
  significant expression of the emotion → **regain all WP** (as Needle; 1/chapter). Pick one: once/chapter take an
  injury for the other (no armor/magic reduction); take a Condition or Tilt meant for the other at the moment it's
  levied (not Clarity Conditions; at any distance); once/chapter **swap Glamour or WP pools**; **always able to find**
  each other. Breaking: share ex-partner's wound penalties (max −3), −3 Initiative vs ex-motley, breaking point.
  Personal oaths with Huntsmen are possible (heart restoration, p. 264).
- **Hostile** (sworn enmity, duels): Beat when the enemy wounds/inconveniences you (1/chapter); +1 WP in any scene you
  wound/inconvenience him. Must stay enemies or be oathbreaker. Common with Huntsmen.
- True Fae oaths: see 06 (p. 270).

**Bargains** [pp. 214–215]: changeling ↔ mortal (not with fae; other supernaturals possible with odd side effects).
She must appear **without her Mask** and propose terms she can actually fulfil, usually an ongoing service; she gains
**Obliged**. Benefit: camouflage — **Huntsmen and Gentry perceive her as a natural part of the landscape** (not
foolproof) while the bargain is ongoing. Breaking: lose the protection, maybe Notoriety. A mortal who reneges is
punished by the Wyrd (usually stumbles into the Hedge). The mortal's payment can be a bank of favors ≈ a 1–3-dot
Merit once per story.

## 13. Oneiromancy [pp. 215–222]
**Gate of Ivory** (mind): sleeping changeling rolls **Resolve + Composure** (can roll even once asleep; not if
Comatose) → lucid in her **own Bastion** in **dream form** (no Mask, mien shown; body unconscious). Leaving her Bastion
to roam = **no WP regained** that night. Bring sleeping guests: physical contact + **1 Glamour each** (fae may resist with
1 Glamour, others 1 WP, supernaturals Clash of Wills). Dreamsteps Contract enters another's Bastion directly.
**Dream form traits**: **Power** (= Presence for changelings / Intelligence others; for Int, Str, Pre tasks), **Finesse**
(= Manipulation / Wits; Wits, Dex, Man), **Resistance** (= Composure / Resolve; Res, Sta, Com). **Dream Health** =
current Clarity + Wyrd-based Attribute max (5 at Wyrd 1–5) for changelings; others Resistance + Attribute max. Wound
penalties apply. "Killed" in own Bastion → wakes; elsewhere → wakes with **Lethargic**; killed by dreamweaving/fae
magic in any Bastion → **Soul Shocked**. **Damage in one attack above her Attribute max spills to Clarity.**
