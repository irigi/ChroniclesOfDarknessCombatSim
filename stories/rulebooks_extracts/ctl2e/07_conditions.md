# CtL 2e — Conditions & Tilts

Source App. 3 Tilts pp. 327–332 (L28813–29315), App. 4 Conditions pp. 333–346 (L29316–30352).
Format: effect · **+N Clarity** = changelings with it **add N dice to Clarity damage rolls** · sources · resolution · Beat.
(P) = Persistent. Generic mechanics of Conditions/Tilts → `../cod_core/07_conditions.md`.

## A. Tilts
CtL reprints the core Tilts (Arm Wrack, Beaten Down, Blinded, Deafened, Drugged, Immobilized, Insane, Insensate,
Knocked Down, Leg Wrack, Poisoned (moderate/grave), Sick (moderate/grave), Stunned; Environmental: Blizzard,
Earthquake, Extreme Cold, Extreme Heat, Flooded, Heavy Rain, Heavy Winds, Ice, Inferno) — use core. New:
- **Flesh Too Solid** (Personal): whenever she'd take **Dream Health** damage she takes **physical Health** damage
  instead. Cause: dramatic failure on oneiromantic actions/Contracts (e.g. Dreamsteps DF). Ends at scene end.
Notes: Bedlam/Contracts that inflict Tilts end them when the Contract ends (03 §0).

## B. Conditions
- **Addicted (P)**: needs regular fix; without → Deprived. Res: gain/lose Integrity, exceptional on a breaking point; for
  changelings raise max Clarity or exceptional success contesting a fae power. Beat: fix over obligation.
- **Amnesia (P)**: lost period of memory. **+2 Clarity**. Res: regain memory (maybe a breaking point). Beat: forgotten
  trouble surfaces.
- **Arcadian Dreams (P)** (fae-touched): aware of promise-bound in Arcadia; in the Hedge knows direction, +1 to navigate
  toward him; feels his pain. Beat: choose to fail a roll. Res: reunion, or his death (breaking point).
- **Awestruck (P)**: kneels/grovels before a glorious terrifying figure: −2 attacks vs source, −3 contesting its Social
  actions, −3 to Composure/Resolve vs its powers. **+2 Clarity**. Sources: compulsion, oneiromancy. Res: no contact with
  source (incl. phone, Bastion) for a full scene. Beat: act to serve the source's demands.
- **Berserk**: each turn Resolve + Composure or attack nearest target; even on success −3 to anything else. **+1
  Clarity**. Res: unconscious or no targets left. (Red Revenge, Sunburnt Heart, Blood Pennon.)
- **Bestial**: −2 to resist physical impulse, −2 Defense; rolls to compel impulsive aggression/flight exceptional on 3.
  Res: damage someone's last three Health boxes.
- **Blind (P)**: sight rolls chance die (−3 if another sense substitutes); Blinded Tilt in action. Res: sight back.
- **Bonded**: animal bond (+2 to influence it once/scene; it adds your Animal Ken to resist fear/coercion and to one
  roll). Res: animal dies.
- **Broken (P)**: −2 Social & Resolve rolls, −5 Intimidation. Source: **severe Clarity damage in 3 rightmost boxes**.
  Res: (changelings) raise max Clarity, or exceptional success contesting a fae power; (mortals) gain/lose Integrity or
  exceptional on a breaking point. Beat: back down / fail due to it.
- **Comatose**: lost in a dream believing she's awake; can't roll the Gate of Ivory unaided. From mild-damage overflow:
  resolving another Clarity Condition in-dream can wake her; from severe: only someone convincing her she dreams
  (oneiromancy etc.). Source: Clarity track full (05 §4). Res: regain Clarity / realize and wake (+1 Clarity).
- **Competitive**: in direct competition −2 on rolls without WP; tempting her to compete exceptional on 3. Source:
  Bedlam (wrath). Res: win/lose a competition where someone reaches a breaking point.
- **Confused**: −2 Intelligence & Wits rolls. Sources: head blow, DF, mild Clarity damage in rightmost 3. Res: half an
  hour to clear mind, or take any lethal.
- **Connected (P)**: +2 with a group; or shed for one auto-exceptional success with them (burns bridges).
- **Cowed**: −2 Physical & Social rolls opposing the inflicter unless spending WP. **+1 Clarity**. Res: injure/intimidate
  them, or regain WP via Needle.
- **Delusional (P)**: holds a false belief; 1 WP rationalizes an exception. Sources: **losing last Touchstone**, severe
  Clarity damage in rightmost 3. Res: disprove it or **gain a new Touchstone**. Beat: cling to it despite evidence.
- **Demoralized**: WP adds only +1 die on attacks; −4 Initiative; −2 Resolve/Composure when resisting/contesting; fades
  after a week. **+1 Clarity**. Res: exceptional attack, win a fight, or survive one unharmed.
- **Deprived**: −1 die on Stamina, Resolve, Composure pools (not derived traits). Sources: unfed addiction, **0 Glamour**.
  Res: indulge / gain any Glamour.
- **Disabled (P)**: Speed 1 (wheelchair rules).
- **Disoriented**: −2 Physical actions, no ranged attacks (Defense normal). **+1 Clarity**. Res: landmark or friend;
  if from a power, when it ends.
- **Dissociation**: feels unreal. Source: mild Clarity damage in rightmost 3, DF. Res: player chooses to **fail a roll
  that breaks with the mundane** (e.g. a Contract roll) to affirm reality.
- **Distracted**: no extended actions; −2 perception/concentration/precision. Res: leave environment; if from Clarity
  damage, regain all WP.
- **Dream Infiltrator / Dream Intruder / Dream Assailant** (Shift Conditions, from paradigm shifts in another's dream):
  - *Infiltrator*: eidolons −2 peaceful / −3 unnoticed; impressions −1; subtle shifts +1 success cost.
  - *Intruder*: −3 / −4; failures involving non-fought eidolons are dramatic; impressions −2; all shifts +1 cost.
  - *Assailant*: −5 peaceful/unnoticed; such failures dramatic; impressions Hostile; paradigm +2 cost, **no subtle
    shifts**; another shift risks waking dreamer (05 §13).
  - Resolve by (a) leaving the dream (Infiltrator: until he sleeps again; Intruder: twice; Assailant: a week), (b)
    reintegrating: meaningful actions = dreamer's Resolve without shifting (downgrades one step), or (c) a meaningful
    action with an important eidolon/prop tied to the change you want → impose a **waking effect**:
    Infiltrator — subliminal message; store Wits successes as bonus dice for Manipulation vs him next chapter;
    impose/remove an attitude Condition (Swooned, Leveraged, Paranoid…); ±2 impression steps; open Doors = Empathy;
    no WP from tonight's rest; drain WP or Glamour = Presence + Wyrd to yourself; ±1 Fortification for the story.
    Intruder — any Infiltrator effect, or: a subliminal suggestion acted within 24 h (no breaking point); Manipulation
    clues using Wits + Empathy; impose/remove a Persistent mental Condition (Obsession, Awestruck, Amnesia, Madness…);
    Clarity attack on a changeling dreamer with pool = your Presence; ±1 to a Mental or Social Attribute/Skill for the
    story. Assailant — any milder effect, or: a subliminal **command** (any one action within 24 h); clues with Wits +
    Empathy + Wyrd; grant/remove a Mental or Supernatural Merit dot for the story; **Ravaged**; change/add an anchor or
    Aspiration for the story; Flesh Too Solid on his dream form.
  - Changing someone via Infiltrator/Intruder/Assailant = breaking point 1/2/4 dice for the changeling.
- **Embarrassing Secret**: if revealed → Notoriety.
- **Fatigued**: every 6 h Resolve + Stamina or sleep; cumulative −1 (−2/−3 if strenuous).
- **Fragile** / **Volatile** (equipment & plans): Fragile lasts uses = builder's Skill dots; Volatile turns failures
  into dramatic failures, then −2 bonus. Each gives involved players a Beat on resolution.
- **Frightened**: must flee the source; can't approach/act against it; collapses if cornered; lasts the scene; 1 WP
  suppresses for a turn. **+1 Clarity**. Sources: Bedlam (fear), Pounce, Overpowering Dread, Mien of the Baba Yaga.
  Res: escape the source.
- **Fugue (P)**: when circumstances echo the trauma, Resolve + Composure or ST controls her for a scene (she flees the
  conflict). Source: severe Clarity damage. Res: as Broken. Beat: enters fugue.
- **Glamour Addicted (P)**: must harvest/reap **½ Wyrd ↑ Glamour within Resolve days** or 1 lethal/day (unhealable) +
  Deprived. **+1 Clarity**. Source: harvesting daily for a week at Wyrd 6+. Res: exceptional success harvesting.
- **Goblin Queen (P)**: as Hedge Denizen plus: Mantle rejects her (no Court Contracts); Status (Goblins) 4; fae no longer
  add her Wyrd to track her; can't reintegrate her Icon; leaves the Hedge only wrapped in it, Wyrd hours/day (Deprived);
  Retainers (hobgoblins) = Wyrd dots; **+2 Clarity**. Res: leave a human child in her place (2-die Clarity attack;
  she regains old self and the Fae resume the hunt) or work off 5 Debt → back to Hedge Denizen. Beat: collect Goblin
  Debt from someone.
- **Guilty**: −2 Resolve/Composure vs Subterfuge, Empathy, Intimidation. Res: confess and make restitution.
- **Hedge Addiction (P)** (fae-touched): Resolve + Composure to resist entering the Hedge, −1 per prior entry this story
  (max −5). Beat: enters. Res: avoid the Hedge a full story.
- **Hedge Denizen (P)** (10th Goblin Debt point): mien warps (one tell); ST can't spend her Debt (must work it off);
  Arcadian Contracts all at non-favored cost; keeps Court Contracts but can't buy new, loses other Mantle benefits; at
  each Hedge sunrise **re-pick her Goblin Contracts**; **Goblin Contracts cost no Debt**; Deprived outside the Hedge/
  Arcadia; can sell Contracts and deals. **+1 Clarity**. Res: *Returning*: work off ≥1 Debt by a task for the creditor,
  then heal 1 Clarity → old self; *Embracing*: suffer ≥2 Clarity attacks (take damage from one) and lose a Touchstone
  → Goblin Queen. Beat: work off Debt without resolving.
- **Hunted (P)**: a serious threat (Huntsman, loyalist, fetch) pursues. **+1 Clarity**. Beat: they find her. Res: stop
  them (violence, lifestyle change, freeing the Huntsman).
- **Informed**: on a relevant (non-combat) roll, resolve: failure → 1 success; success → exceptional.
- **Inspired**: on a relevant action, resolve: exceptional on 3 and +1 WP.
- **Lethargic**: **can't spend WP**; −1 per 6 h awake, Stamina + Resolve to stay awake. Sources: Bedlam (sorrow),
  dying in dream form, escaping a crumbling Bastion. Res: full night's sleep.
- **Leveraged**: comply with the specified character's request without resisting to resolve (or Leverage them back).
- **Lost**: can't progress to the goal until she navigates (Wits + Streetwise / Survival; **Hedge: a navigation
  chase**). **+1 Clarity**. Res: abandon goal or navigate.
- **Madness (P)**: ST pool **10 − Integrity (or current Clarity)**, once per chapter applied as penalty to a Mental or
  Social roll. Res: as Broken. Beat: fail because of it.
- **Mute (P)**.
- **Notoriety**: −2 Social vs those who know; +1 Door in Social maneuvering.
- **Numb (P)**: −2 to all mundane physical actions. Source: severe Clarity damage. Res: exceptional mundane physical
  success, a week with no magic, or full WP via Needle. Beat: fail a mundane physical roll.
- **Oathbreaker (P)**: −1 Social with changelings; can't seal; immune to being sealed. **+1 Clarity**. Beat: once/session
  choose to auto-dramatically-fail a Contract roll or Social action with a changeling. Res: restitution to all oath
  parties (their tasks) + the Wyrd's forgiveness (a story).
- **Obliged (P)** (bargain with a mortal): **Huntsmen must win a Clash of Wills to use Hunter's Senses on her; fae don't
  add her Wyrd to track her when her Mask drops**; once/chapter at the bargain's site she can hide undiscovered for the
  scene from any Wyrd-touched pursuer. Beat (once/story): great risk/harm fulfilling it. Res: either side breaks it
  (protection lost).
- **Obsession (P)**: 9-again on rolls pursuing it, no 10-again otherwise.
- **Paranoid**: −2 perception, Social, and Allies/Contacts/Mentor/Retainer/Staff/Status pools. **+1 Clarity**. Res: a
  week with no fae threat, or an ally's exceptional Social success convincing her.
- **Ravaged**: **−2 to all rolls**, no WP from sleep. **+1 Clarity**. Sources: reaping, oneiromancy. Res: regain full WP.
- **Reckless**: −2 Perception/noticing. Res: she or an ally suffers harm/setback from her recklessness.
- **Shaken**: may opt to fail a roll the fear could hinder to resolve. Source: mild Clarity damage in rightmost 3.
- **Sleepwalking (P)**: loses time, false memories of tasks. Source: severe Clarity damage. Res: exceptional success on
  oneiromancy or an extended action. Beat: miss an obligation thinking it done.
- **Soul Shocked**: roll current WP points (no 10-again, no chance die): keep one per success, lose the rest; no WP from
  Needle/Thread until resolved. Sources: "killed" in dream form by fae magic; escaping a shift-crumbled Bastion. Res:
  regain full WP.
- **Spooked**: captivated by the uncanny. Sources: mild Clarity damage in rightmost 3; seeing a changeling with Mask
  dropped (mortals). Res: fascination causes a complication.
- **Steadfast**: resolve after a failed roll → treat as 1 success (chance die → roll a normal die).
- **Stoic**: +2 Subterfuge to hide feelings, no untrained penalty on Subterfuge; −2 Hedgespinning; **can't heal Clarity
  damage**; can't spend WP on actions revealing feelings. Res: opt to fail resisting Empathy/emotion-reading, or enact a
  Hedgespinning paradigm shift. (Vow of No Compromise, Winter's Curse DF.)
- **Swooned**: −2 on rolls that would harm the beloved; they get +2 Social vs him and +1 impression step. Res: endanger
  himself for them or opt to fail resisting them.
- **Wanton**: −2 Composure/Resolve to resist temptation; the inflicter's tempting rolls exceptional on 3. Source: Bedlam
  (desire). Res: indulge in something that is a breaking point.
- **Withdrawn**: −2 to all rolls interacting with others. Source: mild Clarity damage in rightmost 3. Res: regain WP via
  Thread.

**Clarity Conditions** list (05 §4): Broken (P), Comatose, Confused, Delusional (P), Dissociation, Distracted, Fugue (P),
Numb (P), Shaken, Sleepwalking (P), Spooked, Withdrawn. Resolving one heals 1 Clarity (Persistent: 2).
**Clarity damage-roll dice adders** (sum all that apply, plus Touchstone −1/−2/+2): +2 Amnesia, Awestruck, Goblin
Queen; +1 Berserk, Cowed, Demoralized, Disoriented, Frightened, Glamour Addicted, Hedge Denizen, Hunted, Lost,
Oathbreaker, Paranoid, Ravaged.
