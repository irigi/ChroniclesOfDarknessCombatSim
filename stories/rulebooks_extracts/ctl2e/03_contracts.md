# CtL 2e — Contracts

Source Ch3 pp. 126–162 (L10909–14256). Format per Contract: **Cost · Pool · Action · Duration** → effect; seeming
benefits (**B**east, **D**arkling, **E**lemental, **F**airest, **O**gre, **W**izened); **LH** = Loophole.
Pools "vs X" = contested (target resists reflexively; willing target may waive). "− X" = resisted (subtract).

## 0. General rules [pp. 126–128]
- Duration **one scene unless noted**; may end at will. Ending early **does not** shed Conditions it inflicted
  (resolve normally); **Tilts end when the Contract ends**.
- Glamour cost above the Wyrd per-turn limit: pay over several turns, invoke on the last. **At Wyrd 1 (1 G/turn) every
  2-G Contract takes two turns** unless its Loophole is used — matters for reflexive ones like Fae Cunning.
- "vs … + Wyrd" for non-changelings: use their Supernatural Tolerance (hobgoblins, Huntsmen, Hedge ghosts, True Fae: Wyrd;
  mortals: 0) [p. 100].
- **Failed invocation (0 successes) still pays the cost.**
- **Loophole**: fulfilling it invokes the basic effect **with no Glamour** (Willpower and seeming-benefit costs still
  paid). Fulfil Loophole **and still pay 1 Glamour** → invocation becomes **reflexive** instead of instant. A given
  Contract only once per turn reflexively; others may also be made reflexive (paying Glamour each).
- **Seeming benefits**: free for that seeming when the Contract is bought; others learn them from a teacher of that
  seeming (Pupil's Devotion) for **1 XP** each. Use any/all, paying all costs.
- **Pupil's Devotion** (sidebar p. 129): swear a personal **oath** to a teacher who has the Contract in his favored
  Regalia → learn it at favored cost; one oath per Contract.
- **Contracts and Clarity**: vs non-changelings "Clarity" means Integrity/Humanity/etc.; a Clarity attack forces an
  equivalent breaking point. Never works on beings whose trait measures something else (Prometheans).
- **Clash of Wills** [p. 126]: each side rolls **Wyrd + an appropriate Finesse Attribute** (others use their own book's
  pool); most successes wins, **ties reroll**. WP may be spent only if both physically present and aware of the clash
  (may need kenning). Lasting effects get a bonus: +1 lasts a night, +2 a week, +3 a month, +4 a year.
- **Arcadian Regalia**: Crown (rule), Jewels (temptation/lies), Mirror (perception/self-transformation), Shield
  (protection/healing), Steed (movement/beasts), Sword (strength/aggression). Common 3 XP / Royal 4 XP, −1 if favored.
- Dropping the Mask makes successful rolled Contracts exceptional (05 §6).

## 1. Crown (Fairest) [pp. 128–132]
**Common**

  open, animals & hobgoblin sentries avoid her; + companions = Presence in her sight. Hollow/supernatural dwelling →
  Clash of Wills with owner; no effect if owner also knows it. B: animal sentries serve her. F: +2 on the Clash.
  LH: carries an object belonging to the owner/resident.
- **Mask of Superiority** · 1 G · Presence + Subterfuge + Wyrd · instant. Viewers believe him a respected member of
  their organization (Status = Presence; one org chosen via primary target). Slip-ups: Presence + Subterfuge vs Wits +
  Empathy. Exc: trusted visiting ally (Allies = Presence), only roll for absurd behavior. DF: hostility. F: −1 all
  starting Doors vs affected. O: +successes (max +5) Intimidation vs believers. LH: loosely copies the dress code.
- **Paralyzing Presence** · 2 G · Presence + Intimidation + Wyrd vs Composure + Wyrd · contested. Target needs to pay
  attention. Success: **Insensate** Tilt. Exc: + **Cowed**. DF: changeling **Shaken**. D: Manipulation for Presence.
  F: +3 dice. LH: victim otherwise alone and he touches him.
- **Summon the Loyal Servant** · 1 G · none · instant. Blood drop animates a substance (flame, shadow, leaves) into a
  dog-smart servitor: **Power 1, Finesse 3, Resistance 1**, derived as Hedge ghost (p. 247), only frailty iron, no
  Influences/Numina; Size 1–7, natural traits of its substance; one scene. E: +1 G +1 WP merge with it: use its
  Attributes where chosen, immune to its element, +1L weapon modifier. F: 1 WP bind it to his Hollow (stays animate
  there; one at a time). LH: did a significant favor for the material this scene.
- **Tumult** · 2 G · Presence + Empathy + Wyrd − Resolve · instant, instant duration. Needs to see target (true name
  on folded origami bird). Learns if target has Disoriented, Guilty, Lost, Paranoid, Obsession, Spooked, Stoic,
  Withdrawn; **per success** inflict one of those Conditions, or delay to a trigger. Exc +1 effect. DF: self gets one.
  F adds Inspired, Swooned, Wanton. O adds Bestial, Cowed, Frightened. LH: planted an origami bird on target this scene.

**Royal**
- **Discreet Summons** · 1 G object / 2 G hobgoblin · Manipulation + Persuasion + Wyrd (vs Composure + Wyrd for
  hobgoblin) · instant/contested. Pull a Size 1, Availability ≤3 basic item previously seen/handled from a container;
  or open a door "I have a guest!" → hobgoblin (Wyrd ≤3) does one task (enticing offer = uncontested). Vanishes at
  scene end / leaves hand / ignored. Exc: Size ≤5, Avail ≤5; hobgoblin fulfils spirit of the task. DF: Volatile item or
  ill-meaning hobgoblin. D: from any shadow. F: hobgoblin serves until next sunrise/sunset. LH: object from enemy's
  location; or promise hobgoblin something hard to procure, and keep it.
- **Mastermind's Gambit** · 2 G · none · instant (≥5 min) · one chapter. Monologue a concrete goal → plan or
  repository (p. 196) as **+5 equipment** until chapter end or plan resolves. E: 1 WP → lasts the story, covers one
  Mental Specialty. F: may create an organization of mortals instead. LH: written on paper ≥50 years old.
- **Pipes of the Beastcaller** · 1 G · Manipulation + Animal Ken + Wyrd vs Resolve + Composure (hostile) · instant/
  contested. Call all animals of one named species within **Animal Ken miles**; obey simple verbal commands. Exc: day
  and night of control, new commands; abuse → new resistance roll. DF: hostile animal/swarm attacks. B: empathic link,
  remote commands. F: +1 G second species. LH: does a little dance mimicking them.
- **The Royal Court** · 3 G + 1 WP · none · instant. As leader of a meeting, **no one present can inflict bodily harm**;
  supernatural coercion to violence → Clash of Wills; doesn't stop violence already begun. F: also no intimidation/
  threats. W: also mental harm incl. Clarity attacks (not breaking points). LH: ≥5-min speech.
- **Spinning Wheel** · 3 G + 1 WP · Intelligence + Occult + Wyrd − Resolve · extended (10 min/roll; target = subject's
  higher of Wyrd or Resolve) · until lunar month ends or event occurs. Name a reasonably probable event; rolls toward it
  +successes, against it −successes (max ±5). Max pending = Wyrd. Exc: also name an action that prevents it. DF:
  dramatic failure on a crucial roll in the next chapter. F: +3 for positive event; self Inspired. O: +3 for negative;
  self Steadfast. LH: prick target to draw blood.

## 2. Jewels (Wizened) [pp. 132–135]
**Common**

  Socialize. W: object keeps bonus all scene. LH: accepts payment, then buries it.

  target's next roll, or **±1 exceptional threshold** (min 1; never on chance die); can target self. Once per target per
  chapter (repeat = DF). Exc: set a trigger (expires at scene end). DF: turns on her per success. O: target Shaken when
  using cursed pool until next sunrise/sunset. W: after seeing the roll, force a reroll; second stands. LH: she
  dramatically failed an action this scene (and a later DF this scene refunds the Glamour).
- **Light-Shy** · 1 G · none · instant. **Invisible to the mind, all senses** (recordings still detect). Ends on any
  aggressive action, harm, or supernatural effect on anyone. D: also hidden from recordings. W: on an object (people
  rationalize its absence; ends if bumped). LH: stood unmoving in darkness/deep shadow for 1 minute.
- **Murkblur** · 1 G · Manipulation + Subterfuge + Wyrd vs Wits + Wyrd · contested · **one turn**. **Blinded** (both
  eyes). Exc: + **Deafened** (both). DF: self Disoriented. E: target believes he's in an element (ST picks Tilts).
  W: + Disoriented. LH: eats a creature's eye.
- **Trivial Reworking** · 1 G · none · instant. Masks a mundane item ≤ Size 3 to look/feel like another of similar
  shape (psychological; fails counterfeit tests; those who see through Masks immune). D: −Manipulation to detect
  forgery. W: any Size. LH: touched a similar real object this scene.

**Royal**
- **Changeling Hours** · 1 G +1 per 5 Size · none · instant · one chapter. Rewind (repair 1/turn, up to Crafts in
  Structure), Speed (1 damage/turn ignoring Durability up to Crafts), or Freeze (immovable, immune to damage). E: also
  her element. W: +1 WP makes freeze permanent (override → Clash). LH: names a former owner.
- **Dance of the Toys** · 2 G · Manipulation + Crafts + Wyrd · instant. Animates a device in sight within **10 × Wyrd
  yd** to follow one simple command; overriding needs Strength + Resolve > her successes; damaging motion attacks with
  her successes as pool. Exc: buy as 1-dot Retainer (permanent), else lasts a chapter. DF: device opposes her. B:
  complex/conditional commands. W: moves outside normal range at Speed = Wyrd. LH: names the object, calls it by name.
- **Hidden Reality** · 1 G · none · instant. Make a minor, plausible, unobserved feature of a lifeless object real
  (hidden latch, door); ends at scene end/when ignored. F: instead change a person's Virtue or Vice (or anchor):
  Manipulation + Subterfuge + Wyrd vs Resolve + Wyrd. W: +1 G create whole plausible objects (keys, a parked car).
  LH: makes a show of searching, acts surprised (1 minute).
- **Stealing the Solid Reflection** · 1 G + 1 WP · Strength + Larceny + Wyrd · instant. Pull a mirrored, non-magical
  copy of a reflected object through a reflective surface; neither has a reflection while it lasts. Exc: lasts to next
  sunrise/sunset. DF: surface shatters, original takes Wyrd damage ignoring Durability. F: copy a living being (traits =
  lower of original or her Wyrd; no supernatural traits; friendly; DF → vengeful mirror person). W: +1 G keeps mystical
  properties (e.g. goblin fruit). LH: owner expressed a sense of debt to her this scene.
- **Tatterdemalion's Workshop** · 2 G · none · instant. Jury-rig Build Equipment (p. 197): any mundane item ≤ Size 5 in
  one turn, no proper parts/tools; jury-rig penalty reduced by **½ Wyrd (round up)**; works normally. O: implausible
  parts OK. W: any Size. LH: wears large old glasses, archaic tools.

## 7. Court Contracts — rules [p. 150]
- Pools use **Mantle** (or **Court Goodwill** if no Mantle there).
