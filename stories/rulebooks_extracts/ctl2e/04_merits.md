# CtL 2e — Merits

Source Ch3 pp. 111–126 (L9470–10909). **Sanctity of Merits** [p. 111]: Merit dots lost through story (Allies die,
Touchstone Merit lost…) refund as Experiences; rebuy the same Merit only next chapter.
**Motley** tag: members pool dots; each contributor accesses the combined total (max 5 effective); non-contributors
get nothing. **Style** Merits: each dot is a separate maneuver, cumulative.
Changelings may take any core Merit except **Supernatural Merits, Vice-Ridden, Virtuous**. Mortals can't take
supernatural Merits unless noted.

## D. Fae-touched (the Avowed) — template & Merits [App. 1, pp. 310–321]
Mortals who kept a big promise to someone later taken, were drawn into the Hedge, and came out with a sliver of Wyrd.
**Creation**: mortal creation (p. 89), **Integrity not Clarity**, no Lost template, plus:
- **Promise**: +1 die on actions that fortify it. **Wyrd effectively 0** (can never rise). **Favored Regalia ×1 + two
  Common Contracts in it**; Royal Contracts later at changeling XP rates. Must be **taught** every Contract (Court ones
  need Goodwill; **no Mantle ever**); seeming benefits only via Pupil's Devotion + XP; **can't use Loopholes**.
- **Glamour**: holds max 10, spends 1/turn; harvests from emotions / Hedge bounty only (no dreams); fae can harvest
  from her (she loses Glamour). Can witness sealings (not seal fae); can swear oaths only with changelings (or when
  Fae initiate); a changeling's bargain with her gives **no** concealment. No innate oneiromancy.
- **Drawbacks**: starts with **Madness** (regained on every Hedge entry after resolving), **Arcadian Dreams** and **Hedge
  Addiction** Conditions.
- **Reunion** with her promised changeling: automatic **personal oath** (Wyrd-set terms if unsworn); each may harvest
  Glamour from the other; 1 G senses the other's state, distance and direction.
- May take Court Goodwill, Glamour Fasting, Hollow (as motley only), human Merits, and:
