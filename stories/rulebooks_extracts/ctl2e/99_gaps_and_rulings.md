# CtL 2e — Gaps, extraction defects, rulings

Everything here is either a defect of the .txt conversion (reconstructed) or a RAW-silent point ruled **[interp]**.
Deferred to core (`../cod_core/`): combat engine, grapple, weapons/armor tables, Social maneuvering, chases,
investigation, Build Equipment, mortal stat blocks, generic Tilts, core Numina (p. 136) & Dread Powers (p. 144),
fighting-style Merits (not reprinted in CtL), ephemeral-entity basics.

## A. Extraction defects (reconstructed)
1. **Wyrd table** (p. 101, L8450–8570): columns scrambled. The frailties column's 5 entries are assigned to Wyrd 2/4/6/8/10,
   each holding for 2 dots, so Wyrd 1 has none. Evidence: Hedge-ghost table pattern; Carey Ives (Wyrd 3, 1 minor frailty).
   This **corrects an earlier Wyrd 6–10 reading**, found by external review. Disease-penalty reduction "by one/two/three/four" and goblin
   fruit counts cross-check with the separate goblin-fruit table (p. 208).
2. **Sample breaking points** (p. 106, L9017–9112): two-column sidebar interleaved; items marked * in 05 §4 are
   reconstructed.
3. **Hobgoblin Wyrd table** (p. 252, L21816–21989): trait-limit column reconstructed by order (5,6,7,8,9,10…).
4. **Summer Contracts** High Summer's Zeal / Fiery Tongue (L13413–13452) and Solstice Revelation / Sunburnt Heart
   (L13522–13604), **Autumn** Mien of the Baba Yaga / Riding the Falling Leaves (L13709–13760), **Winter** Touch of
   Winter / Ermine's Winter Coat (L13912–13960): roll-result blocks interleaved; assigned by content (damage rebuke →
   Fiery Tongue; flee-prevention → High Summer's Zeal; light radius → Solstice Revelation; Berserk → Sunburnt Heart).
5. **Ipswich Tide Court Mantle dots** (p. 287–288): interleaved; some dots unassigned (08 §E).
6. Hedge-navigation pool table (p. 201): the row reads "Any character's current Clarity **+1** … lower than half its
   maximum". The +2 that follows belongs to "pressing time limit". So: **+1 once** if any traveler is below half Clarity.
7. Hedge ghosts' "roll Attribute + Rank" (p. 247) is a leftover from core — read **Attribute + Wyrd** [interp].

## B. RAW-silent / ambiguous points — rulings
- **Starting Glamour**: new character **full** (official sheet reportedly: Glamour 10 at Wyrd 1; unverified). For an
  established character dropped into a story with unknown recent history, use **half max** [interp]. (Glamour max at Wyrd 1 = 10, spend 1/turn.)
- **Glamour per-turn limit vs multi-Glamour costs**: pay across turns, invoke on the last (RAW p. 126). A Wyrd 1
  changeling therefore needs 2 turns for any 2-Glamour Contract unless using a Loophole [RAW consequence — note it!].
- **Loophole + 1 Glamour = reflexive** even for Wyrd 1 (1 G fits the per-turn limit).
- **Cold iron damage rate** [interp]: touching = 1 aggravated per turn of contact (as major bane); cold-iron weapon hits
  deal aggravated. Ordinary iron weapons deal normal lethal but ignore fae armor/Defense magic.
- **"Current Clarity"** = max Clarity − boxes with any damage [interp], used for perception mods, kenning pool, Hedge
  Initiative, Madness pool.
- **Clarity attack with 0 dice pool** (heavy negative modifiers) → chance die [interp: CoD standard].
- **Breaking points per scene**: RAW silent; [interp] one roll per distinct event.
- **Touchstone damage-roll modifier vs attack roll**: Touchstones and Condition adders modify the **Wyrd damage roll**,
  not the attack roll (RAW p. 98, 105).
- **Bedlam targets**: everyone who perceives her (incl. allies) contests individually with **Composure + Wyrd** (mortals
  use Supernatural Tolerance 0 → Composure only).
- **Seeming Blessing default activation**: always-on unless the text names a cost/trigger (Darkling: 1 WP; Fairest/Ogre
  /Wizened/Elemental: free unless the Glamour condition applies).
- **Ogre blessing**: "deals any damage" includes grapple damage moves [interp].
- **Beast blessing** lethal unarmed: choose per attack [interp].
- **Portaling vs grapple timing**: reflexive, so usable on the opponent's turn when grabbed [interp: any time].
- **Hedge ghosts' Defense vs firearms** applies (RAW p. 248).
- **True Fae "automatically succeeds" at shaping in Arcadia/Hedge** (RAW: the target may spend 1 WP for a chance to resist).
  The pools are **[house]**: Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance vs the Title's Wyrd + an appropriate Power Attribute.
- **Goblin Debt from deals vs Contracts**: both add to one tally; ST spends either.
- **Huntsman "auto-success" tracking a Mask-dropped changeling within 10 yd × Wyrd** (05 §6) vs Obliged Condition
  (fae don't add Wyrd dice): RAW wording — Obliged removes the dice bonus, not the auto-success. Optional **[house]**: Obliged
  suppresses both.

## C. Rulings added after the self-test (2026-10-12)
- **Siren Song / Bedlam-style "everyone who hears"**: allies are affected too (RAW). Nightsinger's Kith Blessing (3 =
  exceptional) applies to the Siren Song roll [interp], but there is no special exceptional effect. "Rooted" victims can't
  move but may take other actions, including grapple contests, and keep Defense. The singer must keep singing **audibly**;
  this is **not** an instant action each turn, but silence, unconsciousness or a vocally incompatible action ends it
  [interp]. Forcing it on unwilling people may be the "Glamour forcing behavior" breaking point (3 or 5 dice; see 05 §4).
- **Portaling vs iron**: she knows it can't work — no Glamour spent unless she tries blind [interp].
- **Surprise Entrance vs Vigilance of Ares**: Vigilance detects the threat; the Huntsman still appears and the Shaken roll
  applies. Whether it gets its free action before she can react is a power conflict → **Clash of Wills** [interp].
- **Glamour harvest**: instant action; failed harvest costs the target nothing; retry at successive-attempt penalty; pool by
  method (Presence + Empathy/Intimidation/Expression…) [interp].
- **Sealing**: reflexive-speed (spend on hearing it). A negative promise ("never come back") breaks when the subject does it.
- **Opening a Hedge door**: instant action; open-turns count from opening; while dormant **any** Lost (opener included) reopens
  it free (RAW p. 198);
  Huntsmen can use any open or dormant Hedgeway [interp].
- **Breaking point dramatic failure**: a player may turn a failed Clarity attack into a dramatic failure (Beat) and also gain
  the +1 WP — ST may cap at once per scene [interp].
- **Clarity damage pool ≤ 0** after Touchstone/Condition modifiers → chance die.
- **Fairest blessing**: WP spent for an ally counts as that action's one WP; spend at the moment the ally acts.
- **Flickering Hours** Fleet of Foot doesn't stack with the Merit (use the higher).
- **Season of rule**: solstices/equinoxes (northern hemisphere): Spring Mar 20–Jun 20, Summer to Sep 22, Autumn to Dec 21,
  Winter to Mar 19 [interp; local freeholds vary].
- **Huntsman's +½ master's Wyrd**: applies when the effect would make it act against its Keeper's wishes. That includes
  being rooted or diverted mid-hunt (so it applies vs Siren Song while hunting), but not idle listening [interp].
- **Wyrd bought with Merit dots** counts as Merit dots spent for creation only.
- **Dramatic failure on the Wyrd damage roll** (as opposed to the Clarity attack roll): no damage, no bonus WP.
- **Portaling vs a supernatural creature's grapple**: an ordinary grapple (even with Vigor-boosted Strength) is mundane — no
  Clash; only magical bindings/forces need a Clash of Wills [interp].
- **Multi-turn Glamour payment for reflexive Contracts**: may prepay on earlier turns and invoke reflexively when the last point
  is spent [interp].
