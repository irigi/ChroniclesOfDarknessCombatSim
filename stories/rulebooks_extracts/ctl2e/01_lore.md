# CtL 2e — Lore (how the Lost and their world work)

Sources: Intro pp. 10–17 (L1552–2186), Ch2 pp. 63–85 (L5568–7350), Ch3 "Wonders and Terrors" pp. 100–110, Ch5.
Rules details live in 05 (systems) and 06 (antagonists); this file is the map.

## Premise [pp. 10–11]
Humans **lured or abducted by the Gentry (True Fae)** through the Hedge into **Arcadia**, reshaped over a **durance**
into roles (seemings/kiths) for their Keeper's fantasies, then **escaped back through the Hedge** — leaving soul-pieces
(**Icons**) on the Thorns. Home, they find time has passed and often a **fetch** living their life. They hide behind
the **Mask**, feed on human emotion (**Glamour**), wield **Contracts** (pacts the Fae made with the world), and band
into **motleys**, **courts** and **freeholds** to resist the **Wild Hunt** (Huntsmen sent to drag them back). Themes:
Beauty + Agony, Clarity + Madness, Lost + Found. "Madness" = refusing to let Faerie *or* mortality dictate your
perceptions (Clarity). Ch7 has safety tools (Lines & Veils, stoplight, X-card, door is always open, debriefing).

## Arcadia & the durance [pp. 63–68]
Time in Arcadia is unreliable (an instant or centuries). The Fae take people for whims; a Keeper reshapes its captive
from "your bones and their whims". Huntsmen, Fae explorers' wild hunters, live in Arcadia's cold empty fringes.
Abduction often follows an accepted offer, a wrong door, a fairy ring… Changelings are told it was their fault — "the
Gentry are liars".

## The Wyrd [p. 68]
Reciprocity as natural law: when you gain, you pay; if you don't name the price, the Wyrd does. Hence fae deals,
binding promises, punishment of oathbreakers, Goblin Debt, and the Gentry's own binding rules and loopholes.

## The Hedge [pp. 68–75, 198–209]
Borderland wrapping the mortal world at lonely, uncanny, liminal places; shapes itself to travelers' hearts and to what
lies nearby; paths labyrinthine; time runs differently. Objects work whimsically. **No Mask inside** — the changeling is
herself; it's also where Icons, trods, Hollows, Goblin Markets (part tourist trap, devil's haunt, county fair), goblin
fruit, Dreaming Roads and the mirror-maze ("Halls of Mirrors", home of mirror-people and some fetches) are found.
Hobgoblins were fellow servants in Arcadia, ranked below changelings; now they set the prices. Hedge ghosts: those who
died in the Hedge, emotions, stray Icons. Huntsmen are stronger in the Hedge but also more swayable (nearer their
hearts).

## Return [pp. 75–78]
Time has passed; career, tech, loved ones moved on; most can't simply go home. Clarity = the balancing act of holding
both worlds as true. **Fetch**: Keeper-made duplicate from a piece of the victim's shadow + detritus, with her memories,
which has lived her life; may not know what it is until she returns.

## Society [pp. 78–83]
- **Freeholds** watch common Hedge exits to intercept newcomers (observe-and-protect; screening — privateers pose as new
  arrivals). Newcomers get ~a month's hospitality, then may swear the **freehold oath** (protect members, never aid the
  Fae; some add tithes/obedience). Betrayal → exile, the Wyrd, or worse.
- **Courts**: communal defense via Bargains with patrons; the common model is the **Seasonal Courts** — Spring (desire),
  Summer (wrath), Autumn (fear), Winter (sorrow) — ruling the freehold in turn each season; **handing power on** is
  alien to the Gentry and thus a disguise; tyranny draws them. Joining = an oath, grants Mantle (see 04, 08).
- **Motleys**: close-knit groups, usually bound by a motley oath (mechanical benefits 05 §12).
- **Loyalists** (term used for all courtless): Bridge-Burners, privateers, True Loyalists (06 §B).

## Mask & mien [pp. 83, 107–108]
Mask fools mortal senses and machines (claws feel like nails; clockwork organs pass a medical exam); fae beings and
those with mystical senses see the mien. Blessing effects still work, rationalized away. Mien grows stronger with Wyrd
and is flavored by Mantle. Dropping the Mask is dangerous (opens Hedge gates nearby, beacon to Huntsmen).

## Contracts & pledges [pp. 83–85, 126–127, 209]
Contracts are ancient pacts the Fae struck with concepts/the world; changelings, as Arcadia's denizens, may claim them
(Arcadian), receive them from their court's patron (Court), or buy them from goblins (Goblin, with Debt). Each has a
**Loophole** — a ritual act appeasing the other party in lieu of Glamour. Pledges are how the Lost turn the Wyrd's
reciprocity to their own ends; oaths are serious currency among them; bargains with mortals hide them from the Hunt.

## Longevity & death
+~20 years of lifespan per Wyrd dot [p. 101]. A changeling killed by a major bane unravels (thorns, leaves, wings); the
Mask leaves an ordinary corpse otherwise. The Lost generally believe vampires cannot become changelings (needs a living soul) [p. 55].

## The Gentry [pp. 266–273]
Not persons but **Names wrapped in Titles and pacts**; each manifestation is one Title (with a tell). Many Fae have no
interest in humans. Their power is pacts with Regalia, each needing a physical representation; break the pact → lose the
power. They war, devour each other's Titles. Sample Keepers: Grandmother, Grandmother; the Man with the Ergot Smile; the
Three Androgynes; the Year of Plague.
