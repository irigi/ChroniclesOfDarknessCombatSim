# WtF 2e — Conditions & Tilts [App. 2, pp. 306–313; rules p. 178]

General [p. 178]: resolving a Condition = Beat; expiring by time = no Beat. Persistent: once per session a Beat when it
impacts life. Improvised Conditions ≈ ±2 to a type of action. Only one copy of a Condition unless about different things.
Spirit Influence/Manifestation Conditions (**Resonant, Open, Controlled, Reaching, Shadow Gate, Materialized, Fettered,
Urged, Possessed, Claimed**) → `06_antagonists.md` §H. Core Conditions not reprinted here (Broken, Obsessed, Embarrassing
Secret, Deprived…) → `../cod_core/07_conditions.md`.

05 §5. Lunacy Conditions (Atavism, Delusion, Reception) fade after **PU days** if unresolved (no Beat) — 05 §9.
Optional Pregnancy Conditions → 05 §15.

## Tilts [pp. 312–313]
| Tilt | Effect | Cause | Ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Arm Wrack** | Drop held item; no attacks with it; off-hand penalties; both arms: chance die on manual dexterity, −3 other Physical. | Targeted arm (−2) dealing > Stamina; hand (−4) any damage; powers; Urshul Weaken the Prey | Damage heals (agg = permanent for humans; werewolves regrow). |
| **Blinded** | One eye: −3 vision rolls, Defense halved; both: −5 and no Defense. | Eye shot (−5) (exceptional = both); Dex + Athletics −3 dirt/sand vs Defense = one turn | Damage heals; agg permanent. |
| **Earthquake** (env.) | −1 to −5 Dex pools & Defense; 1–3 L/turn (Sta + Athletics downgrades/cancels). | Massive power | Over in ≤1 min. |
| **Heavy Winds** (env.) | −3 aural Perception; −(1–5) Physical incl. Drive; bashing = rating/turn (Dex + Athletics avoids). | Weather, Weather Gifts | Get to shelter. |
| **Knocked Down** | Lose action if not yet acted; prone; attack from ground −2. | Weapon ≥+2 melee/+3 firearm; targeted legs (−2, damage halved); Urshul, Down the Prey, Broaden | Stand (action); or Dex + Athletics − weapon before acting to avoid. |
| **Leg Wrack** | Speed halved, −2 movement rolls & Defense; both legs: Knocked Down, Speed 1, chance die on movement. | Targeted leg dealing > Stamina; powers; Urshul | Damage heals. |
| **Poisoned** | Moderate: 1 bashing/turn; grave: 1 lethal/turn. | Venom, Toxic Bite, Essence Venom, Moon Taint | Sta + Res each turn (−3 if acting) negates that turn's damage; medical help. |
| **Stunned** | Lose next action; Defense halved until then. | Damage ≥ Size in one hit (head: Size −1); stun weapons ×2 modifier; pepper spray; Rapture; Twilight Purge | One turn; or WP reflexively to act at −3. |
Also referenced (from core): Deafened, Extreme Cold/Heat (Hunt of Fire and Ice), Insane, etc. → `../cod_core/07_conditions.md`.
