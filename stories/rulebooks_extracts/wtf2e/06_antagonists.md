# WtF 2e — Spirits, Prey & Antagonists [Ch4 pp. 178–193; Ch5 pp. 197–245]

**WtF 2e spirit rules supersede the CoD-core ephemeral-entity rules for spirits** (core's Manifestation/Influence model
is the same family; WtF adds Hisil details, Rank table, Gauntlet penalties, Fetter→Urged, Claim, Lunes etc.).
Ghosts use core (`../cod_core/06_antagonists.md`); spirits and ghosts are "out of phase" and can't see/touch each
other without a Numen [p. 183].

## A. Spirits — procedure summary (read this first)
1. A spirit lives in the **Hisil**; to act in Flesh it must (a) **Reach** across (powers through the Gauntlet, penalised by
   Gauntlet dice modifier) or (b) **cross** (Gauntlet Breach / Shadow Gateway / a locus) and arrive **in Twilight**.
2. In Flesh it **bleeds 1 Essence/hour** unless protected by a Condition (Fettered, Possessed, Materialized, Claimed).
3. Everything it does to Flesh runs on a ladder of **Conditions** on the place/object/person:
   **Resonant → Open → Controlled**, which unlock Manifestations: Image (Resonant), Materialize/Fetter/Possess/
   Shadow Gateway (Open), Claim (Controlled).
4. Pays **Essence** for Influences (by effect level + duration), Manifestations and Numina; rolls **Power + Finesse**
   (contested by victims' **Resolve/Composure (+ PU)** variants as listed).
5. Regains Essence: **+1/day near any Condition relating to it** (even across the Gauntlet), **gorging** (once/day Power +
   Finesse near a suitable Condition; Gauntlet penalty if gorging from the Hisil), stealing from spirits, being fed
   (gathra). Spends **1 Essence/day** to stay active.

## B. Rank & traits [pp. 183–185]
| Rank | Title (First Tongue) | Trait max | Attribute dots | Max Essence | Numina |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Muthra (mote) | 0 | 0 | 5 | 0 |
| 1 | Hursih | 5 | 5–8 | 10 | 1–3 |
| 2 | Hursah | 7 | 9–14 | 15 | 3–5 |
| 3 | Ensih | 9 | 15–25 | 20 | 5–7 |
| 4 | Ensah | 12 | 26–35 | 25 | 7–9 |
| 5 | Dihir | 15 | 36–45 | 50 | 9–11 |
Rank 6 Dihar, 7–8 Ilusah: beyond game mechanics (plot devices; Rank 5 "Royal Avatars" possible) — **Ilusahim can't cross
the Gauntlet**. **Rank = Supernatural Tolerance** (like PU) and **−1 per Rank to attempts to forcibly bind** it. Spirits
sense relative Rank; contested **Finesse** to appear the same Rank as the observer.
- **Attributes**: **Power** (Str/Int/Pre), **Finesse** (Dex/Wits/Man), **Resistance** (Sta/Res/Com). No Skills; no unskilled
  penalty for actions fitting its nature: roll **Attribute + Rank** for concept actions, **Attribute + Attribute** for
  generic (surprise, perception).
- **Corpus** = Resistance + Size (no wound penalties). **Willpower** = Resistance + Finesse (max 10); +1 WP/day and +1 WP
  per 3 Essence gorged/stolen. **Initiative** = Finesse + Resistance. **Defense** = lower of Power/Finesse (**Rank 1: higher**;
  dormant: none); **applies vs firearms too**. **Speed** = Power + Finesse + species factor (objects 0). **Size**: default =
  Rank. No Integrity/Virtue/Vice.
- **Language**: First Tongue (Uratha speak it with an accent); often learn local human languages.
- **Boosting**: spend Essence 1:1 to raise a trait for a scene (max +Rank+2 to one trait; takes a turn; one Attribute per turn).
- **Dormancy** [p. 184]: most object/plant/feature spirits start dormant; **waking costs 1 Essence**. Destroying the object,
  big Shadow upheavals, or same-umia spirits can wake them.
- **Influence dots** = Rank (max per Influence = Rank; may split; trade 1 Numen per extra Influence dot).
- **Manifestation**: **Twilight Form + Rank** Manifestation Effects (trade 1 Numen per extra Effect).
- Uratha **smell** a spirit: **Primal Urge − spirit's Resistance** → highest Influence (exceptional: all) [p. 186].

## C. Essence economy [p. 184]
- **−1 Essence/day** to remain active (even in the Hisil). At 0 → hibernation; in Flesh it drifts back across the Gauntlet
  over hours equal to the Gauntlet **strength** (p. 184 says "dice penalty in hours" — a negative number; use strength).
- **In Flesh outside a protecting Condition: −1 Essence/hour (bleed).** A spirit that wants to stay needs Fettered,
  Possessed, Materialized or Claimed (Fetter is the only one that's cheap and lasting). Running out from bleed → **1 lethal + hibernation**.
  Protected by: **Fettered, Possessed, Materialized, Claimed** (not by Twilight Form, Image, Reaching).
- **+1 Essence per day** in proximity to any Condition relating to it (Resonant etc.), **even across the Gauntlet**.
- **Gorge**: once/day near a suitable Condition, **Power + Finesse** → regain successes (Gauntlet penalty if from the Hisil).
- **Steal** from another spirit: Power + Finesse vs Power + Resistance → take up to successes.
- **Senses Essence sources** within 1 mile, even across the Gauntlet without penalty (Seek extends).
- **Aggravated wound → also −1 Essence.**
- Locus: generates **3 × rating Essence per day** in the Hisil.

## D. Banes & bans [pp. 185–186]
- **Ban**: compulsion; Rank 1 mild/easily triggered; Rank 2–3 moderate, curtails activity (e.g. loses all WP if it doesn't kill
  monthly); Rank 4–5 complicated, catastrophic consequences, esoteric triggers.
- **Bane** (substance/energy): Rank 1 common (burning plastic fumes); 2–3 hard-to-get but natural (pine stake); 4–5 highly
  specific (silver bullet from a melted original dollar).
  - Voluntary contact: spend **1 WP + Power + Resistance − Rank**.
  - Banes are **solid to spirits even in Twilight**, but don't affect them across the Gauntlet.
  - Touching it: **1 agg/turn if bane in the Hisil or spirit Materialized**, and any relevant Condition ends unless the spirit
    rolls **Rank dice** (each turn of contact).
  - Bane touches an item/person the spirit is **Fettered** to: **1 lethal/turn** while contact holds; spirit must Unfetter to escape.
  - Touching it **in Twilight** (non-manifest): **1 lethal/turn**.
  - **As a weapon**: **aggravated** if both in the Hisil, or bane physical and spirit **using a Manifestation**; **lethal** if spirit
    is in Twilight and bane physical. (A bane weapon can hit a Twilight spirit — only banes can, absent powers.)
  - **Rank dominance**: a spirit **2+ Ranks higher** counts as its opponent's **bane** with unarmed/claws/teeth. **Uratha use
    Renown-Rank** (05 §6) vs spirits only: a werewolf outranking a spirit by 2+ deals bane wounds with claws/bite; a high-Rank
    spirit can't "burn" a werewolf by touch [Tooth and Claw box, p. 186].

## E. Spirit combat [p. 186]
- Attack: **Power + Finesse** (− target Defense); **bashing** unless its nature says lethal (metal fists etc.); ephemeral weapons
  add damage.
- **Twilight spirits can only attack/be attacked by other spirits, unless the attack uses the bane.** (Also by powers that
  allow it — Barghest is for ghosts; Whisper Knife fetish; Twilight Purge rite; Gauru does **not** by itself hit Twilight.)
- **Physical attacks on a Manifest spirit (or one in the Hisil) that would do lethal do only bashing**, unless using its bane
  (no organs). Bane/agg sources as per D.
- Wounds marked/healed like humans; **Corpus 0 from lethal/agg** → bursts into ephemera, **not dead**: if ≥1 Essence it
  reforms in a safe Resonant place **hibernating** until Essence ≥ Corpus dots, then spends 1 to wake. **Destroyed only if it
  loses all Corpus *and* all Essence** (Sacred Hunt Essence-draining, Siskur-Dah devouring).
- Healing via **Regenerate** Numen; while Possessing, 1 Essence/turn heals 1 B/L (host).

## F. Influence [pp. 186–187]
- Use: total of **effect level + duration level ≤ Influence rating**; pay cost; roll **Power + Finesse**. Mind/emotion of a
  sentient being: contested by **higher of Resolve/Composure + Primal Urge** (humans PU 0).
- **Effects**: **•Strengthen** (1 Ess: +Rank Health/Structure or Defense to a beneficiary, stronger emotion; can shift
  **Resonant → Open** for the duration) · **••Manipulate** (2 Ess: minor changes, nudge emotion/object function) ·
  **•••Control** (3 Ess: dramatic changes, dictate object/animal; can shift **Open → Controlled** for the duration) ·
  **••••Create** (4 Ess: new example; instil emotion; temporary **Resonant** on a subject) · **•••••Mass Create** (5 Ess: Rank
  examples, or one permanent instance/Resonant w/o duration cost; never permanently alters a sapient mind).
- **Durations** (add level): 0 = 1 min/success (+0 Ess); • = 10 min/success (+0); •• = 1 h/success (+1 Ess); ••• = 1 day/
  success (+2); •••• = permanent (+2).
  Example: Influence 3 → Manipulate (2) for 10 min/success (1) = total 3, cost 2 Ess; or Control (3) for 1 min/success.
- Fettered: −1 Essence on Influences on the Fetter, but can't use Influence/Numina on other targets.
- Across the Gauntlet needs the **Reaching** Condition (except at a locus) and suffers the Gauntlet dice modifier.

## H. Influence & Manifestation Conditions [pp. 187–191]
Tiered: higher ones need lower ones; advancing **freezes** the prerequisite's remaining duration; removing a prerequisite
removes everything built on it (most advanced remaining resumes its clock).
**Fast track (RAW, the book's own example, p. 188):** a spirit can use **Strengthen Influence to make a Resonant subject
temporarily Open (instant)**, then immediately **Fetter** (or Possess) — the Open's remaining duration is **frozen** while
the Fetter lasts, so the Fetter is effectively permanent. The slow "scenes acting in concert" route is for spirits without a
suitable Influence (or vs subjects the Strengthen roll can't beat).
- **Resonant** — subject/place matches the spirit's purview (anything matching one of its Influences). Natural. Ends when the
  phenomenon ends (grief healed, fire out). Summoning rites instil it; Create Influence makes it; banishing rites suppress.
- **Open** — prerequisite Resonant. Caused by: extended fine-tuning with the subject **for scenes = higher of spirit's Rank or
  living subject's Resolve** (subject and spirit "acting in concert"); **Strengthen Influence upgrades Resonant→Open
  temporarily as an instant action**; **Lunacy** and some Wolf-Blooded Tells cause Open spontaneously. Ends if Resonant ends;
  **exorcism removes Open** (reverting to Resonant). Locus zones are permanently Open (to matching resonance).
- **Controlled** — prerequisite Open tagged to the spirit; caused by **Control Influence (•••) temporarily (RAW p. 187 — lets a
  Rank 3+ spirit with Claim skip the possession grind while it lasts)**, or by **successfully Possessing the subject on separate
  occasions = higher of spirit's or subject's Willpower** (any Possession ended early resets progress); or Control Influence
  temporarily. Ending it/Claim against the spirit's will reverts to Open.
- **Reaching** — 1 scene conduit (see G).
- **Shadow Gate** — 1 scene hole in the Gauntlet.
- **Materialized** — physical; all spirit traits apply minus Twilight; no bleed; lasts 1 h/success.
- **Fettered** — see G; spirit pays −1 Essence for Influences on the Fetter, can't use Influences/Numina on other targets.
- **Urged** (host of a Fetter) — spirit can **read surface thoughts: Power + Finesse vs Resolve + PU**; **urge an action: Power +
  Finesse vs Resolve + Composure**; host following the urge **gains a Beat**. Ends with the Fetter.
- **Possessed** — living host in a **coma-like state** (missing time; flashbacks). Spirit **can't use Numina or Influences while
  controlling**; no bleed. **1 Essence/turn heals 1 B/L (or Structure) on the host.** Objects: operate as if used (switches,
  keyboards; Finesse for rolls). Corpses/mannequins/robots: host's Physical, spirit's Mental/Social; 1 Ess → spirit's Attributes
  for a Physical task for a turn (costs host 1 lethal/Structure). **Living host: uses the host's Attributes; read mind Finesse
  −4; host's Physical Skills at −3, Social/Mental Skills at −4; these penalties are *reduced* by 1 die per day the spirit has been Fettered to the host** (so a spirit Fettered 3+ days has no Physical penalty).
  Spirit stays in Twilight superimposed → **bane on the host/weapon of bane wounds the spirit's Corpus**. Lasts **1 scene**
  (ends early if abandoned, host killed/destroyed; abjuration, exorcism, forced bane/ban contact can motivate release).
  Dead-by-damage corpse starts incapacitated.
- **Claimed** — permanent merge; living subject stays conscious, **Urged** during fusion (several days); **+1 spirit Attribute dot
  per day** to host (Power→Str/Int/Pre, Finesse→Wits/Dex/Man, Resistance→Sta/Com/Res), body mutates. Corpses/objects: start
  Mental/Social 0 (objects Dex 0, use Structure/Durability for Str), no Urged phase. Claimed use the spirit's **Influences**
  (not Numina/Manifestations), may develop **Dread Powers**; have the spirit's **Essence, ban and bane**; are **material**
  beings; ex-spirit Claimed cross at a locus with **Int + Presence**. Ending (living): spirit chooses to leave: **its Power +
  Finesse − Rank vs host's Resolve + Composure** (incl. gained dots); host keeps scars, extra dots fade 2/day. Non-living
  hosts decay: −1 Physical dot per 3 days; at 0 disintegrates, spirit freed to Twilight. **Expel rite doesn't work on Claimed.**

## I. Numina [pp. 191–193] (activation **Power + Finesse** unless stated)
**[R] = usable across the Gauntlet with the Reaching Condition** (or at a locus). The icon is lost in the .txt. **Provisional
reconstruction** (external: a Chinese transcription of WtF 2e that keeps "(R)" marks, corroborated for 8 entries by *Mummy:
Book of Lasting Death*; not verified against the printed page): **[R] = Dement, Emotional Aura, Entropic Decay, Firestarter,
Implant Mission, Pathfinder (moderate confidence), Rapture, Seek, Telekinesis.** All others (incl. **Hallucination, Sign,
Drain, Awe, Blast, Aggressive Meme, Omen Trance**) are **not** [R] — the spirit must be on the target's side of the Gauntlet.
**Key distinction:** Twilight ≠ Hisil. A spirit already in Flesh (in Twilight) uses *any* Numen/Influence on material targets
without Reaching or Materializing (cf. Left-Handed Spanner, Telekinesis, Sign, Firestarter all act on matter from Twilight,
pp. 192–193); it just bleeds unless protected. Reaching only matters when the spirit stays in the Hisil.

## J. Hisil geography effects [p. 179]
- **Shoal**: leaving needs **Resolve + Composure** (−1 per failure; dramatic = must be dragged out).
- **Glade**: fighting needs a Resolve roll; violent actions −2; Uratha must roll to enter Gauru even in Death Rage (failure ends
  the rage).
- **Wound**: −2 to resist Kuruth; all injuries inside **+1 point** (Health or Corpus).
- **Barren**: Gifts, rites, Numina, Manifestations, spirit powers **−3**.

## K. Loci [pp. 179–180]
| Rating | Zone | 
|---|---|
| • | 2 yd |
| •• | 15 yd |
| ••• | building floor / forest clearing |
| •••• | whole building / forest section |

- Zone permanently **Open** (to its resonance). Matching spirits there **heal Corpus ×2** and **+2 to cross the Gauntlet**.
  **No spirit needs Reaching to use powers across the Gauntlet in the zone.** Matching spirits can **merge/conceal** in it

## L. Spirit society & gathra [pp. 180–182]
- Ranks (behaviour): muthra (food), Hursihim (instinctive, often bound to one source), **Ensihim** (independent; most of the
  spirits that flee into Flesh; usual Gift-teachers; totems usually Hursah, may grow to Ensih), Dihim (dominate a region),
  Ilusahim (gods; can't cross).
- Umia examples [p. 181]: Nature (Animals, Fish, Birds, Reptiles, Trees, Plants, Fungi, Insects); Artificial (Vehicles, Structures,
  Tools, Weapons, **Information** — books, televisions, computers, Objects); Elementals (Air, Fire, Earth, Water); Lunes & Helions;
  **Conceptual** (Emotions — fear, love, joy; Ideologies; Reactions — pain, lust, hunger); **Magath** (hybrids, shunned).
  **Broods** = spirit packs.
- **Gathra**: negotiate as **Social maneuvering**; most spirits start **average or hostile** toward Forsaken; **spirits can't agree
  to deals until paid — no Door rolls until gathra given**. Minimum gathra ≈ **2 Essence**, removing a Hursih from politics, or a
  material change that yields a little Essence (moves a Hursih's impression). **Hard Leverage** (threat of Siskur-Dah) can force
  acceptance after a perfunctory gift (see 05 §14 for Harmony-based Door removal).
- Spirits crossing over: refugees desperate not to return; anchor to resonant objects/people ("cursed items"); Urge hosts to
  reflect their nature for Essence → possession → Claimed [p. 182].

## Q. Spirit-Ridden (hithimu) [pp. 207–209]

## S. Idigam (Moon-Banished, Earth-Bound) [pp. 212–245]
Spirits of change locked on the moon by Luna; returned via lunar missions since 1969 (and data streams). **Earth-Bound** never
left (pacts with early humanity) — their bans/banes **always involve humanity**. They hate Uratha. Lunes were their jailors.
- **Formless**: no Influences; **bleed Essence ×2 in Flesh**; **ban & bane change every scene**; Essence Shaping only on itself;
  only the **Shifting** Dread Power. Weakest known Formless ≈ Rank 3.
- **Coalescing**: **Rank +1**; Influences/traits/ban/bane fixed (ban & bane reflect the place/moment of Coalescence — clue for
  hunters); full Influences, Essence Shaping (~Rank powers), 3–6 Dread Powers. Most Coalesced Rank 4–5 (Gurdilag was 6).
  Return to Formless: when sent dormant, spend WP = 2 × Rank.

  but never leashed. **Essence pool ×2** for Rank; **no daily Essence loss in the Shadow**; Coalesced bleed normally in Flesh.
  Shadow Gaze may fail to pierce them; other spirits fear them.
