## Auspices (moon phase at First Change) [pp. 15–33]
Each auspice: 3 **Moon/Shadow Gift affinities** (auspice Moon Gift + 2 Shadow Gift categories), 3 **Auspice Skills**,
1 **Auspice Renown**, a **Hunter's Aspect** (used in the Siskur-Dah/Hunter's Aspect system → 05), and a once-per-chapter
**Auspice Benefit**.

| Auspice | Moon | Gifts | Auspice Skills | Renown | Hunter's Aspect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cahalith** (prophet/howler) | Gibbous | Gibbous Moon, Inspiration, Knowledge | Crafts, Expression, Persuasion | Glory | Monstrous |
| **Elodoth** (judge/walker between) | Half | Half Moon, Insight, Warding | Empathy, Investigation, Politics | Honor | Isolating |
| **Irraka** (stalker) | New | New Moon, Evasion, Stealth | Larceny, Stealth, Subterfuge | Cunning | Blissful |
| **Ithaeur** (spirit master) | Crescent | Crescent Moon, Elemental, Shaping | Animal Ken, Medicine, Occult | Wisdom | Mystical |
| **Rahu** (warrior) | Full | Full Moon, Dominance, Strength | Brawl, Intimidation, Survival | Purity | Dominant |

Hunter's Aspect flavour [pp. 18–32]: Monstrous = world shows the hunter as iconic horror; Isolating = prey shunned/alone,
people ignore howls; Blissful = prey has no idea anyone is coming; Mystical = prey senses spirits/superstition, own senses
betray him (vs. a spirit: it senses the material world); Dominant = prey spoils for a fight, thinks he can win.

### Auspice Benefits (each once per chapter)
- **Cahalith — Prophetic Dreams** [p. 18]: ST may grant prophetic dreams any time (Int + Occult to interpret facets).
  After a night of >4 h uninterrupted sleep, player may declare an action/event *foretold*: if beneficial → **+3** to one
  action (self or packmate) **or −3** to one ST-character action (ST may just let it auto-succeed/fail); if detrimental →
  player may choose to auto-fail the action / suffer the damage or setback **and take a Beat**. Once per chapter.
- **Elodoth — Darkness Into Light** [p. 23]: once per chapter either **cure a werewolf of Death Rage** (target is
  **Stunned** for the turn after leaving it, if it was in Gauru/Basu-Im... text says "Basu-Im") or **force** a werewolf into
  Death Rage: contested **Presence + Empathy + Primal Urge vs Resolve + Composure**; win → target enters Kuruth (Wasu-Im).
  Uratha only.
- **Irraka — Closer Than You Thought** [p. 26]: once per chapter, one of: (a) move Initiative to within 1 of a target's
  (stay on the same side of it); (b) move close enough to use teeth/claws on one opponent who isn't attacking her;
  (c) **−2 Doors** needed in Social maneuvering.
- **Ithaeur — Spirit Howl** [p. 29]: once per chapter, **1 Essence**, no roll: silent in Flesh, echoes in Shadow. Spirits of
  **lower Rank than the werewolf** flee/hide/go dormant; stronger ones avoid him out of deference; allies of pack/tribal
  totem may help. Only outright hostile spirits ignore it — and their **Defense is penalized by his Wisdom** Renown.
- **Rahu — Tenacious** [p. 33]: once per chapter, ignore effects of any Conditions/Tilts hindering him in combat for
  **2 turns**; they return afterwards (doesn't resolve Condition/end Tilt).

| Tribe | First Tongue | Firstborn | Prey | Oath (tribal vow) | Gift affinities | Tribal Renown |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Blood Talons** | Suthar Anzuth | Fenris-Ur, Destroyer Wolf | other werewolves (Pure, oath-breakers) | "Offer No Surrender You Would Not Accept" | Inspiration, Rage, Strength | Glory |
| **Bone Shadows** | Hirfathra Hissu | Kamduis-Ur, Death Wolf | spirits, ghosts, ephemeral beings | "Pay Each Spirit in Kind" | Death, Elements, Insight | Wisdom |
| **Hunters in Darkness** | Meninna | Hikaon-Ur, Black Wolf ("Silent Mother") | **Hosts** (shartha: Azlu spiders, Beshilu rats) + territory violators | "Let No Sacred Place in Your Territory Be Violated" (territory = *mus-rah*) | Nature, Stealth, Warding | Purity |
| **Iron Masters** | Farsil Luhal | Sagrim-Ur, Red Wolf | humans & human institutions; other monsters hiding among humans (vampires, mages…) | "Honor Your Territory in All Things" | Knowledge, Shaping, Technology | Cunning |
| **Storm Lords** | Iminir | Skolis-Ur, Winter Wolf | **Ridden** (Urged, Possessed, Claimed) | "Allow No One to Witness or to Tend Your Weakness" | Evasion, Dominance, Weather | Honor |
| **Ghost Wolves** | (not a tribe; *nuzusul*) | none | personal | none | **no affinities; start with only one Shadow Gift** | **none; cannot start with the 2-dot Moon Gift** [p. 50] |

Flavour notes: Talons — military, pack-first, "Thu Ibiru" (All War). Bone Shadows — ritualists, bargainers, taboos.
Hunters in Darkness — trap-laden territory, runs. Iron Masters — adaptation, live among humans. Storm Lords — self-reliance,
*Abni-Gur* (crucible of becoming), hunt their own Claimed kin alone.

The **Pure** (Anshega/Pure Tribes: Fire-Touched, Ivory Claws, Predator Kings) reject Luna → see `06_antagonists.md`.
