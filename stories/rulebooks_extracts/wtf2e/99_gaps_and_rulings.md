## RAW-silent points & rulings (spirits; the test case focus)
- **Can a Twilight spirit use Numina on material targets without Manifesting?** RAW: Numina are discrete powers; Twilight
  only restricts physical interaction; Reaching is needed only *across the Gauntlet*. **Ruling: yes**, a spirit present in
  Flesh (Twilight) may use Numina/Influences on material targets; it still bleeds 1 Essence/hour unless protected (06 §C).
- **Does a Fettered spirit bleed?** No — Fettered protects (p. 190). But a Fettered spirit **can't use Influences or Numina on
  anything except the Fetter** (p. 190). To act on others it must **Unfetter** (1 Ess, scene) — and then it is outside its
  protecting Condition and **bleeds 1/hour** during that scene [interp: Unfetter suppresses the Fettered Condition].
- **Possession vs Fetter**: Possess lasts **one scene**, puts a living host in a coma (missing time); spirit can't use
  Numina/Influences while possessing. Building **Controlled** needs successful possessions on separate occasions = higher
  Willpower of spirit or host.
- **Open on a person**: needs Resonant first (the person matches the spirit's Influence — e.g. a grieving person for grief),
  then scenes of "acting in concert" = higher of Rank or Resolve, **or** a Strengthen Influence (instant, temporary), **or**
  Lunacy / Tells. RAW gives no roll for the extended fine-tuning — [interp] treat it as a narrative clock of scenes.
- **Resisting Fetter on a human**: Resolve + Composure + PU (humans PU 0); **Urged** then lets the spirit read thoughts and
  push urges (separate rolls).
- **Spirit Defense vs firearms**: spirits apply Defense to **all** attacks (p. 185) — but bullets can't hurt a Twilight spirit
  anyway; vs a Materialized spirit, lethal becomes bashing unless bane.
- **Mortals hitting a Twilight spirit**: impossible except with its **bane** (lethal per turn of contact / as weapon lethal)
  or powers. A bane weapon vs a Twilight spirit deals **lethal** (agg only if the spirit is Manifesting or both in Hisil).
- **Image** makes a spirit visible but not tangible; still Twilight → still bleeds.
- **Essence regain** while lurking near its Resonant source: +1/day automatic (even across Gauntlet) + one gorge roll/day.
  So a Rank 1–2 spirit loitering in Flesh outside a Fetter loses 24/day and gains ~1–4/day → **must Fetter** to survive.
- **Ghosts vs spirits**: mutually invisible without a Numen (p. 183).
- **Abjuration/exorcism by mortals**: WtF only gives rites (Banish, Expel, Shadowbind) for Uratha/Wolf-Blooded; mortal
  abjuration/exorcism rules are in core (`../cod_core/05_systems.md`); exorcism removes **Open** (p. 189).

## Self-test round 1 (2026-10-12) — gaps found and rulings adopted
Test: a Rank 2 loneliness spirit vs two mortals (Reach → Hallucination → Open → Breach → Fetter/Urged → Possess →
abjuration → Drain → Breach home). No rules errors traced to the extracts; one misleading phrase fixed (possession
penalties **shrink** by 1/day Fettered). RAW-silent points, ruled [interp]:
- **Starting Essence** of a spirit: not given → ST choice, default ≈ ⅔ of max (or max if well fed).
- **Speed species factor**: only "objects 0" given → conceptual/emotion spirits 0, animal spirits use the animal's factor.
- **Essence on a failed roll**: spent anyway (cost is paid to activate).
- **Bleed timing**: 1 Essence per **started** hour unprotected in Flesh (round up).
- **Daily order**: upkeep −1, then +1 proximity, then the gorge roll; Essence above max is lost.
- **Gorging while Fettered**: allowed (not an Influence/Numen). Gorging is passive feeding on resonance; it does not harm
  the source.
- **Spirit perception across the Gauntlet**: a spirit with the Reaching Condition perceives the other side at the conduit
  (it already senses Essence sources within a mile across the Gauntlet, p. 184).
- **Open "acting in concert"** [HR]: a scene counts only if the spirit *actively* strengthens/exploits the resonance **and** the
  subject's behaviour reinforces it (e.g. he actually isolates himself) — merely feeling it near the spirit doesn't count
  (that's just Resonant). A scene where the subject clearly rejects it (e.g. wins a contest vs its power) doesn't count.
  Simpler: Strengthen fast track (06 §H).
- **Controlled**: "Willpower" = permanent Willpower dots; possessions must be in separate scenes.
- **Claim prerequisite**: the spirit must *have* both the Fetter and Possess Manifestations (book wording, p. 187).
- **Urged**: pools are RAW (read thoughts P+F vs Resolve + PU; urge P+F vs Resolve + Composure — no extra core die for WtF
  spirits); no Essence cost listed. [HR] instant actions, at most one attempt per urge per scene.
- **Possessed host in combat**: host's Defense/Initiative (spirit's Initiative if it leaves mid-fight → new roll); spirit
  may spend **its own** Willpower.
- **Drain** (RAW reading): winner gains its own successes (not net), capped by what the loser has; vs humans only Willpower.
  [interp] not mental influence → Indomitable doesn't apply.
- **Hallucination**: senses altered = successes **over** the contesting roll (RAW, core p. 137). [HR] lasts at most a scene.
  [HR] Sign: extra successes = longer/clearer message.
- **Influence on its Fetter**: −1 Essence, minimum 0.
- **Contested Influence durations** count the spirit's total successes (not net).
- **Abjuration** (core p. 140) applies to WtF spirits and suppresses **all Conditions tagged to the entity** within the
  abjurist's Willpower in yards for a day — **including natural Resonant** (RAW: core p. 140 says "tagged by"; p. 135 lets
  abjuration suppress Resonant). The person stays lonely/grieving; the spirit just can't use that correspondence. So an
  abjured spirit with nothing else Resonant nearby **can't Gauntlet Breach out** (Breach needs Resonant) and is stuck bleeding.
  [HR] When suppression lapses the Fetter resumes if the spirit is within 5 yd; otherwise it is lost.
- **"Abjurist counts as its bane"** (exceptional abjuration): her touch/unarmed strikes count as the bane (06 §D), and the
  spirit must spend WP + Power + Resistance − Rank to touch her voluntarily.
- **Bane touching a Possessed host**: [interp] 1 lethal per turn to the spirit (as for a Fetter); a bane weapon striking the
  host deals **lethal** Corpus damage (the possessor is in Twilight, p. 190), not aggravated.
- **Ban violation**: **no universal penalty in RAW** (p. 185 gives per-spirit examples). Write the compulsion *and* the
  consequence into each spirit's ban. Default if forgotten [HR]: the spirit can't knowingly break it; forced → loses all WP.
- **Ties** in ordinary contested rolls: the defender wins [interp]. **Exception (RAW): Clash of Wills ties are rerolled** (p. 115).
- **Spirit perception across the Gauntlet**: RAW only lets it *detect Essence sources* within a mile (p. 184). Seeing/hearing
  ordinary people across is [HR] (via a Reaching conduit). At a locus no Reaching is needed for powers (p. 184).
- Labels: starting Essence, Speed factor, started-hour bleed, daily order, Open criteria, Urge frequency, Hallucination
  duration, Sign extras, ban defaults and post-abjuration Fetter are **house rules**; Essence-on-failure and gorging while
  Fettered are strong interpretations.
