# WtF 2e — Gifts, Rites, Fetishes [Ch3 pp. 114–149]

## 0. Gift rules [pp. 114–115]
- Three kinds: **Moon Gifts** (one per auspice, written by Luna at the First Change), **Shadow Gifts** (granted by spirits —
  the spirit **tears the mark in: 1 lethal damage** — or taken at the end of a Siskur-Dah; a spirit can only grant Gifts
  matching its nature), **Wolf Gifts** (natural; all always "unlocked").
- Each Gift has **5 Facets**. Moon Gift Facets are rated •–••••• and tied to auspice Renown. Shadow/Wolf Gift Facets are
  each tied to one **Renown** (Cunning, Glory, Honor, Purity, Wisdom).
- Facet **dice pool = Attribute + Skill + the Facet's Renown**. Some contested vs **Resolve/Composure + Primal Urge**
  (non-werewolves add their power stat: vampire Blood Potency, changeling Wyrd…; spirits typically use **Resistance**
  or Rank where stated).
- **Acquisition/XP** (also in 02): Moon Gift Facet free at each auspice Renown dot (must be in order, ≤ auspice Renown);
  extra Moon Gift 5 XP + 2 XP/Facet. **Shadow Gift: unlock 3 XP (affinity) / 5 XP (other), gives one Facet; more Facets
  2 XP each; can never have a Facet whose Renown is 0.** Each new Renown dot: a free Facet of that Renown in a Gift you
  have (banked if none available). **Wolf Gift Facet: 1 XP** [p. 115] (p. 85 says 2 — see 99), needs ≥1 dot in its Renown.
- **Facet dice modifiers** [p. 114] (dice pools only, not Renown-based static values):
  +1 in area of a locus rated 1–3 · +1 vs a target whose blood she has tasted · **+2 vs her tribe's favoured prey** ·
  **+2 during her auspice moon phase** · +2 near a locus 4+ · **−2 in presence of an Uratha in Death Rage** ·
  **−3 in contact with silver**.
- **Clash of Wills** [p. 115]: conflicting powers → each rolls **Primal Urge + Facet's Renown** (or auspice Renown for
  other abilities, e.g. Urhan Chase Down vs Celerity); ties reroll; WP allowed if present & aware; enduring effects +1
  (day) / +2 (week) / +3 (month) / +4 (year+). Vampires use Blood Potency, etc. **A spirit uses its Rank; + its dots in the
  Influence if the clash comes from an Influence.**
- Many 3-dot Moon Facets "can only be activated when the werewolf gains the **Siskur-Dah** Condition" (see 05 §Siskur-Dah).

  about its immediate area (exceptional: longer answer; dramatic: lie).
- ••• **Shadow Hunter** — 1 Ess, needs Siskur-Dah: in the Shadow pursuing the prey, +Wisdom to Perception, to **resisting
  Numina & Dread Powers**, and to travel/chase rolls in the Shadow.

  successes (+ its ban or bane) from a perceived spirit; appear as that kind of spirit; suspicious behaviour lets spirits
  roll Finesse + Resistance − mimicked Rank to see through (exceptional: also −Wisdom). Dramatic: gains its ban as Ban Condition.
- ••••• **Panopticon** — Essence = max Rank affected, **Int + Occult + Wisdom**, extended 10 successes (1 min/roll), 1 scene
  (+1 Ess/scene): aware of all spirits up to that Rank within **Wisdom×100 yd** (−3 own Perception), rough sense of what
  each perceives; instant action to ride one spirit's senses; 1 Ess → −Wisdom to that spirit's Perception for a scene.

## 4. Rites [pp. 138–146]
**Procedure**: ritemaster (any Uratha knowing it; Wolf-Blooded with Horse/Spirit Double/Waystone Tells may lead **Pack Rites**)
+ helpers knowing it = teamwork. **Wolf Rites**: werewolves only (any packs). **Pack Rites**: all participants same pack;
non-knowers can take part (no teamwork bonus) and benefit; Wolf-Blooded can learn them. Each rite lists **symbols** which must
all be incorporated meaningfully — **missing one = dramatic failure**. **Pool = Attribute + Skill fitting the performance**
(ST & players agree; sample given). Mostly **extended**; **interruption (e.g. a participant injured) = dramatic failure**.
Gauntlet does **not** affect rites (except Shadowcall across the Gauntlet).
Results: dramatic → fails, ritemaster gains **Shadowlashed**; failure → no successes + **Stumbled**; success → accumulate;
exceptional → ritemaster gains **Symbolic Focus**.
Modifiers: +1 auspice moon in sky; +1 tribe symbolised; +1 all in Dalu; +1 all show shared symbols; +1 totem involved; −1
distracting; **−1 between dawn and dusk**; −1 another pack's territory; −2 very distracting (combat).
Learning: teacher/source + **1 XP per dot**. Can't invent rites, only discover them.

## 5. Fetishes & talens [pp. 146–149]
- **Fetish** (•–•••••, = Rank of the bound spirit): made with the Fetish rite (1 Ess/dot). **Activation: Resolve + Composure
  − rating, or spend 1 Essence instead.** Over-feeding a forced spirit may let it escape. Destroying a fetish releases its
  Essence to the destroyer (05 §7). Identify with Int + Occult; any werewolf touching one senses its power.
  Power guide: • minor (Specialty-ish bonus, Influence 1); •• useful (+3 to a Facet, minor Tilt on first strike); •••
  potent (vehicle +3 Handling, short flight in Shadow, Influence 3); •••• strong (weapon damage+, +3 & 9-again all Social,
  +3 Defense vs spirit type); ••••• legendary (cuts Essence/WP, 8-again + rote, rule-breaking).
