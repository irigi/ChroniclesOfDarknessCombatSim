# WtF 2e — Character Creation, XP, Anchors, Pack & Totem [Ch3, pp. 81–93]

## Steps [pp. 81–83; quick ref p. 84]
1. **Concept** + **3 Aspirations** (1–2 short-term, 1 long-term).
2. **Attributes 5/4/3** (1 free dot in each Attribute first; max 5).
3. **Skills 11/7/4** (max 5).
4. **3 Skill Specialties.**
5. **Forsaken template** (everything here is **free**, not XP):
   - **Auspice** (moon at First Change, not chosen in-fiction): **+1 free dot in one of the 3 auspice Skills** (can't exceed 5)
     + **1 dot of auspice Renown**. Note Auspice Benefit & Hunter's Aspect. (Table → `08_factions_map.md`.)
   - **Tribe** (chosen): **+1 dot of tribal Renown**. Ghost Wolf: no tribal Renown, no Gift affinities.
   - **Renown**: +1 more dot anywhere, but **no Renown at 3 dots** at creation. Totals: tribal Uratha **3 Renown dots**
     (e.g. auspice 1 + tribe 1 + 1 free; if auspice=tribe Renown e.g. Rahu Hunter = Purity 2 + 1 elsewhere); Ghost Wolf **2**.
   - **Blood** and **Bone** archetypes (Anchors, p. 85).
   - **Two Touchstones**: one **physical (Flesh)**, one **spiritual** (p. 87) — usually set during pack creation.
   - **Kuruth triggers**: choose 1 personal set (Blood/Moon/The Other/Pack/Territory/Wounds) [p. 84, 103].
   - **Essence**: starts at **7** [quick ref p. 84] (max by Primal Urge → 05).
   - **Gifts**: (a) **1st Facet of auspice Moon Gift**; (b) **one Facet each of two Shadow Gifts** from tribe/auspice
     affinities; (c) **one Wolf Gift Facet — or the 2nd Moon Gift Facet if auspice Renown is 2**. Can't take a Facet whose
     Renown you have 0 dots in. Ghost Wolf: only **one** Shadow Gift and **can't start with the 2-dot Moon Gift** [p. 50].
   - **Rites: 2 dots.**
6. **Merits: 10 dots** (Forsaken Merits p. 105, general p. 110). Merit dots may also buy:
   **Primal Urge at 5 Merit dots per dot** (PU 2 = 5, PU 3 = 10; **max PU 3 at start**) and up to **5 Merit dots → rites**
   (1:1). **Free: Totem • (one dot)** and **Language (First Tongue)**.
7. **Advantages** (Hishu values; forms modify → 05):
   - **Defense** = lower of Dex/Wits + Athletics. **Health** = Size + Stamina. **Willpower** = Resolve + Composure.
   - **Initiative** = Dex + Composure. **Speed** = 5 + Str + Dex. **Size 5**.
   - **Primal Urge 1** (unless bought). **Harmony 7**.
   (Quick ref on p. 84 says "Defense is the lower of Dexterity and Wits" — omits +Athletics; p. 83 and core include it.)

### Free-trait checklist for a starting Uratha
auspice Skill +1 · auspice Renown 1 · tribal Renown 1 · free Renown 1 · Moon Gift Facet 1 · 2 Shadow Gift Facets ·
Wolf Gift Facet (or Moon Facet 2) · rites 2 dots · Totem 1 · First Tongue · PU 1 · Harmony 7 · Essence 7 · 2 Touchstones ·
Blood/Bone · Kuruth trigger set. **None of these cost XP.**

## Experience [pp. 84–85]
5 Beats = 1 Experience. **Costs:**
| Trait | XP |
|---|---|
| Attribute (per dot) | 4 |
| Skill (per dot) | 2 |
| Specialty | 1 |
| Merit (per dot) | 1 |
| Gift, **affinity** Shadow Gift (unlocks + 1st Facet) | 3 |
| Gift, **non-affinity** (or extra Moon Gift) | 5 |
| Additional Facet of an unlocked Gift | 2 |
| Wolf Gift Facet | quick-ref table says 1; text says **2** [p. 85] → see 99 |
| Renown (per dot) | 3 (must also earn it by a Renown-worthy deed) |
| Rites (per dot) | 1 (needs a teacher) |
| Primal Urge (per dot) | 5 |
Rules [pp. 85, 99, 115]: **every** new Renown dot grants a free Shadow or Wolf Gift Facet of that Renown; a new **auspice**
Renown dot *additionally* grants the next Moon Gift Facet — i.e. **two Facets**. **All Wolf Gifts count as unlocked.** Moon Gift Facets must be bought **in order**
and can never exceed the auspice Renown dots. Harmony can't be bought — only breaking points move it [p. 104].
Renown: "spend 3 Experiences to seal the deal" after a Renown-worthy deed (Lunes judge; sacrifice/stakes required) [p. 99].
Can pre-allocate future XP or bank the deed (ST choice).

### Beats [p. 85]
Fulfil Aspiration · resolve Condition · Persistent Condition triggers · once per scene take dramatic failure instead of
failure · **lethal damage in one of the rightmost Health boxes** · end of session. Optional **pack Beats** (pool, split
evenly at chapter end). Cahalith "foretold detriment" also gives a Beat (08).

## Anchors [pp. 85–89]
### Blood & Bone (replace Virtue/Vice for Uratha)
- **Blood** (behaviour on the hunt): regain **1 WP** for a clearly bad heat-of-the-moment choice per archetype; regain
  **all WP** when submitting to Kuruth or calling a hunt on a whim per archetype.
- **Bone** (identity): regain **1 WP** forcing down rage to act per archetype; **all WP** when letting rational mind
  interfere with the hunt / cause pack conflict per archetype.
- Blood archetypes: **Alpha, Challenger, Destroyer, Fox, Monster, Soldier**. Bone: **Community Organizer, Cub, Guru,
  Hedonist, Lone Wolf, Wallflower** (each has 1-WP and all-WP trigger, pp. 86–87).

### Touchstones [pp. 87–88]
- **Physical Touchstone** → **+2 to rolls to resist Harmony *loss*** (breaking points toward Spirit).
- **Spiritual Touchstone** (spirit, totem, locus, philosophy) → **+2 to resist Harmony *gain*** (toward Flesh).
- **Harmony 8+**: no access to spiritual Touchstone. **Harmony ≤2**: no access to physical Touchstone.
- **Losing** a Touchstone: Harmony moves **1 point away** (physical dies → toward Spirit, i.e. −1 Harmony; spiritual fades →
  toward Flesh, +1). Making a new one = breaking point in the other direction.
- Meaningful reinforcement of a Touchstone bond: **+1 WP**; risking life/pack for it: **all WP**.
- Examples: Abuser, Ex, Old Gang, Parents, Religion, Sponsor (physical); Ambitious Totem, Buddy Spirit, Future Self,
  Locus, Lune, Prey, Wilds (spiritual).

## Pack creation [pp. 89–93]
Tiers: **Uratha** (PCs) → **Wolf-Blooded** (1 per player suggested) → **Periphery** humans → **Totem**.
- **Supporting Wolf-Blooded** [p. 90–91]: 2 Aspirations, **Virtue & Vice**, Attributes 5/4/3, **15 Skill dots** any
  split, one **Tell** (Appendix 1), **5 Merit dots** (Totem max 1), derived traits as normal. **Never suffer Lunacy.**
  (Full Wolf-Blooded PC rules → `05_systems.md` §Wolf-Blooded.)
- **Periphery human** [p. 91]: name, concept, 1 Aspiration, **three dice pools at 5/4/3** (actions, e.g. "Dirty Fighting"),
  **all other actions 2 dice**, **Willpower 2**.
- Others (spirits, wolves, vampires…) can be pack members at Wolf-Blooded level [p. 90].

### Totem [pp. 91–92]
- Pack always has the **Resonant Condition** for its totem [p. 91]; totem helps the hunt in the Shadow.
- Totem **Aspiration** = 4th Aspiration for each member. Totem **ban** appropriate to its Rank; member who disregards
  it once gains it as a **Ban Condition**; breach again → loses Totem Merit dots.
- **Totem points** = sum of members' Totem Merit dots (max 5 per character).
- **Pack advantage** (every member, incl. Wolf-Blooded/humans) bought with XP pool: **1–8 pts → 1 XP; 9–14 → 3; 15–20 → 5;
  20+ → 10** (Attribute, Skill, Specialty or Merit; Skill/Attribute may exceed normal max by 1; humans at 5 get 8-again;
  duplicate Specialty → Area of Expertise; can't grant Dedicated Locus/Totem).
- **Totem's traits**: 1 Attribute dot per Totem point (min 1 each; no more than half in one); Rank by spirit Attribute
  table (06); Influence 1 dot per Rank; max Essence = lower of Totem points or Rank max; **1 Numen + 1 per 4 Totem points**.
- Totem Merit also adds its dots to Social actions with that totem.
