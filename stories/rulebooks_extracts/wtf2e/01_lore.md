# WtF 2e — Lore: the Uratha, the Shadow, the Oath [Intro + Ch2, pp. 8–77]

Mechanics for everything named here live in `05_systems.md` (forms, Essence, Harmony, Kuruth, Lunacy, Reaching) and
`06_antagonists.md` (spirits, Hosts, Ridden, idigam, Pure). This file = what the world *is*.

## Core identity [pp. 8–11, 57–63]
- Uratha = werewolves, descendants of **Urfarah (Father Wolf)** and **Luna (Mother Luna)**. Not created by bite — "If you're
  bitten by a werewolf, you die" [p. 9]. Born to it; the **First Change** (usually in teens/20s) is triggered by stress
  once the *nuzusul* (pre-Change werewolf) is pushed to a breaking point; spirits harass him beforehand [p. 57].
- Moon phase at First Change = **auspice** (role). Tribe = **chosen** (sworn later) [pp. 15, 34].
- **Five forms**: Hishu (human), Dalu (near-human), Gauru (war/killing form, carries Rage), Urshul (near-wolf),
  Urhan (wolf) [pp. 61–63]. Intelligence kept in all forms (except in Kuruth) [p. 9].
- Regenerate fast; spending Essence to heal feels like a euphoric chill (some get addicted) [p. 60].
- **First Tongue** (*Uremehir*): understood (gist) immediately after the First Change; fluency must be learned (from
  Uratha or a spirit). Spoken in Hishu (roughly), most fluent in **Dalu/Gauru**, still possible in Urshul, **lost in
  Urhan**. Both wolf forms talk to wolves via body language/vocalisation [p. 57]. (Merit: Language (First Tongue) is
  free at creation [p. 83].)
- Weaknesses (see 05): **silver**, Luna's curse of madness (Kuruth/Death Rage), Harmony drift.

## Pack, territory, protectorate [pp. 63–68]
- Pack = core of werewolves + optionally **Wolf-Blooded** (*uragarum*) and humans; holds a **territory** on both sides of
  the Gauntlet; has a **totem** spirit. Wolf-Blooded rarely taken into the Hisil.
- **Protectorate** = loose bottom-up federation of packs in a region (city/county) for threats too big for one pack.
- Territory changes in Flesh change the Shadow and vice versa: killing spirits alone doesn't fix resonance; must change
  human conditions too [pp. 66–67].

## Mythic history — the Sundering [pp. 64–65]
- **Pangaea**: Flesh and Spirit once overlapped via the **Border Marches**; Urfarah hunted spirits that crossed, thinned
  humanity, warred with progenitors of the Hosts (Plague King, Spinner-Hag).
- Luna (*Amahan Iduth*, the warding moon, an Ilusah) and Urfarah created the Uratha. Urfarah weakened; his **ban**: not to
  defend himself against a killing blow from those who could replace him; **bane**: his children's teeth.
- The first werewolves + five great wolf spirits (later the **Firstborn**) killed him. His **death howl** shattered the
  Border Marches → **the Gauntlet**. Luna cursed them with madness and burning at silver's touch.
- **Pure** (Anshega) = followers of the wolf spirits who didn't take part; think killing Urfarah was a mistake, reject Luna.
  **Forsaken** = Tribes of the Moon, took up Father Wolf's duty, pledged to Luna. (Name "Forsaken" coined by the Pure.)
- **Idigam** (Moon-Banished): spirits of vanished concepts locked on the moon by Luna at Urfarah's request; **released by
  the moon landing**; hate Urfarah's descendants [p. 8] → 06.

## The Oath of the Moon [pp. 68–71]
Sworn to Luna; judged by **Lunes** (bipolar moon spirits). Tenets:
1. **Urum da takus — The Wolf Must Hunt** (no werewolf can transgress; failing to hunt invites rival packs).
2. **Imru nu fir Imru — The People Do Not Murder the People** (beating another werewolf OK; killing a defeated foe =
   murder; many count Wolf-Blooded/human pack members as People; debate whether Pure count).
3. **Sih sehe mak; mak ne sih — The Low Honor the High; the High Respect the Low.**
4. **Ni daha — Respect Your Prey** (kill from necessity; humans can be prey, but…)
5. **Nu hu uzu eren — Do Not Eat the Flesh of Man or Wolf** (rush of Essence, drags toward spirit; Kuruth temptation).
6. **Nu bath githul — The Herd Must Not Know.**
7. **Uratha safal thil lu'u — The Uratha Shall Cleave to the Human** (keep human ties).
Tribal vows add a tribe-specific tenet (see 08). Oathbreaker = **Sanghba'zir**. Punishments: pack judgement (Elodoth),
ritual injury, silver amputation, exile, death, imprisonment by Lunes [pp. 70–71].
(Mechanically, Harmony breaking points are based on the Flesh/Spirit balance, not the Oath per se → 05.)

## The Shadow (Hisil) [pp. 71–76]
- **Resonance**: emotional/conceptual flavour of a place, generated by events in Flesh, produces **Essence** in the Shadow;
  feeds spirits. Psychics, Uratha and some Wolf-Blooded sense it.
- **Gauntlet** (*Zathu*): membrane between worlds; **thickened by human activity**, thin in quiet still places.
  Crossing = **Reaching** (*far'huf*); needs a **locus** for most Uratha/spirits; travellers hang a few seconds in a grey
  void, **safe from attack and unable to move** while crossing. Some beings Reach without a locus (low-Harmony Uratha,
  some Wolf-Blooded, powerful spirits/sorcerers). Beshilu gnaw it thin; Azlu web/thicken it [pp. 71–72].
- **Day vs night**: by day the Gauntlet's current pushes *away* from the Hisil (harder to enter); fewer active spirits;
  shafts of Helios-light increase "gravity". Night = Shadow alive, Luna visible [p. 73].
- Shadow shows inner nature of places; remembers demolished buildings; **no spirits of humans** (people not reflected);
  dormant spirits hard for humans to spot, obvious to Uratha (scent of Essence). Senses work as synesthesia for Uratha.
- Hierarchy: **umia** (taxonomic choir), **ilthum** (voluntary like-natured group), **courts** (local spirits, strong rule weak).
- Special places: **Places-That-Aren't** (distance warped), **Shoals** (emptiness; drain will to leave), **Glades** (peace;
  Kuruth distant), **Wounds** (ruptures at atrocity sites; taint spirits; ruled by **Maeljin**, Lords of Wounds),
  **Barrens** (Essence-starved; even Gifts hard to use) [pp. 75–76].
- Urban Shadow: courts of buildings/roads/vehicles; edges of civilisation most dangerous (orphaned nature spirits, magath).
  Rural: older, simpler, might-makes-right courts; may remember the Sundering.
- Sample spirits [pp. 74–75]: Blightlings, Glass Eyes (snitches for Essence), Vermin (Nihhilim), Travels Below, Allholders,
  Darkenings (grief fog); Wolf-Brothers (Uralath — teach Gifts), Swift Waters, Old Men (mountains; Ensah+, Everest =
  Dihir+), Those That Wait (oaks; mediators), Beach Carrion Thieves (gulls; immune to magath-ing), Iron Horses (trains).
