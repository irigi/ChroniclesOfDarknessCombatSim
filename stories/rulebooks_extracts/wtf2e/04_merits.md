## Werewolf Fighting Merits [pp. 108–110] (prereqs use Hishu traits)
- **Call Out** (2; Honor 2, Intimidation 2, Com 3): instant action to challenge; that opponent takes **−Honor** to attack anyone
  else; if he does, add **Honor** to your attacks on him. (If someone else attacks him, challenge breaks; he gets +Honor vs you
  for the scene.)
- **Efficient Killer** (2; Purity 2, Brawl 3, Medicine 2, Str 3; **Gauru only**): when an opponent is completely denied
  Defense (all-out attack, ambush, etc.), sacrifice your Defense this turn → **Killing Blow** (§core). Living targets with weak
  points only. Common Kuruth trigger (drawback).
- **Flanking** (2; Cunning 2, Wits 3, Stealth 2, Brawl 2): on a successful attack, may apply successes as a **penalty to the
  victim's Initiative and Defense this turn** instead of damage.
- **Instinctive Defense** (2; PU 2, Athletics 2): in **Urhan/Urshul**, Defense uses the **higher** of Wits/Dex.
- **Relentless Assault** (Style 1–5; Str 3, Sta 3, Brawl 2; Brawl attacks only; usable in Kuruth):
  •Drop of a Hat: first turn of a fight **+3 Initiative** if making an all-out attack. ••Eye of the Tiger: all-out attack vs
  chosen target keeps Defense vs him. •••Dig Deep: remove 1 die before rolling → +1 claws/teeth weapon modifier.
  ••••Grin and Bear It: all-out attack → **1/1 armor** that turn. •••••The Warpath: filling an opponent's last box with L/A →
  immediate extra attack vs another in reach; if it damages, **enter Basu-Im** (no resist).
- **Spiritual Blockage** (2; Wisdom 2, Brawl 1, Occult 3, Wits 3): Brawl/Weaponry attack at **−2** vs an Essence-user; if it
  damages, victim **loses 1 Essence** (2 on exceptional) — counts as *spent* (eats per-turn limit).
- **Tactical Shifting** (Style 1–5; Wits 3, Dex 3, Athletics 2; each move needs a reflexive shift; "up" = larger Size):
  •Springloading: shift down at start of round → **+2 Init, +5 Speed** this turn. ••Broaden: shift up → adjacent foes who
  don't Dodge take bashing = Size difference; damaged → Dex + Athletics or **Knocked Down**. •••Fluid Movement: when
  attacked shift down → attackers −1 per Size lost. ••••Suck it Up: when struck shift up → ignore 1 B/L damage.
  •••••Crush: grappling/biting, shift up → automatic lethal = Size difference.
- **Warcry** (2; Glory 2, Pre 3, Expression 2, Intimidation 2; **Gauru/Urshul/Urhan only**): instant **Presence + Expression**;
  affects listeners of your choice up to successes: **−1 Defense and attack rolls, −2 Initiative** for the scene (once per
  scene per target). (Howl echoes in the Hisil: curious, maybe hostile, spirits arrive within 3 turns.)

## Human Merits reprinted in WtF [pp. 110–113] (any character; more in `../cod_core/04_merits.md`)
- **Allies** (1–5 per group): favours totalling rating per chapter free; beyond → Manipulation + Persuasion + Allies − favour
  rating, lose a dot (returns end of chapter; exceptional keeps it); can block another's Allies/Contacts/Mentor/Retainer/Status.
- **Alternate Identity** (1–3): superficial / papers / deep; +1 (••• +2) Subterfuge to defend it.
- **Anonymity** (1–5; no Fame): −1 per dot to find via paper trail.
- **Area of Expertise** (1; Res 2 + Specialty): that Specialty gives +2 instead of +1.
- **Barfly** (2; Socialize 2): no roll to blend into social functions; spotting her as outsider −Socialize.
- **Contacts** (1 per sphere): info via Manipulation + Social Skill (±3); can dig up Social Merits & Conditions.
- **Fame** (1–3): +dot Social with impressed; +dot to find/identify her.
- **Fleet of Foot** (1–3; Athletics 2): +1 Speed/dot; pursuers −1/dot.
- **Indomitable** (2; Resolve 3): **+2 to contest supernatural mental/emotional influence**, or **−2 to the power's pool** if
  resisted passively (e.g. Lunacy?, charms, ghost fear) — mundane persuasion unaffected.
- **Inspiring** (3; Pre 3): Presence + Expression (−1 clique, −2 small crowd, −3 large) → listeners gain **Inspired**; not self.
- **Iron Will** (2; Resolve 4): spending WP to resist/contest Social → add **Resolve instead of +3**; contested → 8-again.
- **Language** (1 per language).
- **Mentor** (1–5): 3 Skills (or Resources); once per session a favour; auto-successes = rating.
- **Parkour** (Style 1–5; Dex 3, Athletics 2): •Flow (−Parkour successes needed in foot chase; ignore env. penalties ≤ rating)
  ••Cat Leap (reduce falls incl. lethal; + rating) •••Wall Run (10 ft + 5/Athletics dot without roll) ••••Expert Traceur
  (WP → rote Athletics; no Defense that turn) •••••Freeflow (after 1 min running, reflexive Athletics once/turn; WP = +3
  successes in chase).
- **Resources** (1–5): Availability ≤ rating once per chapter; +1 above → −1 Resources for a month; 2 below → unlimited.
- **Retainer** (1–5): field rolls = 2×rating dice; other rolls = rating.
- **Safe Place** (1–5; shareable): +rating Initiative when attacked there (investors only); breach attempts −rating; traps up
  to rating lethal (needs Crafts 1; Dex + Larceny − rating to avoid).
- **Sleight of Hand** (2; Larceny 3): one reflexive Larceny instant action per turn; unnoticed unless watched for.
- **Staff** (1–5): per dot one assistant type + Skill; auto 1 success on mundane tasks.
- **Status** (1–5 per group): +Status to Social rolls in the group; facilities; once per chapter block a lower-rated Merit.
- **Striking Looks** (1–2): +1/+2 Social where looks matter; same bonus to spot/remember her.
- **Sympathetic** (2): at start of Social maneuvering accept a Condition (Leveraged, Swooning…) → remove 2 of subject's Doors.
- **Unseen Sense** (2; **humans only**, not Uratha/Wolf-Blooded): sense a chosen supernatural type nearby; once per chapter take
  **Spooked** to pinpoint the source (fails vs powers that cloak supernatural nature).
