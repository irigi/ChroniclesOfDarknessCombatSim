## B. Book inconsistencies / traps
- **Character-sheet creation summary (p. 328) is a 1st-edition leftover** ("favored Resistance Attribute by Path", "Arcana 2/2/1 +1", "Merits 7", "Starting Mana = Wisdom", 5th dot costs
  double). **Ignore it** — use p. 79–82: any of Composure/Resolve/Stamina +1, 6 Arcana dots, 10 Merits, Mana full by Gnosis. *(Confirmed by external audit.)*
- **Entity Defense**: "lower of Power or Finesse (Rank 1: higher)" [p. 255] vs "lower of Finesse or Resistance" [p. 257]; Stalwart [p. 263] says "Resistance instead of lower of Power or
  Finesse". **Book error → use p. 255** (two of three passages agree; audit concurs). Defense applies vs firearms.
- **Possess** contest lists "Resolve + Composure + **Primal Urge**" [p. 259] (a Werewolf trait). **Book error (copy-paste)** → Resolve + Composure + the target's **Supernatural Tolerance**
  (Gnosis for mages, Blood Potency for vampires, Primal Urge for werewolves, nothing for mortals); matches Fetter's Gnosis wording on the same page.
- **Shadow Scream** is printed as Unmaking though it's Spirit •••• [p. 183]. **Book error → Unraveling** (Spirit •••• grants Unraveling; reported errata agrees). Lethal = Potency attack.
- **Purge Illness / Lure and Repel / Heightened Senses** practice lines are scrambled in the .txt (L14408–14482); pairing by Primary Factor gives Lure & Repel = Ruling/Duration,
  Purge Illness = Shielding/Potency, Heightened Senses = Ruling/Duration. Treat Purge Illness's Practice as **uncertain** (reported errata: Ruling — affects nothing but Practice labels).

## B2. Confirmed RAW (formerly flagged; agreed by external audit and re-checked against the text)
- **Mana for improvised Common/Inferior spells**: 1 Mana [p. 86, 111]; **Rotes and Praxes waive it** (not other Mana costs) [p. 112–113]. A Legacy's extra Ruling Arcanum counts as Ruling.
- **Free Reach** = Arcanum − required + 1 (Mind 4 on a Mind 3 spell = 2) [p. 112]. **Rote**: counts the caster's Arcanum as 5 **for free Reach only** — the Primary Factor bump still uses the
  caster's **actual** Arcanum − 1 [p. 113].
- **Mudra** = encoded Skill dots, **+1 if it is one of her Order's Rote Skills** [p. 121].
- **Withstand ≠ resistance**: Gnosis/Blood Potency don't add to Withstand [p. 127] but do add to contested resistance vs other creatures' powers and Numina. Subject may choose not to
  Withstand; may spend WP on the Resistance Attribute only if aware [p. 114]. Direct Fraying/Unraveling damage isn't Withstood [p. 127]. **Multiple Withstand ratings: highest +1 per extra**
  (Resolve 3 via a Medium link 2 → 4) [p. 114]. **Combined spells: each component Withstood separately** [p. 114].
- **Aimed spells**: range Gnosis × 10/20/40 yd; **must use area Scale**; the Aimed roll (Gnosis + Athletics/Firearms − Defense) is **separate and reflexive, rolled after** casting & Paradox —
  a cast spell can still miss; no weapon-style damage bonus from attack successes [p. 113–115].
- **Dice pool floor**: penalties may exceed −5; after Yantras, **−5…0 = chance die, ≤ −6 = impossible** [p. 111, 327].
- **Yantras**: first one reflexive, each further one +1 turn on instant spells; number by Gnosis; **cap +5 net after offsetting penalties** (−6 factors + 9 Yantras = +3) [p. 112, 119, 327].
- **Primary Factor bump**: only the primary factor, (actual Arcanum − 1) steps; moving to Advanced still needs Reach [p. 113].
- **Paradox**: Gnosis-column dice **per** Reach over free (Gnosis 3, 2 over = 4 dice); witnesses add **+1 die total** and a dice quality (few 9-again, large group 8-again, crowd rote);
  **if Paradox dice were ever added but mitigated to 0 → roll a chance die; if none were ever added → no roll** [p. 115, 327]. Contain/release declared **before** the roll; containment as `03` §0.10.
- **Resistant damage** can't be healed or sped by magic incl. Pattern restoration [p. 224] — **except** explicit powers: Prelacy's **Crown of Doctrine** (Paternoster) heals resistant damage via
  Pattern restoration [p. 103].
- **Starting Mana** = full Gnosis maximum (10 at Gnosis 1) [p. 80]. **Active spells without Reach = Gnosis**; extra spells +1 Reach each, +1 more per spell already over [p. 118]. **Relinquish
  safely = 1 WP dot**; unsafely = 1 WP point + chapterly risk [p. 118]. **Spell stacking**: same aspect → only highest Potency applies, others suppressed [p. 118].
- **Counterspell** [p. 192–193]: needs that Arcanum's Counterspell (or Universal Counterspell, Prime 2); must see the casting with **Active Mage Sight** (any Arcanum shows castings, p. 92);
  instant action usable out of Initiative order if she hasn't acted; Clash Gnosis + Arcanum each; counters the highest Arcanum; 1 Mana if the caster's rating in it is higher. Can't counter
  Attainments or already-resolved spells.
- **Clash of Wills ties reroll** [p. 117].
- **Nimbus Tilt**: strength = spell Potency, lasts Reach turns (min 1), affects anyone with **Resolve ≤ strength — including people without Mage Sight, who feel only subtle cues** [p. 89]
  (willing targets may submit). Deliberate flare: 1/scene, 1 Mana, Gnosis roll, visible in the Fallen World.
- **Active Mage Sight costs** [p. 91–92]: reflexive with Ruling Arcana only; instant if adding a Common/Inferior (1 Mana per scene each); leaving reflexive; −2 to non-magic rolls; Gnosis minutes,
  then 1 WP for the scene. Sees only its Arcana's phenomena — except **all Awakened castings**.
- **Casting in a grapple**: allowed win or lose; −3 if lost; no movement Yantras [p. 118].
- **Mana spending**: may be pre-spent over several turns for costs above the per-turn limit (lost if interrupted) [p. 86]. **[interp; reported errata agrees]** spending Mana is reflexive.

## C. RAW-silent points — rulings [interp]
- **Mage Armor + worn armour**: RAW silent. Ruling: general armour **stacks** with worn armour (one is equipment, the other an Attainment; only one Mage Armor at a time is RAW). Ballistic and
  general apply as usual: worn 2/1 + Forces Armor 3 = 2 ballistic / 4 general. Defense bonuses add to Defense. *(Developer forum post reportedly supports this.)*
- **Mage Armor activation**: **reflexive**, 1 Mana, scene (switching is RAW-reflexive; reported errata makes activation reflexive too).
- **"Obvious"**: covert if a Sleeper could attribute it to chance/nature (luck, a heart attack, weather shift); obvious if visibly impossible (lightning from a hand, levitation). Guidance, not exhaustive.
- **Touch spell in melee**: RAW = must touch (Dexterity + Brawl − Defense, no damage) [p. 118, 219] but timing is unstated. Ruling, mirroring Aimed: cast the instant spell, then the touch
  roll is **reflexive in the same turn**; on a miss the spell is wasted (still counts as cast, Paradox already rolled).
- **Combined spells & exceptional success**: "ignore Withstand" applies to all components.
- **Sleeper witness counts**: 1 witness: +1 die only; 2–5: +1 die, 9-again; 6–20: 8-again; 20+: rote. Same tricks on the single Dissonance roll.
- **Counterspell timing**: declared when the Imago is seen forming, resolved **before** the target's Paradox and casting rolls; a countered spell fails, Mana spent is lost, no Paradox roll.
  Counterspell Mana counts against the per-turn limit. With Universal Counterspell and 0 dots in the target Arcanum, the caster always "has a higher rating" → 1 Mana.
- **Active Mage Sight −2 (−3 Scrutinizing)** applies to every roll not part of casting/perceiving magic — incl. reflexive physical rolls (Knocked Down avoidance, staying conscious). Aimed,
  Clash of Wills and Counterspell rolls are magic (no penalty).
- **Aimed spell area**: one Aimed roll vs the intended target; others inside the arm's-reach area are affected without Defense (Withstand still applies where relevant).
- **Spell-imposed Tilts** (+R Knocked Down etc.): no avoidance roll unless the spell says so. (House variant: Dex + Athletics − Potency.)
- **Breaking points / Acts of Hubris per scene**: one breaking point per Sleeper per scene (worst event); one Hubris roll per distinct act.
- **Dissonance scope**: only phenomena still present at scene end; ended spells and non-Legacy Attainments (Mage Armor) unaffected.
- **Nimbus radius**: within the caster's sensory range (RAW gives none).
- **Scrutinizing a foreign spell**: any Arcanum in Active Sight; the spell's own Arcanum gives the richest deep info, Prime gives structure. Opacity 0 + Revelation = all deep info; excess
  Scrutiny successes are lost.
- **Experienced characters**: no RAW table. Guideline: apprentice-veteran +35–75 XP, adept +100–150, master +200+. Praxes and Wisdom need **Arcane** XP — default ⅓ of extra XP is Arcane.
- **Surrender between mages**: core surrender rules; spells in place stay unless part of the terms.
- **Numina [R] list**: use `../wtf2e/06` only for identically named powers; a Mage-only Numen reaches across the Gauntlet only if its text says so (none here do explicitly).

## E. Reported later errata — NOT in this .txt (external audit 2026-10-12, unverified here)

may adopt them wholesale — say so on the character sheet and don't mix the two states.
