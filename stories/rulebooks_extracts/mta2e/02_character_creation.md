# MtA 2e — Character creation, advancement, Gnosis table

## Steps [p. 79–82]
1. **Concept** + 3 **Aspirations** (2 short-term, 1 long-term).
2. **Attributes**: 1 free dot each; priority **5/4/3** extra; max 5.
3. **Skills**: priority **11/7/4**; (unskilled −3 Mental, −1 Physical/Social, per core).
4. **3 Specialties**.
5. **Mage template**:
   - **Path** → 2 Ruling + 1 Inferior Arcana (`01_lore.md` table).
   - **Order** → **3 Rote Skills** (table in `01_lore.md`); if in an Order: **free Order Status •, free +1 dot Occult, free High Speech Merit** [p. 80, 82].
     Nameless: none of these (may model with Mystery Cult Initiation) [p. 80].
   - **Nimbus** (describe; design the **Nimbus Tilt**, see `05_systems.md` §Nimbus).
   - **Dedicated Magical Tool** (one; see Yantras in `05_systems.md`) [p. 80, 121].
   - **Arcana: 6 dots**; max one Arcanum at 3; **3–5 dots in Ruling Arcana, each Ruling ≥1**; **0 in Inferior**.
     Allowed spreads: Specialist 3/2/1 or 3/1/1/1; Balanced 2/2/2 or 2/2/1/1; Generalist 2/1/1/1/1 [p. 80].
   - **Rotes: 6 dots' worth** (this printing; reported later errata: 3 rotes of any level — `99` §E) (rote's dot = its highest Arcanum; must be ≤ the mage's Arcana) [p. 80].
   - **Gnosis 1** by default; **Gnosis 2 costs 5 Merit dots, Gnosis 3 costs all 10** [p. 80].
   - **Mana**: start full (max by Gnosis).
   - **Obsessions**: 1 at Gnosis 1–2; 2 at Gnosis 3–5; 3 at 6–8; 4 at 9–10 [p. 80, 84].
   - **Praxes: one per dot of Gnosis** (any spell level, each = 1 Praxis) [p. 81].
   - **Resistance Attribute**: +1 free dot to **Composure, Resolve, or Stamina** (max 5) [p. 81].
6. **Merits: 10 dots** (minus dots spent on Gnosis). Mage Merits in `04_merits.md`; general Merits from `../cod_core/`.
7. **Advantages**: Size 5; Health = Stamina + Size; Speed = 5 + Str + Dex; Willpower = Resolve + Composure;
   **Wisdom 7**; Initiative = Dex + Composure; Defense = min(Wits, Dex) + Athletics [p. 81].
   Also: **Virtue & Vice** (core-style, referenced in Ch5 p. 207).

## Experience [p. 82–84]
5 Beats = 1 Experience; 5 **Arcane Beats** = 1 **Arcane Experience**.

| Trait | Cost per dot |
|---|---|
| Attribute | 4 |
| Skill | 2 |
| Merit | 1 |
| Arcanum up to its untrained limit | 4 (regular or Arcane XP) |
| Arcanum above limit | 5 (needs teacher; **regular XP only**) |
| Gnosis | 5 (regular or Arcane XP); each Gnosis dot also grants **1 free Praxis** |
| Rote | 1 |
| Praxis | 1 (**Arcane XP only**) |
| Wisdom | 2 (**Arcane XP only**; needs "Becoming wiser" Obsession progressed for ≥1 story) [p. 88] |
| Willpower | 1 (rebuys dots spent, e.g. relinquishing spells; max = Resolve + Composure) |
| Legacy Attainment | 1 tutored (any mix); 1 untutored (**Arcane XP only**) |

- Arcane XP can buy only magical traits: Gnosis, Arcana, Praxes, Legacy Attainments (and Wisdom) [p. 84].
- **Arcanum untrained limits**: Ruling 5, Common 4, Inferior 2 [p. 84].
- **Beats** (normal, core): Aspiration fulfilled or major headway; end of session; resolve a Condition; take a dramatic failure on a failed roll; ST award.
  1 Beat per category per scene [p. 81].
- **Arcane Beats** [p. 83]: fulfil or major headway on an Obsession; resolve a Condition caused by spellcasting, Paradox or magic (letting a spell-caused Condition
  just expire doesn't count); turn a failed **spellcasting** roll into a dramatic failure; **risk an Act of Hubris** (any Wisdom
  degeneration roll); a scene being tutored in a Legacy or tutoring; a meaningful new supernatural encounter (ST).
- Resolving an Obsession also gives **+1 Mana** [p. 84]. Obsessions count as Aspirations for Social maneuvering, and add
  **+1 die to Focused Mage Sight Scrutiny** when they apply [p. 84].
- One-shot games: Beats → tokens (regain 1 WP, or 8-again on a roll, or +1 success in a contested action) [p. 85].

## Experienced starts
RAW gives no age/XP table; ST awards XP. **[interp]** Mentor guideline: an Awakened Mentor should have Gnosis ≥ the PC's + ½ the Mentor dots, and
Arcana dots ≥ the PC's + 2× the Mentor dots (still within Gnosis limits) [p. 106]. A useful yardstick for NPC mages.
