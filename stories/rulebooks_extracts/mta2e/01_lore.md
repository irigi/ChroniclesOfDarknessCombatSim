# MtA 2e — Lore (Intro, Ch1 Paths/Orders, Ch2 Through Awakened Eyes)

## Premise [p. 11–12, 59–60]
- Mages = humans who **Awakened**: on a pilgrimage (waking-world dream or Supernal journey) their soul reached a
  **Watchtower** in one of five Supernal Realms and signed it; that fixes their **Path** [p. 61]. Awakening is permanent;
  Gnosis can't be switched off [p. 12].
- **The Lie / Fallen World**: concrete reality is a prison built by the **Exarchs** (tyrant Supernal beings) from the
  symbolism of oppression. **Supernal World** = true symbolic reality. **Abyss** = everything that can't be; it leaks in
  through **Paradox**; its entities: **Gulmoth** (reality-corrupting), **Acamoth** (Astral, soul-corrupting),
  **Annunaki** (stillborn universes) [p. 60].
- **Quiescence (the Sleeping curse)**: Sleepers carry a shard of the Abyss; they forget/rationalize overt magic, and
  their presence makes Paradox worse (Sleeper witnesses) [p. 12, 60]. **Sleepwalkers** are humans immune to it [p. 12].
- **Pattern / Forms / Tapestry**: a subject's total Supernal information for an Arcanum is its "X Pattern"
  (e.g., "Life Pattern") [p. 65]. **Imago** = the imagined spell-effect the mage forces onto reality [p. 66].
- **High Speech**: the Supernal ideal of "language", learned at Awakening. It can't be used to lie, sounds like gibberish to
  Sleepers (subject to Quiescence, not a breaking point), and is used as a Yantra (Merit) [p. 66, 91].
- **Mysteries**: magic can only be learned by experiencing them; mages are compelled to investigate the uncanny [p. 66].
- **Hubris** is the great flaw. Morality = **Wisdom** [p. 12, 87].

## Paths [p. 19–33; table p. 82]
Each Path has 2 **Ruling** Arcana (one Subtle, one Gross), 1 **Inferior**, and the other 7 are Common.

| Path | Realm (abode of) | Ruling | Inferior | Supernal entities | Nicknames / tarot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Acanthus** | Arcadia (Fae) | Time, Fate | Forces | Moirae (Fate, recondite), Anachronisms (Time, manifest) | Witches/Enchanters; the Fool |
| **Mastigos** | Pandemonium (Demons) | Space, Mind | Matter | Wraiths (Mind), Imps (Space) | Warlocks/Psychonauts; the Devil |
| **Moros** | Stygia (Shades) | Matter, Death | Spirit | Specters (Death), Apeirons (Matter) | Alchemists/Necromancers; Death |
| **Obrimos** | Aether (Angels) | Forces, Prime | Death | Cherubim (Prime), Seraphim (Forces) | Theurgists/Thaumaturges; Strength/Hierophant |
| **Thyrsus** | Primal Wild (Beasts) | Life, Spirit | Mind | Totems (Spirit), Atavisms (Life) | Shamans/Ecstatics; the Moon/Hermit |

- Subtle Arcana: Death, Fate, Mind, Prime, Spirit. Gross Arcana: Forces, Life, Matter, Space, Time.
- Path flavours **Mage Sight** and the **Nimbus**. Long-Term Nimbus by Path: Thyrsus, spirits and odd diseases; Moros, hauntings, rust and breakdowns;
  Obrimos: revelations, weather swings, blackouts; Acanthus: odd luck, visions; Mastigos: fears, inner devils [p. 89].

## Orders (sects) [p. 35–55, 67–72; rote Skills p. 82]
**Pentacle** = 4 **Diamond** Orders + Free Council, allied against the Seers.

| Order | Identity | Rote Skills |
|---|---|---|
| **Adamantine Arrow** | warrior-guardians; "challenge is magical"; Talons, First Talons, Thunderbolt Guardians, Adamant Sage, Banner Wardens | Athletics, Intimidation, Medicine |
| **Guardians of the Veil** | secret police & mystery religion (Hieromage); keep magic hidden (Labyrinths of false cults); Masques; Interfectors (executioners) | Investigation, Stealth, Subterfuge |
| **Mysterium** | magic-as-living-force mystery religion; collectors/librarians (Athenaea, Censoria); Acquisitors, Censors, Savants, Curators | Investigation, Occult, Survival |
| **Silver Ladder** (théarchs) | would-be rulers/liberators of humanity; invented Consilium, Convocation, Lex Magica, Duel Arcane; Acolytes, Lictors, Factotums, Clavigers, Deacon/Archdeacon | Expression, Persuasion, Subterfuge |
| **Free Council** (Libertines) | young democratic order; "humanity is magical", magic in modern culture/science; Assemblies | Crafts, Persuasion, Science |
| **Seers of the Throne** | servants of the Exarchs; Iron Pyramid; Ministers → Tetrarchs → Pylons | Investigation, Occult, Persuasion **or** by Ministry: Hegemony: Politics, Persuasion, Empathy; Panopticon: Investigation, Stealth, Subterfuge; Paternoster: Academics, Occult, Expression; Praetorian: Athletics, Larceny, Intimidation |

- **Nameless** / apostates: no Order; no Rote Skills (a Nameless Order can be built with Mystery Cult Initiation; its 3rd dot may grant 3 Rote Skills) [p. 80].
- Seers' **Great Ministries**: Hegemony (Unity, nations), Panopticon (Eye, surveillance), Paternoster (Father, faith), Praetorian (General, violence).
  Lesser Ministries include Mammon (Chancellor) [p. 72].

## Mage society [p. 67–72]
- **Cabal** (2–30 mages; themed symbolism → Cabal Theme Merit); **Solitaries** exist.
- **Consilium**: Hierarch + 4–5 Councilors (one per Path ideally); Provosts; **Sentinels** (enforcers); Heralds; **Interfector**
  (a masked Guardian executioner) [p. 69].
- **Lex Magica**: four layers of law. **Gold** (Time Before), **Silver** (local charters), **Bronze** (precedent), **Iron** (oaths and
  treaties); higher layers override lower ones. Common Precepts: **Secrecy** (the Veil), **Protectorate** (sanctum inviolate), **Hubris** (no
  harming others with magic without reason), **War** (rules of engagement; prefer the Duel Arcane) [p. 69].
  Punishments: reprimand, debt or penance, prison, banishment, death, death plus destruction of the soul.
- **Left-Handed** = soul abuse, destroying magical potential, interfering with Awakenings, Abyss contact, Lower Depths,
  cheating death [p. 69–70]. Using another mage's *sympathetic (birth) name* is very rude, and a crime in some Orders [p. 67].
- **Caucus** = regional Order body (spans 3–6 Consilia). **Assembly** = Free Council voting body. **Convocation** = inter-Consilium
  gathering run by **Magisters** (anyone with mastery of an Arcanum) and a court of appeal [p. 70–72].
- **Shadow Name**: the magical persona. It shields the sympathetic name and confines Long-Term Nimbus effects to the mage's
  magical life [p. 67].
- **Legacies**: soul-shaping sub-traditions granting **Attainments**; adepts without one are unusual; Left-Handed
  Legacies are banned by Silver Law [p. 72–73]. Rules in `03_spells.md` §Legacies.

## Awakening [p. 60–65]
Stirring (Nascent Obsession / First Mystery / Sudden Noise) → **waking-world dream** (body acts in the real world while the soul
journeys; only same-Path mages with Mage Sight see it) or **Supernal journey** (comatose) → reach and mark the Watchtower.
Interfering may abort it.

## Supernal entities (summary; rules in `05_systems.md` §Summoning)
Manifest (Gross) vs recondite (Subtle). Each entity is built around 2 Arcana and casts spells up to its Rank. It takes Corpus
damage the longer it stays in the Fallen World. Each has a **Trial** (like a ban) [p. 95–97].
Also: **Ochemata** = Exarch servants (see `06_antagonists.md`).

## Souls [p. 97–99]
Souls exist; are seen with Death Sight (integrated) or as auras in Subtle-Arcanum Sight (removed). They can't be created.
Soul loss → **Soulless**, then **Enervated** at Wisdom/Integrity 1, then **Thrall** at permanent Willpower 0 (`07_conditions.md`). A destroyed soul is a
sacrament Yantra for Death/Fate/Mind/Prime/Spirit spells. Soul stones and Demesnes → `05_systems.md`.

## Ch6 world map (detail in `06_antagonists.md`)
Left-Handed mages: **the Mad** (Wisdom 0), **Banishers** (mage-killers), **Liches** (soul-thieving immortals), **Reapers**
(soul-eaters), **Scelesti** (Abyss-worshippers). Realms Invisible: **Shadow** (spirits), **Underworld** (ghosts), **Astral**
(Oneiros → Temenos → Anima Mundi/Dreamtime), plus Supernal/Abyssal **Verges** and **Irises**.
