## Sample settings (Ch7) [p. 267–283] (mechanics in `05_systems.md` §12)
- **London, UK**: ancient powerful Consilium; High Speech hidden across the city; **Runewalking** (routes spelling High Speech runes).
- **Los Angeles, USA**: Astral Temenos bleeding into reality (mirages, materialized dreams, Goetia possessions); Seers and Guardians strong since Hollywood; Banishers unusually common.
- **Salamanca, Spain**: university library where occult books (*efímeros*) and gates to fleeting worlds appear; Diamond-held university, Sleepwalker librarians.
- **Tokyo, Japan**: **Ansho** Mana-dead zones (Mysterium maps them); Paradox **beacons**; local term Ki for Mana/tass; all six Orders strong (Arrow = Kongou no Ya).
- **Tucson, USA**: no Consilium or Assembly; more Nameless than Pentacle mages; between Mexican Consilium and Phoenix; Seer seat in El Paso/Ciudad Juárez; **Phantom Bells** of Mission San Xavier del Bac.
- Framing fiction: New York (Pentacle lost Manhattan to the Seers in "Turifex's Folly", 2001); Interfector Outis; Gee & Lucretia.

## Myths (App. 2) [p. 312–313]
**Atlantis** / the Time Before (Awakened city; many contradictory Atlantises); the **Fall** (Exarchs seize the Supernal, the Abyss opens, Quiescence); **Ascension** (soul joins the
Supernal; leaves **Sariras** — relic Artifacts — and Verges where it happened). Oracles = possible Ascended masters who built the Watchtowers.
