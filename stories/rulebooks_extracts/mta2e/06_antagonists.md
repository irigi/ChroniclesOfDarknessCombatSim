# MtA 2e — Antagonists & supporting cast (Ch6 Left-Handed mages, invisible entities & Numina, Supernal beings, Bound; App. 1 Sleepers/Sleepwalkers/Proximi/servitors)

## A. Left-Handed mages [p. 235–239]
- **The Mad** (Wisdom 0): Virtue replaced by an Obsession (**Fault**). Fault-related Skill rolls are exceptional on **3 successes**; may cast spells of a specific Practice with
  **any Arcanum, known or not** (in the Fault's field). Presence causes Quiescence like a spell; friends forget her. **+1 Occultation dot per week** not indulging the Fault; when kept from
  the Fault, her subconscious throws out spell effects (**Tulpa**). Can't enter the Temenos. Paradox anomalies she causes last a chronicle/year [p. 235–236, 116].
- **Banishers**: mages who hunt mages (fear, trauma, twisted Awakening, Logophages/Timori Legacies). Mechanically Nameless mages. **Twisted Awakening**: keep **Integrity** instead of
  Wisdom, and Peripheral Mage Sight is painful. Friendship with Banishers is "contagious" [p. 236].
- **Liches**: cheat death (indefinite Life/Death spells with rune Yantras, body theft, ghost-binding, soul in a Dedicated tool, Legacy transformation into ghost-/spirit-mage or
  **Morphean** in the Temenos). Transformed ones keep Gnosis & Arcana and use Supernal-being systems [p. 236–237].
- **Reapers**: steal/consume souls (Death Arcanum; Legacies use souls via other Subtle Arcana). **Tremere** sidebar: Nameless-Order-sized Reaper-Lich Legacy of near-immortal soul-eaters;
  failures become flesh-eating **Pretas** [p. 237–238].
- **Scelesti**: invoke the Abyss. **Inviting Paradox** (any mage can; addictive): don't contest; roll **Gnosis**, each success lets him **control one Reach** of the Paradox anomaly.
  Some kneel to an Abyssal **Ziggurat** (twisted Path), others bargain with Annunaki/Oroboros (more Paradox control, ST-defined). All Orders kill Scelesti on sight [p. 238–239].

## B. Supernal entities [p. 94–97, 253–257]
- Two Arcana at **Rank** dots (one a Ruling Arcanum of their Realm; may have lower others). They cast like mages but roll **Power + Finesse**; **unlimited free Reach**; if they risk Paradox
  the pool is **rote** and deals **aggravated to the entity** = successes (no anomaly) [p. 258].
- Use Mana (not Essence); max Mana by Rank; regain Rank Mana/day in home realm; don't spend Mana to stay active but lose Corpus over time in the Fallen World (summoning lifespan, `05` §6).
- Mundane Attribute + Rank rolls at **Rank 4+ have the rote quality** [p. 255]. Virtue & Vice of pure principle; no Integrity. Speak High Speech as a real language.
- Discorporated outside a Verge/summoning circle → consumed by the Abyss [p. 257]. Banes cause lethal (not aggravated) to them in the Fallen World [p. 257].
- Types by Path/Arcanum: see `01_lore.md`. **Ochemata** = Exarch servants (Ministers' Verge-fortresses; assassinate Pax-breaking archmages) [p. 72, 252].
- **The Bound** [p. 244]: Atlantean-era god-monsters (minotaurs, gorgons…) imprisoned/exiled in ruins; Supernal entities that **lose 1 Mana/day** and sleep when empty; wake near Mana.
  Feed by destroying the source: a mage victim yields total-Scouring Mana + her stored Mana + 1 per Gnosis dot; a few can eat Sleepers. Placated by Mana channelled via spells.
  Atlantean **adamas** can't be affected by Matter spells.
- **Archmages** [p. 251–252]: treat as all 10 Arcana Ruling, all-Arcana Sight free, **auto-win Clash of Wills vs lesser beings**, spells ignore factor limits & can't be countered/dispelled;
  Golden Road (soul realm = Oneiros rules), Chantry (= Emanation), Wending (abandoned Golden Road). Bound by the **Pax Arcanum**.

## C. Ephemeral entities (ghosts, spirits, Goetia; Abyssal Acamoth = Goetia rules, Gulmoth = spirit rules) [p. 252–263]
Same engine as core/WtF (see `../wtf2e/` for the fullest spirit rules); Mage-specific values below.
**Rank table** [p. 254]:
| Rank | Trait max | Attribute dots | Max Essence/Mana | Numina |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 | 5–8 | 10 | 1–3 |
| 2 | 7 | 9–14 | 15 | 3–5 |
| 3 | 9 | 15–25 | 20 | 5–7 |
| 4 | 12 | 26–35 | 25 | 7–9 |
| 5 | 15 | 36–45 | 50 | 9–11 |
Rank = **Supernatural Tolerance** and −1 per Rank to attempts to forcibly bind it. Ghosts are Rank 1–2 at creation and can't rise outside the Underworld.
- **Attributes**: Power (Str/Int/Pre), Finesse (Dex/Wits/Man), Resistance (Sta/Res/Com). No Skills: **Attribute + Rank** for actions within their nature (Attribute + Attribute for perception/surprise).
- **Corpus** = Resistance + Size (no wound penalties). **Willpower** = Resistance + Finesse (max 10 at Rank 1–5; +1/day). **Initiative** = Finesse + Resistance.
  **Defense** = lower of Power/Finesse (Rank 1: higher) — p. 257 misprints lower of Finesse/Resistance — book error, `99` §B. Defense applies **vs firearms**. **Speed** = Power + Finesse + species factor. Size usually 5 for ghosts.
- **Essence**: 1/day to stay active (0 → hibernate; spirits/Goetia pushed home; ghosts reform at nearest Anchor). Bleed **1/hour** outside a suitable Condition (run out from bleed:
  1 lethal + hibernate). Regain 1/day near their Condition; Goetia/ghosts/Supernal regain Rank/day at home; spirits never just regenerate in the Shadow. Boost a trait 1:1 for a scene
  (max +Rank+2 per trait, one Attribute per turn). Sense sources within a mile. Steal from same type: Power + Finesse vs Power + Resistance. Spirits **gorge** 1/day: Power + Finesse (−Gauntlet if across).
- **Virtue/Vice**: ghosts keep theirs **reversed** (Vice = full WP 1/chapter; Virtue = 1 WP/scene), frozen Integrity, no breaking points. Spirits/Goetia: no Integrity/Virtue/Vice; 1 WP per 3 Essence consumed.
- **Ban** (Rank 1 mild → Rank 4–5 complex, explosive). **Bane** [p. 256]: approaching voluntarily = 1 WP + Resolve + Composure − Rank; banes are solid to them in Twilight; touch:
  1 **aggravated**/turn if Materialized or at home (and Rank-dice roll or its Condition ends), 1 lethal/turn in Twilight or to a Fettered entity via its Fetter; bane weapons: aggravated vs
  Manifest/home, lethal vs Twilight/Supernal-in-Fallen. Entities **2+ Ranks above** an opponent of the same type count as its bane with natural attacks.
- **Combat** [p. 257]: attack **Power + Finesse**, bashing unless nature says lethal; Twilight beings only interact with same-phase Twilight (or magic) unless the attack uses the bane.
  **Lethal physical attacks on a Manifested/solid entity deal bashing** (no organs) unless bane. **−1 Essence per aggravated wound**. Out of Corpus from lethal/agg: discorporate; if any
  Essence remains it **hibernates** at a safe Conditioned place until Essence ≥ Corpus, then 1 to wake; 0 Essence = destroyed.
- **Twilight phases**: ghosts / Death-Twilight objects & people together; spirits / Spirit-Twilight together; Goetia / Mind projections together [p. 254].
- **Influence** [p. 256–258]: ghosts/spirits/Goetia start with Influence dots = Rank (split allowed; trade Numina 1:1). Effect dots + duration dots ≤ Influence rating; pay Essence; roll
  **Power + Finesse**; vs a sentient mind contested by **higher of Resolve/Composure + Gnosis**. Effects: • Strengthen (1 Ess; can shift Anchor/Resonant→Open) / •• Manipulate (2) / ••• Control (3) /
  •••• Create (4; temporary Anchor/Resonant) / ••••• Mass Create (5; Rank instances or one permanent; never permanent mind changes). Durations: 0 = 1 min/success; • 10 min; •• 1 h (+1); ••• 1 day (+2); •••• permanent (+2).
- **Manifestation Effects** [p. 259] (all Power + Finesse; ghosts/spirits get Twilight Form + Rank effects; Goetia have none naturally — need Death/Spirit/Mind magic):
  Twilight Form (free); Discorporate (free, voluntary); **Reaching** (1 Ess, −Gauntlet); **Gauntlet Breach** (Resonant; 3 Ess; −Gauntlet); **Gateway** (Open; 3 Ess or Gauntlet
  for Shadow); **Image** (Anchor/Resonant; 1 Ess; visible a scene); **Materialize** (Open; 3 Ess; 1 h/success); **Fetter** (Open; 2 Ess; living target contests **Resolve + Composure + Gnosis**;
  living target gains **Urged**); **Unfetter** (1 Ess; a scene); **Possess** (Open; 3 Ess; living target contests **Resolve + Composure + Primal Urge** [book error → Supernatural Tolerance, `99` §B]);
  **Familiar** (only via the spell).
- **Conditions** [p. 258–261]: **Anchor** (ghost no bleed within Rank×3 yards), **Resonant**, **Open** (extended: scenes = Rank or living subject's Resolve; or Control Influence/Arcana
  instantly), **Reaching**, **Gateway** (scene; anyone may pass), **Materialized**, **Familiar** (no bleed; Connected link; share senses reflexively — casting through it needs remote-viewing
  Reach but not Sympathetic Range; pass Mana↔Essence with 1 lost per exchange; reforms at a place with the mage's Signature Nimbus), **Fettered** (within 5 yards; −1 Essence on Influences on
  the Fetter; can't target others), **Urged** (read thoughts: P+F vs Resolve + Gnosis; urge an action: P+F vs Resolve + Composure; obeying gives the host a Beat), **Possessed** (a scene;
  living host comatose; host's Physical Attributes; read memories Finesse −4; host's Physical Skills −3, Social/Mental −4, each −1 per day Fettered; 1 Ess/turn heals host 1 wound;
  corpses/objects: 1 Ess for own Physical for a turn = 1 lethal to host). Advancing a Condition freezes its prerequisites' duration; removing a prerequisite removes dependants.
- **Familiars** [p. 253 sidebar]: Connected link to the mage; forcing it against its ethics/ban is an Act of Hubris (Enlightened/Understanding); severing by another needs an Unmaking spell.
- **Ghost ranks** [p. 245]: Plasm 0, Manes 1, Lemures 2–3, Lares 4–5, Kerberoi 6+ (Geists → Sin-Eaters). **Spirit ranks** [p. 246]: Mote 0, Gaffling 1–2, Jaggling 3–4, Incarna 5–7, Celestine 8–9.
  Goetia rank by centrality of concept; a mage's **daimon** is a strong Goetia of her Oneiros [p. 247–248].
- **Chthonians**: Underworld natives, ghost rules without Anchors/Integrity [p. 245].

## E. Supporting cast (App. 1) [p. 298–311]
### Sleepers & the Curse of Quiescence (systems also in `05_systems.md` §10)
- **Integrity** (not Wisdom) for Sleepers, Sleepwalkers, Proximi. Breaking point: **Resolve + Composure** + Integrity modifier (8–10: +2; 6–7: +1; 4–5: 0; 2–3: −1; 1: −2) [p. 299].
  Any result → a Condition (Guilty, Shaken, Spooked); failure: −1 Integrity; dramatic: Beat + worse Condition (Broken, Fugue, Madness); exceptional: +1 WP + Beat, no Condition.
  Integrity 1 is the floor. Raise: rest/counselling, 3 XP per dot.
- **Sleepers witnessing obvious Supernal or Abyssal magic always suffer a breaking point** (Integrity). Causing it is an **Act of Hubris (Enlightened & Understanding)** for the mage [p. 298].
- **Obvious** = clearly impossible effect and the Sleeper is conscious & cognizant of it; a spell not obvious when cast avoids the Paradox die but may cause Dissonance when later seen.
- **Quiescence**: in the following scene the Sleeper forgets or mundanizes the memory; prodding it (or magically correcting it) = new breaking point, then forgets again. Also caused by
  Atlantean ruins, the Mad, Emanations, Supernal/Abyssal entities [p. 300].
- **Sleeper Merits** [p. 302–303] (require Integrity): **Actively Oblivious** •• (1 WP: skip a breaking point; then not a Paradox witness — still Quiescence & Dissonance; gain Strained);
  **Communal Sleeper** • (Empathy ••; leading ≥1 other Sleeper: Paradox dice trick one step worse); **Detail Oriented** •• (exceptional on 3 on Investigation/Perception that a mage or
  Sleepwalker failed); **Liar** • (her witnessing = **+2 Paradox dice** and +2 Dissonance dice; Open for Abyssal entities; −1 to Abyss breaking points); **Strained** •• (Integrity ≤5:
  take Strained instead of a Supernal/Abyssal breaking point).
- **Mystery Cults**: Sleeper cults run by Orders (Merits: Mystery Cult Initiation/Influence). Examples: the Guild (Free Council MMO raid team), TALC (Guardian distraction cult),
  Sleep to Dream (K-pop band led by a Banisher) [p. 300–301].
### Sleepwalkers [p. 303–306]
- Immune to the Curse: no Paradox increase, no Dissonance, no forgetting. Anyone with Integrity + a Supernatural Merit reflecting an inborn/internal power is one (ghoul with powers,
  wolf-blooded); a cursed object doesn't count. Stealing Fire makes one temporarily.
- **Carrying spells**: a Sleepwalker with a **Medium** link to the mage can hold **1** of her spells, **Strong** **2** (Banner-Bearer +1–3); held spells **don't count against her spell
  control**; she keeps control (cancel/relinquish). Requires the Sleepwalker to understand roughly and **freely agree** (magical coercion → the spell fails). Illegal with Sleepers [p. 303–304].
- Can assist ritual casting as an environment Yantra (teamwork) [p. 119].
- **Sleepwalker Merits** (confer Sleepwalker status; not for Awakened) [p. 305–306]: **Banner-Bearer** •–••• (+1 held spell/dot); **Deadpan** ••• (no fear/revulsion rolls vs magic;
  auto-Withstand fear spells; else Composure +2); **Fitful Slumber** • (after 3+ Supernal breaking points; +2 to resist them); **Loved** ••• (no one else can make a Strong link to you;
  the lover senses your wounds/low WP); **Proxy Voice** •–••• (Mentor •+; use mentor's Status); **Relic Attuned** ••• (use Artifacts: 1 WP per activation, roll WP or Artifact dice);
  **Ritual Martyr** •• (take a mage's Paradox Condition onto yourself if it could affect a Sleepwalker; mage gains Humbled toward you); **Ritual Savvy** •• (Occult ••; prep roll, each
  success a bonus die mages buy for 1 WP each, outside the Yantra limit); **Sleepwalker** • (Integrity; the status itself); **Slippery** •• (1 WP: suspicion falls on others first).
- Archetypes: Damned-sel, Occult Investigator, Hacker, etc. (fluff) [p. 304–305].
### Proximi [p. 306–309]
- Sleepwalkers from Supernal-tainted bloodlines (**Dynasties**), each with a **Parent Path**. **Blessings**: Merit-bought spells (≤3 Arcanum dots) from the Parent Path's 2 Ruling
  Arcana + 1 chosen Arcanum; up to 30 dots of Blessings. **Mana max 5**; regain at Hallows or by a Dynasty Oblation (+1); spend 1 Mana/turn.
- **Casting**: no Gnosis/Arcana — pool = **Willpower** − factor penalties; **no Yantras**; ritual interval **5 hours**; **no free Reach** — each Reach = +1 Paradox die; can't contain
  Paradox: any Paradox success **cancels the Blessing** and worsens the **family Curse**; obvious Blessings provoke Paradox, Quiescence, Dissonance like spells.
- **Familial Curse**: two-stage Condition (persistent mild form with a Beat trigger; severe form when forced/escaping; reverting gives a Beat).
- Awakening: loses Blessings & Curse (Sanctity of Merits; ST may convert to Arcane XP). Proximi Awaken more often.
- **Sisters of the Mountain** (Thyrsus; Fate/Life/Spirit Blessings: Oaths Fulfilled, Exceptional Luck, Shifting the Odds, Monkey's Paw, Shared Fate; Cleanse the Body, Analyze Life,
  Body Control, Lure and Repel, Purge Illness, Degrading the Form, Honing the Form, Knit; Coaxing the Spirits, Gremlins). Curse: plants under the skin, must sleep on soil nightly;
  persistent 1 unhealable lethal; severe: +1 unhealable lethal per night missed (and on Paradox) [p. 308–309].
### Seer servants [p. 310–311]
- **Profane Urim**: 6-dot Artifact (Possession via Sympathetic Range on Sleepers with Medium/Strong link; grants Connected link while worn). Seers may pool Merit dots.
- **Servitors**: Myrmidons (Praetorian Proximi), Hive-Souls (Hegemony), **Grigori** (Panopticon Shroud of Observation, 8-dot Artifact, Seer Status 3+: Sleepwalker becomes a Rank 2 Goetia spy,
  Influence: Watching; 1 Mana/day; 1 Mana to teleport to target), **Hollow Ones** (Paternoster: Sleepwalker, Mana cap 5, burns 1/day, can't refill; at 0 Mana Mental/Social Attributes,
  Virtue/Vice/Aspirations and Skills vanish; Mind spells on them are automatically Indefinite at no cost; 2-dot Merit for Paternoster Seer with Mind 4+, Status 3+).
