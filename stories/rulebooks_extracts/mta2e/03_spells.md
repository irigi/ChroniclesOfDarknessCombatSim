# MtA 2e — Magic: spellcasting rules, Practices, creative thaumaturgy, all Arcana spells, Attainments, Legacies
Quick reference version (App. 4, p. 325–328) is folded into §0.

## 0. SPELLCASTING [p. 111–127, 325]

### 0.1 Procedure (App. 4 order)
1. **Choose spell** (Arcanum + dots, Practice, Primary Factor, Withstand).
2. **Casting method**: improvised / Praxis / Rote.
3. **Assign Reach** (free Reach vs Paradox).
4. **Set spell factors** (Casting Time, Range, Potency, Duration, Scale) → dice penalties.
5. **Yantras** (bonus, max +5 after offsetting penalties; number limited by Gnosis).
6. **Check dice pool**: if penalties take the pool below 0 by **another −5** (i.e. worse than −5 past a chance die, after Yantras), the spell is **too complex: automatic failure**. Penalties
   to spellcasting **can exceed the usual −5 cap** [p. 111].
7. **Pay Mana**.
8. **Paradox**: build pool, mitigate with Mana, choose **contain or release**, roll.
9. **Roll spellcasting** (Paradox successes as a penalty if released).

### 0.2 Base spell [p. 111]
- **Pool = Gnosis + highest Arcanum in the spell** (+ Yantras − factor penalties). **1 success = cast** to spec.
- Base effects (before factors): ±1 die, or 1 point of damage, or heal 1 wound; **1 turn**; **1 subject Size ≤5** or arm's-reach area; **touch/self**; ritual casting time by Gnosis.
- **Mana**: improvised spell using a **Common or Inferior** Arcanum (i.e. not Ruling) = **1 Mana** (Praxes and Rotes exempt) [p. 111, 112–113].
- Other Mana costs (always cumulative) [p. 125]: **Indefinite Duration** (1), **aggravated damage** (1), **rote quality** granted (1), **automatic success/failure**, **healing aggravated** (1),
  **boosting a trait beyond max or altering the max** (1); spell-specific costs.

### 0.3 Reach [p. 111–112]
- **Free Reach = (caster's Arcanum dots − spell's required dots) + 1** — "one free Reach per dot of her highest Arcanum that meets or exceeds the requirement".
  Ex.: Mind 4 casting a Mind 3 spell → **2** free Reach [p. 112]. **Rote**: caster counts as having **5 dots** in the spell's highest Arcanum for free Reach [p. 113].
- Each Reach beyond free adds **Paradox dice = Gnosis column** (1 at G1–2, 2 at G3–4, 3 at G5–6, 4 at G7–8, 5 at G9–10) [p. 86].
- **Common Reach effects** (1 Reach each): Ritual → **Instant** casting time; change the Primary Factor; Standard → **Advanced Potency**; Standard → **Advanced Duration**;
  Advanced Duration → **Indefinite** (+1 Mana; a second Reach); touch → **sensory Range**; sensory → **remote viewing** (camera, scrying window);
  Standard → **Advanced Scale**; **cast with no spell-control slots left** (+1 Reach per spell already over) [p. 112].
- Spell write-ups list extra "+1 Reach:" options; "+1 Reach, 1 Mana" etc.

### 0.4 Praxes & Rotes [p. 112–113]
- **Praxis**: exceptional success on **3** successes (not 5); no Mana for Common/Inferior. 1 free per Gnosis dot; more for 1 Arcane XP. Must be castable improvised.
- **Rote**: Order-taught spell. Cast from memory with a **mudra**: add the rote's **Skill dots** as a Yantra (+1 more die if it's one of her Order's 3 Rote Skills) [p. 121];
  needs free hands for the gestures. Free Reach as if Arcanum 5 (**free Reach only** — the Primary Factor bump still uses her actual Arcanum − 1). Signature Nimbus indistinct (needs full Scrutiny to identify). No Mana for Common/Inferior.
  **From a Grimoire**, or a Rote she designed herself: pool gains the **rote quality**, but can't Reach to cast instantly, and ritual time ×2. Rotes **can't be combined**; Praxis and Rote benefits
  don't stack (choose). 1 XP per Rote.
- **Down and dirty spellcasting**: trivial spells → just roll Gnosis + Arcanum; successes may set number of subjects or area (e.g., 5 m radius each) [p. 117].

### 0.5 Spell factors [p. 113–115, 325–326]
- **Primary Factor** (Potency or Duration, fixed per spell): after penalties, raise it for free by **(highest Arcanum − 1) steps** on its chart (voluntary).
  Ex.: Forces 3, Duration primary, −2 penalty chosen → 5 turns [p. 113].
- If a spell uses a factor twice (truck → elephant Size), use the largest penalty.

**Potency**: level 1 free; **−2 per level beyond 1**. **Advanced Potency** (Reach): **+2 Withstand vs Dispellation**, +1 die in Clash of Wills [p. 114, 118].

**Duration** — Standard: 1 turn (0), 2 turns (−2), 3 turns (−4), 5 turns (−6), 10 turns (−8), +10 turns per extra −2.
Advanced (Reach): **1 scene/hour (0)**, 1 day (−2), 1 week (−4), 1 month (−6), 1 year (−8), **Indefinite (−10, +Reach, +1 Mana)** [p. 113].
An instant effect with Advanced Duration **recurs every ritual interval** (Gnosis 1 healing for a day heals Potency every 3 h) [p. 114].
**Lasting**: after-effects that persist (fire keeps burning, healed wounds stay healed) [p. 113]. Making a narrative effect Lasting = +2 Reach [p. 127].

**Scale** (number of subjects, max Size, or area) [p. 114]:
| Penalty | Standard: subjects / Size / area | Advanced (Reach): subjects / Size / area |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 / 5 / arm's reach | 5 / 5 / large house or building |
| −2 | 2 / 6 / small room | 10 / 10 / small warehouse, parking lot |
| −4 | 4 / 7 / large room | 20 / 15 / large warehouse, supermarket |
| −6 | 8 / 8 / several rooms, one floor | 40 / 20 / small factory, mall |
| −8 | 16 / 9 / ballroom, small house | 80 / 25 / large factory, city block |
| −10 | — | 160 / 30 / campus, small neighbourhood (then −2 per ×2 subjects or +5 Size; area can't grow further) |
Area spells can't single out subjects in the area (except via Fate's Warding Gesture). **Aimed spells must use area** centred on the aim point [p. 113].

**Range** [p. 114]: Standard **touch/self**; **Aimed** (see below) to hit without touching; Advanced (Reach) **sensory**: must see/hear/sense; **can't be dodged, no Aimed roll**; +1 Reach for remote real-time
viewing. **Sympathetic Range** (Space 2 Attainment) / **Temporal Sympathy** (Time 2) need no senses (§Attainments).

**Casting Time** [p. 114–115]: **ritual** by Gnosis interval (3 h at G1–2 … 1 min at G9–10); **one roll** regardless of time; each extra full interval = **+1 die (max +5)**.
**Instant** (Reach): 1 turn; no time bonus; Yantras beyond the first add a turn each; **no teamwork** for instant spells.

### 0.6 Yantras [p. 112, 119–123]

- **First Yantra is reflexive; each additional one adds +1 turn** to an instant spell (ritual spells already have time).

| **Environment** | +1 | place/time linked to the spell (not just to the mage) |
| **Supernal Verge** | +2 | its Realm's Ruling Arcana; ritual spells not involving the Path's Inferior |
| **Concentration** | +2 | spell with Duration > 1 turn; if she's **hurt or takes a non-reflexive action** while it's active, the spell **ends** |

### 0.7 Withstand [p. 114, 127]
- A spell vs. a resisting subject lists a **Withstand** trait: **reduce Potency by the Withstand rating**. At 0 Potency it still occupies spell control but does nothing.
  Multiple subjects: compare per subject. Multiple Withstand ratings: highest +1 per additional (e.g., Resolve + sympathy level).
- Typical: **Stamina** (body), **Resolve** (mind control), **Composure** (emotion, perception, soul); **ephemeral beings: Rank**; Gauntlet-crossing: **Gauntlet strength**;
  spells vs Dispellation: caster's highest Arcanum in the spell (+2 if Advanced Potency).
- **Supernatural Tolerance (Gnosis, Blood Potency…) is NOT added to Withstand.** Contested only when a power interferes with the Imago (Countermagic) [p. 127].
- **Direct-damage Fraying/Unraveling spells are never Withstood** (Stamina is already in Health) [p. 127].
- Subject may choose not to Withstand (reflexive). **WP +2 to a Resistance Attribute for Withstand only if aware** of the spell [p. 114].

### 0.8 Aimed spells [p. 115]
- Touch-range spell thrown as a bolt: after the casting & Paradox rolls, same turn, reflexive: **Gnosis + (Athletics or Firearms) − target's Defense**; cover applies;
  **range bands: short Gnosis×10, medium ×20, long ×40** (yards/metres). Success = hits. (Fast Spells Merit: Defense doesn't apply.)
- **Touching** an opponent in combat: Dex + Brawl − Defense, no damage [p. 118, 219]; **[interp]** rolled reflexively right after the instant casting, like Aimed (`99` §C). Casting over several turns keeps Defense but no combat actions [p. 118].

### 0.9 Casting results [p. 115]
- **Dramatic failure**: no effect + a Condition tied to the intended outcome (if a Paradox occurred: + Paradox Condition).
- **Failure**: nothing.
- **Success**: works.
- **Exceptional** (5, or 3 for Praxis): regain 1 WP **and** choose one: +1 step in the Primary Factor; a free Reach in the Primary Factor; a Condition (on mage or subject) giving
  Arcane Beats; refund all Mana spent +1; **ignore Withstand** (full Potency).

### 0.10 Paradox [p. 115–117, 326–327]
**Paradox pool** (checked before casting; once ≥1 die is added, roll even if reduced to chance die):
| Factor | Dice |
|---|---|
| Each Reach beyond free Reach | + Gnosis column (1–5) |
| Spell **inured** (Wisdom) | +2 |
| Each previous Paradox roll **this scene** for this caster | +1 cumulative |
| **Sleeper(s) witness an Obvious casting** | +1 (a few: 9-again; large group: 8-again; full crowd: **rote**) |
| **Dedicated magical tool** used | **−2** |
| **Mana** spent (reflexive) | **−1 per Mana** (within per-turn limit incl. casting Mana; may pre-spend over turns) |
| Paradox Conditions (Abyssal Backlash), soullessness (+1, rote), Abyssal Nimbus taint (+1 per spell) | special |
(App. 4 reconstruction from a scrambled table; the Sleeper +1 and Dedicated −2 are confirmed by text on p. 115 and 123.)

**Decide before rolling: contain or release.**
- **Release**: ST rolls the pool. Dramatic failure: no Paradox, +1 WP, next roll this scene skips the cumulative +1. Failure: none. **Success: Paradox** — its successes are a **penalty on the
  spellcasting roll** (may fail it) and the ST spends them as **Reach** on an **anomaly**: 1 Reach: undo or apply a Common Reach effect (not Casting Time); 1: Paradox Condition on the caster;
  2: **change the subject**; 3: **Abyssal Environmental Tilt**; 5: summon a **Rank 2 Abyssal entity** (+1 Rank per extra Reach). If the casting roll dramatically fails → also a Paradox Condition.
  **Exceptional**: Paradox Condition + anomaly. **Anomaly lasts** by caster's Wisdom: Enlightened 1 scene; Understanding 1 chapter/day; Falling 1 story/month; Mad 1 chronicle/year.
- **Contain**: roll **Wisdom** as a pool, **each success cancels one Paradox success**; **each cancelled success = 1 resistant bashing** to the mage. If any Paradox successes remain, the
  spell is **not penalized and no anomaly** occurs, but the mage gains a **Paradox Condition** (severity = remaining successes). Not attempting to contain a severe Paradox is an Act of
  Hubris (Understanding) [p. 88]. Soulless mages can't contain [p. 98].
- **Paradox Conditions** (gained only by: dramatic-failed casting after a Paradox, exceptional released Paradox, or failing to fully contain). Two resolutions: accept the bad outcome, or
  lapse after time by Wisdom: **Enlightened 1 story/month; Understanding 1 chapter/day; Falling 1 scene; Mad 1 turn**. While it lasts, magic can't mitigate it. If allowed to lapse, the
  Abyss enters her Pattern: shows in Nimbus, **every spell gains a Paradox die even without Reach**; removal = Scour (1 lethal, no Mana); lapsing grants an Arcane Beat.
  Examples: **Abyssal Nimbus** (mage and anything her Nimbus touches gain Resonant for Abyssal entities; exceptional → Open); **Abyssal Imago** (every spell must spend extra Reach = the
  Paradox successes or it auto-fails and adds her casting pool to the Paradox pool; resolve by casting a no-Paradox-risk spell); **Abyssal Backlash** (next Paradox-risking spell adds
  dice = those successes; resolve by fully containing a Paradox) [p. 116–117].

### 0.11 General considerations [p. 117–119]
- **Clash of Wills**: contested **Gnosis + Arcanum** each (other beings use their own CoW pools); ties reroll; winner's effect applies (loser may resume after winner ends).
  Mages may spend WP (+3) and always know when their spells clash. Duration bonus: day +1, week +2, month +3, year/indefinite +4; **Advanced Potency +1** [p. 117–118].
- **Dispellation** = Prime spell (Dispel Magic, Supernal Dispellation). **Countermagic** = Attainment (§Attainments).
- **Casting in a grapple**: allowed whether or not she won the grapple roll; **−3 if she lost** it; no movement Yantras (already-incorporated ones still count) [p. 118].
- **Spell stacking**: same aspect → only highest Potency applies (lower is suppressed; durations keep running) [p. 118].
- **Spell control**: active spells ≤ **Gnosis** (+Sanctum dots inside it); each extra needs a Reach, +1 Reach per spell already over. Instant action: reduce a spell's factors (uniformly).
  Cancel reflexively. **Relinquish**: 1 WP point → spell no longer counts, ST rolls each chapter (1 die per Reach over free, else chance die; success = ST gets a Reach of anomaly;
  exceptional = ends; dramatic failure = skip a chapter); or **1 WP dot = relinquish safely**. Death relinquishes all her spells as if 1 WP point [p. 118].
- **Combined spells**: max 2 (Gnosis 3), 3 (G6), 4 (G9); pool = Gnosis + **lowest** Arcanum − 2 per extra spell; one spell for spell control; same Scale unless a Reach separates
  them; Rotes can't be combined; Praxes only if all are Praxes [p. 118–119]. Each effect Withstood separately.
- **Teamwork** (ritual only): all helpers need ≥1 dot in the Arcanum; leader needs the full rating. Qualified helpers roll their own casting and add successes as dice to leader;
  unqualified-rating helpers roll **Gnosis − 3**; each rolls Paradox separately and any release affects the whole spell; non-casters (Sleepwalkers, Proximi) = environment Yantra [p. 119].

### 0.12 Practices [p. 123–125]
| Dots | Practices |
|---|---|
| • Initiate | **Compelling** (nudge a possible outcome), **Knowing** (direct knowledge), **Unveiling** (perceive hidden things) |
| •• Apprentice | **Ruling** (direct control, no fundamental change), **Shielding** (protection), **Veiling** (conceal / hide from) |
| ••• Disciple | **Fraying** (weaken; **direct damage = bashing**), **Perfecting** (bolster, repair), **Weaving** (alter properties) |
| •••• Adept | **Patterning** (transform wholesale), **Unraveling** (severely damage; **direct damage = lethal**, aggravated for **1 Mana + 1 Reach**) |
| ••••• Master | **Making** (create ex nihilo), **Unmaking** (annihilate) |

### 0.13 Creative thaumaturgy guidelines [p. 125–127]
- **Damage**: direct only by Fraying (bashing) or Unraveling (lethal; aggravated +1 Mana +1 Reach) = **Potency**; indirect (fire, falls) uses normal damage rules.
- **Healing**: Ruling halves recovery time (quarter +1 Reach); Perfecting heals **bashing** / repairs objects; Patterning heals **lethal** (aggravated +1 Mana +1 Reach) — Potency wounds.
- **Conditions**: Compelling only intensifies existing states; **Ruling** creates most non-Persistent mundane Conditions; supernatural Conditions (Soulless, Manifestation…) need
  **Weaving**; Persistent need Patterning/Unraveling. Magic-made beneficial Conditions give Beats only via exceptional success. Spell Conditions **last only the Duration**;
  resolving early ends the spell and gives a Beat; expiry isn't resolution. Removing a Condition ≥ Ruling. Tilts: Reach on an attack spell, or Fraying/Patterning.
- **Trait bonuses**: Skills/Merits via **Ruling**, Attributes via **Perfecting**; equipment bonuses count as traits; total dots = **Potency**; beyond max = 1 Mana; can't grant Advantages (Gnosis, BP) or powers.
- **Dice**: Ruling grants **9-again** (8-again +1 Reach); Perfecting grants **rote quality** (+1 Mana); affects one roll per Potency.
- **Protection** (Shielding): immunity to mundane phenomena; vs supernatural attacks up to Potency, which must win a **Clash of Wills**. **Hiding** (Veiling): total vs mundane senses, Clash vs mystical detection.
- Patterning transformations are never Lasting; Unmaking kills are always Withstood by a Resistance Attribute [p. 127].
- Withstand: see §0.7. Primary Factor: whichever you'd increase for "more powerful".

## 2. ATTAINMENTS [p. 192–197]
Not spells: no Imago, **can't be Counterspelled** (but a spell with an Attainment added can be). Gained automatically with Arcanum dots.

### • Counterspell (one per Arcanum)
- Must **see the casting with Active Mage Sight** (incl. that Arcanum? — RAW: "a spell of the appropriate Arcanum"). **Instant action**, but may be used **out of Initiative order if she hasn't acted yet this turn**.
- **Clash of Wills**: caster's Gnosis + Arcanum vs counterer's Gnosis + Arcanum; always counters the target spell's **highest** Arcanum; may counter any spell using that Arcanum.
  Countering someone with a **higher rating in that Arcanum costs 1 Mana**. Initiates can counter Masters [p. 192].
