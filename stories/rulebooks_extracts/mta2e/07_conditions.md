# MtA 2e — Conditions & Tilts [App. 3, p. 314–324] (+ Paradox Conditions p. 116–117, Manifestation Conditions p. 258–261)
Rules for Conditions/Beats: resolve = Beat (1 per category per scene); Persistent ones give Beats on their Beat trigger. Spell-created Conditions last only the spell's Duration;
letting them expire isn't resolution. Resolving a Condition caused by magic/Paradox gives an **Arcane** Beat [p. 83, 126]. Creating Conditions/Tilts: ±2 typical; dramatic ones may
give exceptional-on-3, ±5 tied to a trait, temporary Virtue/Vice/Aspiration/Obsession, ±1 Door, 8-again on an Attribute's rolls, one rote use; negatives remove 10-again [p. 288–290].
Two-column scramble: Steadfast/Soulless/Strained/Soul Shocked/Swooning/Thrall blocks re-paired by content (L31093–31181).

### Paradox Conditions [p. 116–117] (lapse time by Wisdom: Enlightened 1 story/month, Understanding 1 chapter/day, Falling 1 scene, Mad 1 turn; lapsing = Abyss in Pattern (+1 Paradox die on every spell until Scoured) + Arcane Beat)
- **Abyssal Nimbus**: mage & everything her Nimbus touches gain **Resonant** for Abyssal entities (exceptional Paradox: **Open**); Long-Term Nimbus twisted. Resolve: an Abyssal entity Manifests through it, Scour, or lapse.
- **Abyssal Imago**: each spell needs extra Reach = the Paradox successes or auto-fails and adds its casting pool to Paradox. Resolve: cast a spell with no Paradox risk, Scour, lapse.
- **Abyssal Backlash**: next Paradox-risking spell +dice = those successes. Resolve: fully contain a Paradox, Scour, lapse.
