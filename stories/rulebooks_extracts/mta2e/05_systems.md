# MtA 2e — Systems (Mana, Wisdom, Nimbus, Mage Sight, Mysteries, summoning, souls, Demesnes; Ch5 deltas; Ch6 realms)
Spellcasting & Paradox → `03_spells.md` §0. Combat engine → `../cod_core/05_systems.md` (Ch5 deltas in §9 below).

## 1. Mana [p. 86–87]
Max and per-turn spending by Gnosis (`02` table). Pattern Mana and tass.
**Spend Mana to:**
- Improvise a spell **not in a Ruling Arcanum** (1 Mana) [p. 86].
- **−1 Paradox die per Mana** spent [p. 86].
- Pay spell costs for effects that need Mana (some spells, Attainments). A Legacy Attainment based on a spell that costs ≥2 Mana costs 1 Mana.
- **Pattern restoration**: **3 Mana = heal 1 bashing or lethal**, *or* remove 1 Mental Condition (no Beat) or 1 Physical Tilt [p. 86].
- If a feat needs more Mana than the per-turn limit, spend over several turns beforehand (lost if interrupted) [p. 86].
**Gain Mana:**
- **Oblation** at a Hallow: Gnosis + Composure, 1 hour; 1 Mana/success up to the Hallow's limit. Legacy members may do a special Oblation anywhere [p. 86].
- **Channel Mana** (Prime 3) from Hallow/tass; tass in food can be eaten [p. 86].
- Fulfilling an Obsession: +1 [p. 84].
- **Scouring**: −1 Physical Attribute (and derived traits) for 24 h **or** 1 **resistant lethal** → 3 Mana. Times/day: Gnosis 1–4: 1; 5–6: 2; 7–9: 3; 10: 4 [p. 86–87].
- **Blood Sacrifice** (Act of Hubris): small animal 1 Mana; human = victim's remaining Integrity. Ignores per-turn limit if part of a spell's casting [p. 87].

## 2. Wisdom [p. 87–88]
Starts at 7. **Act of Hubris**: if the mage's Wisdom ≥ the act's tier, roll the **tier's dice** (the pool depends on the act, not on the mage's Wisdom):
| Tier | Wisdom | Dice | Example acts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enlightened | 8–10 | 5 | Using magic for something doable mundanely with little risk; innocent bystanders affected by your spells |
| Understanding | 4–7 | 3 | Letting a Sleeper witness Obvious magic; making a soul stone; not trying to contain a severe Paradox; forcing a sapient being (Sleeper, spirit…) against its interests, altering its nature long-term or binding it to a task; premeditated murder or violence that leaves long-term injury |
| Falling | 1–3 | 1 | Killing in a rage; destroying an Awakened soul; letting a Supernal being be consumed by the Fallen World (incl. killing a summoned one, p. 96); dealing with the Abyss |
| 0 | — | — | The mage becomes one of **the Mad** |
- Modifiers: **−1 die** if the act pursued an Obsession; **+1** if done in defence of her Virtue; **−1** if it indulged her Vice [p. 87–88].
- Results: **dramatic failure**: −1 Wisdom + Persistent **Megalomaniacal** or **Rampant** (resolved only by regaining Wisdom); **failure**:
  −1 Wisdom + Megalomaniacal or Rampant; **success**: no loss; **exceptional**: +1 extra Arcane Beat. **Any** risk roll gives an Arcane Beat [p. 88].
- **Inuring**: after losing Wisdom to a spell, the mage may make that spell never risk Wisdom again; instead it **always has a base 2 Paradox dice**.
  Max inured spells = Gnosis [p. 88].
- **Raising**: Obsession "Becoming wiser", ≥1 story of progress, then 2 Arcane XP per dot [p. 88].
- Wisdom tier sets the reach of the Long-Term Nimbus: Enlightened = Strong ties only; Understanding = Medium; Falling = even Weak [p. 89].
- Wisdom 1–3 also matters for Paradox (see `03_spells.md` §0.9).

## 3. Nimbus [p. 88–90]
- **Long-Term Nimbus**: story coincidences by Path; potency by Gnosis (obvious at 6+); spreads along sympathetic ties by Wisdom tier.
- **Immediate Nimbus**: flares when casting (visible to any active Mage Sight). The flare imposes the mage's own **Nimbus Tilt**:
  - From a spell: strength = spell's **Potency**, lasts **Reach turns** (min 1).
  - Deliberate: 1/scene, **1 Mana**, roll **Gnosis** (+Potent Nimbus); successes = strength; visible even to non-Sight (Sleepers suffer Quiescence after).
  - Affects witnesses with **Resolve ≤ strength** (or willing). Works on non-Sight viewers subtly.
  - **Designing the Tilt**: bonus/penalty dice totalling **½ Gnosis (round up)** spread across Attributes or Skills (bonus Skill also
    gets 8-again; penalized Skill loses 10-again). Redesign whenever Gnosis rises. Ex. Gnosis 7: −1 Composure, −1 Wits, +2 Presence [p. 90].
- **Signature Nimbus**: a trace on everything her spells/Praxes/Rotes/Attainments touch; recognised by Focused Sight. Gnosis 6+: +1
  die per Gnosis above 5 to Revelation about that Pattern, fading 1 die/week. Deliberate imprint: 1 Mana, +Gnosis to rolls to
  scrutinize it, fades 1/week (1 WP: per month) [p. 89–90].
- **vs other auras** (vampire Predatory Aura, etc.): roll **Resistance Attribute + Gnosis** (Str-based aura → Stamina; Presence → Composure;
  Intelligence → Resolve). This is automatic, contested, and flares her Immediate Nimbus. Her successes subtract from the attacker's; if she nets more, he suffers her Nimbus Tilt [p. 90].

## 4. High Speech [p. 91]
Can't lie in it; usable for Persuasion/Intimidation; **Expression and Subterfuge in High Speech → chance die**. Sleepers hear gibberish
(Quiescence, no breaking point). As a spell Yantra: needs the High Speech Merit (+2, see `03` §0.6).

## 5. Mage Sight [p. 91–94]
- **Peripheral** (always on): notices any **active** supernatural effect (Essence spent, a Numen used, Manifestation, spells) but gives no details. Any
  supernatural concealment beats it automatically (no Clash of Wills) [p. 91].
- **Active**: reflexive to enter with the Ruling Arcana; **instant action** if it includes a Common/Inferior Arcanum, which costs **1 Mana/scene** each. A
  3rd Ruling Arcanum is free (Legacy). Leaving is reflexive. **−2 to all rolls unrelated to magic**. Lasts **Gnosis minutes**; then 1 WP
  for the rest of the scene. Sees all spells as they're cast (Nimbus flare), enabling Counterspell. Doesn't see through walls or into Twilight.
  To pierce concealment that it logically could pierce: **Clash of Wills**, Gnosis + Arcanum vs the concealment's pool [p. 91–92].
  Per-Arcanum basics: **Death**: Anchor Condition, manifested ghosts, whether something has a soul / is dead. **Fate**: highlights exceptional
  successes and dramatic failures, Destiny presence. **Forces**: motion, environmental Tilts, fire/electricity, powered devices. **Life**: life
  signs, injury level, toxins, disease, Personal Tilts. **Matter**: Durability/Structure, Availability and equipment bonus. **Mind**: thinking beings,
  sleep/coma/projection; aware when an observed character gains/spends WP. **Prime**: usable Yantras, the presence of spells and Attainments, tass, Hallows.
  **Space**: distances, range bands, cover modifiers; scrying windows, Irises. **Spirit**: Gauntlet strength, Resonance, Essence sources,
  manifested spirits. **Time**: **Initiative of all combatants**; knows when someone is about to act (even reflexively) and **may preempt it**
  if able; temporal warps [p. 91].
- **Focused** (needs Active; one subject ≈ a person, object or small room). Two actions:
  - **Revelation** (instant): **Gnosis + Arcanum − Opacity**. Dramatic failure: nobody can Scrutinize or Reveal it for 24 h (Cleanse Pattern clears this);
    failure: nothing; success: **surface info**; exceptional: surface info + Opacity −1 or 1 piece of deep info. Can't Reveal twice without Scrutiny in between.
  - **Scrutiny**: **1 WP** to start; penalty to non-magic rolls rises to **−3**; extended, **Gnosis + Arcanum, 1 roll/turn**, no roll cap.
    Accumulate successes equal to the current Opacity → Opacity −1 and successes reset (max 1 level per turn). **Mana**: +1 success
    per Mana, only if the roll succeeds (lost if it fails; per-turn limit). After **Gnosis + Arcanum** rolls (−1 per extra Arcanum used), each
    failed roll adds ½ Gnosis (round up) to Opacity. Dramatic failure: +2 Opacity (and past the limit: a Supernal entity of her Path takes notice).
    Exceptional: apply all successes, *or* 1 Mana to add Gnosis to Opacity (blot it), *or* 1 Mana to hide her Nimbus (−Gnosis to trace her). In
    a Clash of Wills vs concealment while Scrutinizing she gets the **rote quality**. Obsession applies: +1 die [p. 92–93].
  - Plot-critical info: refund all but 1 Mana spent on Scrutiny at end of scene [p. 93].
- **Building Mysteries** [p. 93–94]: Opacity ≈ highest Arcanum of the spell, +1 per extra Arcanum; non-Supernal phenomena ×1.5.
  To fully unravel: Opacity 4 = 10 successes; Opacity 6 = 21.
  **Surface**: is it Awakened magic? Which Arcana? The caster's Signature Nimbus (unless cast as a Rote), its age, and optionally its Practice.
  **Deep**: Nimbus of Rote/Attainment users, spell factors and remaining Duration, Paradox (released or contained), whether relinquished, links to other
  Mysteries, power relative to her Gnosis, Supernal/Abyssal link, Arcanum-specific truths.

## 6. Summoning Supernal entities [p. 94–97]
- Summoner must be of the matching **Path**, use one of her Ruling Arcana at **≥3**. The entity has 2 Arcana (2nd chosen for +1 Mana if
  she has another Arcanum ≥3) and casts spells up to its Rank.
- **Extended**, **Gnosis + Arcanum**, **1 roll/hour**, cost 1 Mana (2 to pick the 2nd Arcanum). Failure: stop or take a Condition to continue.
  Dramatic failure: Abyss floods the space; the mage and any mages within 50 ft take **aggravated = summoner's Gnosis**; taint lasts Gnosis days.
- **Target successes**: base 10; +5 per Rank above 1 (max Rank 5); +1 per success of stay duration (30 min each, else damage at once);
  +1 per Abyss-shielding roll; +1 per Sleeper present (they suffer breaking points); +1 per mage of another Path present; +1 in a
  Demesne of another Realm; +1 if she caused a Paradox in the last week; **−3 in a Demesne of the right Realm**; −1 per 2 successes on
  Intelligence + Occult for good correspondences.
- **Abyssal intrusion**: free rolls = Resolve + Composure + shielding successes; after that, the ST secretly rolls her **Gnosis** each roll; when accumulated
  ≥ Gnosis + Arcanum, an **Abyssal** entity arrives instead.
- **Lifespan**: after protected time, 1 Corpus/hour in the circle, every 30 min outside, every 15 min near Sleepers, +1 per success of any
  Paradox within 50 ft. Each damage: roll Power, Finesse and Resistance separately; each failed roll → that trait −1. Corpus damage is resistant. Dying outside the
  circle or by deliberate attack → lost to the Abyss (an Act of Hubris for Falling Wisdom).
- **Trial**: each entity demands a test (like a ban); hints via Intelligence + Occult.
- Entity names by Arcanum: Death = Specters, Fate = Moirae, Forces = Seraphim, Life = Atavisms, Matter = Apeirons, Mind = Wraiths,
  Prime = Cherubim, Space = Imps, Spirit = Totems, Time = Anachronisms [p. 96–97].

## 7. Souls, soul stones, Demesnes [p. 97–99]
- **Soul loss**: **Soulless** → at Integrity or Wisdom 1 **Enervated** → at permanent WP 0 **Thrall**. Soulless mage: every spell risks Paradox (+1 die
  and the Paradox roll is **rote**); can't contain Paradox or enter the Astral; as a Thrall, can't cast. Soul regained: drop those Conditions at once, WP 0 → 1; then WP back
  +1 each time she regains WP via rest/Virtue; Gnosis back 1/scene (soul from a mage) or 1/chapter (from a Sleeper); then 1 Integrity/Wisdom per rest [p. 98].
- **Soul stone**: object of Size ≤2 + imprint Signature Nimbus (1 Mana) + **1 Willpower dot**. Lowers her **max Gnosis by 1** while it exists. **Act of
  Hubris** (Understanding). Uses: Demesne foundation; Connected sympathy to its creator; **tool Yantra +2 for others (+3 if the creator's Gnosis is higher)**;
  counts as Dedicated for its creator; the creator senses magic on it; Legacy study. Customary return price: 3 services [p. 98].
- **Demesne**: 1–5 soul stones (area: 1–2 rooms … sprawling estate). Extended **Gnosis + Arcanum**, 3 successes/stone, 1 h/roll, 1 Mana per stone.
  Dramatic failure: all stones shatter, each contributor takes 1 aggravated. Losing a stone shrinks it; the creator may remake one [p. 98–99].
  Demesne effects: Ch6 (see §10).

## 8. Ch5 "Fallen Laws" — deltas from the core engine [p. 207–231]
Ch5 reprints the CoD core rules (Attributes, Skills, actions, Social maneuvering, combat, injury, equipment, Conditions/Tilts). Use `../cod_core/`; Mage-specific points:
- **Supernatural Tolerance for mages = Gnosis** (added to contested resistance vs other supernatural powers). **Spells are Withstood, not resisted/contested** [p. 213].
- Specialties: "casting Rote spells" can't be a Specialty; "Aimed Spells" (Athletics) etc. can [p. 209].
- **Doors** = lower of Resolve/Composure; **+2** if the goal would be a breaking point **or Act of Hubris**; **+1** if it blocks an Aspiration **or Obsession**; +1 vs Virtue [p. 215].
- **Killing blow** (dice pool + weapon, ignores armour) = Act of Hubris for Enlightened & Understanding Wisdom, even if the victim survives [p. 219]. Using a **human shield** likewise [p. 218].
- Defense vs firearms only with a spell/Attainment/power granting it (Fate/Space/Time Mage Armor, Acceleration…) [p. 217].
- Touching an opponent to deliver a touch spell: Dexterity + Brawl (or Weaponry) − Defense, no damage [p. 218]. Grapple casting −3 if you lost the grapple roll [p. 118, 218].
- **Resistant damage**: marked with a dot; **can't be healed or sped by magic** (incl. Pattern restoration) — only natural rates (bashing 15 min, lethal 2 days, aggravated 1 week). For mages usually
  from containing Paradox or Scouring [p. 224].
- Extreme environments, fire, electricity, poison, disease, drugs: as core (p. 224–226).

## 9. Places of power & realms (Ch6) [p. 239–252]
- **Sancta**: Safe Place + Sanctum Merits (Sanctum needed for the extra spell control) [p. 240].
- **Hallows**: Mana source (Hallow Merit); 3+ dots almost always on ley lines. **Ley lines/Nodes**: each line has a Resonance keyword; tass from a Node Hallow carries it; spending that Mana on a
  spell matching the Resonance = **+1 persona Yantra**. Node bonuses (~100 yd per ley line) to anyone who studies it with Sacred Geometry/Unveiling: **Forces** power stationary tech; **Life**
  advance plant life cycle ≤1 yr (a day + Int + Crafts/Survival); **Matter** aligned stationary objects heal 1 Structure/day; **Space** +2 to movement tasks; **Time** +2 Initiative [p. 241–242].
- **Verges** (natural) & **Demesnes** (soul-stone): **+2 Yantra** to spells using the Realm's Ruling Arcana (thematically fitting); such spells **don't risk Paradox while they stay in the Verge,
  unless a Sleeper is present at any point of the spell's Duration**; +2 to summon that Realm's entities. Natural Verges: Supernal phenomena manifest, no Dissonance; Demesnes: no spontaneous
  intruders, **are** subject to Dissonance; can launch Astral journeys from one's own (or permitted) Demesne. **Abyssal Verges**: −2 to natural-law Skill rolls; +2 to Gnosis rolls to invite
  Paradox (Scelesti). **Exarchate Verges**: bonus & Paradox immunity only for the Exarch's Arcanum, **+1 Reach** to such spells; entities loyal to the Exarch [p. 242–243].
- **Irises** (need their Key): Aberrances (Abyss — one-way), **Avernian Gates** (Underworld; every graveyard), Distortions (Space), **Shadowed Doors** (Shadow; Loci), Scars (Lower Depths — one-way),
  Soul Eyes (Wendings), **Synesi** (Astral, entered in trance), **Thurae** (Emanations; +2 to summoning there), enigmatic gates [p. 243].
- **Lower Depths**: realms missing a constituent of existence; entering = one-way annihilation short of archmastery [p. 244].
- **Underworld**: ghosts, chthonians, Autochthonous Depths, rivers, Deep Dominions ruled by Kerberoi, Old Laws. **Death spells −2 Paradox dice there**; living visitors' **Virtue/Vice reversed**
  (Vice = full WP, Virtue = 1) [p. 245].
- **Shadow**: everything crossing becomes ephemera (returns to normal on return; native ephemera arrives in Twilight). **Spirit spells −2 Paradox dice there** [p. 245–246].
- **Astral** [p. 246–250]:
  - Own dreams: **Resolve + Composure** to meditate while falling asleep (Mind spells bypass/enter others'); dream spells aren't Obvious to Sleepers.
  - **Entering the Astral**: at a place of power — own/same-Path/permitted **Demesne**; a **Hallow** (absorb & spend Mana = 6 − Hallow rating); or a **Synese** (its Key). Then
    **Resolve + Composure**: success → Oneiros over a scene/hour (exceptional: 1 turn). Astral Adept Merit: anywhere with ceremony + 1 WP.
  - **Mind spells −2 Paradox dice in the Astral.** In her **own Oneiros her spells never risk Paradox** (if they stay inside); in a Sleeper's Oneiros all magic risks Paradox. Killed by her own Oneiros' natives: just wakes (no Soul Shock).
  - **Dream form** traits: **Power = higher of Int/Pre** (others: Int), **Finesse = higher of Wits/Man** (others: Wits), **Resistance = higher of Res/Com** (others: Resolve); **Dream Health =
    Resistance + Gnosis-based trait max** (5 at G1–5, 6 at G6…); "killed" → **Soul Shocked**, return. **Amnion** (mages, instant): general armour = lower of Gnosis or highest Subtle Arcanum vs
    physical-seeming attacks & Ecstatic Wind; −2 Finesse tasks, −1 Defense; doesn't stack with "physical" armour but stacks with spells/Attainments. Path tools can be willed into being (ones used before).
  - Layers: dreams → **Oneiros** (personal soul; daimon & Goetia) → **Temenos** (collective; each step requires surviving a vision challenge; deciphering it = Arcane Beat; **every point is a +1 place
    Yantra**) → **Omphalos** → **Anima Mundi** (Swath, Spire Perilous, Dreaming Earth, Sidereal Wastes) → shore of **Oroboros** (Aeons; touching it = Abyss). Each threshold: Resolve + Composure.
  - **Ecstatic Wind** (Anima Mundi): Omphalos/Swath/Spire weak: roll **Gnosis + 1** damage every 24 h; Dreaming Earth: **Gnosis + 2** every hour; Sidereal Wastes: **Gnosis + 3** every action.
    Killed by it → Soul Shocked, −1 WP dot, often a Paradox Condition.
  - Returning: from own Oneiros reflexive; otherwise Resolve + Composure per threshold (no challenges), or instantly at the cost of **Soul Shocked**.
- **Emanations**: material realms per Arcanum (Mists = Time, Place Between = Space/Abyss, Yggdrasil = Spirit…), Verge benefits for their one Arcanum [p. 250–251].
- **Atlantean ruins**: adamas immune to Matter; Bound (see `06`) [p. 243–244].


## 10. Sleepers & magic [App. 1, p. 298–300]
- **Obvious magic** witnessed by a conscious, comprehending Sleeper: **+1 Paradox die** (a few: 9-again; foot traffic: 8-again; crowd: rote) — may make a no-Paradox spell risk it;
  Sleeper suffers a **breaking point**; mage commits an **Act of Hubris (Understanding/Enlightened)**; Sleeper forgets afterwards (**Quiescence**).
  Covert magic (no visible impossibility) avoids all this [p. 298]. **[interp]** "Obvious" = the effect itself is visibly impossible; cause-ambiguous effects (luck, a "heart attack")
  are covert.
- **Dissonance** (end of a scene where a Sleeper witnessed obvious Supernal magic): roll the Sleeper's **Integrity**, Withstood by the spell's dots / Supernal entity's Rank / Demesne's soul-stone
  count (multiple Sleepers: one roll with the dice trick). Dramatic failure: nothing + Sleeper breaking point. Success beyond Withstand: **spells lose one factor level per success** (primary
  last; then destroyed); Supernal entities take that much lethal; Demesnes suppressed for successes weeks. Exceptional: also a Reach effect stripped / aggravated / Demesne destroyed.
  Legacy Attainments & Emanations immune; Abyssal entities instead **heal and gain Essence** from it [p. 299].
- Sleepwalkers: no Paradox increase, no Dissonance, no Quiescence; can hold spells (`06` §E).

## 11. Duel Arcane [p. 294–295]
Formal Lex Magica duel inside a **Display of Power** (Prime 3) space; nothing physically happens.
1. **Stakes** agreed, plus the Condition the loser will suffer.
2. **Doors** = each duelist's **Willpower dots**; −1 if lovers; +1 if she declared her rival a nemesis; +1 if the rival declared intent to destroy her; +1 rival from an allied sect;
   +2 rival from a hostile sect.
3. **Initiative = Wits + Composure** (+d10 **[interp: as normal]**).
4. Actions: **Attack** = any **Attribute + Arcanum** fitting the described "could-have" spell; no Yantras, no Reach beyond free Reach; account for factors & Withstand. Success opens **one**
   Door (extra successes become bonus dice on her next roll); a failed attack bars reusing that Arcanum on that closed Door. **Defend** = forgo attack; incoming attacks become contested
   vs her Attribute + Arcanum. **Negotiate** = offer a mystically sealed promise; acceptance (any time) opens a Door.
5. Last Door opened → loser loses WP = Doors lost and gains **Defeated**; winner refreshes WP fully and gains **Triumphant**. Any execution happens afterwards in the Fallen World.
Display of Power +2 Reach + 1 Mana: Counterspells in the area are rote (seconds watch for cheating).

## 12. Ch7 setting mechanics (sample cities) [p. 267–283]
- **London**: Runewalking — routes through the city spelling High Speech words reward travellers with matching outcomes (ST; treat as Yantra/inspiration) [p. 267].
- **Los Angeles**: Astral entry via a Hallow costs 1 less Mana; entering the Temenos needs a **Gnosis** roll or you land in a different (LA-linked) realm; ~1000 Goetia-possessed people [p. 271].
- **Salamanca**: fleeting worlds via gates; bring an item back = 1 WP + Gnosis roll [p. 275].
- **Tokyo**: **Ansho** dead zones: −1 Mana/turn, no new spells (Attainments work), own unrelinquished spells fade after Gnosis turns, spells on those inside halve Potency each turn.
  **Beacons**: released Paradox alerts all supernatural beings (incl. Supernatural-Merit humans) within 10 yd × Gnosis + 10 yd per Paradox success [p. 277–278].
- **Tucson**: phantom mission bells ring for the dead / soon-to-die [p. 279+].
