## 2. Grappling is contested—but only after the grab succeeds

The rules divide grappling into two stages.

### Establishing the grapple

To grab someone:

> Strength + Brawl − target’s Defense

This is an ordinary attack roll with Defense as a passive penalty. The target does not make a separate roll.

Therefore Marik’s initial attempt was:

- Strength 2 + Brawl 1 − Isabella’s Defense
- Against the original Defense 6: reduced to a chance die
- Against revised Defense 7: also reduced to a chance die

So using Defense for that particular roll was correct.

On an ordinary success, the grapple is established. On an exceptional success, it is established and the attacker immediately chooses a grapple move.

### Once they are grappling

On subsequent turns, grappling becomes contested:

> Strength + Brawl versus Strength + Brawl

This happens on the higher of the two participants’ Initiatives. Defense is ignored. The winner chooses one grapple move; an exceptional success permits two.

With the revised sheet:

- Marik: Strength 2 + Brawl 1 = **3 dice**
- Isabella: effective Strength 4 + Brawl 1 = **5 dice**

Possible moves include Break Free, Damage, Hold, Control Weapon, Disarm, Drop Prone, and Restrain.

Handcuffs are a special additional grapple option:

> Strength + Brawl − opponent’s Strength

Thus handcuffing is not penalized by Defense either. The handcuff rules use Isabella’s effective Strength 4, giving Marik another chance die: \(2 + 1 - 4 = -1\).

Because Marik failed the initial grab in the scene, no grapple was established and the contested stage never occurred. That was why Isabella did not roll against her.

One additional correction surfaced during verification: the officer’s Merit should have been **Iron Will 2**, not “Indomitable 2.” Iron Will does not simply add two dice. When Marik spends Willpower in a Social contest, it substitutes her Resolve 4 for the usual +3 Willpower bonus, and contested rolls gain 8-again. Consequently, the earlier Majesty rolls involving Marik used incorrect resistance pools and should be rerolled rather than treated as valid results.
